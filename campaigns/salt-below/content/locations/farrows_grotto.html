Farrow's Grotto

LOCATION · The Shallows

A low reef cave mouth opens into a chain of air-pocketed grottoes just off the main reef line — sheltered enough that [[freedivers]] uses the outer chamber to train newcomers on breath control and confined-space work. It's popular precisely because it feels safe: shallow, close to Port Calder, and forgiving of a bad first dive.

Most crews only push past the outer chamber at slack tide, when the current stops fighting the entrance and the trapped air pockets go still. Even then, the inner chambers are informally off-limits — not by any Concordat rule, just an unspoken understanding among divers who've been in far enough to hear something move that wasn't them.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The trapped air pockets carry a faint sulfur smell that gets stronger, not weaker, toward the third chamber.
  • Sound behaves strangely past the outer chamber — every noise arrives twice, a beat apart, like a bad echo that isn't quite an echo.
  • [[ivo_marlstone]] has quietly pushed further into the grotto than the Freedivers' own safety line allows, twice now.

DM Notes

Something has been dragging prey remains into the third chamber and stacking the bones with unsettling neatness — a light, early foreshadowing of a Shallows creature (see the creature entities from t015) without committing to a specific stat block yet. This is a good low-stakes site to let a curious or overconfident party (or Ivo, if they're following his lead) get a first scare that costs them nothing but nerve.
