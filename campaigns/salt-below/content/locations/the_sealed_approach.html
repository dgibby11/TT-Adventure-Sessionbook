The Sealed Approach

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

[[the_long_hush]] ends at a gate — the first deliberately-built structure since the colonnade, and by far the largest: two ward-carved stone leaves, easily thirty feet high, set into rock that was cut specifically to hold them. This was built to close, not to welcome. Ward-glyphs matching the pattern from [[the_wardwrights_undercroft]] cover every visible surface, wired to the same failing network.

A crack runs the gate's full height, wide enough to swim a hand through. It wasn't always there — the stone around its edges shows old, deliberate repair work, cracked open again since.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The crack is the source of the directional pull felt as far back as [[threshwater_stair]] — current runs steadily through the gap, one direction, into the dark beyond (no check needed).
  • The repair scarring around the crack is old — decades, maybe longer — but the crack itself is recent enough that its edges haven't weathered smooth (Investigation DC 15).
  • A single dark ward-stone, larger than any seen at the undercroft, sits in a housing at the gate's center — the one link in the whole network the party can see failing in real time, if they linger (no check needed, but unsettling enough to warrant a Wisdom save at the DM's discretion for particularly dread-sensitive characters).

DM Notes

This is the literal threshold into the climactic dungeon (t024), the same structural role [[the_last_landing]] and [[the_last_descent]] played at their region transitions. Everything beyond this gate belongs to that task — hold all containment-site content, all direct sign of [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]], and any combat until then. The crack and its failing central ward-stone are meant to visually resolve [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]] for players who've been paying attention: this is what "the deal is breaking" looks like, made literal and physical, not abstract.
