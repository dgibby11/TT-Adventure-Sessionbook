The Kettle

LOCATION · The Shallows

A wide, bowl-shaped depression in the reef where the current runs in a slow, permanent gyre — divers who go slack for even a moment find themselves circling back to where they started, sometimes for hours. Locals call it the Kettle because of the way loose silt and small debris churn at the bottom like something's stirring the pot.

It's rich water: the gyre keeps concentrating salvage at the bowl's center, which is exactly why the Concordat licenses it heavily and taxes it hard. Every crew that dives the Kettle files a report, and every report gets read.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The current is stronger, and stranger, than the depth should produce — something below is affecting the flow, though nothing here says what.
  • Debris at the bowl's center skews older and stranger than the reef around it: worked metal, fragments of old-kingdom glasswork, the occasional bone that isn't fish.
  • [[salvage_concordat]] licensing officers are unusually strict about the Kettle specifically, more than the site's difficulty alone would explain — [[petra_wexler]] in particular reads every report filed on it personally.

DM Notes

Good site for the party's first sense that the Concordat's caution isn't purely bureaucratic instinct in every case — the Kettle is one of the "irregular" sites referenced in [[salvage_concordat]]'s DM notes, quietly over-licensed rather than closed outright because closing it would draw questions the guild doesn't want asked. Keep the gyre itself unexplained for now; it's a slow-build hook toward the central mystery (see [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]]), not something to resolve at this depth.
