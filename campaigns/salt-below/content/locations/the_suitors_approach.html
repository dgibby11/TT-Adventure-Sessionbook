The Suitors' Approach

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A formal processional street leading toward [[vantmoor_hall]], lined with narrow townhouses that once housed petitioners waiting on the crown's judgment. Every door still bears its plaque, legible under a film of silt if wiped clean — a name, a date, and a case number that was never closed.

A few [[drowned_choir|Drowned Choir]] converts squat in the emptiest houses — [[corin_draw]] among the longest-settled — but otherwise the street is quiet in a way that makes salvage crews walk faster than they'd admit to.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The plaques list unresolved petitions — several naming plaintiffs against the crown itself.
  • One plaque bears a surname a sharp-eyed player might recognize from Port Calder's founding families.
  • Nothing moves here that isn't the current.

DM Notes

The founding-family surname is a plantable hook, not a fixed fact — pick a name from an existing Port Calder NPC's lineage on the fly if a player bites, or leave it as unresolved color if no one does. Either way, it should suggest the old kingdom's grievances never actually got settled, just drowned.
