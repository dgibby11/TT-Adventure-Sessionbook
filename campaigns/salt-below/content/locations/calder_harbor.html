Calder Harbor

LOCATION · Port Calder

The harbor that gives the city its name — a deep, sheltered cove walled in by breakwaters, half of them built from salvaged old-kingdom seawall stone. Every dive crew in Port Calder launches from here, and every haul that comes up out of the water passes through it first.

By day the water is a chaos of dive boats, salvage barges, and Concordat inspection skiffs jockeying for room. Licensed crews fly the Concordat's blue-and-gold pennant; the rest keep their heads down and their manifests vague.

Notable Features

  • [[the_long_docks]] — The piers proper, where crews load and stage.
  • [[harbormasters_post]] — Overlooks the harbor mouth; tracks every berth and tide.
  • [[the_drowned_bell]] — Hung at the breakwater's outer edge.
  • The old breakwater stone — half-submerged old-kingdom masonry, worn but still carved, visible at low tide.

DM Notes

Good default location for introducing dive-contract hooks, Concordat inspectors, or a first uneasy sighting of something surfacing that shouldn't. The harbor connects narratively to every region in the level arc — anything pulled up from the Shallows, Drowned Districts, or Deep Kingdom eventually passes through here.
