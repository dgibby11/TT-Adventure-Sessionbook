The Marrow Trawler

LOCATION · The Shallows

A mid-sized fishing trawler rests level on a sand shelf in open, well-lit water — easy to find, easy to see coming, which somehow makes it worse. Sails have long since rotted away, but the hull and rigging are otherwise intact, and the nets are still coiled on deck, heavy with a catch that was never hauled in.

[[harbor_watch_garrison]] keeps the Marrow nominally closed pending an incident review that's gone nowhere in three years. Nothing stops unlicensed crews from working it anyway, and plenty do — there's real salvage value in an intact hull this size, and the closure order has no teeth behind it.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Unnervingly little marine growth on the hull for a wreck this old.
  • The ship's log, still legible in the waterlogged cabin, ends mid-sentence — no storm noted, no distress called, just silence.
  • Every net on deck is still weighted and coiled for hauling, as if the crew meant to come right back to it.

DM Notes

The Marrow's full crew of nine were never found, alive or dead. This is the third of [[harbor_watch_garrison]]'s three unresolved duty-board incidents — the only one with no bodies and no obvious cause, which is exactly why the Watch keeps quietly re-filing it instead of closing it. [[fenn_hurley]] would rather this one stay buried; [[tam_ostler]] is the one still privately working it off the books and is a strong candidate to hand the party this thread directly. Leave the actual cause open for now — a good later tie-in once the Drowned Districts or Deep Kingdom creatures (t020/t025) are authored.
