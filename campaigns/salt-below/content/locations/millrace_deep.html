Millrace Deep

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

The flooded machinery pit of an old kingdom watermill, wheel and gearing still largely intact. Both [[freedivers]] and [[salvage_concordat]] crews work it in rotation for scrap-metal contracts — profitable, mundane, and genuinely dangerous.

Rusted gearing groans and shifts under the current, occasionally catching and turning without warning.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Jagged, half-turning gear teeth can snag gear or limbs if a diver isn't careful navigating the pit.
  • Both salvage crews maintain a rough truce over shift schedules to avoid working the pit at the same time.
  • No omens, no supernatural weight — just old, dangerous machinery doing what old, dangerous machinery does.

DM Notes

Straightforward hazard/salvage site — jammed machinery as an environmental obstacle, no supernatural element needed. Useful as a mechanical breather between omen-heavy sites like [[thornwake_hollow]] or [[graves_end_locks]].
