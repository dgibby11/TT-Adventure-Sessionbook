The Harbormaster's Post

LOCATION · Port Calder

A weathered two-story shack on stilts at the harbor mouth, signal flags snapping overhead and a small tide-bell strung from the roofline. From here the harbormaster tracks every berth, every tide, and every boat that comes and goes — officially under Harbor Watch authority, though the job is more logbook than law.

The ground floor is open to the public for berth assignments and weather postings; the upper floor is [[yselda_wrenn|Harbormaster Yselda Wrenn]]'s own — charts, tide tables, and a logbook she keeps with more care than the job strictly requires.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The tide-board is updated obsessively, tracking currents down to the hour.
  • [[yselda_wrenn|Harbormaster Wrenn]] logs "unusual finds" separately from standard cargo manifests — a habit no one asked her to keep.
  • She's quick to warn crews off certain currents without always explaining why.
