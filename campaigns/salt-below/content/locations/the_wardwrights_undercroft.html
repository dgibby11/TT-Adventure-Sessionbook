The Ward-Wrights' Undercroft

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

A low workshop-archive reached through a side passage off [[suppliants_colonnade]], built and used by the Ward-Wrights — the old kingdom's binding-artificers, the tradesfolk who actually cut and set the containment work rather than the Wardens who guarded it or the ritualists who cast it. Rack after rack of fist-sized ward-stones line the walls, each etched with the same repeating glyph-set, each connected by corroded bronze conduit to somewhere deeper in.

Roughly a third of the ward-stones have gone dark. Not shattered — dim, the way a coal dims rather than the way glass breaks. The workshop's own tools are still racked in careful order, as though the Ward-Wrights meant to come back.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The dark ward-stones aren't clustered — they're scattered evenly through the racks, suggesting slow, uniform failure rather than a single point of damage (Investigation DC 15).
  • Schematic tablets nearby show the ward-stone network extending well past this room, toward [[cindrel_vault]] on one line and toward [[velmarch_spire]]'s original working-schedule on another.
  • One workbench holds a half-finished replacement ward-stone, tools laid down mid-work and never picked back up.

An Item of Note

Among the finished stock is a ward-stone unlike the others — larger, more heavily worked, clearly meant as a master component rather than a standard replacement part. See [[the_wardwrights_binding_seal]].

DM Notes

This is the campaign's clearest mechanical (rather than narrative) evidence for [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]] — the binding isn't failing because of one dramatic act, it's failing because roughly a third of its physical infrastructure has quietly gone dark over three centuries, worsened by every relic hauled up by [[salvage_concordat]] crews and every ritual performed at [[the_drowned_bell]] by [[drowned_choir]]. The half-finished replacement stone is a small tragedy, not a mystery to solve: the Ward-Wrights ran out of time before the kingdom drowned, same as everyone else. Do not resolve why they stopped — [[mireths_reckoning]] already answers that at the "how much longer do we have" level; this room answers it at the "here is physically what's failing" level.
