The Sunken Rostrum

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A raised formal speaking platform facing a collapsed public square, near [[the_chancellors_rest]]. Silent and empty now — the old kingdom's court of public appeals, long since adjourned.

The platform itself is oddly undamaged compared to the ruin facing it, as though the water rose around it rather than through it.

What the Party Might Notice

  • A weathered but legible inscription references a final address given from this rostrum — a sovereign's decree, not a plea for rescue (History DC 14).
  • The square facing the rostrum shows signs of having been full at the time of the address — worn stone in patterns consistent with a large gathered crowd.
  • No bodies, no remains — whatever happened here, people had time to leave after the address, not during a sudden catastrophe.

DM Notes

Pairs with [[the_leaning_archive]]'s sealed-wards decree — together they build the picture that the drowning was ordered from the top, not suffered as an accident, feeding [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] directly. Good site for the party to piece together the "evacuation, not disaster" reveal if they've already found the archive.
