The Last Accord

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

Past the unfinished alcove at [[the_binding_lattice]]'s edge, a small side-chamber holds a single unbroken text, carved wall to wall in the same formal hand seen throughout the Deep Kingdom's public monuments — but whole here, not fragmentary. This is the covenant itself: the terms the old kingdom's rulers struck with what they found beneath their capital, set down in full and never damaged or defaced.

Where [[mireths_reckoning]] gave a private confession and [[ilyndras_sanctum]] a devotional relief, this is the plain administrative record — clauses, terms, and a witnessed signing, written by people who understood exactly what they were doing.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The text states outright that the kingdom sank itself deliberately as the price of containment, not as an accident interrupting one — the definitive confirmation of [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] (Investigation or Religion DC 14 to translate the formal old-tongue legal phrasing; the substance is clear to anyone who can read it at all).
  • A list of witness-signatories at the text's foot includes a name that matches [[mireths_reckoning]]'s carved confession exactly — Mireth was present at the original signing, not merely a later custodian of the deal (no check needed once both rooms have been read).
  • A closing clause describes the deal's terms as renewable "by hand and seal, so long as one remains who can carry both" — a direct, plain-language pointer toward whatever is waiting at [[the_renewal_font]] (Investigation DC 15).

DM Notes

This is the single cleanest, most unambiguous confirmation of [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] in the whole campaign — safe to use as the "full reveal" moment referenced in that entity's DM notes. Do not gate the finale's choice on the party finding this room; it closes the historical mystery, not the mechanical one, and [[the_binding_lattice]]/[[the_renewal_font]] work on their own without it. The renewal clause is a deliberate nudge toward the "renew" branch for a party that's been leaning destroy-only without realizing an alternative exists.
