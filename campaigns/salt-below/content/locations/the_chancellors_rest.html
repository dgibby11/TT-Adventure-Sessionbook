The Chancellor's Rest

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A fortified mausoleum-house, sealed under old-kingdom state wardwork rather than religious protection — a distinction that matters. Unlike most of the district, no [[drowned_choir]] presence has claimed it; the silt here is undisturbed, the current dead still, as if the whole building has been quietly waiting.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The seal wards are recognizably state magic, not devotional work.
  • Nothing in the room drifts the way loose silt normally would.
  • The seal was built to keep someone out, not just keep something in.

DM Notes

The chancellor's private papers inside can seed a real fragment of [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] — a fragment implicating the crown's own choices in what happened, not just misfortune. A strong player-found-lore location; don't over-explain it in the room text, let the papers (as a handout or DM-read excerpt) do the work.
