Bellwrit Span

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A processional bridge of formal old-kingdom stonework, spanning a wide flooded plaza beneath [[vantmoor_hall]]. Structurally sound, and avoided more out of habit than any particular danger.

When [[the_drowned_bell]] is rung topside, it isn't heard here so much as felt — a pressure change underwater ears shouldn't be able to register, moving through the span like something settling its weight.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The pressure shift is reliably timed to a known ringing of the bell, not random or continuous.
  • No sound accompanies the sensation — it registers as touch and inner-ear pressure, not hearing.
  • Local divers avoid crossing the span in the minutes right after a ringing, without a clear explanation why.

DM Notes

A clean, repeatable way to let the party physically feel the bell's reach without confirming what it means — good for building dread ahead of the Deep Kingdom chapters. Coordinate timing with the DM ringing [[the_drowned_bell]] topside if the party is exploring both sites in the same session.
