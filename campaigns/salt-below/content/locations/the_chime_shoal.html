The Chime Shoal

LOCATION · The Shallows

A low reef shoal scattered with old-kingdom debris — window-glass, chain, fragments of something like a bell — that hums faintly when the current runs through it just right. Divers say the shoal "sings" on certain tides; sailors passing over it sometimes hear the hum through the hull.

It's not a rich site by salvage standards, and the Concordat licenses it cheaply, but the [[drowned_choir]] treats it as sacred ground: small, unofficial pilgrimages happen here on tide-nights, candles set adrift over the shoal rather than carried to [[the_drowned_bell]] itself.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The hum is faint but unmistakable on the right tide — not quite music, not quite noise.
  • Choir pilgrims leave small offerings on the shoal: coins, carved wood, the occasional old-kingdom fragment returned rather than sold.
  • Fisherfolk avoid working nets over the shoal on tide-nights, less out of belief than habit passed down from those who did believe.
  • [[sera_vondt]] tends the vigil most tide-nights; newer converts follow her lead without being told to.

DM Notes

The Chime Shoal's hum resonates with the same working that binds [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]] — a faint, harmless echo of whatever [[the_drowned_bell]] does at full strength (see [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]]). Keep this connection purely atmospheric at this depth; it should feel like local color and Choir superstition, not a mechanical clue, until much later chapters make the pattern worth noticing. A good site for the party's first ambient exposure to Choir practice without meeting a named member.
