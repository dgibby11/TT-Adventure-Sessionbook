The Ledger Wreck

LOCATION · The Shallows

A modest Concordat records-cutter, officially "lost with all documents" in a storm two years back. It sits in shallow water close enough to town that the story never quite made sense to the divers who actually looked at it — the hull is scored open along one seam, deliberately, not stove in the way a storm wreck should be.

[[salvage_concordat]] refuses to license this site, no exceptions, no explanation given beyond "settled matter."

What the Party Might Notice

  • A strongbox is visible through a breached cabin window, still sealed, still exactly where it sank.
  • The scoring along the hull's seam is even, deliberate, and clearly tool-made — not storm damage.
  • No Concordat license has ever been issued for this wreck, which in a city that licenses everything is its own kind of confession.
  • The strongbox, once opened, holds a modest 40 gp in old coin and Concordat scrip — a strange amount to scuttle a boat over, which is exactly the point.

DM Notes

This is where [[coraline_bray]]'s private incident ledger should surface, or a partial early version of it — she had this cutter scuttled rather than let its cargo of settled "irregularity" reports reach the mainland archive. Recovering the strongbox is a strong Concordat-internal plot hook: proof the cover-ups aren't just bureaucratic caution but active, deliberate concealment. Bray would go to real lengths to keep the party from opening it.
