The Drowned Bell

LOCATION · Port Calder

A massive bell of green-black bronze, unmistakably old-kingdom make, hung in a timber frame at the outer edge of the harbor breakwater. Salvage crews raised it from the reef decades ago — long before licensing, back when anyone with a boat and nerve could dive — and the town has rung it on the hour ever since, a duty-watch tradition nobody remembers starting.

Its tone is low and carries far, audible across the whole harbor and out past the breakwater on a calm day. Most of Port Calder sets its day by it without a second thought.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The bell bears old-kingdom lettering along its rim — not a maker's mark, but something none of the town's scholars have ever fully translated.
  • Longtime dockworkers insist the bell's tone changes, very slightly, before bad weather — and lately, before nothing in particular.
  • It was rung out of rhythm recently, off the duty schedule, with no one owning up to it.

DM Notes

The bell was cast as part of the original working that keeps It That Kept the Kingdom contained — the lettering is a warning, not a dedication. Ringing it in a specific pattern (not known on the surface) can be felt below by whatever's down there. Treat the "rang out of rhythm" rumor as a slow-burn hook: something below is testing the bell, or something on the surface already knows more than it should.
