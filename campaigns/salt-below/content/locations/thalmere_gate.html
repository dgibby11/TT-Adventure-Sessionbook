Thalmere Gate

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

Past [[the_last_descent]], the dark resolves into shape: a monumental gatehouse, twin towers still standing to their full original height, doors long since rotted from titanic hinges that remain intact. This is the first old-kingdom architecture a diver meets that isn't ruin — merely empty, and colder for it.

Formal old-tongue lettering spans the lintel, whole and legible where every equivalent inscription in the Drowned Districts above was worn or broken. Nothing has looted it. Nothing, this deep, has needed to.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The lintel inscription is a title, not a name — a formal address to whoever approaches, in a register no living scholar in Port Calder fully reads (History DC 16 for a partial translation).
  • The gate's scale dwarfs anything built topside; this was clearly meant to announce a capital, not merely mark a boundary.
  • No silt drift, no scavenger life, no current — the water past the gate is unnervingly still.

DM Notes

This is the formal opening of the Deep Kingdom region — play the shift in scale and stillness as the headline beat, not a specific danger. The untranslatable lintel title can be revisited later once the party has more fragments to cross-reference (see [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]]); don't resolve its meaning yet.
