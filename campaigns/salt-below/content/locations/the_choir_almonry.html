The Choir Almonry

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A converted almshouse, old-kingdom bones with practical Choir repairs — nothing ornamental, all function. [[deacon_rill]] runs supply and shelter here for stragglers, lapsed converts, and the occasional lost diver.

It's the warmest, most welcoming space the Choir maintains anywhere in the district: mended nets, lantern-light, and no expectation that anyone who shelters here owe the Choir anything in return.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Deacon Rill treats every visitor, faithful or not, with the same practical care.
  • Supplies here are genuinely stretched thin — this isn't a wealthy operation, it's a determined one.
  • No recruitment pressure, no doctrine pushed on anyone who simply needs shelter.

DM Notes

Deliberately humanizing site for [[drowned_choir]] — genuine charity, no hidden agenda. A good place for a party to owe the Choir a real, uncomplicated debt before the campaign eventually pushes them toward opposition. Adjoins [[the_hollow_choir_yard]] for scene pacing if the party visits both.
