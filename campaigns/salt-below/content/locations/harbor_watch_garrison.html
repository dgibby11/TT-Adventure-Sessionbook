The Harbor Watch Garrison

LOCATION · Port Calder

A squat stone blockhouse overlooking the harbor mouth, built around the bones of an old-kingdom seawall tower. It's patched, drafty, and chronically undermanned — Harbor Watch is the closest thing Port Calder has to law, and it has nowhere near the numbers the job actually needs.

Recruits drill in the yard most mornings; the rest of the garrison is buried in incident reports that never seem to get fully resolved.

Notable Features

  • A drill yard, usually short a few bodies.
  • A duty board tracking open incidents — long, and rarely getting shorter.
  • The old tower stair, leading up to a lookout post with a clear view of the harbor mouth.

DM Notes

Home base for [[harbor_watch]] — its key NPCs [[fenn_hurley]], [[tam_ostler]], and [[brack_fennimore]] are stationed here. The duty board's three unresolved Shallows incidents are a ready-made hook into early mid-game plot: two now trace to specific sites — [[anchors_grave]] ("the wrecks moving against each other") and [[reavers_ribs]] (two vanished apprentice divers) — leaving one incident still unplaced for a future iteration to seed.
