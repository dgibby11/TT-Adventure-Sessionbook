The Brined Anchor

LOCATION · Port Calder

A broad, low tavern built from the timbers of a decommissioned salvage ship, with a second floor of cheap, clean rooms above. It's loud, it's crowded most nights, and it's the closest thing Port Calder has to neutral ground — Concordat clerks, Harbor Watch off duty, and Freediver crews all drink here without much trouble.

A corkboard by the door carries the day's job postings. Most bear a Concordat seal; a few don't, and those tend to pay better for exactly that reason.

Notable Features

  • The job-postings corkboard, official and otherwise.
  • A hearth-side table that's unofficially reserved for whoever's buying the next round of stories.
  • Rooms upstairs, cheap and clean — a natural party base of operations.

DM Notes

Recommended default rest/home-base location for the party. The innkeeper is a strong candidate for the Freedivers' first point of contact once that faction is authored (t008) — trades in information as freely as ale, and has opinions about who's diving too deep, too fast.
