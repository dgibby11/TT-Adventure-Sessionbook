Thornwake Hollow

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A sunken garden-hollow behind a collapsed manor wall, once ornamental, now a dead zone of bare stone. It's noticeably colder than the surrounding district, and nothing lives here — no fish, no growth, no scavengers picking at the ruin the way they do everywhere else.

The [[freedivers]] have surveyed it twice as a potential site. [[ivo_marlstone]] has pushed both times to chart it further; [[ruen_saltmarrow]] and [[sela_vantry]] have both quietly discouraged a third attempt.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Even scavenger fish that pick at every other wreck in the district give this hollow a wide, consistent berth (Survival DC 13).
  • The cold is even and total, not a thermocline or current effect a diver would recognize.
  • Freedivers gear left behind from the last survey is untouched and undamaged, just abandoned mid-task.

DM Notes

Ivo's ambition versus Ruen and Sela's caution (seeded t008) can come to a head here — a good site to force that internal Freedivers tension into the open if the party is embedded with the faction. Keep the hollow itself passive and wrong rather than actively hostile; the dread is in what it withholds, not what it does. See [[silt_wraith]] for what a lingering sighting here might actually be — never confirm it as the cause of the hollow's wrongness, just one possible thread.
