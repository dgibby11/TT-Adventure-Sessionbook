The Open Wound

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

Past [[the_sealed_approach]]'s broken gate, the tone changes completely. Nothing here was built for people — no floors, no doorways, no ceilings scaled to a human stride. Ward-conduits from [[the_wardwrights_undercroft]]'s network dive straight into raw, unworked rock, as if the kingdom's masons had reached the edge of their own architecture and simply kept the wiring going into whatever was already there. This is the site the kingdom was built around, not a room the kingdom built.

The gate's current pours through here and drops away into a wide, dark shaft at the chamber's center, the true source of every pull felt since [[threshwater_stair]]. Three passages lead onward from the shaft's rim, each marked with its own distinct hand: precise Ward-Wright glyphwork, blunt Warden watch-marks, and a third set, cruder and older-looking than either, that matches nothing seen anywhere above.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The three onward passages are individually marked and clearly meant to be chosen between, not walked in a fixed order (Perception DC 16 to read the three glyph-sets as distinct rather than decorative).
  • Every wall surface here is either bare rock or ward-conduit — nothing carved for habitation, worship, or record-keeping the way every other Deep Kingdom site has been (Investigation DC 15).
  • The pull toward the central shaft is strongest here of anywhere in the region; dropped or loose gear drifts steadily toward it rather than settling (no check needed).

DM Notes

This is the climactic dungeon's hub room. The three marked passages lead to [[the_binding_lattice]] (Ward-Wright marks), [[the_vigil_wrack]] (Warden marks), and [[the_listening_deep]] (the unmatched third set) — the party can take them in any order. [[the_last_accord]] and [[the_faithfuls_end]] branch off those first three rather than off this hub directly (the_last_accord off the lattice route, the_faithfuls_end off the vigil route) so the party doesn't have to visit all eight rooms to reach the finale; treat this hub as a mission-select screen, not a mandatory checklist. All roads eventually rejoin at [[the_undoing]] at the shaft's bottom. Keep this room itself free of combat — it's tone-setting and navigation, not an encounter.
