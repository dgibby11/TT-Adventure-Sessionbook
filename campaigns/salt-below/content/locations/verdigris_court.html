Verdigris Court

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A small, formal courtyard dominated by a bronze statue gone entirely green, its face worn to near-blankness. A faint metallic taste hangs in the water near its base. It functions as a shrine annex to [[chancel_deep]], though [[sister_meck]] visits alone more often than official [[drowned_choir]] business would explain.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The statue's face was deliberately smoothed at some point — not simple decay.
  • No plaque or marker identifies who the statue depicts.
  • A single unlit candle-stub is often left at the statue's base.

DM Notes

A good spot for a private, unguarded party–Meck scene — she prays here without an audience, which is unusual enough for the Choir to be worth a player noticing. Useful as an early crack in the Choir's united front if the DM wants to seed Meck's doubt ahead of a larger role later.
