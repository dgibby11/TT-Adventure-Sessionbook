The Licensing House

LOCATION · Port Calder

A blocky, well-kept stone hall facing the harbor, its doors flanked by Concordat banners. Every legal dive in Port Calder starts here — a manifest filed, a site permit stamped, a cut of the haul promised in advance. The line outside runs long most mornings.

Inside, clerks work behind a long counter, checking permits against a public manifest board that lists every currently licensed dive site. Guild officers move through the crowd, polite and watchful.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The manifest board changes daily — some sites are delisted overnight with no explanation posted.
  • Clerks get visibly uncomfortable if asked directly about a delisted site.
  • Guild officers escort certain "recovered materials" out a side door instead of through the public counter.
  • No manifest is stamped without a clerk sighting both a [[pressure_ward_token]] and a [[brinelung_mask]] on every diver listed — the one part of the process the Concordat never cuts corners on, whatever else it lets slide.

DM Notes

This is the Salvage Concordat's public face — bureaucratic, not menacing. The delisted-sites detail is a standing hook: the Concordat is quietly pulling licenses for dive sites where something bad has been found, rather than warning divers off directly, to avoid panic and lost revenue. See the Salvage Concordat faction entity (once authored) for the guild's full agenda.
