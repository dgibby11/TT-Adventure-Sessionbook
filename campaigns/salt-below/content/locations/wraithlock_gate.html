Wraithlock Gate

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A monumental formal gatehouse, once a toll checkpoint into the old kingdom's inner wards. Disputed ground today: [[harbor_watch]] patrols it as an extension of [[ashgate_wardhouse]]'s beat, while the [[salvage_concordat]] claims salvage rights on the gatehouse vaults beneath it.

Neither side has filed a formal complaint about the other's presence — both would rather quietly coexist than draw outside attention to what's really behind the gate. [[dessa_wrack|Corporal Dessa Wrack]] walks this beat for the Watch and is the reason the arrangement has held as long as it has.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Fresh rope and lantern-oil residue from both Watch patrols and Concordat crews, neither acknowledging the other's activity (Insight DC 12).
  • The vault doors behind the gate are locked with a Concordat seal, but the gatehouse itself is marked with Harbor Watch patrol chalk.
  • Neither faction reacts with hostility to the other's presence if the party mentions seeing both.

DM Notes

A low-stakes jurisdiction friction site — useful if the party ever needs a faction favor and has to weigh which side to help, however small the ask. Not a plot-critical location; keep it light unless a player leans into the tension. A [[warden_husk]] guarding the vault approach would explain why neither faction has pushed past the gate itself, if the DM wants to raise the stakes.
