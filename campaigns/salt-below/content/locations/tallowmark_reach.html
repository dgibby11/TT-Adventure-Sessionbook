Tallowmark Reach

LOCATION · The Shallows

A long stretch of collapsed formal wall runs along the reef here, old-kingdom boundary stones still standing at regular intervals down its length. It's a second, lesser-known checkpoint to [[aldergate_shoal]]'s — [[salvage_concordat]] surveyors mark it on their charts as the outer edge of the "licensed zone" proper, past which the guild stops guaranteeing rescue response times.

By day the water here is open and sunlit, unremarkable but for the boundary stones themselves, each humming faintly underhand at a slightly different pitch from its neighbors. Well after dusk, the stones hold onto a little residual glow that no Concordat surveyor has satisfactorily explained — officially "mineral phosphorescence," unofficially a subject nobody presses too hard.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Each boundary stone carries a different formal old-kingdom title, as though marking the edge of nine separate estates rather than one continuous wall.
  • The stones' faint hum shifts in a pattern that seems to track something, though nothing is visible to explain what.
  • The wall's collapse pattern reads as deliberate rather than storm damage — pushed outward, from the inside.

DM Notes

A quiet companion piece to [[aldergate_shoal]] — reinforce that the Shallows outskirts were once a real, inhabited border, not just ruins. The "pushed outward from the inside" detail is a small, deniable clue toward [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]] for a party that's paying close attention; it doesn't need to be resolved here.
