The Regnant's Seat

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

Where [[aurewake_concourse]] was always leading: a domed audience hall raised on a stepped dais, built to be seen — and heard — from the avenue's full length. The dome's acoustics are still perfect, amplifying even a diver's own bubbles into something uncomfortably close to a held breath. This is the old kingdom's true center, the room every side avenue and terrace ultimately answered to.

The dais itself is empty. No throne, no seat of any kind — just a plinth, its surface as deliberately bare as the statue-footprints outside. Whatever sat here was taken as carefully as everything else the capital's last custodians chose to remove.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The dais holds no throne — an empty plinth, the seat removed as deliberately as the statues on [[aurewake_concourse]] (Investigation DC 15).
  • Wall reliefs depict a coronation rite matching no ritual practiced anywhere in modern Port Calder, old-kingdom or Choir (History DC 16).
  • A cold, clean draft moves through the dome that shouldn't exist this deep — the water here is not as still as everywhere else in the capital.

DM Notes

The capital's true hub — branches to [[hallowmere_gallery]], [[ilyndras_sanctum]], and onward via [[threshwater_stair]]. The unexplained draft is the campaign's first non-symbolic hint that [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]] is close and active rather than merely a story the party has been told — play it as atmosphere only, never a direct manifestation. The missing throne is deliberately never explained here; save it, if at all, for the climactic dungeon (t024).
