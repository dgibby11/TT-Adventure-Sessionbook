Sawback Reef

LOCATION · The Shallows

A shallow ridge of serrated coral just past Calder Harbor's mouth, close enough to town that a rowboat can reach it inside an hour. It's the first real dive site most Port Calder divers ever work — not dangerous by Shallows standards, but jagged enough to teach respect for the reef before crews go looking for trouble further out.

By midmorning the ridge is crowded with training dives and licensed contract crews working the outer shelf; by dusk it empties out to a handful of night-fishers and the odd freediver working alone, unwilling to share the easy money with a crowd.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The coral here grows in unnaturally straight rows along old, buried stonework — the reef grew over something built, not the other way around.
  • Freedivers use Sawback as an informal proving ground; a crew that can't handle its currents isn't ready for anything deeper.
  • Licensed [[salvage_concordat]] contracts here are common, cheap, and closely inspected — a low-stakes way for new PCs to get their first taste of salvage work.

DM Notes

Sawback Reef is the campaign's easiest onramp into actual dive-site play — good for a session-one contract with [[freedivers]] before the party has any reason to distrust anyone. The buried stonework under the coral is old-kingdom outskirts architecture (see [[port_calder]]'s geography), a quiet first hint that "the ruins" aren't as far from the surface as Port Calder likes to pretend.
