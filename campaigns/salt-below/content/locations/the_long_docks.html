The Long Docks

LOCATION · Port Calder

A sprawl of wooden piers stretching along the harbor's inner shore — some recently built, some resting on stone pilings far older than the town itself. This is where dive crews stage their gear, load their boats, and haggle over cargo before and after a run.

The docks never really close. Early risers prep for dawn dives; late crews stagger in with the tide, hauling whatever the sea gave up that day.

Notable Features

  • Licensed berths near the harbor mouth, flying Concordat pennants.
  • A crowded stretch of unlicensed independent moorage further in — Freediver territory.
  • One unmarked pier at the far end that sees regular traffic at odd hours, no flag, no manifest.

DM Notes

The unmarked pier is a plant for later — it can be tied to the Drowned Choir's dockside activity, Concordat cover-up logistics, or both, depending on how the party engages with those factions. Good site for a stakeout or a tail-the-cart scene.
