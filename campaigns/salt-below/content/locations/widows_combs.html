Widow's Combs

LOCATION · The Shallows

A stand of tall, blade-thin coral spires that comb the current into unpredictable channels — beautiful from a boat, brutal up close. Every experienced diver in Port Calder knows the name, and most know it because they lost someone there, or nearly were someone lost there themselves.

The Combs aren't forbidden — the Concordat licenses them, at a price few bother paying twice — but freedivers treat a Combs contract as a rite of passage rather than a job: the kind of dive you take to prove something, not for the coin.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The spires shift position slightly, dive to dive — not fast enough to see happen, but enough that no two charts of the Combs agree.
  • Salvage here skews toward weapons and armor, old-kingdom make, more than the household relics found elsewhere in the Shallows.
  • [[freedivers]] maintain an informal memorial marker on a nearby rock — names, dates, nothing more.
  • [[bram_coswick]] keeps the informal roster of who's dived the Combs and when — worth finding before attempting a contract, not after.

DM Notes

The shifting spires are a natural hazard, not (yet) a supernatural one — reserve any deliberate "the reef is protecting something" reveal for later regions; here it should read as simply a very dangerous dive site with a body count. The weapon-heavy salvage is a good breadcrumb toward the old kingdom's militarized outskirts (relevant once the Drowned Districts locations are authored) without needing to say so directly.
