Ashgate Wardhouse

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A barracks-like guardhouse, weapon racks rotted down to iron ghosts of their original shapes in the silt — the old kingdom's answer to what the [[harbor_watch_garrison|Harbor Watch Garrison]] is to Port Calder today. An old alarm-bell mechanism still hangs intact near the entrance, and [[harbor_watch|Harbor Watch]] has taken a quiet, unofficial interest in the site.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The alarm mechanism still partially works if triggered.
  • Its clang carries a surprising distance underwater.
  • Someone has recently cleared silt away from the entrance — not the Choir's usual style of upkeep.

DM Notes

The cleared entrance is [[rees_holt|Scout Rees Holt]]'s tracks — a [[harbor_watch|Harbor Watch]] scout working the site off the books at [[tam_ostler|Sergeant Ostler]]'s request, per Holt's own entity notes (a DM who wants a rival-crew subplot instead of or alongside Holt can still use that framing). A [[warden_husk]] may still be posted here, patrolling a route it hasn't abandoned in three centuries — a strong surprise for whichever crew investigates first, Holt included.
