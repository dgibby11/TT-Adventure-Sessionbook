The Binding Lattice

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

The Ward-Wright glyph-trail from [[the_open_wound]] opens into a chamber too large for any lantern to fully light: a cathedral-scale lattice of ward-stones, each one no bigger than the standard stock racked at [[the_wardwrights_undercroft]], but here numbering in the thousands, bound together into a single working that covers every surface. This is the containment network's true heart — not the failing patchwork felt everywhere else, but the whole structure it was patching.

A low, resonant hum comes off the working stones, felt through the water as much as heard. It is not even. Whole patches of the lattice sit dark and silent, and the silence is spreading — not randomly, but outward from a scatter of specific failure-points, the way frost spreads from a crack.

What the Party Might Notice

  • A rough headcount of dark versus lit stones puts the network at somewhere close to two-thirds still functioning — a concrete, countable answer to how much time the deal has left (Investigation DC 16, no exact number required — give a range).
  • The failure-points aren't scattered evenly; they cluster near a handful of identifiable stones whose loss would cascade fastest, structurally distinct from the rest (Arcana DC 17).
  • An alcove near the chamber's edge holds an obviously unfinished section of lattice, tools left mid-work, wired to nothing — a dead end that doesn't connect to anything (no check needed).

DM Notes

This room is the mechanical target of the "destroy the binding" branch of the finale (see [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] and [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]]): the Arcana-identified failure-points are exactly what a party choosing to finish tearing the containment down would strike. Leads to [[the_last_accord]] (a side chamber off the unfinished alcove) and [[the_renewal_font]] (a sealed side-passage past the working stones); both are optional side branches, not required to reach [[the_undoing]]. Keep the two-thirds figure loose and table-scalable per [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]]'s guidance — this room should make the ticking clock concrete without pinning an exact number of sessions to it.

Layout & Hazards

Hazard — Arcing Failure-Point. A creature that physically touches or strikes one of the identified failure-point stones (rather than merely observing them at range) without first succeeding on a DC 15 Arcana check to ground the discharge triggers an arc of ward-energy. That creature and any other creature within 10 feet must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 18 (4d8) lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. This can trigger once per failure-point cluster per visit — it isn't a repeatable damage pump, just a real cost for reckless tampering ahead of a deliberate destroy attempt.

Creature placement. None. This room is a puzzle/lore-and-hazard space tied to the destroy branch, not a monster encounter — keep it that way so the failure-point hazard above reads as the room's actual danger.
