The Market Quarter

LOCATION · Port Calder

Terraced stalls and permanent shopfronts climb the bluffs above the harbor, three streets deep at their widest. Everything that comes up out of the water eventually passes through here — sorted, appraised, priced, and sold, often within the same hour it was hauled off a boat.

Guild criers work the crowd from raised platforms, reading out the day's licensed dive postings and Concordat notices. Underneath the noise, appraisers make their real money quietly steering unlicensed finds toward buyers who won't ask where they came from.

Notable Features

  • [[dive_outfitters_row]] — The gear shops, a short walk toward the docks.
  • [[the_brined_anchor]] — A tavern fronting the market's north edge.
  • [[saltbone_curios]] — A cramped relic shop tucked behind the main stalls.
  • A public appraisal board, updated hourly with the Concordat's current buy rates.

DM Notes

Good default location for a party's first exposure to the Concordat's soft corruption — an appraiser quietly low-balling an unlicensed find is a low-stakes, concrete way to show the guild's self-interest before it becomes plot-relevant.
