Vaelgard Terrace

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

A tiered residential quarter of old aristocracy, town-houses stacked up a gentle rise off [[aurewake_concourse]] in diminishing grandeur — the higher terraces reserved, unmistakably, for whoever mattered most. Doors sit open or missing throughout; nothing about the quarter suggests anyone fought to keep anyone else out.

Personal effects remain in nearly every house, undisturbed by looting in a way nothing in Port Calder's own history behaves: a set table, a child's toy still on a stair, a wardrobe never emptied. It doesn't read as tragedy so much as a life mid-sentence, interrupted and never resumed.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Nothing shows violence or panic — no forced doors, no scattered belongings, no signs of a struggle anywhere in the quarter.
  • Personal effects are consistently left rather than packed, as if residents expected to return.
  • The highest terrace houses are noticeably larger and better preserved, hinting at where the kingdom's most influential families lived.

DM Notes

Another strand of the "evacuation, not disaster" reveal — ordinary residents left in an orderly hurry, believing they'd return, which fits a planned containment-working rather than a sudden flood (see [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]]). Seed site for a future item entity (t026) — a household heirloom left behind here would fit a mid-game find naturally. Keep the tone melancholy rather than horrific; this is the campaign's tonal midpoint, not yet the finale's dread.
