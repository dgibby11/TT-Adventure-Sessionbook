The Susurrus Deep

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A long flooded avenue of collapsed row-houses, oddly clear of the silt drift that clings to every neighboring street. Divers who work this stretch of the [[millhollow_undercroft]] approach report a constant, low whispering — never louder, never quite resolving into words — with no visible source anywhere along the avenue.

It isn't dangerous, exactly. It's just wrong in a way that's hard to explain to anyone who hasn't heard it, and most divers who've worked the Districts a while have learned not to linger here without a reason.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The whisper seems to trail slightly behind wherever a lantern points, never quite ahead of it (Perception DC 14).
  • No debris, silt, or scavenger life disturbs the avenue's center, though the buildings flanking it are as ruined as anywhere else.
  • Divers who've spent time near [[the_weeping_stair]] say the feeling is similar, just quieter.

DM Notes

This is the first deliberately "clear" omen the party should encounter in the Drowned Districts — no longer as deniable as the Shallows' salt_warped_grouper, but still nothing they can act against directly. Per [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]]'s house rule: pressure and dread, never a confrontation. Escalate sparingly; this site should read as one data point among several, not a puzzle with an immediate answer.
