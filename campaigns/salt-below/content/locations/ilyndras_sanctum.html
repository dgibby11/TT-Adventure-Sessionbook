Ilyndra's Sanctum

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

A circular sanctum off [[the_regnants_seat]], its walls carved floor-to-ceiling with a binding-rite in relief — the same iconography [[the_hollow_choir_yard]] echoes above in the Drowned Districts, but whole here, original rather than copied and worn.

No sign the Choir has ever reached this deep. They venerate this rite fervently, in a fragmentary form, without ever having actually stood in the room where it was first carved whole.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The relief depicts the binding as a covenant struck willingly, not a spell cast in desperation — a nuance the modern Choir's teachings have lost entirely (Religion DC 15).
  • A working still faintly active in the stone confirms the binding is ongoing, not a one-time casting (Arcana DC 17).
  • The carvings seem to draw what little lantern light reaches them, leaving the rest of the sanctum darker than it has any right to be.

DM Notes

The strongest single location for a player who wants to understand the [[drowned_choir]] sympathetically — they're not wrong that something sacred happened here, only wrong about what freeing it would mean. This room clarifies the nature of the deal for [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]] — that it's a covenant requiring something ongoing to hold — but should not resolve how much longer it has left. Save that answer for later.
