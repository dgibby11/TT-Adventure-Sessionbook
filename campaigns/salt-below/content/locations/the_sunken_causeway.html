The Sunken Causeway

LOCATION · The Shallows

A broad paved road, still largely intact, sloping gently down from the reef flats into deeper dark — the first unmistakably built-to-travel structure most divers ever find. Sawback Reef and the Kettle grew over old-kingdom stonework; the Causeway simply is old-kingdom stonework, clean-edged and purposeful, running somewhere no licensed chart has ever fully marked.

[[salvage_concordat]] survey crews plant a numbered marker along it every season, more out of habit than any real intent to chart its end. Morning dives along the upper stretch feel almost pleasant; past the third marker the light drops fast and the current takes on a faint, steady pull — downward, and inward, like the road still wants traffic moving along it.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The paving stones carry formal, titled old-kingdom script, wholly different in tone and craftsmanship from anything reused as salvage stone in [[port_calder]].
  • No licensed survey has ever formally charted where the Causeway ends.
  • The current's pull grows steadily stronger with depth, gentle enough to ignore at the start and hard to swim against by the third marker.

DM Notes

This should be the party's first real introduction to the archaic/formal old-kingdom naming convention (see CAMPAIGN_BIBLE.md) — read an inscription aloud in full rather than summarizing it, and let the tonal shift from Port Calder's practical names land. The Causeway is the physical spine connecting the Shallows to the Drowned Districts; treat it as a recurring landmark parties can return to and measure their own progress against as later regions open up.
