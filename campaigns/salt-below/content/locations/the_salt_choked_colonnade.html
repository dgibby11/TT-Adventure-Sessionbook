The Salt-Choked Colonnade

LOCATION · The Shallows

Dozens of fluted pillars still stand in formal ranks, roof long gone, opening onto what was once a processional plaza near [[the_warders_stair]] and [[hollowmarch]] — the first site in the Shallows that reads unmistakably as grandeur rather than ruin. Visibility here is strangely good for the depth, and the current channels between the pillars with a faint, tonal hum on certain tide angles.

[[salvage_concordat]] licenses it only for supervised survey work, officially as a "heritage site" — a designation Port Calder's divers note, correctly, is a polite way of saying it isn't worth the guild's trouble to loot.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Faint remnant wards still cling to several pillars — old-kingdom protective work, long depleted but not fully dead.
  • The current's hum shifts pitch with the tide, never quite the same twice.
  • The plaza's far end opens onto darker water than its depth alone should produce.

DM Notes

The lingering-but-depleted wards are a quiet, early-game echo of [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]] — the same kind of binding-work that holds [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]], on a much smaller and safer scale. Nothing here should feel dangerous yet; the point is a player noticing, much later, that they've seen wards like this before. The plaza's far end can serve as the transition point into the Drowned Districts once that region is authored.
