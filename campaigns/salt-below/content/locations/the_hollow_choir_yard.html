The Hollow Choir Yard

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

An open courtyard ringed with worn devotional carvings, adjoining [[verdigris_court]]. The [[drowned_choir]] gathers here at set hours for call-and-response chants; attendance has grown noticeably in recent months.

On the last few gatherings, something has answered the chant's final response line back — faint, wrong, and not from any Choir throat present.

What the Party Might Notice

  • [[deacon_rill]] treats the answering echo as proof of the Choir's faith rewarded; [[sister_meck]] treats it as the first real evidence something is badly wrong (Insight DC 13).
  • The echo only ever answers the final line, never anything sung before it.
  • Newer converts seem energized by the phenomenon; older ones grow quieter each time it happens.

DM Notes

A strong wedge scene for Sister Meck's arc (seeded t009) — she can pull a sympathetic party aside here without breaking from the Choir outright. Keep the answering voice ambiguous in tone; it should read as unsettling, not obviously malevolent, consistent with [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]]'s house rule against direct appearances this early. If the Choir ever needs to physically hold this space, a [[choir_thrall]] posted quietly at the yard's edge fits without undercutting the devotional tone.
