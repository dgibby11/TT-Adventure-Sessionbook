Aldergate Shoal

LOCATION · The Shallows

Two broken gate-towers flank a collapsed archway at the foot of [[the_sunken_causeway]] — the formal edge of the old kingdom's outskirts, marked as such on purpose. The lintel stone, cracked but legible, still reads, translated: "Aldergate, Warden of the Low Approach." It's the first place in the Shallows where the old kingdom names itself rather than simply being found.

Most licensed crews treat the shoal as a turnaround point — close enough for a good story, far enough that going further needs a better reason than curiosity. The water runs noticeably colder past the arch, a pressure change divers describe as walking through a doorway that isn't there anymore.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Two distinct sets of tool-marks scar the collapsed archway — one old-kingdom, one recent, and the recent one isn't Concordat work.
  • The gate-towers' fallen stone still bears traces of a formal heraldic device, worn past clear identification.
  • Crews who've actually passed through trade "beyond the gate" rumors like currency at [[the_brined_anchor]].

DM Notes

The recent, non-Concordat tool-marks are a quiet hook — leave the source unstated for now (Drowned Choir pilgrims, an unlicensed rival crew, or something else entirely are all plausible; pick when it matters to the story). Aldergate is a good recurring checkpoint the party can note deeper regions relative to once the Drowned Districts open up.
