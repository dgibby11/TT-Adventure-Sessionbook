Mireth's Reckoning

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

A side-chamber off [[suppliants_colonnade]], sealed behind a door that was never meant to be a grand one — no ceremony, no procession route, just a private room someone wanted kept. Inside: a single stone seat, a worked-bronze writing table still bearing the ghost-shapes of tools long dissolved, and wall after wall of tightly-carved text in a hand distinct from the colonnade's formal liturgical script. This is personal, not official.

The carvings identify their author as Mireth, a Warden of the old kingdom (see [[warders_hold]]) who oversaw the binding's containment work in its final years — not the one who performed the rite itself, but the one responsible for making sure it held.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The writing shifts tone partway through the far wall — early passages read as dutiful reports, later ones read as something closer to a private confession (Investigation DC 14 to place the shift, no check needed to notice the room feels different by the end).
  • The five reversed statues at the colonnade's midpoint are named here: Mireth turned them personally, one entry states, "so that some part of this place would face the truth instead of the ritual."
  • A short, unfinished final passage breaks off mid-sentence.

DM Notes — What Mireth Actually Wrote

The full truth (see [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]]): the binding was a last resort, struck by the kingdom's rulers when they realized they could not destroy what they'd uncovered beneath their own capital. Mireth's early passages describe the containment holding as designed. The later ones describe growing unease that the binding was never meant to survive centuries of the kingdom's own descendants — let alone outsiders — continuing to dig near it. Mireth's private judgment, never made official: the "drowning" bought time, not a solution, and someone would eventually have to finish the job the original rite-workers couldn't. The unfinished final passage is exactly that — cut off, not by narrative device but because Mireth stopped writing here and, per [[ilyndras_sanctum]]'s records, was never recorded again afterward.

This is a strong direct-evidence source for the party if they need a way to learn the truth without an NPC simply telling them. Pairs with the_leaning_archive and the_sunken_rostrum (Drowned Districts) as the third and most complete piece of the "evacuation, not disaster" reveal.
