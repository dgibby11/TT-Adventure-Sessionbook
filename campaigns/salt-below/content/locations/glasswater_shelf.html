Glasswater Shelf

LOCATION · The Shallows

A flat, sun-bright terrace of reef where the water runs so clear that boats overhead can watch divers work the bottom without a glass. It's the safest real dive site in the Shallows — shallow, calm, and easy to see trouble coming — which makes it Port Calder's default site for training, inspections, and showing outsiders what the trade actually looks like.

Midday the Shelf is bright and almost festive: apprentice divers, guild-sponsored demonstrations, the occasional noble or investor brought out to watch a "safe" dive. After dark it's nearly empty; there's little salvage left worth the trip once the easy finds are picked clean.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The Shelf is worked constantly and thoroughly picked over — anything genuinely valuable here would already be long gone.
  • [[salvage_concordat]] uses the Shelf as its standard site for licensing exams and guild demonstrations.
  • Despite being the safest dive in the Shallows, a couple of longtime divers still avoid it, muttering that "picked clean" doesn't mean "empty."

DM Notes

Use Glasswater Shelf as a low-stakes social and training location more than a loot site — a good place for the party to meet Concordat officials, take a licensing exam, or overhear guild politics without any real dive danger attached. The "picked clean doesn't mean empty" rumor is a hook you can activate later (something long-buried finally surfacing here would land harder for being the last place anyone expected trouble) or simply leave as color if unused.
