Reaver's Ribs

LOCATION · The Shallows

A raider longship's broken ribs jut from a sand shelf, hull split nearly in two — old damage, but the site's reputation is recent. Gear goes missing here more than any comparable wreck, and two apprentice divers have vanished off licensed crews in the last few years with nothing found but a cut tether line.

Most crews that still work it dive in daylight only, in pairs at minimum, and don't linger past the point the light starts fading through the ribs.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Fresh scrape marks score the ribs' inner hull — too large, and too recent, to be old salvage damage.
  • Patches of the surrounding water run noticeably colder than the rest of the Shallows, with no current to explain it.
  • [[harbor_watch_garrison]] contracts here pay a premium specifically because so few crews will take them.
