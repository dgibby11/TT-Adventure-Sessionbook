Port Calder

LOCATION · Settlements

Port Calder is a boomtown built on the ribs of a kingdom that drowned three centuries ago. It sits on a natural harbor where the old coastline crumbled into the sea, and every generation since has hauled a little more of the sunken city up onto its docks — coin, relics, the occasional still-breathing thing. The trade built the town, and the town runs on the trade: brash, prosperous, and riotously multicultural, equal parts fishing village and gold rush.

Above the waterline, Port Calder is loud and alive — guild criers, tavern brawls, dive contracts changing hands over morning ale. But everyone here has heard, or half-believes, that the kingdom below didn't drown by accident, and that whatever pulled it under hasn't finished with the surface yet.

DM Notes

Port Calder is the campaign's home base and should function like Phandalin or the Village of Barovia in the other campaigns — a return point between dive expeditions, and the place to dispense faction hooks. Tonally, Port Calder should stay in the adventurous/mercantile register even once the party has learned uncomfortable truths below — the dread belongs to the depths, not the surface.
