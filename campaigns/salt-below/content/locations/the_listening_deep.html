The Listening Deep

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

The third, unmatched glyph-trail from [[the_open_wound]] leads nowhere built at all — an unshaped cave space, walls that seem to lean inward a fraction more than they should, in a way no measurement ever quite confirms. This is the closest approach to [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]] the site allows without full descent to [[the_undoing]], and it is felt rather than seen.

Sound arrives wrong here — whispers in voices that resolve, for a moment, into people the listener knows before dissolving back into current-noise. Nothing here has ever been confirmed to touch anyone. Something here has also never been confirmed to be listening, and every diver who's come this far leaves certain that it is.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The whispers use recognizable voices, drawn from people each listener has actually met — never anything a DM has to invent, only misuse (Wisdom save at the DM's discretion for characters who linger and listen closely).
  • A previous explorer's gear lies undisturbed near the entrance, abandoned rather than lost — no sign of struggle, just gear set down and never picked back up (Investigation DC 15).
  • [[the_unmade]] may be present, more defined here than [[silt_wraith]] ever was above — a shape that holds longer, and reacts, faintly, to being spoken to (Perception DC 17 to notice it before it notices you noticing).

DM Notes

Run this exactly per [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]]'s "pressure, omens, proxies, never a confrontation" guidance — this is the last room where that rule fully applies before [[the_undoing]] breaks it. [[the_unmade]] should not initiate violence; if attacked it disperses without consequence, same as [[silt_wraith]]'s house rule, just closer and more unsettling. The whispered voices are a strong lever for individual player dread — use sparingly and specifically rather than every visit.
