Vantmoor Hall

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

Past [[the_last_landing]], the paving resumes — and the first thing a party finds waiting for them is [[vantmoor_hall|Vantmoor Hall]], a formal civic assembly hall with its roof long collapsed into a half-dome of broken stone. Tiered benches still stand in orderly ranks, and the assembly dais at the far end still bears the old kingdom's seal, untouched by whoever gathers here now.

That "whoever" is the [[drowned_choir]]. A handful of lanterns strung along the surviving rafter-lines mark out a small vigil space, and converts hold quiet gatherings here without much interest in outsiders — this is the first real Choir presence most parties meet, and it's deliberately unthreatening. They watch. They don't fight to keep anyone out.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The benches are worn in patterns suggesting regular recent use, not decades of neglect.
  • The dais seal is untouched — deliberately, not by accident.
  • A cold draft through the broken vault carries chant-echo from somewhere deeper in the district.

DM Notes

Play this as the party's soft introduction to the Choir — curious, watchful, unthreatening. [[amos_kell|Brother Amos Kell]] tends the vigil space here and is a good default face for that first conversation rather than a fight, and a natural spot to point travelers toward [[chancel_deep]] or [[the_suitors_approach]] depending on which direction the DM wants the session to go next.

Hook: Amos asks the party, quietly and without pressure, to carry word to a convert's family in [[port_calder]] who don't know where their kin ended up — a small, skippable errand that costs nothing to decline. Following through earns a Known Face favor with [[drowned_choir]] (see [[salt_below_campaign_overview]]'s Faction Standing), the campaign's first real chance for the party to owe the Choir something before the plot gives them reason to be wary of it.
