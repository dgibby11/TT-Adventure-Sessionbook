Underbell Cistern

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A claustrophobic network of vaulted cisterns and narrow flooded tunnels running beneath the district, linking [[millhollow_undercroft]] and [[chancel_deep]] by routes the [[drowned_choir]] uses to move discreetly. Strong tidal currents surge through parts of the network on a schedule only the Choir seems to know.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Currents strong enough in places to sweep an unbraced diver badly off course.
  • Choir way-markers, subtle and easy to miss, guide safe passage through the worst sections.
  • The tunnels connect several known sites once mapped.

DM Notes

Functions as a shortcut network connecting several Drowned Districts sites once discovered — useful for pacing a return trip through the district, not required for the main path. Treat the currents as an environmental hazard (a Strength save or similar to avoid being swept into a side-tunnel) rather than a combat encounter. A [[deep_maw_eel]] may lair somewhere in the network — a good way to add a genuine physical threat without changing how the currents themselves resolve.
