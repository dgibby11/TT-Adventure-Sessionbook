The Drowned Chancellery

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

The old kingdom's seat of governance, a fortress-palace complex off [[aurewake_concourse]] — where [[the_sunken_rostrum]] held public address and [[the_chancellors_rest]] held one official's private grief, the Chancellery held the actual work of running a kingdom, and, in its final days, the actual decision that ended one.

Its state archive hall survives largely intact, sealed behind the same wardwork style found at [[the_chancellors_rest]] — deliberate, official, built to keep something in rather than merely keep water out. A narrow scholars' tower, [[velmarch_spire]], rises from the complex's edge, still ringed with the empty mounts of instruments long since carried off.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The archive seals bear a chancellor's formal seal-mark, matching the one on [[the_chancellors_rest]]'s door exactly (Investigation DC 15).
  • Administrative ledgers, where legible, show ordinary kingdom business continuing right up until a final, dated entry — then nothing.
  • One chamber's furniture is arranged for a meeting that was never adjourned: chairs still drawn up to a table, an unfinished draft document still weighted open.

DM Notes

The centerpiece location for confirming [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]]'s "evacuation, not disaster" reveal in full, once the party has gathered enough fragments elsewhere ([[the_leaning_archive]], [[the_sunken_rostrum]], [[the_chancellors_rest]], [[vaelgard_terrace]]) to make this room's meaning legible rather than confusing. The unfinished draft document is the strongest single piece of physical evidence in the campaign for the binding-working theory — treat it as a capstone find, not an early one, and consider gating full comprehension behind a Deep Kingdom-appropriate skill check or a translator NPC.

Key Areas

  • Processional Antechamber (Area 1): The formal entry off [[aurewake_concourse]] — a colonnaded reception hall built to make petitioners wait. Silt has drifted the floor into shallow dunes; anything dropped or set down sinks slowly out of easy reach within a few minutes (no check needed, just DM color). No hazard, no creatures — this room is pure tone-setting.
  • The State Archive Hall (Area 2): Sealed behind wardwork doors matching [[the_chancellors_rest]]'s seal-mark (Investigation DC 15 to notice the match, as in the room description above). Forcing the seal without the right approach triggers a Ward-Snap hazard: DC 13 Dexterity save or take 2d6 lightning damage and alert anything else in the complex to the party's presence. Shelved ledgers here are the primary source for the "ordinary business continuing right up until a final entry" detail already established above.
  • The Unadjourned Chamber (Area 3): The meeting room with chairs still drawn to the table and the unfinished draft document — the capstone evidence room per the DM Notes above. No hazard here by design; this find should land clean, not be gated behind a trap. Creature placement: a single [[warden_remnant]] may be posted at the chamber's inner door if the DM wants a guardian between the party and the draft document — omit it entirely for parties who should reach this find without a fight.
  • Lower Vaults (Area 4): A narrow stair beneath the Archive Hall descending toward sealed storage the surviving records never itemize. Left deliberately unresolved — a hook for a future task or table improvisation, not a mapped room; if opened, a DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) check reveals fresh (relatively — within decades) disturbance in the dust, hinting someone from Port Calder has already been this deep.
