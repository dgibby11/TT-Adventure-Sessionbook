The Drowned Chancellery

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

The old kingdom's seat of governance, a fortress-palace complex off [[aurewake_concourse]] — where [[the_sunken_rostrum]] held public address and [[the_chancellors_rest]] held one official's private grief, the Chancellery held the actual work of running a kingdom, and, in its final days, the actual decision that ended one.

Its state archive hall survives largely intact, sealed behind the same wardwork style found at [[the_chancellors_rest]] — deliberate, official, built to keep something in rather than merely keep water out. A narrow scholars' tower, [[velmarch_spire]], rises from the complex's edge, still ringed with the empty mounts of instruments long since carried off.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The archive seals bear a chancellor's formal seal-mark, matching the one on [[the_chancellors_rest]]'s door exactly (Investigation DC 15).
  • Administrative ledgers, where legible, show ordinary kingdom business continuing right up until a final, dated entry — then nothing.
  • One chamber's furniture is arranged for a meeting that was never adjourned: chairs still drawn up to a table, an unfinished draft document still weighted open.

DM Notes

The centerpiece location for confirming [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]]'s "evacuation, not disaster" reveal in full, once the party has gathered enough fragments elsewhere ([[the_leaning_archive]], [[the_sunken_rostrum]], [[the_chancellors_rest]], [[vaelgard_terrace]]) to make this room's meaning legible rather than confusing. The unfinished draft document is the strongest single piece of physical evidence in the campaign for the binding-working theory — treat it as a capstone find, not an early one, and consider gating full comprehension behind a Deep Kingdom-appropriate skill check or a translator NPC.
