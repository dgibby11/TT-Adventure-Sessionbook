Hollowmarch

LOCATION · The Shallows

Rows of modest, formally-built rowhouses stand in orderly ranks along a straight street grid near [[aldergate_shoal]] — the old kingdom's boundary district, named for what it was: land held at the edge of things. Roofs collapsed generations ago, but the walls still stand close enough together that the street plan reads clearly even choked in silt.

A popular site for small independent salvage — low risk, low reward, mostly household goods and the occasional coin cache drifted into a corner. Sand has drifted deep into every lower room; kelp hangs in doorless frames like curtains nobody bothered to take down.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Every house still bears its formal door-name plaque, oddly undamaged for structures otherwise fully collapsed.
  • [[freedivers]] crews favor Hollowmarch for training new divers on room-to-room search technique — safe, structured, and genuinely productive.
  • Nothing here has ever turned up anything the [[salvage_concordat]] considers worth restricting.

DM Notes

Low-stakes location, good pacing beat between tenser Aldergate/Warders' Stair content — a place the party can breathe and do straightforward salvage work. The intact door-plaques are a flexible flavor hook; a specific plaque naming a figure relevant to later plot can be planted here in a future pass without needing to rewrite the entity.
