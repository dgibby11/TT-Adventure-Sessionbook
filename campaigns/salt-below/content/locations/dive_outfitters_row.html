Dive-Outfitter Row

LOCATION · Port Calder

A tight row of narrow shopfronts between the Market Quarter and the docks, each one packed floor to ceiling with diving gear — helms, weighted boots, warded lines, pressure charms, and the occasional piece of salvaged old-kingdom equipment nobody's fully figured out yet.

This is where crews outfit before a run. Apprentices spend their mornings pressure-testing seals on used helms; the good shops won't sell a rig that hasn't been tested twice.

Notable Features

  • [[pressure_ward_token]] — Standard-issue dive charm, sold (and faked) up and down the row.
  • [[brinelung_mask]] — Sold as the token's standard companion piece; every reputable shop stocks both and most won't sell one without the other.
  • A dozen small outfitters, quality and honesty varying wildly shop to shop, [[corwin_hask|Corwin Hask]]'s shopfront best-stocked among them.
  • A backroom smith who reworks salvaged old-kingdom fittings into usable modern gear.

DM Notes

[[corwin_hask|Hask]]'s unlicensed supplier is a good hook into either the Freedivers (if the supply is honest smuggling around Concordat tax) or something worse (if the "supplier" turns out to be something dredged up rather than traded for). Leave the answer open until a faction NPC needs it.
