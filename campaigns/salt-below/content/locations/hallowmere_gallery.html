The Hallowmere Gallery

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

A long memorial hall off [[the_regnants_seat]], lined with portrait-reliefs of the old royal line in unbroken succession — ruler after ruler, kept in the same orderly register as the houses of [[vaelgard_terrace]], down to a final frame noticeably larger than the rest.

That last frame is roughed in but never finished. Tools sit neatly beside it rather than dropped, as if the sculptor simply stepped away mid-work, meaning to come back.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The succession is unbroken and complete — every ruler accounted for, right up to the last, unfinished frame (History DC 15).
  • The final relief is roughed in but not carved to completion, tools left neatly beside it (Investigation DC 14).
  • The frame's oversized scale suggests whoever it was meant for mattered more than any ruler before them.

DM Notes

The unfinished final portrait is a quiet, human-scale echo of [[drowned_chancellery]]'s unfinished draft document — the same last ruler, caught mid-decision in two different rooms. Keep both discoveries available independently of each other; a party that finds this first should still feel the weight without needing the Chancellery to explain it, and vice versa.
