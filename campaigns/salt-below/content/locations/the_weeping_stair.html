The Weeping Stair

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A tight switchback stairway descending in stepped terraces below [[vantmoor_hall]] and [[millhollow_undercroft]], water sheeting visibly down every riser as if the stone itself were crying. Little traffic passes this way — the cold deepens noticeably with every landing, and the water seems to thicken the further down a diver goes.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The temperature drop is sharp enough to be disorienting.
  • Sound behaves strangely on the lower landings, the same way it did at [[the_last_landing]].
  • Nothing about the stair itself is dangerous yet — it's atmosphere, not a trap.

DM Notes

This is the eventual continuation point deeper into the Drowned Districts and, later, toward the Deep Kingdom — the same threshold role [[the_last_landing]] played for the transition out of the Shallows. Keep it non-lethal and purely atmospheric until the Deep Kingdom region is authored; the point is the party feeling the depth increase, not a mechanical challenge.
