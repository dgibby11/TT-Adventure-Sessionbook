Shellhome Ward

LOCATION · Port Calder

Port Calder's boom drew people from a dozen coastlines, and Shellhome is where most of them settled — taller, denser housing built up rather than out, with as many building styles as there are homelands represented. Shared courtyards do double duty as marketplaces, meeting halls, and festival grounds depending on the week.

Street-shrines change hands and faiths more often than anywhere else in the city; nobody minds much, as long as the courtyard gets swept afterward.

Notable Features

  • Communal courtyards shared across households and traditions.
  • [[the_tideward_chapel]] — On the ward's edge, open to any faith.
  • A rotating string of small street-shrines, added and retired informally, kept by [[rosalind_vey|Rosalind Vey]] — including one new arrival Vey didn't invite and can't quite place.

DM Notes

The unfamiliar courtyard shrine is a quiet [[drowned_choir|Drowned Choir]] recruitment foothold — a foothold in a ward that would otherwise have no obvious reason to encounter them. [[rosalind_vey|Vey]] is mildly annoyed by the new shrine rather than alarmed, and doesn't know it's the Choir unless the party tells her or pushes to investigate.
