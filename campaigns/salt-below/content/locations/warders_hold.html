The Warders' Hold

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

A fortified barracks-monument off [[aurewake_concourse]], its long entry hall lined with the same carved-name alcoves divers have been finding since [[the_warders_stair]] — except here, every alcove is intact, and every name has a face: worn statuary busts, one per Warden, cataloguing what must have been the kingdom's entire final garrison.

A handful of the names match ones the party may already have collected from shallower sites. Here, for the first time, they mean something: rank markings, a formal roll of who stood post the night the kingdom drowned — and, by the gaps in the roll, who did not stand it willingly.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Several busts have been deliberately defaced — not by time, but by something sharp and intentional, long ago.
  • The roll's gaps cluster together rather than scattering at random, suggesting a specific group left, fled, or was removed.
  • A [[warden_husk]] may still be posted somewhere in the Hold, patrolling a route it hasn't abandoned in three centuries.
  • A restricted stair off the barracks descends to a sealed strongroom — [[cindrel_vault]] — that the Wardens' own roll marks off-limits.

DM Notes

This is where [[the_warders_stair]]'s long-burn name-collecting hook starts paying off — let players who tracked names feel that payoff directly, but keep the full story (why the defaced busts were defaced, who the missing Wardens were) for the climactic dungeon rather than resolving it here. A [[warden_husk]] encounter here should feel like meeting one of the roll's names in person, not a random fight.
