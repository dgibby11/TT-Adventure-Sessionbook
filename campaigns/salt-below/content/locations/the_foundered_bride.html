The Foundered Bride

LOCATION · The Shallows

A merchant marriage-barge, hull nearly whole, resting upright in a sand hollow just off the reef line — sunk on its maiden crossing decades ago with a hold full of dowry goods still aboard. It's the most photographed wreck in the Shallows, if Port Calder had a way to photograph things: every licensed crew dives it at least once, and every storm season the shifting sand buries a little and uncovers a little more.

Morning light through the broken upper deck turns the hold a hazy gold; by afternoon the sand hollow's current picks up and visibility drops to arm's length.

What the Party Might Notice

  • A sealed dowry chest sits untouched in the lower hold despite decades of open salvage — divers avoid it on principle as much as superstition.
  • [[freedivers]] treat a first dive on the Bride as something close to a rite of passage for new crews.
  • Storm-shifted sand exposes new cargo every season, which is exactly why [[salvage_concordat]] keeps re-licensing a wreck this thoroughly picked over.

DM Notes

The untouched dowry chest is a good candidate to pay off later as a campaign item (t026) — its contents can be tuned to whatever hook the table needs, from a mundane fortune to something with old-kingdom provenance the "bride's family" never should have owned. Low-stakes, well-loved location; good for an early session that's pure salvage adventure before factional politics take over.
