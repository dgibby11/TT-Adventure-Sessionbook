Velmarch Spire

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

A tall, narrow scholars' tower off [[drowned_chancellery]], its upper chamber ringed with brass instrument-mounts whose actual instruments — sextants, orreries, whatever they held — are long gone, taken like everything else the capital's last custodians bothered to carry out.

The tower's height lifts a diver above the worst of the ambient silt; visibility here is the best anywhere in the Deep Kingdom, which only makes the empty mounts more conspicuous rather than less.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Star-charts and tide-tables sit pinned open rather than shelved, abandoned mid-use (Investigation DC 14).
  • The final months' entries track tidal and astronomical cycles with obsessive precision — someone here was timing something exactly.
  • Annotations cross-reference the charts against a ritual calendar, not a navigational one (Arcana DC 16).

DM Notes

Pairs directly with [[the_leaning_archive]]'s sealed decree — the scholars here were calculating the correct timing for the binding-working itself, another concrete plank in the "evacuation, not disaster" case for [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]]. A good location for a party working the mystery methodically rather than stumbling into its answer.
