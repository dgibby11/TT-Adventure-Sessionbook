Sarelle's Repose

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

The old capital's central marketplace, named — per a single surviving stall-plaque — for a merchant no other record remembers. Stalls stand in orderly rows, goods long since rotted or dissolved, coin still sitting in more than one abandoned till.

Nothing here was looted before the water came. Whatever the kingdom's last ordinary day looked like, it ended mid-transaction, not mid-panic — the ledgers of [[drowned_chancellery|the Drowned Chancellery]] and the empty plinths of [[aurewake_concourse]] tell the same story from different angles.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Coin remains untouched in stall-tills throughout the market — a fortune in old-kingdom currency, worthless to modern buyers but a curiosity to collectors.
  • Goods show signs of careful closing-up (shutters drawn, stalls tidied) rather than a hasty abandonment.
  • The Sarelle plaque is the only personal name recorded anywhere in the market — everything else is priced, not signed.
  • The scattered tills hold roughly 180 gp in old-kingdom currency — worthless as legal tender, but a Port Calder collector would pay that much and more for the novelty.

DM Notes

A quieter, human-scale companion piece to [[vaelgard_terrace]] and [[drowned_chancellery|the Drowned Chancellery]] for the same "evacuation, not disaster" throughline — ordinary people closing up shop for what they thought was a normal night. The untouched coin is a good low-stakes reward for a party that wants to loot without complicating anything; there is no supernatural cost attached to taking it.
