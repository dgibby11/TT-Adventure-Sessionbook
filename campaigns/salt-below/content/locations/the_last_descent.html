The Last Descent

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A final formal stairwell, wider and colder than [[the_weeping_stair]] above it, descending past the last intact district wall. Nothing lives at this depth; even the [[drowned_choir]] does not linger past this point.

The dark beyond lantern range here is absolute, and the pressure is one a diver feels in their teeth, not just their ears.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The stairwell's carved formal balustrade continues unbroken as far as light reaches, suggesting real scale below.
  • No debris field, no scavenged wreckage — nothing has been pulled up from past this point yet.
  • A palpable sense of crossing a threshold, distinct from the ordinary transitions elsewhere in the district.

DM Notes

Marks the transition threshold out of the Drowned Districts and into the Deep Kingdom (t021+), the same structural role [[the_last_landing]] played between the Shallows and here.
