The Loom Quarter

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

Rows of loom-houses, their threads long since rotted away, frames still standing in eerie orderly ranks like a forest that grew straight lines. It's one of the district's few genuinely low-dread spots — a handful of independent squatters and [[drowned_choir]] converts actually live here, and it feels almost domestic by comparison to the rest of the Drowned Districts. [[ness_thatchford|Ness Thatchford]] is the most talkative of the squatters, restringing a dead loom-house one scavenged thread at a time.

What the Party Might Notice

  • A few looms have been recently restrung with scavenged wire and kelp-fiber.
  • Someone here is genuinely trying to weave again, not just scavenge.
  • Residents are wary of strangers but not hostile.

DM Notes

Use this as a breather location between dread beats — low-stakes social encounters, minor salvage or trade with [[freedivers|Freedivers]]-adjacent squatters, no real danger. It's a good place to let the party rest, gather rumors, or just talk to people who aren't converts or corpses.
