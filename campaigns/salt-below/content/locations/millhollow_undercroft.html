Millhollow Undercroft

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

The gutted undercroft of the district's old grain-mill, its wheels stalled and locked in silt mid-turn. Machinery groans occasionally as it settles further, a sound that carries oddly even through water. Mostly abandoned — the [[drowned_choir]] uses the tunnels beneath it as a discreet route between [[chancel_deep]] and elsewhere in the district.

What the Party Might Notice

  • One of the mill-wheels can still be forced to turn with real effort.
  • Forcing it open reveals a sealed passage beneath.
  • The passage trends toward [[the_weeping_stair]].

DM Notes

Reward a party that finds and forces the mill-wheel passage with a genuine shortcut deeper into the district — don't gate it behind a specific class feature or spell, any reasonable Athletics check should do. This should feel like clever play paying off, not a puzzle with one correct solution. The stalled machinery pit is also a plausible lair for a [[deep_maw_eel]], if the DM wants a physical threat guarding the passage rather than a pure puzzle.
