The Tideward Chapel

LOCATION · Port Calder

A modest chapel and healer's hall on the edge of Shellhome Ward, built partly from the reclaimed stone of an older, drowned shrine. It serves any faith that walks through the door — Port Calder never settled on one god, and the chapel long ago stopped trying to make it.

Healers here see more dive injuries than anything else: crushed limbs, burst eardrums, and the occasional case no one wants to talk about too closely. A small ossuary in the back holds the remains of divers who were recovered but never claimed.

Notable Features

  • An open shrine room, unaffiliated with any single faith.
  • A healer's hall equipped for dive-related injuries.
  • A modest ossuary for unclaimed drowned divers.

DM Notes

The chapel's careful remembrance records double as the closest thing Port Calder has to a casualty log for missing divers — a legitimate, sympathetic way for the party to research disappearances tied to the central mystery without needing Concordat or Harbor Watch cooperation.
