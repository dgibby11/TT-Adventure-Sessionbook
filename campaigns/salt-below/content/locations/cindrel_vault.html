The Cindrel Vault

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

A sealed strongroom beneath [[warders_hold]], reached by a restricted stair the Wardens' own roll marks off from the rest of the barracks. The vault door still bears its locking marks — deliberate, orderly work, not a hasty last-minute seal.

Inside, the silence is total. Current and drift, present everywhere else in the Deep Kingdom in some faint degree, simply stop at the threshold, as though the room itself has been holding still for three hundred years.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Shelving racks stand three-quarters empty, and neatly so — most of the treasury was deliberately removed before the seal went up, not looted after (Investigation DC 16).
  • What remains is modest for a royal treasury: coin, minor regalia, a handful of sealed document tubes — the valuable things clearly went somewhere else on purpose.
  • The inner seal shows no damage or forced entry of any kind, ever.
  • What remains amounts to roughly 250 gp in coin and minor regalia — modest for a royal treasury, and clearly not the point of what was left behind.

DM Notes

Good seed location for a future item entity (t026) — a minor regalia piece or a sealed document tube fits naturally here as a find. A [[warden_husk]] may still be posted at the inner seal, still guarding a room whose contents mostly left long before it did.
