Aldermourn Close

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A short close of modest old-kingdom townhouses, better preserved than most of the district. No Choir presence here, no salvage claims — just settled silt and closed doors, near [[vantmoor_hall]] and [[the_suitors_approach]].

It's the closest thing the Drowned Districts offer to peaceful. Even the ambient unease that clings to the rest of the district eases noticeably within the close.

What the Party Might Notice

  • A few homes still hold undisturbed personal effects — combs, toys, a half-set table — the kind of ordinary human detail easy to miss between omens and salvage claims (Investigation DC 12).
  • Nothing here reacts to the party; no traps, no hostile presence, no cross-links to any faction's agenda.
  • The quiet itself is worth noting after everything else in the district.

DM Notes

Deliberate low-dread breather site between heavier beats — use it to pace tension across a session, not to hide anything major. A good place for quiet roleplay or a moment of genuine sorrow at what was lost, without any mechanical hook attached.
