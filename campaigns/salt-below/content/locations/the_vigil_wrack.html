The Vigil Wrack

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

The Warden watch-marks from [[the_open_wound]] lead to a chokepoint unlike anything at [[warders_hold]] above: a barricade of collapsed stone and old war-material, thrown up in a hurry rather than built to stand. Statuary busts line the approach the same as the Hold's entry hall, but here they're cracked, several toppled outright, in a room that otherwise shows real battle damage — scorch, breakage, gouges in stone that isn't supposed to gouge.

This is where the last of the Wardens made a final, organized stand, and lost enough of it that the barricade was never fully rebuilt. A single warden still walks the wreckage on patrol, sharper and more alert than [[warden_husk]]'s wordless challenge above — this one still remembers, at some level, that it's on duty because something got past it once.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The damage pattern reads as an assault from the inner side of the barricade outward, not an attack repelled from outside — whatever the Wardens fought here, they fought going the wrong direction (Investigation DC 16).
  • A vigil-roster carved into the barricade's remaining upright section lists names in the same formal hand as [[warders_hold]]'s alcove busts, closing that earlier thread for a party that's been tracking it (no check needed once both sites are visited).
  • The sentinel here challenges with more urgency than a standard husk and does not stand down as readily if pressed — it is guarding against something specific, not performing a rote patrol (Perception DC 14 to notice the difference in behavior).

DM Notes

[[warden_remnant]] is posted here — a combat-capable escalation of [[warden_husk]], the region's first real "gatekeeper" fight rather than a non-hostile patrol encounter, appropriate to a level 9-10 party. Play the inward-facing damage as a deliberate hook: something already breached this chokepoint once, from the containment side, and was contained again at cost — a concrete, physical omen of how close the deal has come to failing before. Leads onward to [[the_faithfuls_end]] as a side branch (not required to reach [[the_undoing]]).
