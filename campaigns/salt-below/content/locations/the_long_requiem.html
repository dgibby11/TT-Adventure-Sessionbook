The Long Requiem

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A long colonnade lined with hundreds of formal memorial markers, organized by old-kingdom district of origin rather than family name. The [[drowned_choir]] tends the markers constantly, keeping names legible against the silt — even the least devout converts lower their voices here.

[[isolt_vane]] and [[deacon_rill]] both come to the Requiem, though for different reasons: Isolt to remember what was lost, Deacon to be certain it can be found again.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Markers grouped by district rather than family — whole neighborhoods appear to have gone down together.
  • Fresh markers are occasionally added, carved in a hand that doesn't match the old-kingdom script around them.
  • No hostility here, regardless of who the party is.

DM Notes

A strong site for Deacon Rill's most sincere, least cult-like scene — grief rather than dogma. If the party needs a moment of real sympathy for the Choir before the campaign pushes them toward opposition later, stage it here.
