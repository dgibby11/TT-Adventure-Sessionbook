The Last Landing

LOCATION · The Shallows

At the base of [[the_warders_stair]], the dressed paving finally gives way to open dark — a broad formal landing marking the last built surface before the reef floor simply falls away. It's the furthest point most licensed Shallows crews will chart, and the furthest most are willing to go on purpose.

A few worn mooring rings are set into the landing's stone, as if someone once meant to keep coming back to this exact spot. Past the edge, the water is noticeably colder, and sound behaves strangely — swallowed rather than echoed, the way a shout dies in a very large empty room. Divers who work this far down keep their lamps lit no matter the cost in oil; it's less a rule than a habit nobody's willing to be the one to break.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The paving stones at the landing's very edge are worn smooth in a path leading down and out — not from salvage traffic, which stops here, but from something that used to go further.
  • The mooring rings show wear consistent with regular use, not a single moored vessel decades ago.
  • Past the edge, a diver's light reaches noticeably less far than it should for the water's clarity.

DM Notes

This is the literal transition point into the Drowned Districts once that region is authored (t016) — the Warders' Stair leads here, and the Last Landing is where a party first commits to going past the Shallows for good. Nothing here should be dangerous yet; the point is atmosphere and threshold, a clear narrative "line" the party crosses when they're ready to go deeper.
