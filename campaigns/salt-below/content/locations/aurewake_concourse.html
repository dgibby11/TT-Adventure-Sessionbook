The Aurewake Concourse

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

A grand processional avenue running straight from [[thalmere_gate]] into the capital proper, flanked by statuary plinths on both sides — every statue missing, cleanly removed rather than broken, leaving only footprints in stone and the occasional inscribed plaque. Whatever stood here mattered enough to be taken; nothing mattered enough to be left.

The avenue itself is wide enough for a formal procession twenty abreast, paved in fitted stone that hasn't shifted in three centuries. Side avenues branch off toward [[warders_hold]], [[vaelgard_terrace]], and [[drowned_chancellery]], all still distant enough to be lantern-dark from here.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Every plinth's plaque names a title and a date, not a person's name — a formal honor roll, not a memorial (Investigation DC 14 to find one still legible).
  • The removal of every statue looks deliberate and organized, not looted piecemeal over time.
  • Distance and scale here make lantern light feel smaller than it did in the Drowned Districts — the capital was built for a light source nothing a diver carries can match.

DM Notes

The systematically-removed statuary is a quiet piece of the "evacuation, not disaster" reveal (see [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]]) — someone had time to strip the honor roll before the water rose, which argues for intent over catastrophe. Don't spell this out; let a sharp player connect it to [[the_sunken_rostrum]] and [[the_leaning_archive]] on their own. Functions as the region's hub, branching toward [[warders_hold]], [[vaelgard_terrace]], and [[drowned_chancellery]].
