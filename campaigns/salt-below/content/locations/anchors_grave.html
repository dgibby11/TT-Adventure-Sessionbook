Anchor's Grave

LOCATION · The Shallows

Half a dozen wrecks fused into one tangled reef by decades of coral growth and shifting sand — nobody agrees where one hull ends and the next begins anymore. It's rich, contested salvage ground, and the Concordat assigns crews staggered dive days specifically so two licensed parties never end up fighting over the same cargo hold.

By day it's the loudest wreck site in the Shallows — winches, marker buoys, crews shouting claims back and forth across the tangle. After dark the buoys go dark too, and the wrecks belong to whoever's willing to dive without a permit.

What the Party Might Notice

  • One wreck's hull timbers don't match the others' era or construction — centuries older, and driven into the tangle rather than sunk alongside it.
  • [[salvage_concordat]] staggers licensed dive days here more strictly than the site's difficulty alone explains.
  • [[harbor_watch_garrison]]'s duty board lists more than one "unresolved incident" tied to this site's name.

DM Notes

One of the [[harbor_watch_garrison]] duty board's three unresolved Shallows incidents happened here — a crew that reported "the wrecks moving against each other" and then stopped filing reports at all. The older, out-of-place hull is the actual outskirts of the old kingdom's outer defenses, driven into the wreck-tangle by whatever originally sank the newer ships; a good site to seed a territorial Shallows creature (t015) if one hasn't been placed yet.
