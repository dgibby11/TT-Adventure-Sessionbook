Graves End Locks

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A derelict set of canal locks and sluice gates, once used to manage the old kingdom's inner waterways. The gates still shift and groan on their own, slow and rhythmic — far more than the weak current here should explain.

Divers who've heard [[the_drowned_bell]] rung topside swear the groaning keeps the same rhythm, syncing up for a while whenever the bell sounds before drifting back out of step over the following hour.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The gate-groan briefly locks into rhythm with the bell if timed against a known ringing, then drifts loose again (Perception DC 15).
  • The gates themselves are structurally sound — this isn't decay, it's motion with no clear cause.
  • No creature or mechanism is visible causing the movement.

DM Notes

A physically, roughly-verifiable version of the omen tone — not proof of anything supernatural, but a strong beat for a party starting to connect [[the_drowned_bell]] to something deeper than kingdom history. Pairs well with a visit to [[bellwrit_span]] for parties chasing the connection further.
