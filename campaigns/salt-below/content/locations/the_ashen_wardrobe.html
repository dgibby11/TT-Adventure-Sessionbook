The Ashen Wardrobe

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A private aristocratic vault-chamber, built into a collapsed manor cellar and still sealed by a corroded formal lock. It doesn't appear on any [[salvage_concordat]] manifest — [[coraline_bray]] knows it exists and hasn't logged it.

Fine ash-grey silt coats everything inside, undisturbed for centuries and faintly luminous under lantern-light.

What the Party Might Notice

  • A single item among the ash has clearly been handled recently, then carefully replaced exactly where it was (Investigation DC 14).
  • The lock shows fresh tool-marks despite the vault's age, meaning someone has already been inside more than once.
  • No manifest tag, claim stamp, or Concordat seal marks this vault at all — deliberately absent, not merely overlooked.

DM Notes

Seed site for a future mid-game item entity (t026) — hold the specific relic open until that task, but the hook (Coraline's undisclosed personal interest in this vault) is live now. If pressed, Coraline claims ignorance; she is lying.
