Chancel Deep

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

Where [[the_drowned_bell]] above is an echo, Chancel Deep is the source. A soaring former high chapel, its roof long gone, opens straight up into black water — the true seat of the [[drowned_choir]], and the place [[isolt_vane]] is most often found. Vigil-boats moor around the clock at the altar-rail, candle-processions drifting slow circles in the open nave.

The altar mosaic still shows, in fading tile, the old kingdom either embraced or swallowed by something vast — the wear is just even enough that no two viewers agree on which.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Chant carries strangely down here, felt in the chest before it's heard.
  • The mosaic's ambiguity feels deliberate rather than accidental.
  • Isolt Vane, if present, leads without raising her voice once.

DM Notes

This is where Isolt performs the Choir's binding-adjacent rites — unknowingly reinforcing rather than loosening the deal described in [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]]. A strong location for the party to witness her sincerity firsthand, ideally without immediate conflict; the horror of Chancel Deep should be dawning realization, not a fight.
