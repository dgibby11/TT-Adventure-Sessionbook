The Glass Hull

LOCATION · The Shallows

A trade galley whose cargo hold burst open on impact, scattering old-kingdom glass and mirror-shard cargo across the sand in a wide, glittering fan. At midday the light does strange things down here — scattered glints everywhere, even through murky water, like the wreck is still faintly lit from within.

It's a frequent source for [[saltbone_curios]]'s less-legal stock, and on certain low-tide mornings [[drowned_choir]] pilgrims comb the sand for whole pieces before licensed crews ever arrive, leaving nothing behind but bare seabed and a few candle stubs.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Several glass fragments are etched with old-kingdom script, intact enough to translate with patience.
  • Cut hands are common here — the shards are sharp enough to open a glove, let alone skin.
  • The scattered field is picked cleaner after certain tides than after others, with no salvage crew responsible.
  • An intact fist-sized shard fetches 15-25 gp from curio buyers; a careful morning's haul runs 80-150 gp, more for any piece with legible script.

DM Notes

To the [[drowned_choir]] these fragments are "shards of the old light," genuinely religiously significant, not just relics — good ground for a scene where the party meets Choir members mid-ritual rather than mid-conspiracy. The etched script, if translated, can carry a small piece of old-kingdom lore useful for seeding [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] without giving too much away at Shallows depth.
