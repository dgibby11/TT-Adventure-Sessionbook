The Renewal Font

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

A sealed side-passage off [[the_binding_lattice]] opens into a small, quiet alcove that stands apart from the rest of the network in one specific way: it isn't failing. It was never finished. At its center sits an empty housing, precisely shaped for a master ward-stone the working stock racked at [[the_wardwrights_undercroft]] was never meant to be — a socket built for something bigger than a repair part.

Everything about the alcove is unfinished rather than damaged: tool-scars still sharp, conduit ends capped rather than corroded, dust settled evenly rather than disturbed. Whoever built this ran out of time, not resolve.

What the Party Might Notice

  • The empty housing's shape matches [[the_wardwrights_binding_seal]] exactly, for a party that already has it (no check needed if the item is in hand; Investigation DC 14 to work out the match from description alone otherwise).
  • Unlike every other failure point in the region, this alcove shows no wear or decay at all — it was left incomplete, not broken (Investigation DC 13).
  • Faint conduit lines run from the housing toward the deepest part of the lattice, suggesting that completing it wouldn't just patch a local failure but reach the whole network (Arcana DC 16).

DM Notes

This is the mechanical target of the "renew the deal" branch. Setting [[the_wardwrights_binding_seal]] into this housing is the finale's renew option made literal — see that item's DM notes and [[the_undoing]] for how to run the actual moment and its cost. A party without the seal can still identify what this room is for (it just can't act on it yet), which rewards backtracking to [[the_wardwrights_undercroft]] rather than gating the branch entirely behind having found the item earlier. If the party never has the seal, this room simply confirms a renew option exists without being usable — the finale should still function on destroy/invent alone per the item's own notes.
