Vaultward Reliquary

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A fortified reliquary vault of old-kingdom masonry, its formal doors scarred by the [[salvage_concordat]]'s newer pry-marks. [[wick_tallow]]'s licensing stamps on the outer seal are recent — whatever crews are working this vault are still actively pulling from it.

Most of the shelves inside have been stripped clean. A few haven't, and something about them is subtly, persistently warm, in water that should carry no warmth at all.

What the Party Might Notice

  • One emptied shelf's manifest tag has been altered — a find logged as "lost in transit" that clearly wasn't (Investigation DC 13).
  • The warm shelves are otherwise ordinary-looking reliquary niches; nothing about them looks different to the eye.
  • Concordat crews working here move faster and talk less than at other sites.

DM Notes

This is the site behind [[coraline_bray]]'s private incident ledger (seeded t006): a recent haul from here wasn't inert the way salvage should be. This feeds [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]]'s "still-breathing salvage" hook directly — treat it as a rare, escalating incident the party can discover evidence of, not a repeatable mechanic. If the party presses Coraline on it, she deflects hard; she has not told Perrin or Wick the full truth of what was found.
