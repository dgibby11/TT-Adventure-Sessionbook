The Leaning Archive

LOCATION · Drowned Districts

A records hall tilted hard on a collapsed foundation, shelving racked at an unsettling angle but largely intact. [[sister_meck]] has been quietly working this site alone, against Choir custom of never reading the old records unsupervised.

Most shelves are undisturbed dust-silt. One reading nook is not — cleared, and clearly lit by lantern on a regular basis.

What the Party Might Notice

  • A surviving civic decree references sealing "the lower wards" on the sovereign's own order, days before the water came — not an accident, an evacuation order (History DC 15).
  • The cleared reading nook holds several texts stacked in a working order, not a looter's disorder.
  • If Sister Meck is present when the party arrives, she is visibly startled, then guarded rather than hostile.

DM Notes

The first non-Choir textual fragment supporting [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] — the drowning was ordered, not suffered. Ties directly to Sister Meck's doubt arc (seeded t009); this is where her private research happens; if the party earns her trust, she'll share what she's found rather than have them stumble onto it cold.
