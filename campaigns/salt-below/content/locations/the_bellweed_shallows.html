The Bellweed Shallows

LOCATION · The Shallows

A dense kelp forest rises from buried old-kingdom rubble, fronds tall enough by midday to blot out the surface glow entirely. [[salvage_concordat]] licenses it as a hazard site — crews are required to string guide-lines between numbered marker buoys before entering, and the requirement isn't bureaucratic caution for its own sake. Divers who skip the lines have a documented habit of not resurfacing on schedule.

By day the forest is green-gold and dappled, almost pretty. Toward dusk the Concordat's buoy-lanterns get lit one by one along the guide-lines, small warm points strung through the dark fronds — and the rhythmic clacking of the buoy-bells overhead gets steadily harder to place the deeper a diver goes, until it's impossible to tell which direction the surface bell is actually coming from.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Cold pockets sit between kelp stands with no clear current explaining them.
  • The kelp grows in a subtle spiral pattern radiating out from the forest's center.
  • A guide-line buoy near the center shows old, deliberate cut marks — someone left the line on purpose, not by accident.
