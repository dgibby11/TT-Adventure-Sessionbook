The Warders' Stair

LOCATION · The Shallows

Past [[aldergate_shoal]], a wide formal stairway cut in even, deliberate steps descends further into the dark. Empty alcoves line the walls at regular intervals, statuary long since fallen or looted; each still carries a worn name carved into the stone beneath it, in the same formal old-kingdom hand as Aldergate's lintel.

Technically off-limits without a Concordat writ, a rule enforced about as often as the depth discourages casual visitors anyway. Divers who linger report the alcoves breathe — a faint, regular pulse of colder current, like something exhaling on a slow clock.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Every alcove niche carries a carved name beneath it, worn but legible — none matching any figure known to [[port_calder]]'s history.
  • The cold-current pulse from the alcoves keeps a slow, steady rhythm, as if timed rather than natural.
  • The stairway's steps are unnaturally consistent in height and depth for hand-cut stone — clearly built to a formal standard, not local craft.

DM Notes

The carved names are a long-burn hook — a handful can later be revealed as historical Wardens or old-kingdom figures. Don't resolve who they were yet; let the party collect names across multiple dives before any of it means something — see [[warders_hold]] for where this thread begins to pay off. The alcove "breathing" is atmospheric, not yet a mechanical hazard at this depth.
