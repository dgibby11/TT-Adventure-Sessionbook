Tidewrack Ward

LOCATION · Port Calder

The oldest residential ward, built shoulder-to-shoulder along the tideline out of whatever timber and stone was cheapest — a lot of it salvaged, some of it old-kingdom masonry nobody bothered to ask questions about. Home to most of the dive crews and dockworkers who actually keep Port Calder's harbor running.

At low tide, children work the exposed tide pools for anything worth a coin; at high tide, the lowest streets flood ankle-deep and everyone treats it as routine.

Notable Features

  • Close, narrow alleys between patched tenements.
  • Shared drying-lines and cookfires between households.
  • Tide pools that flood the lowest streets on schedule, twice daily.

DM Notes

Good location for grounding the party in the human cost of the salvage trade — dive-crew families here feel every disappearance and every bad haul directly. Gossip here travels faster and more honestly than official channels.
