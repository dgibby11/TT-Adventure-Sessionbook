The Threshwater Stair

LOCATION · Deep Kingdom

A final descending stair behind [[ilyndras_sanctum]], narrower and less formal than anything else in the capital core — as though it were never meant to be a public way at all, only a service route for people who already knew where it led.

This is the last stretch of the old kingdom built with people in mind. Past its bottom step, nothing down here was built for anyone.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Pressure and cold deepen sharply past the first few steps, faster than the descent alone should account for.
  • The faint draft first noticed at [[the_regnants_seat]] resolves here into a slow, directional pull — current with a clear source, in water that shouldn't have current at all (Perception DC 16).
  • The pull is strongest at the stair's lowest visible landing, where the lantern-light gives out.

DM Notes

Marks the threshold out of the capital core and toward the containment site of [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]] (t023/t024) — the same structural role [[the_last_descent]] played entering the Deep Kingdom in the first place. Hold any direct containment-site content until those tasks; this location is dread and anticipation only, never a manifestation.
