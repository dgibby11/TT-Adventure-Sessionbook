Saltbone Curios

LOCATION · Port Calder

A cramped, cluttered shopfront wedged into a gap behind the Market Quarter's main stalls — easy to miss unless you're looking for it. Shelves are stacked with "curios": trinkets, half-corroded fittings, and small relics that the owner insists are all decorative reproductions.

Most of them are. Not all of them.

Notable Features

  • Shelves of "reproduction" old-kingdom relics, most fake, a few unsettlingly not.
  • An owner who asks no questions about where a seller's goods came from.
  • A back room, not open to browsing customers.

DM Notes

A quiet fence for salvage crews avoiding Concordat tax — a good seed for a Freedivers-aligned NPC once that faction is authored (t008). The genuine relics on the shelves can be tuned up or down as plot needs a lead into the Shallows or Drowned Districts.
