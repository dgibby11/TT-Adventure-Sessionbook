The Nightweigh

LOCATION · The Shallows

A slim, fast ketch sits scuttled in a shallow sand cut, hull scored open along the keel rather than broken by wreck — someone sank this boat on purpose. No [[salvage_concordat]] record shows a vessel by this name ever licensed out of Port Calder. Local rumor calls it a smuggler's boat. Local rumor is right.

Shallow enough to work by ordinary daylight, though whoever uses it prefers the water moonless. The hold has a false bottom, empty now but for packing straw and a lingering trace of incense that has no business turning up on a fishing coast.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Fresh straw and rope-burn marks in the false-bottomed hold — used within the last season, not decades ago.
  • No Concordat registration marks anywhere on the hull, unusually thorough for a "lost" vessel.
  • A faint incense smell that clings to the hold longer than it should underwater.

DM Notes

The Nightweigh is a [[drowned_choir]] supply run, worked by [[deacon_rill]], ferrying relics out of [[saltbone_curios]]'s unregistered stock on the way out and new converts in on the way back — a name that dies and gets reused as fast as the Concordat can flag it. A strong later hook: the party stakes out a Choir run night and either intercepts it or gets pulled into Deacon Rill's recruitment pitch instead of a fight.
