Brinelung Mask

ITEM · Dive Gear

A fitted leather-and-glass half-mask wired to a fist-sized bellows-reservoir strapped at the hip, drawing on a stored charge of Concordat-blessed air rather than any true spell of water breathing. Sold and worn alongside the [[pressure_ward_token]] as the other half of standard dive kit — the token keeps the depth from crushing a diver, the mask keeps them breathing while it doesn't.

Location: Sold openly at [[dive_outfitters_row]] alongside the pressure token, typically 20-35 gp new. A spent reservoir can be refilled at any licensed outfitter for 2-5 gp; refilling it slowly for free is possible by leaving it exposed to open air for a full day, though no working diver bothers when a refill costs so little.

Properties (no attunement)

  • Activation: passive — grants water breathing continuously while worn and sealed, no action required.
  • Effect: the wearer can breathe underwater for up to 8 hours per full charge.
  • Warning, not silence: roughly ten minutes before a charge runs out, the reservoir begins an audible hiss and gurgle, rising in volume as the charge drains — a real, escapable countdown for a diver who's paying attention. If the charge fully empties, the wearer begins suffocating under standard drowning rules.
  • Value: 20-35 gp new; 2-5 gp per refill at a licensed outfitter.
  • A poorly maintained or silt-fouled mask can start its warning-hiss early or intermittently, sometimes with charge still left — an unreliable nuisance rather than a lethal silent failure, unlike a fake pressure token.
