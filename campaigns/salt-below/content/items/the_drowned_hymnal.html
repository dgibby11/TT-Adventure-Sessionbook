The Drowned Hymnal

ITEM · Drowned Choir Relic

A songbook bound in something that was once leather, its pages kept legible far longer than they should be by whatever preservation the [[drowned_choir]] worked into the binding. The hymns inside are sung at [[chancel_deep]] and [[the_drowned_bell]] on the Choir's own calendar — not old-kingdom originals, but Choir compositions written since the drowning, addressed to whatever the Choir believes still listens below.

Location: The Choir keeps several copies at [[chancel_deep]] for its own use; a single copy has reportedly gone missing from there, per [[isolt_vane]], which is as good a reason as any for the party to end up with one.

Properties (no attunement)

  • Activation: 10 minutes spent singing a hymn from the book, underwater, within a mile of a site connected to [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]] (DM's call which sites qualify — the Deep Kingdom always does, some Drowned Districts sites do).
  • Effect: advantage on the next Charisma check made to influence a creature native to these depths, and disadvantage on the next Wisdom saving throw against fear or dread effects from the same source. Both apply to the singer only, and each expires the first time it's used (or after 1 hour, whichever comes first).
  • Duration/uses: usable once per long rest per creature — the hymnal itself isn't consumed or drained, but singing it convincingly again too soon rings hollow even to the singer.
  • Value: no honest market price — the Choir will not sell it and would consider the idea obscene. A collector ignorant of what it actually is might pay 40-60 gp for the binding and craftsmanship alone.
  • The Choir will recognize this book on sight and react strongly — anywhere from desperate to reclaim it to quietly grateful someone is finally singing correctly, depending on which Choir NPC and how the party plays it.

DM Notes

Mechanically a double-edged social tool, not combat gear — a way to let the party engage with the Drowned Choir's actual belief system (sincere, half-right, not simple villainy per [[drowned_choir]]) rather than just fighting them. The dread-related disadvantage clause exists so using it always costs something, keeping with the "It should be felt, not fought" guidance in [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]]. A good mid-campaign reason for [[isolt_vane]] or [[sister_meck]] to re-enter the story regardless of how the party first got the book.
