Barnacled Charm

ITEM · Salvage Curiosity

A small carved figure, old-kingdom in style, so thickly crusted with growth after three centuries underwater that its original shape is mostly guesswork. Common enough that most dive crews have hauled up one or two — the kind of thing sold to a curio buyer for drinking money rather than kept.

Location: Turns up regularly at reef and wreck sites throughout The Shallows (a good default find at [[farrows_grotto]] or similar reef sites); [[saltbone_curios]] buys them for 2-5 gp apiece, no questions asked, and occasionally has one or two in stock for sale.

Properties (no attunement)

  • Activation: spoken command word, no action cost beyond holding it out (it lights on the speaker's turn, effectively a free object interaction).
  • Effect: sheds dim light in a 10-foot radius for up to 1 hour. Usable once per day; recharges at dawn regardless of how the previous use ended.
  • Value: 2-5 gp to a curio buyer (see Location above) — worth carrying for the utility, not the coin.
  • No other magical property. Most are exactly this and nothing more.
  • DM option: one in every twenty or so, at your discretion, is subtly different — slightly warmer to the touch, or glowing a shade too long past its hour. Reserve this for a charm you want to matter; see DM Notes.

DM Notes

Designed as ordinary, low-stakes salvage flavor for early levels — a cheap, common, mechanically minor item that makes the Shallows feel lived-in and profitable without any plot weight. The "one in twenty is different" option exists so a DM can quietly seed an early, deniable "still-breathing salvage" beat (see [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]]) through an item the party already has and trusts, if a slow-burn hook is wanted. Not required — most charms should just be charms.
