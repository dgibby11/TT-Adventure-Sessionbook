Pressure-Ward Token

ITEM · Dive Gear

A palm-sized disc of old-kingdom bronze, refitted with Concordat-standard warding sigils and worn on a cord around the neck. Standard-issue gear for any licensed dive crew, and a genuine necessity past the Shallows — the pressure below the reef line will crush an unwarded diver long before they'd otherwise need to worry about air.

Location: Sold openly at [[dive_outfitters_row]], typically 15-25 gp depending on the shop and how recently it was warded. Cheap fakes circulate too — they look identical and fail without warning.

Properties (no attunement)

  • Activation: passive — protection is continuous while worn, no action required.
  • Effect: ignore the crushing effects of deep-water pressure at any depth relevant to this campaign; advantage on saving throws against being knocked prone or pushed by strong currents.
  • Duration/range: unlimited while worn; effect ends immediately if removed or if the ward lapses (see below).
  • Value: 15-25 gp new from a licensed outfitter; genuine fakes are functionally worthless but cost the same at a glance.
  • A fake (or a token whose warding has lapsed) provides no protection at all — usually discovered at the worst possible moment. A DC 15 Arcana check while dry and at rest can confirm a token is genuine before it's needed.
