The Ward-Wright's Binding Seal

ITEM · Deep Kingdom Artifact

A master ward-stone, larger and far more heavily worked than the standard replacement parts racked around it, found finished and unused at [[the_wardwrights_undercroft]]. Where the ordinary stock stones are simple repair components, this one was clearly built to do something to the network as a whole, not just patch a single failing link.

Location: [[the_wardwrights_undercroft]], set apart from the general stock on its own bench — the Ward-Wrights finished this one before they ran out of time for the rest.

Properties

  • Inert until brought into contact with the containment network at the climactic dungeon (see DM Notes) — no effect anywhere else, including at the undercroft itself.
  • At the containment site, the seal can be used to attempt to reinforce the binding (buying the deal significant time, possibly generations, at some cost the DM should design to fit the finale) rather than simply destroying it or leaving it to fail. It does not force this outcome — it makes it possible.
  • Non-magical to Identify or similar detection prior to reaching the containment site; it reads as inert old-kingdom stonework until then. This is intentional — the party should have no clean way to know its full function until the finale gives it context.

DM Notes

This is the mechanical anchor for the "renew the deal" branch of the climactic choice described in [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] and [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]] — destroy, renew, or something the party invents. Placing it at [[the_wardwrights_undercroft]] means a party that explores thoroughly finds their own third option rather than being handed it by an NPC monologue at the finale. The exact cost/ritual for using it belongs to t024 (the climactic dungeon) once that site exists to give it mechanical shape — do not fully resolve here. If the party never finds or takes it, the finale should still function on the destroy/renew binary alone; treat the seal as a bonus path, not a required one.
