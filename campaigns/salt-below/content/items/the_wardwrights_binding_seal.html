The Ward-Wright's Binding Seal

ITEM · Deep Kingdom Artifact

A master ward-stone, larger and far more heavily worked than the standard replacement parts racked around it, found finished and unused at [[the_wardwrights_undercroft]]. Where the ordinary stock stones are simple repair components, this one was clearly built to do something to the network as a whole, not just patch a single failing link.

Location: [[the_wardwrights_undercroft]], set apart from the general stock on its own bench — the Ward-Wrights finished this one before they ran out of time for the rest.

Properties (no attunement — unique, plot-gated)

  • Value: not sold or appraised at any market — a one-of-a-kind finished ward-stone, not stock, so no gp price applies. Non-magical to Identify or similar detection prior to reaching the containment site; it reads as inert old-kingdom stonework until then.
  • Activation: inert everywhere except its matching housing at [[the_renewal_font]]. Seating it there and completing a 1-hour rite (requiring an Arcana or Religion check, DC 16, from whoever presides) is the only way to trigger its effect.
  • Effect — Reinforce the Binding: on a successful rite, the deal is renewed for generations rather than centuries; [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]] returns to full dormancy and Port Calder is safe within any timeframe the campaign needs. On a failed check, the rite can be attempted again (no permanent harm from failure alone), but each attempt — success or failure — costs 1 hour at [[the_undoing]]'s Ambient Pressure hazard (see that location's Layout & Hazards).
  • Cost of success: the presiding creature permanently loses 1 point from one ability score of their choice — Constitution (the deal takes vitality), Intelligence or Wisdom (the deal takes memory), or Charisma (the deal takes luck, felt as an intangible dimming other NPCs half-notice). This loss cannot be restored by any means short of a wish or equivalent. It is deliberately small and permanent rather than large and temporary — a genuine price, not a scary-sounding freebie.
  • Does not force the outcome — it makes it possible. A party without the seal (or that chooses not to use it) still resolves the finale via destroy or invent alone; see [[the_undoing]].

DM Notes

This is the mechanical anchor for the "renew the deal" branch of the climactic choice described in [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] and [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]] — destroy, renew, or something the party invents. Placing it at [[the_wardwrights_undercroft]] means a party that explores thoroughly finds their own third option rather than being handed it by an NPC monologue at the finale. The 1-point permanent ability score cost above is the concrete price the_undoing's own DM Notes left open ("a piece of the party's own vitality, memory, or luck") — deliberately small so renewing the deal reads as a real sacrifice, not a trap option nobody would rationally pick. If the party never finds or takes it, the finale should still function on the destroy/renew binary alone; treat the seal as a bonus path, not a required one.
