The Salt Below — Campaign Overview

REFERENCE · Campaign · Levels 1–10

Premise

[[port_calder]] is a boomtown built on the ribs of a kingdom that drowned three centuries ago, and every generation since has hauled a little more of it back up — coin, relics, and the occasional still-breathing thing. The party are divers in that trade. As they push deeper, past the reef wrecks of The Shallows and into the old kingdom's ruined mid-depth neighborhoods and finally its drowned capital, they learn the truth the surface has spent three hundred years not asking about: the kingdom didn't sink by accident, it was drowned on purpose, as the price of a deal that is still holding something down.

Tone

A balance of light and dark — never grimdark, never comedic. Keep the surface (Port Calder, early Shallows work) leaning adventurous and mercantile; let dread creep in gradually with depth, staying alien and patient rather than gratuitous even at the campaign's darkest point.

Factions at a Glance

  • [[salvage_concordat]] — the merchant guild that licenses and taxes all diving. Political antagonist more than a villain: protects its own profits, actively covers up bad finds.
  • [[drowned_choir]] — a dockside cult of the old kingdom's remaining faithful, convinced the sunken city can be raised again — and half right. Neither simple allies nor simple villains.
  • [[harbor_watch]] — the city's law, chronically outmatched by what's really down there. A faction the party can genuinely ally with.
  • [[freedivers]] — independent salvagers who respect what's below and trade in warnings as much as loot. The PCs' likely home faction and default entry point into the setting.
  • [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]] — not a political faction but a presence with agents, wants, and a plan for the surface. Should almost never appear directly early on; felt through what it sends up, not itself.

Central Mystery

Why did the old kingdom really drown, what deal keeps It from simply rising now, and how much longer does Port Calder have before someone's greed finally breaks that deal? See [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] and [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]] for the DM-facing reveal tracks.

Level Arc

Levels 1–10 across three broad arcs, each with its own planning entity:

  • [[arc_1_port_calder_shallows]] (levels 1–3) — establish Port Calder, its factions, and shallow reef work.
  • [[arc_2_drowned_districts]] (levels 4–6) — mid-depth salvage and first real contact with the Drowned Choir and the kingdom's remnants.
  • [[arc_3_deep_kingdom]] (levels 7–10) — the deep ruins and the old kingdom itself, culminating in the party's own decision about It That Kept the Kingdom.

Geography

Four regions, roughly 120 entities total: [[port_calder]] (surface hub), The Shallows (levels 1–4 dive sites), The Drowned Districts (levels 4–7, Drowned Choir territory), and The Deep Kingdom (levels 7–10, the old capital and the containment site). Surface naming stays salt-of-the-earth and practical; the drowned kingdom uses a fallen aristocracy's formal, archaic naming to contrast — the shift should be felt as the party goes deeper.
