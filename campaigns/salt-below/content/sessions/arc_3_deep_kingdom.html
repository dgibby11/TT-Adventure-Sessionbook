Arc 3: The Deep Kingdom

SESSION · Planning · Levels 7–10

The party reaches the old capital itself through [[thalmere_gate]] and [[aurewake_concourse]], learns the full truth at [[drowned_chancellery]], and ultimately confronts the choice at the heart of the campaign: what to do about [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]] and the deal that has kept it contained for three centuries.

Key Beats

  • Opening the Deep Kingdom proper via [[thalmere_gate]] — this should read as arriving somewhere, not just descending further; the old-kingdom naming and archaic formality should be at their most pronounced here.
  • [[drowned_chancellery]] delivers the full "evacuation, not disaster" reveal — the kingdom's last rulers made a deal to save what they could, and It is what they made it with.
  • Approach to the containment site via [[the_sealed_approach]], building dread toward the climactic dungeon.
  • The climactic dungeon: [[the_open_wound]] as the hub, branching toward the destroy/renew/invent options, converging at [[the_undoing]] for the campaign's final decision.
  • [[the_wardwrights_binding_seal]] is the pre-placed mechanical anchor if the table leans toward renewing the deal rather than breaking or replacing it — see the item's own entry for how it resolves each ending.

The Finale Choice

Per [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]] and [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]], the campaign should end on the party's own answer to what happens to It That Kept the Kingdom: destroy the binding outright, renew the old deal (with new terms, ideally the party's own), or invent a third option the table comes up with at the table. None of the three is scripted as "correct" — [[the_undoing]] is built to run whichever the party lands on.

DM Notes

This arc is the payoff for everything seeded since Arc 1 — the omens in the Shallows, the Choir's half-truths in Arc 2, and the pattern of escalating creatures (silt_wraith → warden_remnant → the_unmade) all converge here. Let the party's Arc 2 stance toward the Drowned Choir meaningfully color how [[the_undoing]] plays out; Choir NPCs met earlier should be able to reappear here rather than being replaced by strangers.
