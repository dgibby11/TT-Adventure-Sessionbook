Arc 1: Port Calder & The Shallows

SESSION · Planning · Levels 1–3

The opening arc establishes [[port_calder]] as home base and gets the party working the reef — legitimately or otherwise. By the end of this arc the table should know the four surface factions, have a working relationship with at least one of them, and have pulled up enough wreckage to sense that not everything down there wants to be found.

Key Beats

  • Arrival/introduction at [[port_calder]] — establish [[the_brined_anchor]] as the party's likely base of operations and get them a [[freedivers]] contract or Concordat license as their entry point into diving.
  • First licensed or gray-market dive into The Shallows — [[sawback_reef]] or [[the_kettle]] work well as an opening site: low stakes, high atmosphere, a taste of the old kingdom's outskirts.
  • Introduce [[salvage_concordat]] and [[harbor_watch]] as the surface's competing pressures — profit vs. order — without either reading as outright villainous yet.
  • First hint of [[drowned_choir]] presence at the docks; a recruiter, a chant overheard, a warning from a Freediver contact.
  • Close the arc on a find or omen that plants [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] without answering it — something salvaged that shouldn't still work, a name nobody in Port Calder recognizes but should.

Tone

Adventurous and mercantile. This arc is a boomtown and a reef crawl, not a horror story yet — let the dread arrive as texture, not threat.

DM Notes

Session count is flexible — this arc can run long if the table enjoys Port Calder's faction/economy layer, or compress to a single extended session if they want to get underwater fast. Use the surface roster's rumor boards (see [[port_calder]]) to seed whichever hook the party bites on first; nothing here is meant to be a fixed order.
