Arc 1: Port Calder & The Shallows

SESSION · Planning · Levels 1–3

The opening arc establishes [[port_calder]] as home base and gets the party working the reef — legitimately or otherwise. By the end of this arc the table should know the four surface factions, have a working relationship with at least one of them, and have pulled up enough wreckage to sense that not everything down there wants to be found.

Opening

Read: "The gangplank drops onto a dock already loud at this hour — gulls, hawkers, the clatter of a winch hauling something dripping and heavy up from a moored dive-boat. Port Calder smells like salt, tar, and fry-oil, and everyone here is selling something, including you, eventually. A weathered sign over the nearest tavern door reads THE BRINED ANCHOR — ROOMS, ALE, HONEST PRICES (MOSTLY)."

First Contract

Whoever the party talks to first — a [[freedivers]] contact at the tavern, or a Concordat clerk at [[concordat_licensing_house]] — makes the same pitch with a different angle: there's reef work available, it pays, and someone always comes back with less than they went down with. Let the party choose their entry point rather than assigning one.

Faction Friction

Read or paraphrase: "A Concordat licensing officer and a Harbor Watch sergeant are having the same argument they've clearly had before, right in the middle of the market — one wants the dive logged and taxed, the other wants it inspected for what came up. Neither one is wrong, exactly." Use this to establish Concordat-vs-Watch tension without either faction reading as the villain yet.

Closing Omen

End the arc on a find that shouldn't still work, or a name nobody in Port Calder recognizes but should — something salvaged from The Shallows that plants [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] without answering it. Let the table leave this session with a question, not an explanation.

Key Beats

  • Arrival/introduction at [[port_calder]] — establish [[the_brined_anchor]] as the party's likely base of operations and get them a [[freedivers]] contract or Concordat license as their entry point into diving.
  • First licensed or gray-market dive into The Shallows — [[sawback_reef]] or [[the_kettle]] work well as an opening site: low stakes, high atmosphere, a taste of the old kingdom's outskirts.
  • Introduce [[salvage_concordat]] and [[harbor_watch]] as the surface's competing pressures — profit vs. order — without either reading as outright villainous yet.
  • First hint of [[drowned_choir]] presence at the docks; a recruiter, a chant overheard, a warning from a Freediver contact.
  • Close the arc on a find or omen that plants [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] without answering it — something salvaged that shouldn't still work, a name nobody in Port Calder recognizes but should.

Leveling Milestones

  • Level 2 — after the party lands their first contract (Freediver or Concordat) and completes an opening dive at [[sawback_reef]] or [[the_kettle]]. This should feel earned by finishing the job, not by a fixed session count.
  • Level 3 — once [[salvage_concordat]] and [[harbor_watch]] are both established as competing pressures and the party has a real hint of [[drowned_choir]] presence at the docks. Roughly the arc's midpoint.
  • End the arc at level 3-4, timed to the closing omen that plants [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]] — the party should cross into Arc 2 already leveled for it, not level up mid-transition.

Tone

Adventurous and mercantile. This arc is a boomtown and a reef crawl, not a horror story yet — let the dread arrive as texture, not threat.

DM Notes

Session count is flexible — this arc can run long if the table enjoys Port Calder's faction/economy layer, or compress to a single extended session if they want to get underwater fast. Use the surface roster's rumor boards (see [[port_calder]]) to seed whichever hook the party bites on first; nothing here is meant to be a fixed order.
