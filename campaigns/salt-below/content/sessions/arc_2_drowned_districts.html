Arc 2: The Drowned Districts

SESSION · Planning · Levels 4–6

The party pushes past [[the_last_landing]] into the old kingdom's mid-depth neighborhoods and makes first real contact with the [[drowned_choir]] — not as a rumor, but as a faction with a headquarters, a leader, and a claim that keeps almost making sense. This arc should start reframing what the party thought it knew from Arc 1.

Key Beats

  • Crossing [[the_last_landing]] as a real threshold moment — the shift from salvage-site framing to genuine ruined-kingdom framing should be felt, not just stated.
  • Establish [[vantmoor_hall]] and work toward [[chancel_deep]], the Drowned Choir's true stronghold — a chance to meet [[isolt_vane]] or another Choir leader as a complicated, not simply hostile, figure.
  • [[the_leaning_archive]] or an equivalent site should surface the arc's central reveal-in-progress: the drowning wasn't a disaster, it was an evacuation. Don't confirm it outright — let the party start doubting the "kingdom sank" version they arrived with.
  • First mechanical or narrative brush with [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]] — wards, warnings, or Choir doctrine that imply something is being actively held back, not just historical.
  • Close the arc with the party choosing (or being pushed toward) a stance on the Choir — ally, opponent, or something unresolved — before the Deep Kingdom raises the stakes further.

Tone

Dread should be rising here, but stay alien and patient rather than gratuitous — the Choir are tragic zealots working from a real (if incomplete) truth, not monsters.

DM Notes

This is the arc to spend [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]]'s first real clues — see the mystery entity's DM notes for the specific reveal beats it's tracking. Keep [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]] mostly implicit until Arc 3; it only needs to be felt here, not explained.
