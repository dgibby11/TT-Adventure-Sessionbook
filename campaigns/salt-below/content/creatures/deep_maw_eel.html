Deep-Maw Eel

CREATURE · Mid-Depth Horrors

A genuine predator, and a straightforward one compared to most of what the Drowned Districts throw at a dive crew — which is exactly why it's dangerous. Long enough to coil twice through a flooded corridor, patient enough to wait out a slow-moving survey team, and drawn to the tunnel networks and stalled machinery pits where prey funnels through narrow, predictable routes.

It hunts [[underbell_cistern]]'s tidal tunnels and the stalled gearing of [[millhollow_undercroft]] alike, using the confined geometry to its advantage rather than any unnatural trick. Divers who work these routes regularly know to move in pairs and never linger at a chokepoint — the ones who don't tend to become the reason everyone else knows the rule.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Scrape marks along tunnel walls at roughly torso height, consistent with something large moving through repeatedly (Survival DC 13).
  • A sudden absence of smaller fish or scavengers in a stretch of tunnel that should have plenty.
  • [[drowned_choir]] way-markers through [[underbell_cistern]] route around one particular junction without explanation.

Traits

Tunnel Coiler. The eel treats flooded corridors and tight chokepoints as if they were open water for the purpose of movement, and it has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made in such spaces.

Actions

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6+4) piercing damage.

Constrict. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 13 (2d8+4) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 16). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained and the eel can't constrict another target.

DM Notes

"Giant Eel" isn't an official 5e SRD/D&D Beyond stat block, so this reskins a Giant Constrictor Snake (stat block above, link below) as the closest confirmed, mechanically-verified match rather than fabricating numbers for an unofficial entry — same base already used for reef_stalker in the Shallows, but played very differently here: this is meant to be a real, winnable combat encounter, not an avoidable ambush. It's the district's answer to the Shallows' wreck_crab: physical danger the party can prepare for and fight, a deliberate breather from the omen-heavy sites so dread doesn't flatten into a single register. The Choir's way-marker detour is a light hook — they know it's there and route around it rather than confront it, which says something about Choir priorities if a player notices.
