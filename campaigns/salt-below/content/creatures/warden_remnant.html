Warden Remnant

CREATURE · Kingdom Remnants

Where [[warden_husk]] is a patrol going through empty motions, a warden remnant is still, in some sense, on duty because something specific once got past it. Posted at [[the_vigil_wrack]], it stands guard at the chokepoint where the last organized defense of the containment site broke, and it hasn't stopped watching since.

It challenges the same way a husk does — formally, wordlessly — but presses the challenge further and doesn't disengage as readily. Something in whatever animates it distinguishes an ordinary trespasser from a real threat, and it treats the party as the latter from the moment they're noticed.

What the Party Might Notice

  • It reacts with more urgency and less ritual formality than a standard husk, closing distance rather than waiting at a fixed boundary (Perception DC 14 to notice the difference before engaging).
  • Its armor bears the same battle damage as the rest of the Wrack — old, but from a real fight, not decorative wear (Investigation DC 13).
  • It does not pursue past the chokepoint it guards, even under pressure — its post matters to it more than winning does (no check needed, observable after one engagement).

Traits

Antimagic Susceptibility. The warden remnant is incapacitated while in the area of an antimagic field. If targeted by dispel magic, it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against the caster's spell save DC or fall unconscious for 1 minute.

False Appearance. While the warden remnant remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a suit of old-kingdom ceremonial armor left standing at its post.

Actions

Multiattack. The warden remnant makes two slam attacks.

Slam. Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8+3) bludgeoning damage.

Reactions

Guardian's Riposte. When a creature the warden remnant can see moves out of its reach without disengaging, or tries to move past it through the chokepoint it guards, the warden remnant makes one slam attack against that creature.

DM Notes

Reskin an Animated Armor or similar construct one tier more dangerous than [[warden_husk]]'s baseline — add a reaction (an opportunity strike on anyone trying to push past it) and slightly better saves, appropriate as the region's first real combat-capable gatekeeper rather than an avoidable curiosity. Unlike the husk, this one should be a genuine, winnable fight if the party presses it, not a non-encounter — it's meant to read as evidence that the containment site has teeth, ahead of [[the_undoing]].
