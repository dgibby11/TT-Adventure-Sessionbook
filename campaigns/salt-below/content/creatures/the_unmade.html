The Unmade

CREATURE · The Deep Presence

What [[silt_wraith]] is in the Drowned Districts, this is at [[the_listening_deep]] — the same drifting, half-held humanoid outline made of disturbed silt and old debris, but more defined, more willing to hold its shape under direct attention, and faintly, unnervingly responsive to being spoken to.

It has never been confirmed to attack. It has also never once failed to know exactly where a party is looking.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Unlike a silt wraith, it holds its shape for several seconds even under direct, sustained attention before dissolving (Perception DC 17 to register the difference from ordinary sightings above).
  • If addressed directly, it turns fractionally toward the speaker — never a response, exactly, but not nothing either (no check needed).
  • It never approaches closer than a fixed, consistent distance, regardless of how the party moves (Investigation DC 15 to notice the distance is exact rather than coincidental).

Traits

Incorporeal Movement. The Unmade can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

Actions

None by design. Like [[silt_wraith]], the Unmade never initiates an attack and has no offensive action in normal play — see DM Notes. If a table forces a genuine confrontation, a Specter's Life Drain (Melee Spell Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 10 (3d6) necrotic damage) is available as a last-resort fallback only.

DM Notes

Same house rule as [[silt_wraith]]: never initiates contact, disperses and reforms elsewhere without mechanical consequence if attacked (ignore the fallback Life Drain action above unless a table specifically wants one rare, unnerving exception). This is the region's clearest deliberate sign that [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]] is aware of the party specifically, not just aware something is present — use it as escalating dread immediately ahead of [[the_undoing]], not as a combat encounter. Defensive stat line is a reskinned Specter, chosen over repeating silt_wraith's Shadow base to give the Unmade a distinct (if never-used) silhouette befitting "more defined" — used here only so a DM has real numbers on hand if a player insists on physically grabbing at it, not as an invitation to fight it.
