Reef Stalker

CREATURE · Reef Predators

A long, muscular eel adapted to reef-cave ambush hunting — patient, territorial, and smart enough to learn a dive crew's rotation schedule. It favors sheltered grottoes and cave systems over open reef, wedging into a crevice to wait rather than actively hunting, and strikes only when prey is close enough that a miss isn't a real risk.

Reef Stalkers aren't aggressive by nature; a diver who stays out of striking range and doesn't corner one in its den is unlikely to be bothered. Cornering one, or lingering too long in its territory, is another matter.

DM Notes

Mechanically, a reskinned Giant Constrictor Snake (stat block above) or a comparable CR 1-2 aquatic ambush predator — whatever the table already has handy. This is the likely culprit behind the bone-stacking in [[farrows_grotto]]'s third chamber; a single stalker denning there is enough to explain the neat little bone-piles without escalating the site's danger level. Keep this encounter avoidable and low-stakes — Shallows tone is adventurous, not yet dreadful.
