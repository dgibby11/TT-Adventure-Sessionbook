Warden Husk

CREATURE · Kingdom Remnants

An animated remnant of the old kingdom's Warden corps — armor and the shape of a body, moving through the same patrol pattern it walked three centuries ago, indifferent to the fact that there's nothing left to guard. Water has stripped it to a husk: rusted plate, waterlogged cloth-of-office gone to rot, a face-shape behind the visor that's better left unconfirmed.

It doesn't hunt. It challenges — anyone crossing its route gets a formal, wordless block, a raised hand or leveled weapon, the same as it would have given an unlicensed traveler in life. Step back, and it resumes its patrol. Press past it, or strike first, and it responds with the same drilled competence it had when the kingdom still needed it.

What the Party Might Notice

  • Its patrol route is fixed and repeats on a predictable circuit, if the party watches long enough to chart it (Investigation DC 14).
  • Faded but legible on its breastplate, a name in the same formal hand as the carvings at [[the_warders_stair]].
  • It never pursues beyond its route's boundary, no matter how far a target runs.

Traits

Antimagic Susceptibility. The husk is incapacitated while in the area of an antimagic field. If targeted by dispel magic, it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against the caster's spell save DC or fall unconscious for 1 minute.

False Appearance. While the husk remains motionless at the end of its patrol circuit, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary suit of derelict armor.

Actions

Multiattack. The husk makes two slam attacks.

Slam. Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6+2) bludgeoning damage.

DM Notes

Mechanically, a reskinned Animated Armor (stat block above) or similarly disciplined construct — a non-aggressive-until-provoked sentinel rather than a mindless monster. Its breastplate name should be drawn from the same pool as [[the_warders_stair]]'s alcove names (see [[warders_hold]] for where that thread starts paying off) — a real thread the party can pull if they start cross-referencing names across sites, not required reading. Post one at [[ashgate_wardhouse]] or [[wraithlock_gate]] as a jurisdictional surprise for Harbor Watch/Concordat crews who assumed the ruin was empty.
