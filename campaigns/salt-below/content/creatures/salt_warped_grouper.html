Salt-Warped Grouper

CREATURE · Early Signs

At a glance, an unusually large grouper — sluggish, solitary, easy to mistake for a strange but mundane fish. Closer inspection is worse: too many teeth set at odd angles, eyes clouded white but clearly still tracking motion, faint patterns beneath the scales that don't match any known markings. It doesn't behave like a predator so much as a fish that has forgotten what being a fish is for.

Sightings cluster in a handful of Shallows locations where the current runs strange or the old kingdom's influence sits closest to the surface — [[widows_combs]], where no two charts of the spires agree, and [[the_chime_shoal]], where the Drowned Choir keeps its quiet pilgrimages. Freedivers who've seen one tend to leave the encounter alone rather than fight it; nothing about the fish suggests it's worth the trouble, and nothing about it is reassuring either.

What the Party Might Notice

  • It doesn't flee from light or noise the way a normal fish would — it simply turns to watch.
  • Its markings, examined closely, resemble old-kingdom script more than any natural pattern.
  • It never appears in groups. Every sighting on record is a lone individual.

DM Notes

Mechanically, a reskinned Quipper (stat block above) or similar minor beast at drastically reduced aggression — this is not meant to be a fight. Its purpose is atmosphere: the first small, unmistakable sign that something below the Shallows is not merely dangerous but wrong, planted well before the Drowned Districts (t016+) make that explicit. It should never initiate combat and should flee (in its slow, unnatural way) rather than press an advantage if attacked — narratively suppress Blood Frenzy entirely unless a DM specifically wants a single unnerving exception. A very light, deniable tie to [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]] — nothing here should be legible as "belonging" to It on a first encounter, only in hindsight.
