It That Kept the Kingdom (Stat Block)

CREATURE · The Deep Presence

See [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]] for the full narrative writeup — this entry is the mechanical shape only, and should stay unread by anyone but the DM until the finale at [[the_undoing]] gives it context.

DM Notes

Do not build a bespoke stat block. Reskin an Aboleth or Elder Brain base (CR 10-14) as the starting point and add 2-3 legendary actions plus a lair-action set drawn from [[the_undoing]]'s environment (a whispered command that forces a save, a momentary collapse of the boundary that deals psychic damage in an area, a "look away" effect that punishes direct confrontation rather than rewarding it) — scale the final CR to roughly CR 13-16 for a 4-person level 9-10 party, adjusting for table strength. Legendary resistance is appropriate; a straightforward damage race is not the intent.

Critically: direct combat should be the least likely path to actually resolving the finale. It is old, patient, and has never needed to win a fight to get what it wants — see [[the_undoing]]'s DM notes for how the destroy/renew/invent branches are meant to work around a confrontation rather than through one. If combat does happen, it should feel like arguing with weather: overwhelming, alien, and not actually the thing that ends the encounter. A full retreat or a bargain struck mid-fight are both valid, even encouraged, outs. Keep description restrained per this campaign's tone guidance in `pipeline/CAMPAIGN_BIBLE.md` — dread and scale, not gore.
