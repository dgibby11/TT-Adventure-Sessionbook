The Faithful Husk

CREATURE · Choir Thralls

What's left of a [[drowned_choir]] pilgrim who reached [[the_faithfuls_end]] and kept going past the point where faith should have stopped them. Where [[choir_thrall]] describes ordinary zealots bound to service in the Drowned Districts, a faithful husk went further and closer than any sanctioned rite prepares for, and came back changed rather than devoted.

It still moves like a pilgrim mid-hymn — the same reverent, unhurried motion — which makes the moment it turns hostile worse, not better.

What the Party Might Notice

  • It hums fragments of hymns from [[the_drowned_hymnal]], slightly wrong, as if the melody is being remembered rather than sung (Perception DC 13).
  • Its devotional posture and gear are intact and clearly cared-for, in contrast to the violence it's capable of (Investigation DC 14).
  • Unlike a standard choir thrall, it shows no fear response and does not flee even when clearly outmatched (no check needed, observable in combat).

DM Notes

Reskin as a stronger variant of [[choir_thrall]] — comparable base creature, higher CR appropriate to a level 9-10 encounter, no morale/flee behavior. Play the tragedy angle from [[the_faithfuls_end]]'s DM notes through to the fight itself: this was a person, and the fight should read as sad rather than triumphant. Not every faithful husk needs to be fought — a party that recognizes what happened and chooses to leave them be, or attempts something merciful, should find that's a legitimate option too.
