Silt Wraith

CREATURE · Mounting Omens

Less a creature than a shape the silt makes when something looks at it wrong — a drifting column of disturbed sediment and old-kingdom debris that holds a roughly humanoid outline for seconds at a time before dissolving back into the current. It has never been confirmed to touch anyone. It has never been confirmed to be harmless, either.

Sightings cluster around the district's dead zones — [[thornwake_hollow]]'s bare stone, the long avenue of [[the_susurrus_deep]] — always at the edge of lantern light, always gone by the time a diver turns to look directly at it. It doesn't flee, exactly. It just stops being where you're looking.

What the Party Might Notice

  • It only ever holds shape in peripheral vision — looking straight at the sighting makes it dissolve immediately (Perception DC 15 to even register it before it does).
  • No current or disturbance explains the silt's movement into a held shape.
  • Divers who've seen it multiple times report the shape getting marginally more defined each time — never confirmed, never denied.

Traits

Amorphous. The wraith can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.

Actions

None by design. The silt wraith never initiates an attack and has no offensive action in normal play — see DM Notes. If a table forces a genuine confrontation, a Shadow's Strength Drain (Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 9 (2d6+2) necrotic damage, target's Strength score reduced by 1d4 until it finishes a long rest) is available as a last-resort fallback only.

DM Notes

Not a combat encounter — it should never initiate contact, and if attacked simply disperses and reforms elsewhere later, with no mechanical consequence either way (ignore the fallback Strength Drain action above unless a table specifically wants one rare, unnerving exception). This is [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]]'s house rule (pressure and dread, never a confrontation) made slightly more visible than the Shallows' salt_warped_grouper was, consistent with the district's escalation. Keep the "getting more defined" detail true but glacial — it shouldn't visibly progress within a single campaign arc's worth of sightings; that thread belongs to the Deep Kingdom chapters if the DM wants to pay it off at all. Defensive stat line (AC/HP/resistances/traits above) is a reskinned Shadow, chosen as the closest published silhouette-in-darkness undead — used here only so a DM has real numbers on hand if a player insists on physically grabbing at it, not as an invitation to fight it.
