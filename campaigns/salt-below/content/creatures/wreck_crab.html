Wreck-Walker Crab

CREATURE · Salvage Hazards

An oversized crab, shell often crusted with barnacles and scavenged metal fittings, that makes its home in the dark hollows of a wreck's hold. Slow and unimpressive in open water, but genuinely dangerous in the cramped confines it prefers — a diver who stumbles into one in a collapsed cabin has far fewer options than one who spots it coming across open sand.

Territorial rather than predatory in the strict sense: it will defend a claimed wreck aggressively but has little interest in chasing prey that retreats. Salvage crews working [[anchors_grave]], [[reavers_ribs]], or [[the_marrow_trawler]] treat a fresh set of claw-drag scars on a hull as a reliable warning sign to work the site in pairs.

DM Notes

Mechanically, reskin a Giant Crab (or scale up slightly with a Giant Constrictor Snake's grapple math if a tougher fight is wanted) — a low-CR hazard best used as an environmental obstacle inside a wreck's tight interior rather than an open-water encounter. Good general-purpose "wreck has a resident" flavor for any of the batch-2 wreck sites without needing to be tied to one specifically.
