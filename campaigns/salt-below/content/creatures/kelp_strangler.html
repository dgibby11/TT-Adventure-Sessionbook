Kelp Strangler

CREATURE · Salvage Hazards

A patch of kelp that isn't quite kelp — dense fronds that hang motionless until something swims close, then lash out to grapple and constrict. From a short distance it's indistinguishable from the surrounding forest, which is exactly the problem in a site like [[the_bellweed_shallows]], where dense growth is the norm rather than the exception.

It doesn't hunt so much as wait at the center of a claimed patch, and multiple stranglers rooted near each other can produce the kind of denser, spiraling growth pattern sharp-eyed divers sometimes notice from a distance — worth flagging to a party that investigates the Bellweed Shallows' odd kelp spiral.

DM Notes

Mechanically, reskin a Vine Blight, keeping its false-appearance/ambush trait as the core gimmick — a party that makes a Perception or Survival check before entering a dense patch should have a real chance to notice something's off. Ties loosely to the_bellweed_shallows' unresolved "spiral growth" curiosity without fully explaining it — a cluster of stranglers is a satisfying, mundane partial answer that still leaves room for something stranger underneath, if a later iteration wants to build on it.
