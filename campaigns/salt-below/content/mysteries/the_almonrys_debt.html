A Debt to the Almonry

MYSTERY · Side Quests

[[the_choir_almonry]] shelters whoever needs it, faithful or not, and [[deacon_rill|Deacon Rill]] never asks anything in return — which means its supplies are always stretched thinner than they should be. This month a shipment of medicine and mending-cloth meant for the almonry went missing between [[the_long_docks|the docks]] and here, and Rill is too proud to beg the Concordat for a replacement he knows they'd tie up in licensing paperwork for weeks.

He'd never ask the party outright. He'll mention it, if asked what's wrong, in the same earnest, unguarded way he mentions everything else.

DM Notes

Deliberately small and skippable. The shipment was diverted — not stolen maliciously, just quietly resold — by a dockside porter behind on his own debts; a short Investigation/Persuasion scene at [[the_long_docks]] or [[market_quarter]] should locate it without requiring combat, though a DM who wants a fight can have the porter's buyer refuse to give it back peacefully.

Recovering and delivering the shipment costs the party nothing but time and roughly 20-30 gp if they choose to just buy it back outright instead of tracking the porter down. The real reward isn't gold: it's the "genuine, uncomplicated debt owed to the Choir" flagged in [[the_choir_almonry]]'s own notes, finally paid the other direction. Rill remembers this specifically and it should soften how the party is treated by rank-and-file Choir members later, even after [[drowned_choir|the Choir]]'s goals stop aligning cleanly with the party's.
