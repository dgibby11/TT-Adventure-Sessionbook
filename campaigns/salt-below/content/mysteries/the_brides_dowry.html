The Bride's Undelivered Dowry

MYSTERY · Side Quests

Every licensed crew that dives [[the_foundered_bride|the Foundered Bride]] knows about the sealed dowry chest in the lower hold — and every licensed crew leaves it alone. Divers call it bad luck to crack open a dead bride's dowry, though nobody agrees on why the superstition started or who started it.

[[ivo_marlstone|Ivo Marlstone]] doesn't believe in bad luck. He's decided this season is the one where somebody finally opens that chest, and he'd rather it be the party than a rival crew — split the take evenly, and he'll vouch for whoever helps him do it.

DM Notes

Small, self-contained, and entirely skippable — a good early-session contract for a party that wants pure salvage adventure before factional politics take over. No combat is required; the chest's lock is old and stubborn (Thieves' Tools or Strength DC 13 to force), not trapped or guarded.

Inside: roughly 300 gp in dowry jewelry and ceremonial dishware, plus a single locket holding a painted miniature and a name that matches no family Port Calder's records recognize. It's a quiet, human-scale echo of the "evacuation, not disaster" throughline that pays off properly at [[vaelgard_terrace]] and [[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery|the central mystery]] — nothing here should feel ominous or require follow-up; if the party doesn't chase it, that's fine. Ivo is thrilled either way and treats the party as friends after this — a Known Face favor with [[freedivers]] (see [[salt_below_campaign_overview]]'s Faction Standing) worth remembering later.
