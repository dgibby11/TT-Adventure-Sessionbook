The Wardrobe's Quiet Visitor

MYSTERY · Side Quests

[[the_ashen_wardrobe]] doesn't appear on any Concordat manifest, and its lock shows fresh tool-marks despite the vault's age. Someone has been inside more than once, careful each time to leave everything exactly as they found it — except for a single item, handled recently, then replaced with visible care.

Word in the right Concordat circles is that [[coraline_bray|Coraline Bray]] has a personal interest in this vault that never made it into any official log.

DM Notes

A self-contained resolution to a hook seeded early ([[the_ashen_wardrobe]]) and left open. The handled item is a small portrait-locket of an old-kingdom aristocrat with Bray's own features, unmistakably — a family connection she has never told Perrin or Wick about, and isn't sure what it means that she can trace her line back before the flood at all.

If the party confronts her directly with what they've found (no combat, a social scene), Bray is rattled but not hostile — she'll admit the truth quietly, ask the party to keep it to themselves, and offer a genuine 150 gp "consulting fee" plus a standing favor: one no-questions-asked Concordat licensing waiver, usable once. If the party simply takes the locket without confronting her, nothing punishes them for it, but the favor never gets offered. Keep this melancholy and personal, not conspiratorial — it's about Bray, not about the Concordat's cover-ups.
