The Vaelgard Signet

MYSTERY · Side Quests

Old Wren Castellan runs a mending stall in the [[market_quarter|Market Quarter]] and has told anyone who'd listen, for years, that her family carries old-kingdom blood — a claim most of Port Calder treats as harmless tavern color. She has one piece of proof: a battered signet ring, worn smooth, engraved with a house-mark nobody in the city can read anymore.

She'll pay well for confirmation — a matching mark, a name, anything that isn't just her grandmother's story. Divers working [[vaelgard_terrace]] have started noticing house-marks on the terrace's better-preserved doors that look close enough to make her ring worth a second look.

DM Notes

Wren Castellan is a minor, one-scene NPC — she doesn't need a full entity, just enough personality to make the payoff land (warm, a little embarrassed by her own hope, quietly moved if the party comes back with an answer). The matching house-mark is on the highest, best-preserved terrace house, alongside a small intact family signet-box holding a second, mirrored ring and a handful of personal effects untouched by looting, matching [[vaelgard_terrace]]'s established "evacuation, not disaster" tone.

Reward: Wren pays 120 gp for confirmation and the matching ring, more if the party also brings back the signet-box's other contents (another 80 gp of sentimental-but-real value to her, though she'd pay it for the story alone). No danger is required beyond whatever ambient Deep Kingdom tone the DM wants to apply to Vaelgard Terrace generally — this quest itself has no hazard or creature attached. Keep the tone gentle; this is a quiet identity/heritage beat, not a mystery to be solved with combat.
