Harbor Watch

FACTION · Guild & Authority

Harbor Watch is Port Calder's law — what there is of it. A patchwork of city-funded constables and a handful of veterans who actually know what they're doing, chronically short-staffed and chronically underpaid relative to what the job demands. They break up tavern brawls, chase down cargo theft, and file incident reports on things considerably stranger than cargo theft, usually with the same tired resignation.

What Harbor Watch lacks in resources it makes up in earnestness. Nobody joins the Watch to get rich — the Concordat pays better and asks less of you. The people who stay, stay because they actually care whether Port Calder holds together, which makes the Watch one of the few institutions in the city a party can trust more or less at face value.

In Play

Based out of the [[harbor_watch_garrison]]. A reliable ally faction and a natural source of investigation hooks — the duty board at the garrison tracks a growing list of unresolved incidents the Watch simply doesn't have the manpower to chase down themselves, which is exactly the gap a capable party can fill.

DM Notes

Harbor Watch should never feel incompetent, only outmatched — write their failures as a resource problem, not a competence problem. As the campaign moves from the Shallows into the Drowned Districts, the Watch's authority effectively ends at the reef line; they know this, and it eats at them. A good long-arc beat: the Watch quietly starts relying on the party (or the [[freedivers]]) to handle exactly the incidents that are actually outside its jurisdiction, because there's no one else to ask.
