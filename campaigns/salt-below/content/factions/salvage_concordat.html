The Salvage Concordat

FACTION · Guild & Authority

The Concordat licenses every legal dive out of Port Calder, taxes the haul that comes back, and — on paper — makes sure the trade that built this city doesn't tear it apart. In practice, it's the closest thing the boomtown has to an aristocracy: old dive fortunes, guild seats bought and inherited, and a genuine, sincere belief that keeping the boom booming is the single most important job in Port Calder.

Nobody at the Concordat thinks of themselves as a villain. They think of themselves as the only adults in the room — the ones willing to make the unglamorous calls (quietly pulling a license, burying a bad report, smoothing over a diver who came back saying something no one wants repeated) so that the docks keep working and the money keeps moving. That's exactly what makes them dangerous: every cover-up is defensible, right up until it isn't.

Leadership

  • [[perrin_ashgrave]] — Guildmaster; runs the Concordat and, functionally, a fair share of Port Calder's civic life.
  • [[wick_tallow]] — Licensing Officer; the face most divers actually deal with.
  • [[coraline_bray]] — "The Adjuster"; handles anything the Guildmaster would rather not have on the record.

In Play

Base of operations is the [[concordat_licensing_house]]. Most parties will first meet the Concordat as pure bureaucracy — permits, fees, inspection stamps — before realizing how much civic weight sits behind that paperwork. Useful as a source of legitimate dive contracts and as a slow-building political obstacle: the Concordat is rarely an enemy to fight, more often a wall to work around.

DM Notes

Play the Concordat's antagonism as institutional self-interest, never cartoon villainy. They genuinely believe covering up bad finds protects Port Calder from panic and protects the trade everyone depends on — they are usually correct about the short-term effect, and usually wrong about the long-term cost. The Concordat has quietly stopped licensing several dive sites near the edge of the Shallows (see [[port_calder|Port Calder's]] Rumors) precisely because of finds it doesn't want examined too closely; this is the party's likeliest first real friction with the guild.

Do not make the Concordat aware of [[it_that_kept_the_kingdom]] directly — their caution is bureaucratic instinct and pattern-recognition from decades of "irregular" reports, not genuine knowledge of what's actually down there. See [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]]: their cover-ups are one of several self-interested pressures quietly wearing down the binding, without anyone at the Concordat knowing that's what they're doing.
