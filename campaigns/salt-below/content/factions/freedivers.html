The Freedivers

FACTION · Independents

Not a guild, not really an organization — more a loose, overlapping network of independent salvagers who trade information as freely as they trade jobs. Where the Concordat licenses the trade and Harbor Watch polices it, the Freedivers are the ones who actually know the reef: which currents to trust, which wrecks are picked clean, and which sites everyone quietly agrees not to talk about at the licensing house.

Freedivers respect what's below in a way the Concordat doesn't bother to. They trade in warnings as readily as loot, and the ones who've been diving longest tend to be the most careful, not the most reckless. For most new arrivals in Port Calder — including, likely, the party — the Freedivers are the natural way in: less paperwork than the Concordat, more honesty than either the Watch or the guild.

Notable Members

  • [[ruen_saltmarrow]] — "Old Ruen"; the network's closest thing to an elder, and its main source of contracts.
  • [[ivo_marlstone]] — Rising star of the crews; fast, sharp, hungry for a name.
  • [[sela_vantry]] — Dives less than she used to, warns more than anyone.

In Play

Freedivers work out of [[dive_outfitters_row]] and the stalls of [[market_quarter]], with no fixed headquarters — the network is people, not a building. The default source of early dive contracts and the campaign's natural "home faction," suitable for PCs who want a straightforward, honest patron rather than a guild ladder to climb.

DM Notes

The Freedivers are the campaign's throughline patron faction — Old Ruen should be the party's first and most reliable contract source in Port Calder, especially once the Concordat starts getting cagey about licensing certain sites (see [[salvage_concordat]]). Internally, the network disagrees about how far is too far: some crews (Ivo's generation) push depth and risk for bigger hauls and bigger names; others (Sela, and increasingly Ruen) think the network has already gone deeper than it should. This tension is a slow-burn source of party-facing choices, not a plot to resolve quickly.
