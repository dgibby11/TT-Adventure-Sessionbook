Isolt Vane, "the Choirmother"

NPC · The Drowned Choir

Leads the [[drowned_choir]] not through authority but through conviction — Vane speaks about the sunken kingdom with the certainty of someone who has genuinely seen enough to believe it, and enough of what she says lines up with fact that it's dangerous to dismiss her outright. Presides over the candlelit processions to [[the_drowned_bell]] and leads the Choir's old-tongue rites herself.

Calm, patient, and unshakeable — Vane does not recruit through fear or pressure, only through the sincerity of her own belief, which makes her considerably more persuasive than a more aggressive cult leader would be.

DM Notes

Vane genuinely believes she is working to liberate something that was wronged, not to unleash a threat — she is functionally sincere and functionally wrong, in the exact proportions that make the Choir dangerous. She should never read as a moustache-twirling villain; if anything, a party that talks to her at length should come away shaken by how much of what she says is true. Keep her direct knowledge of It That Kept the Kingdom (t010) impressionistic and ritual-derived, not concrete — she knows fragments, not the whole shape of the deal.
