Harbormaster Yselda Wrenn

NPC · Port Calder

Runs [[harbormasters_post]] with a precision nobody asked for and everybody's grateful someone provides — berth assignments, tide tables, weather postings, all logged in the same steady hand she's used for eleven years. Weathered, unhurried, and impossible to rattle; she's talked panicked crews off worse ledges than a bad tide.

She keeps a second logbook, separate from the official one, for "unusual finds" — a habit she's never explained to anyone who's asked, including [[harbor_watch|Harbor Watch]], who technically has authority over her post and has never quite gotten around to reading it.

DM Notes

Wrenn has been quietly cross-referencing currents, tide anomalies, and diver reports for over a decade without ever using the words "It That Kept the Kingdom" — she doesn't have the vocabulary the party will eventually have, but her pattern-sense is real and further along than she realizes (see [[the_breaking_deal_mystery]]). She'll share the second logbook with a party that earns real trust (not purchased, not intimidated) rather than official standing; Harbor Watch rank means nothing to her, demonstrated competence does. Play her as a quiet, patient resource the party can return to across the whole campaign, not a one-scene NPC.
