Rosalind Vey

NPC · Shellhome Ward

Keeper of whichever street-shrine currently holds one of [[shellhome_ward]]'s rotating courtyard corners — faiths and keepers change hands often here, and Vey has outlasted three shrines' worth of neighbors by simply being the one everyone trusts to sweep up afterward. Warm, gossipy, and genuinely devoted to whatever's currently on the little altar, without much concern for whether it was there last season.

Lately a new, unfamiliar shrine has appeared in the ward's rotation — one Vey didn't invite and can't quite place the iconography of, though the offerings left at it keep disappearing overnight.

DM Notes

Vey is the NPC hook for [[shellhome_ward]]'s existing "unfamiliar courtyard shrine" note — the new shrine is a quiet [[drowned_choir|Drowned Choir]] recruitment foothold, and Vey (mildly annoyed, not alarmed) is a natural way for the party to hear about it before the Choir becomes an obvious presence. Vey doesn't know it's the Choir unless the party tells her or she's pushed to investigate directly.
