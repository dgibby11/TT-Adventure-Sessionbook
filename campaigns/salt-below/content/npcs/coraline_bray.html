Coraline Bray, "The Adjuster"

NPC · Salvage Concordat

Officially, Bray handles "claims irregularities" for the Concordat. Unofficially, she's the one who makes bad reports disappear — the diver who came back saying something impossible gets a generous settlement, a strong drink, and a very clear sense that the story ends there. She's polite about it. She's always polite about it.

Bray isn't cruel. She's efficient, and she's convinced — correctly, so far — that her discretion has kept Port Calder from a dozen panics it didn't need to have.

DM Notes

Bray is the Concordat's most direct connection to whatever's actually being covered up, and the party's most likely lead if they start pulling that thread. She keeps a private ledger of every "irregular" incident she's settled — not hidden exactly, just never volunteered — that would be a significant find: years of small, dismissed hints about the Drowned Districts and worse, in one place. She won't hand it over willingly; she genuinely believes releasing it would do more harm than good.
