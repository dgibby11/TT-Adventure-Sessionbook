Scout Rees Holt

NPC · Harbor Watch

A [[harbor_watch|Harbor Watch]] scout quietly working [[ashgate_wardhouse]] off the books at [[tam_ostler|Sergeant Ostler]]'s request — Watch brass wants to know what's inside the old wardhouse before the [[salvage_concordat|Concordat]] gets there first, and can't spare an official expedition to find out. Careful, methodical, and used to working alone; three separate solo trips down have left marks scraped clear of silt at the entrance, which Holt assumed nobody would notice.

Hasn't gone inside yet. Something about the wardhouse's stillness has kept Holt circling the entrance rather than committing to it, and Holt isn't proud of that hesitation.

DM Notes

Resolves [[ashgate_wardhouse]]'s "recently cleared entrance" hook as Holt's own tracks rather than a rival crew, per that location's existing notes (a DM who wants a rival-crew subplot instead can still use that option — Holt is additive, not a replacement). A good hand-off NPC: Holt can recruit the party to finish what Watch resources won't let Holt do alone, framing first contact with [[warden_husk]] as backup for a nervous, competent scout rather than a cold solo delve. Report back to [[tam_ostler]] afterward for a Known Face favor with [[harbor_watch]] (see [[salt_below_campaign_overview]]'s Faction Standing).
