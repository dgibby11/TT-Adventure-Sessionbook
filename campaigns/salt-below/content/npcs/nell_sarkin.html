Appraiser Nell Sarkin

NPC · Port Calder

Works a raised appraisal stand in the [[market_quarter]], calling prices off the day's Concordat buy-rate board in a bored, practiced sing-song. Fast, precise, and unfailingly polite — the kind of professional courtesy that makes it easy to miss how consistently her numbers favor the house.

She low-balls unlicensed finds a little more than the board technically allows, and everyone who sells to her regularly knows it. Most sell to her anyway; she's still the fairest appraiser on the bluff.

DM Notes

Low-stakes, concrete demonstration of [[salvage_concordat]]'s soft corruption per [[market_quarter]]'s existing notes — Sarkin isn't hiding anything sinister, she's just quietly steering margin toward the guild the way she's told to. She'll speak plainly about Concordat pricing pressure if a party gains her trust off the clock (a drink at [[the_brined_anchor]] works), making her a useful low-friction source for how the guild actually operates day to day, without any of Coraline Bray's stakes attached.
