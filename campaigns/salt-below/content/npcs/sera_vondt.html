Sera Vondt

NPC · The Drowned Choir

Keeps the tide-night vigils at [[the_chime_shoal]] — sets the candles adrift, waits for the hum, and leaves before the water turns cold again. Vondt isn't a deacon or anything close to Choir leadership; she's simply been coming to the shoal longer than almost any other convert still active, and newer converts follow her lead without being told to.

Soft-spoken and unbothered by outsiders watching, so long as they don't disturb the candles mid-tide. She'll answer honest questions about the shoal plainly, in the same unhurried way she does everything else here.

DM Notes

Gives [[the_chime_shoal]] a named face for the "ambient Choir practice" the location's DM notes call for, without escalating the site into a named-leader encounter — Vondt should read as sincere, low-key devotion, a contrast to [[isolt_vane]]'s more central and more ambiguous role in the faith. A good soft first contact with the Choir for a party not yet ready to meet [[deacon_rill]] or [[isolt_vane]] directly.
