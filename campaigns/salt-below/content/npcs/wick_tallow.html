Licensing Officer Wick Tallow

NPC · Salvage Concordat

The clerk behind the counter at the [[concordat_licensing_house]] that every diver in Port Calder has dealt with at least once. Overworked, underpaid, and surprisingly decent about it — Tallow will bend a rule for a diver he likes and has a good memory for who's owed a favor.

Not senior enough to know what [[perrin_ashgrave|Ashgrave]] and [[coraline_bray|Bray]] actually discuss behind the office door, but sharp enough to have noticed the pattern of "denied, no reason given" stamped across certain permit applications.

DM Notes

A useful low-stakes informant — Tallow can point the party toward which dive sites have had their licenses quietly pulled without knowing (or wanting to know) why. If pushed hard enough with the right leverage, he's a plausible crack in the Concordat's wall, though he genuinely doesn't have much to give beyond suspicion and paperwork trends.
