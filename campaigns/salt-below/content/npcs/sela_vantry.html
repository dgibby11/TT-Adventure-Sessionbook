Sela Vantry

NPC · The Freedivers

Dives less than she used to and talks about it more — Vantry saw something on a deep run a few years back that she still won't describe in full, and has spent the time since making sure other [[freedivers|Freedivers]] don't repeat whatever mistake she made. Works the stalls of [[market_quarter]] most days, trading gear and information in roughly equal measure.

Not unfriendly, but direct to the point of bluntness with anyone she thinks is about to do something reckless.

DM Notes

Sela is the campaign's built-in warning system — she should know, in vague-but-accurate terms, roughly how deep is "still fine" versus "past the point crews stopped coming back from," without ever getting specific about what she saw. Good NPC to reintroduce right before the party crosses a real threshold (into the Drowned Districts, especially). Her account, if the party earns enough trust to hear the full version, should be the first real hint of something beyond ordinary salvage hazards.
