Corporal Dessa Wrack

NPC · Harbor Watch

The [[harbor_watch|Harbor Watch]] patrol officer who actually walks the [[wraithlock_gate]] beat out of [[ashgate_wardhouse]] — and the reason the Watch and [[salvage_concordat|Concordat]]'s quiet coexistence there holds. Wrack knows exactly whose rope and lantern-oil residue is whose, and has decided, unilaterally, that filing a jurisdiction complaint would cost more goodwill than it's worth. Professional, unflappable, and privately relieved nobody's asked Wrack to justify the arrangement out loud.

Will answer straight questions straight, including about the vault doors behind the gate — Wrack doesn't know what's in them and has stopped wondering out loud.

DM Notes

Wrack is the NPC face of [[wraithlock_gate]]'s existing faction-friction hook — a party that wants a small favor from either Harbor Watch or the Concordat can go through Wrack, who'll grant it quietly as long as it doesn't force the arrangement into the open. If the DM raises the stakes with a [[warden_husk]] behind the vault door, Wrack is the one who has to decide whether to report it.
