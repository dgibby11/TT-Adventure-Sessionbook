Ruen Saltmarrow, "Old Ruen"

NPC · The Freedivers

The closest thing the [[freedivers]] have to an elder — not their leader, since the network doesn't really have one, but the diver whose word carries the most weight. Ruen has been diving the reef longer than most of Port Calder has been alive, and has the scars and the caution to show for it. Still dives occasionally, mostly hands out contracts and advice now.

The natural first contact for new arrivals looking for honest work — Ruen vets every crew personally before sending them out, and is famously more interested in whether a diver listens than whether they're strong.

DM Notes

Ruen is the campaign's default patron — this should be the party's first and most consistent source of dive contracts in Port Calder. Ruen has grown quietly more cautious in recent years, increasingly aligned with [[sela_vantry|Sela Vantry's]] view that the network is pushing deeper than it should; this puts Ruen in gentle tension with [[ivo_marlstone|Ivo Marlstone's]] more ambitious crews, a tension the party can be pulled into either side of.
