Petra Wexler

NPC · Salvage Concordat

The licensing clerk who reads every report filed on [[the_kettle]] — and, unlike most clerks assigned that duty, actually reads them, cross-referencing gyre behavior and debris patterns dive after dive in a set of ledgers she keeps at her own expense, not the guild's.

Wexler will trade a sympathetic ear and a genuine expert's read on Kettle conditions for any crew willing to describe what they saw down there in detail — she's not required to ask, and the guild's official interest in the site doesn't explain how much of her own time she puts into it.

DM Notes

A quiet lead-in to [[the_kettle]]'s DM-notes hook about the site being one of the Concordat's quietly over-licensed "irregular" sites — Wexler's personal obsession can be played as entirely her own (an unresolved curiosity the guild tolerates) or, if the DM wants a more active informant, as someone who suspects what the Concordat is covering up and is fishing for confirmation through diver reports. Keep her motive ambiguous rather than committing either way unless play calls for it.
