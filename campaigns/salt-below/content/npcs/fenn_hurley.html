Captain Fenn Hurley

NPC · Harbor Watch

Commands [[harbor_watch]] out of the [[harbor_watch_garrison]] with a permanently tired look and a genuine, stubborn refusal to let the Watch fall apart. Hurley has turned down two standing offers from the [[salvage_concordat|Concordat]] to double her pay for a security-consulting post — she'd rather run an undermanned garrison she believes in than a well-funded one she wouldn't.

Straightforward with the party from the first meeting: the Watch needs help, she'll say so plainly, and she'll pay what the city budget allows, which is never quite enough.

DM Notes

Hurley is a reliable, honest ally faction contact — use her to hand out investigation hooks the Watch genuinely can't staff, especially anything that starts crossing into the Shallows. She knows her authority effectively ends at the reef line and it quietly eats at her; a good long-arc beat is her coming to rely on the party for exactly the incidents outside her jurisdiction.
