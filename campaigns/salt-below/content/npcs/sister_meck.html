Sister Meck

NPC · The Drowned Choir

A longtime member of the [[drowned_choir]] who has started, quietly, to doubt — not the truth of the old kingdom, but whether raising it is really the mercy the Choir believes it to be. Still attends the rites at [[the_drowned_bell]], still wears the Choir's marks, but has begun asking questions [[isolt_vane|Isolt Vane]] doesn't have a comfortable answer for.

Works odd hours near [[saltbone_curios]], trading in old-kingdom oddities the shop won't ask too many questions about.

DM Notes

Meck is the party's most likely wedge into the Choir — close enough to the faith to have real information, doubtful enough to be reachable. She's the member closest to suspecting that "the kingdom" and "the thing kept beneath it" might not want the same outcome, without yet having the full picture herself. A well-run scene with Meck should give the party real, partial truth about the Choir's beliefs without resolving the central mystery outright.
