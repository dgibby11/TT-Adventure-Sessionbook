Ilsa Cray

NPC · Tidewrack Ward

Runs the busiest shared cookfire in [[tidewrack_ward]], which makes Cray the closest thing the ward has to a switchboard — every disappearance, bad haul, or overdue crew gets talked through at that fire before it reaches anyone official. Cray has buried a husband and a nephew to the trade and has no patience left for anyone who treats a missing dive crew as an abstraction.

Blunt, warm to those she trusts, and openly contemptuous of [[salvage_concordat|Concordat]] licensing fees that price out the very crews doing the dying. Knows most of Tidewrack's residents by name and most of their business besides.

DM Notes

Cray is the fastest, most honest rumor mill in Port Calder — use her to deliver ground-level gossip the official channels (Concordat, Harbor Watch) would sanitize or bury, especially anything touching overdue crews or unexplained hauls. A good NPC for grounding the party in what the salvage trade actually costs its own people, distinct from [[coraline_bray]]'s guild-side version of the same information.
