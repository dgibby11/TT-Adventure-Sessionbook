Corin Draw

NPC · The Drowned Choir

One of the [[drowned_choir|Drowned Choir]] converts squatting in the emptied townhouses along [[the_suitors_approach]] — claimed the best-preserved one for himself two tide-seasons back and has been quietly restoring it since, plaque and all, like the original petitioner might someday come back for an answer.

Draw isn't dangerous and isn't hiding — he'll talk to anyone who knocks, at length, about the plaques and the unresolved cases behind each name. He just never quite answers why that matters so much to him, and changes the subject if pressed.

DM Notes

Gives the Approach's "a few converts squat here" line a named entry point without turning the street into a Choir stronghold — Draw is a soft, talkative source for the founding-family plaque hook already seeded in [[the_suitors_approach]]'s player-facing text, and a low-stakes first contact with an ordinary Choir convert (as opposed to a leader like [[isolt_vane]] or [[deacon_rill]]) for a party not yet ready to engage the Choir directly.
