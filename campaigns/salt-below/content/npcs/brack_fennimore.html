Watchman Brack Fennimore

NPC · Harbor Watch

The youngest and newest member of [[harbor_watch]], still visibly proud of the uniform. Fennimore draws the unglamorous shifts — night watch at the [[harbor_watch_garrison|garrison]] gate, escort duty, the calls senior Watch would rather not take — and handles them with more enthusiasm than the job strictly requires.

Eager to prove himself to [[tam_ostler|Sergeant Ostler]] and more than a little starstruck by capable adventurers.

DM Notes

Useful as the party's low-friction first Watch contact — Fennimore will happily over-share rumor and gossip he's not really cleared to share, out of enthusiasm rather than indiscretion. A good NPC to grow over the campaign: a green recruit in Port Calder who could plausibly become a genuinely capable Watch officer by the campaign's end.
