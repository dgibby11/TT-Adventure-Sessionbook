Bram Coswick

NPC · The Freedivers

The freediver who administers [[widows_combs]] contracts — not because the Concordat requires it, but because [[freedivers|the Freedivers]] decided decades ago that nobody dives the Combs for the first time without someone experienced knowing they've gone in. Coswick keeps the informal roster, and the memorial marker's names are ones Coswick can mostly still put faces to.

Blunt, unsentimental about the risk, and the furthest thing from a gatekeeper — Coswick will talk anyone reckless enough out of it exactly once, then help them plan the dive properly if they still want to go.

DM Notes

Turns [[widows_combs]]'s "rite of passage" framing into a concrete quest-giver rather than pure atmosphere — Coswick is the natural NPC to hand a party the Combs as a proving-ground side objective, complete with the roster and memorial-marker detail to make the site's body count feel earned rather than decorative. Pairs naturally with [[ruen_saltmarrow]] or [[ivo_marlstone]] if the DM wants a Freedivers subplot around who gets to call themselves blooded.
