Sergeant Tam Ostler

NPC · Harbor Watch

Runs the [[harbor_watch_garrison|garrison's]] day-to-day — duty rosters, the incident board, and whichever recruit needs steadying that week. Ostler is the Watch's actual institutional memory; [[fenn_hurley|Captain Hurley]] sets direction, Ostler makes sure it actually happens.

Blunt, dry-humored, and the most likely Watch contact for a party working a specific case rather than reporting to the Captain directly.

DM Notes

Ostler is the practical face of the duty board — use them to hand off specific unresolved incidents as discrete quest hooks without needing a scene with Hurley every time. Good NPC for recurring, low-ceremony check-ins between missions.
