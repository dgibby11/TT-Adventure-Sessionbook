Brother Amos Kell

NPC · The Drowned Choir

The quiet, unassuming face of [[drowned_choir|the Drowned Choir]]'s first real presence at [[vantmoor_hall]]. Kell tends the handful of strung lanterns marking the vigil space, greets strangers with unhurried politeness, and answers questions honestly — as far as Kell's own rank in the Choir actually knows anything worth telling. A dockworker before converting, Kell still has the calluses and the plain speech to match; nothing about Kell reads as fanatic.

Kell won't stop a party from exploring further into the district, and won't lie about what's down there either — just offers the Choir's own framing of it (a kept deal, not a curse) without much interest in arguing the point.

DM Notes

A soft first-contact NPC: use Kell to deliver the Choir's basic pitch (see [[isolt_vane]]/[[why_the_kingdom_drowned_mystery]]) in miniature, without committing to leadership-level detail Kell wouldn't actually have. Kell can point a party toward [[chancel_deep]] or [[the_suitors_approach]] as naturally as the location's own notes suggest, giving the DM a face to hang that nudge on instead of ambient narration.
