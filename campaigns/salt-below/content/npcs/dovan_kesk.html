Inspector Dovan Kesk

NPC · Salvage Concordat

Stationed at [[calder_harbor]]'s inspection skiff, checking manifests against hulls as they come in from the reef. Kesk has the job because Kesk is genuinely good at it — a glance at a waterline and a loaded hold tells him roughly what's aboard before the paperwork's even out, and he's rarely wrong twice about the same crew.

Not corrupt, not zealous — just thorough, and thoroughness is its own kind of trouble for anyone hauling something they'd rather the Concordat not log. Crews with nothing to hide like Kesk fine; crews with something to hide learn his rotation schedule.

DM Notes

Gives [[calder_harbor]] a recurring named face without turning the harbor into a checkpoint — Kesk is background texture on an easy day and a real obstacle the one day the party's hauling something they shouldn't be. Useful as a soft timer ("Kesk's rotation is back in an hour") or a source of exactly the kind of detail-oriented gossip a harbor inspector would pick up about other crews' comings and goings.
