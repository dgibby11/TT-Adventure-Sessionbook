Ness Thatchford

NPC · The Loom Quarter

One of the handful of independent squatters who actually live in [[the_loom_quarter]], Thatchford has spent months restringing a dead loom-house with scavenged wire and kelp-fiber, determined to weave something wearable out of a district that mostly just gives up salvage. Gruff, practical, and deeply unbothered by the Drowned Districts' ambient dread — Thatchford's been down here long enough that the omens read as background noise.

Trades finished kelp-cloth and small salvage with [[freedivers|Freediver]]-adjacent crews passing through, and is one of the few Drowned Districts residents willing to talk at length to outsiders — mostly because outsiders are the only ones who haven't already heard every story Thatchford has to tell.

DM Notes

Use Thatchford as the human face of this breather location — a genuine conversation, minor trade, or a rumor-drop that isn't Choir doctrine or omen-dread. A good source for small, ground-level gossip about the district (which houses in [[aldermourn_close]] or [[the_suitors_approach]] are worth a look, which aren't) without Thatchford having any real stake in the party's larger plotline.
