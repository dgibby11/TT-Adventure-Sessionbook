Chaplain Ysbel Corvane

NPC · Port Calder

Tends [[the_tideward_chapel]] and, by extension, most of Port Calder's dive-related grief — crushed limbs, burst eardrums, and the quiet ossuary out back for divers recovered but never claimed. Corvane serves whatever faith walks through the door without ever pushing one of her own; the chapel stopped being any single religion's before she took it over, and she's kept it that way on purpose.

Blunt about triage, gentle about everything else. She's buried enough divers that Concordat and Harbor Watch politics don't move her the way they move most of the city.

DM Notes

Corvane's remembrance records are the closest thing Port Calder has to a casualty log for missing divers (see [[the_tideward_chapel]]'s existing notes) — she'll share them freely with anyone asking in good faith about a specific name, no politics attached, making her a clean research route into disappearance patterns tied to the central mystery. She has no hidden agenda; keep her exactly what she appears to be, a genuinely caring healer worn smooth by the job.
