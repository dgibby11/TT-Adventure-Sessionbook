Guildmaster Perrin Ashgrave

NPC · Salvage Concordat

Runs the [[salvage_concordat]] with the calm of a man who has never once lost an argument he cared about winning. Ashgrave built his own fortune on two lucky wreck finds decades back and has spent every year since making sure Port Calder never has another bad enough season to threaten the trade that made him. Publicly gracious, quietly ruthless about anything that touches the guild's bottom line.

Treats the party as a resource the moment they prove competent — legitimate, well-paid contract work, so long as they don't ask too many questions about which sites the Concordat has quietly stopped licensing.

DM Notes

Ashgrave is not lying when he says he protects Port Calder — he genuinely believes panic would do more damage than whatever's actually down there. That belief is exactly what makes him willing to bury reports the party might think should go public. Play him as reasonable, persuasive, and never once acknowledging he's done anything wrong.
