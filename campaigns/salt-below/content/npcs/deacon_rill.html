Deacon Rill

NPC · The Drowned Choir

Recruits for the [[drowned_choir]] at the docks and taverns, catching new converts the way a good salesman catches a mark — except Rill isn't selling anything, he genuinely believes every word. Younger than [[isolt_vane|Isolt Vane]] and considerably more eager to talk to strangers about the Choir's faith, which makes him the party's most likely first point of contact with the cult.

Not dangerous in a direct sense, but persistent, and the kind of true believer who reads any skepticism as a challenge to overcome rather than a reason to stop.

DM Notes

Use Rill as the Choir's low-stakes, recurring "on-ramp" NPC — a party can encounter him repeatedly before ever meeting Vane, and his earnestness should make the Choir feel more unsettling than a more obviously sinister recruiter would. Rill wants the certainty faith gives him more than he understands what he's actually asking the party to help unbind.
