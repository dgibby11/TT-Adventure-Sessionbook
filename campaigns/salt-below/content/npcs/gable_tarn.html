Foreman Gable Tarn

NPC · Millrace Deep

Tarn runs the shift schedule that keeps [[freedivers|Freediver]] and [[salvage_concordat|Concordat]] crews from working [[millrace_deep]]'s jammed machinery pit at the same time — a rough truce Tarn brokered out of pure self-interest after one too many near-misses in the gearing. Neither faction officially recognizes Tarn's authority here, but both quietly defer to the schedule anyway, because it works.

Blunt, practical, and allergic to anything that smells like a bigger story. Tarn wants the scrap contracts filled and the crews unmangled, in that order, and has no patience for divers who go looking for omens in a jammed watermill.

DM Notes

Tarn is a good grounding NPC precisely because there's nothing supernatural to find here — a mundane hazard site needs a mundane authority figure to match. Useful if the party needs a small, apolitical favor (a work slot, a fair-price scrap sale) without wading into Freediver/Concordat faction politics.
