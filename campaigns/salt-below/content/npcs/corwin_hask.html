Outfitter Corwin Hask

NPC · Port Calder

Runs the best-stocked shopfront on [[dive_outfitters_row]] — better seals, better lines, better prices than gear this quality should cost. Cheerful, fast-talking, genuinely good at his trade; he'll fit a new diver for a full rig in under ten minutes and undersell every shop on the row doing it.

None of it comes through [[salvage_concordat]]'s licensed supply chain, and Hask has gotten very practiced at changing the subject whenever a customer asks where the good gear actually comes from.

DM Notes

Hask's supplier is deliberately left open per [[dive_outfitters_row]]'s existing notes — could be honest Freediver smuggling around Concordat tax (a sympathetic, victimless-crime framing) or something worse dredged up rather than traded for. Decide when a faction needs the answer; until then, Hask himself should read as likable and a little too incurious about his own supply chain, not sinister. A good recurring gear-shop contact for a party that outfits regularly.
