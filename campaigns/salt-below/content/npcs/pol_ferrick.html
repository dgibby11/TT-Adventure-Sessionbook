Pol Ferrick

NPC · The Long Docks

Works the unmarked pier at the far end of [[the_long_docks]] — no flag, no manifest, regular traffic at odd hours. Ferrick will say, if asked directly, that the pier serves "private charters" and leave it there. Quiet, courteous, and impossible to fluster; Ferrick has clearly been doing this long enough to have a rehearsed non-answer for every version of the question.

In truth Ferrick ferries goods and the occasional passenger for whoever pays and doesn't ask what's in the crates — which, depending on the week, has included [[drowned_choir|Drowned Choir]] supply runs and [[salvage_concordat|Concordat]] cargo the guild would rather not log.

DM Notes

Resolves [[the_long_docks]]'s "unmarked pier" hook with a named face rather than leaving it purely atmospheric — Ferrick can be tied to whichever faction's dockside logistics the DM wants to spotlight (a Choir supply run per [[deacon_rill]]'s [[the_nightweigh]] smuggling hook, or Concordat cover-up cargo) without committing to only one. A stakeout or tail-the-cart scene plays naturally with Ferrick as the pier's constant, watchable presence.
