Ivo Marlstone

NPC · The Freedivers

The [[freedivers|Freedivers']] rising star — young, fast, and hungry to make a name the way [[ruen_saltmarrow|Old Ruen]] did a generation ago. Ivo leads one of the more ambitious crews working the reef, consistently pushing further and deeper than most, and consistently coming back with better hauls to show for it. So far.

Friendly and competitive rather than hostile toward the party — sees them as either useful rivals or a crew worth recruiting, depending on how a first meeting goes.

DM Notes

Ivo represents the ambitious, risk-forward wing of the Freedivers, in quiet tension with Ruen and [[sela_vantry|Sela's]] growing caution. A good source of competitive-contract hooks (racing another crew to a site) and a plausible complication generator — Ivo's crew is exactly the kind that might push past a boundary the Freedivers have quietly agreed not to cross.
