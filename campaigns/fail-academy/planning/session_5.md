## DM decisions, 2026-10-05

1. **The speaking stone is a pair; Guntrah's father holds the other.** He uses it
   to check in.
2. ***Leomund's Tiny Hut* blocks *sending*.** That is why Voss's first attempt
   failed, and she will ask about it.
3. **Neverwinter is not reached in Session 5.** Too much progression too quickly.
4. **The settlement is Logger's Camp on the DM's map** — the crew's own name
   for it is **Kettle Bend**, which is on no map. A plain logging camp, a
   pass-through for regional information. Suspicious but not bad; somewhat
   helpful.
5. **The camp is 15 miles from the clearing.** Guntrah's twelve was an estimate.
   The loggers keep what they think is a safe distance after two disastrous
   encounters with the dragon, **and are closer to his range than they know.**
   Not important; do not build on it.
6. **Two-hop *plane shift* is allowed** if the party ever obtains a fork for a
   second plane. Vrenn will mention it, to put it on the table. Not expected to
   be pursued.
7. **The Evil Eye deformity most likely ends on this rest.** Tavian's aura and
   Guntrah's Flash of Genius will probably both go on the save. Keep a
   contingency for a failure.
8. **Ted lied.** He was lied to and holds that it licenses a lie in return:
   **"a lie for a lie."** He does not intend to let them leave.
9. **Morvek does not appear.** His reach in this session is **three high-level
   drow assassins**, agents from somewhere nearby (the region sits over large
   drow populations; the exact place does not matter). He gave them orders as
   soon as he learned where Vrenn had gone. Their job, once Vrenn is out of
   reach, is **the steel tuning fork**.
10. **One of the drow carries a credit note bearing the T.A.C. monogram** —
    the Thornwick mark, never the name — redeemable at a brokerage in
    Neverwinter.
11. **The party know about Voss's contact list and what is on it.** They have
    the paper and discussed it with the DM off-microphone during Session 3 or 4.
    It has never been read out *on a recording*, which is all the older notes
    saying "never read aloud" can actually support. **Do not treat the list as an
    unspent reveal.**
12. **The party do not go back into the Breach.** A second fomorian fight would
    not be fun and the DM would run it ruthlessly. It must be closed off without
    reading as a railroad — see "Keeping them out of the Breach" below.
13. **Ted can die, but most likely will not.** He is stronger than a CR 15 green
    and probably short of CR 22. If it comes to a fight the DM keeps it close
    and has him **retreat to his lair**, with the party sensibly choosing not to
    follow.
14. **Vrenn stays in the Wood either way.** The DM does not want him travelling
    with the party any longer. He pushes hard to remain with Ted **to protect
    the party from Morvek**, and may stand his ground on it. The party will not
    offer magic items in his place, so there is no competing bid to plan for.
15. **Voss will not bring them home.** She would rather they went to Neverwinter
    and carried on the investigation, and she says so. She **may teleport in
    once, with supplies, as a one-time favour**, and makes clear how costly
    teleporting is so that it is understood to be the last.
16. **The drow are "elite drow assassins."** The DM owns their stat blocks.
17. **Scrying is run as a telephoto lens** closing in on the subject, so it takes
    in far more than the ten feet the spell text gives. DM flavour, used with
    these players in a previous campaign, and what they expect. It is why the
    scry on Vrenn catches the dragon and fixes the place.
18. **Voss's visit is unlikely and depends on the party's reply.** Her *sending*
    asks for their whereabouts and a status update. Keep a basket ready in case:
    **healing potions**, probably nothing else. If she does come, she talks at
    length about her suspicions of the Consortium **and names it** — she turned
    the name up from the logo in her own investigation.
19. **The fork brings the drow to the edge of Neverwinter Wood; after that they
    track the party.** They are expert trackers. Once they have the trail they
    follow the *players*, not the fork, and **set the ambush where they choose.**
    They assume the party still has the fork until Morvek tells them otherwise —
    and even then Morvek may pay to have the party killed anyway, for helping
    Vrenn escape and for the damage done to his operation in the Abyss.
    *(Supersedes the earlier note that parting with the fork negates the plot.
    It now only does so if the fork never reaches the Wood's edge with them.)*
20. **The fox does not leave Ted's range.** It has no reason to. If it ever did,
    it would stay out, be afraid of his domain, and refuse to go back.
21. **Voss is competent, not powerful.** She is fairly weak, her duties are
    mostly administrative, and she rarely leaves campus or does field work — she
    relies on more capable people. In the DM's words she is "more competent, but
    not necessarily more capable in a skirmish." **She cannot cast her own
    teleportation; others do it for her.** Recorded on her live entity
    (2026-10-05). *Not settled: whether her* sending *is her own casting or

22. **If Voss visits, her escort brings her and takes her back.** Silas is not
    asked to send her home and never sees the ring's sigil sequence. **The
    escort is not Professor Crumb** — he is on her radar as a person of
    interest. *Who it is: see the proposal under "If Voss visits".*
23. **The party does not get the teleport ring's sigil sequence for a while.**
    It is closely held, the ring is logged, and Voss would almost certainly lose

### 2. Dawn — "a lie for a lie"

The party rests. When they wake, [[tedrovaxilliath]] tells them they are not
leaving.

**His grounds are on the recording.** On the first evening, *before the dome went
up*, the party discussed Morvek, the lich, the tuning fork and "he's our friend"
out loud in the clearing — and the DM ruled Ted was **"within earshot."** So he
may have known the "prisoner" was no prisoner before he made the deal, and let
them do his work first. *(Assistant's observation; use or ignore.)* The fox also
watched Vrenn fight beside them in the cave.

Two ways it ends:

- **Vrenn offers himself.** He tells the party, with confidence, that he will be
  fine, and stays voluntarily. Ted is probably persuadable to it. This is
  Vrenn's own Session 4 plan — pit the dragon against the lich — and his first
  free choice.
- **A fight with an adult green dragon**, which may be fatal. The DM can bring
  [[the_standing_treant]] in on the party's side if they need it. Vrenn fights
  for them without reserve.

Either way they leave the Wood or die trying.

### 3. The logging camp (Kettle Bend)

Fifteen miles south-west — most of a day through forest, arriving around supper.
Resupply, regional information, on toward Neverwinter. Framework below.

### 4. The road — the drow

Three **elite drow assassins** ambush the party for the fork. The party kills

## The logging camp — Kettle Bend

**DM brief (2026-10-05):** about thirty people, with tents, tools and carts. It
may have a name, but not one that is on any map or that the party would
recognise. No secrets — enough to answer questions and carry some short
dialogue. Everything below that brief is the assistant's *proposal*.

**What it is.** A company cutting camp on a shelf of cleared ground above a bend
in the river. The crew call it **Kettle Bend**. It is a workplace under canvas,
not a village: no inn, no shop, no temple, no families. The whole camp can be
struck and loaded in a day, because it has had to move before.

**Who is there — about thirty.**
- The camp boss.
- The cook and a chore boy.
- Two teamsters, who handle the oxen and carts.
- A saw-filer who doubles as smith.
- Roughly two dozen fallers and haulers. Mostly human, a few dwarves, two
  half-orcs.

**What the party sees walking in.**
- **Tents:** a dozen canvas wall tents in two rows, plus one spare.
- **The cook tent:** a big fly over plank tables, the centre of camp life.
- **The boss's tent,** with the tool wagon and strongbox beside it.
- **The ox line** and four carts: two heavy timber carts, a supply cart and a
  water cart.
- **A filing bench and saw pit.**
- **The landing:** log stacks along the bank, waiting for the spring flood.
- **The burn pile,** kept going day and night to clear brush. *That is the smoke
  Guntrah saw.*

**Geography.**
- **The line** — a creek east of camp with blazed trees along it. Nobody cuts or
  walks past it.
- **The old camp** — further east, abandoned after the second disaster.
- **The cart track** — west along the river toward the coast road, swinging wide
  of an old ruin on the way.
- **No boats.** Timber goes downriver on the spring flood; in late autumn the
  water is low and nothing is moving.

**Culture.**
- Up before dawn, asleep at dark.
- **No talking at meals.** You eat, you leave.
- No drink except on the rest day.
- **Hardly any coin in camp.** The crew are paid at season's end with a company
  note cashed in Neverwinter. *(Deliberate: it shows the party what a credit
  note is before they find one on a dead drow.)*
- They call the dragon **"the Green"**, say as little about him as they can, and
  have no idea he has a name.

**Named people.** Four is enough.
- **Agda Stroud**, camp boss. Fifties, blunt, fair. She decides whether the party
  stays.
- **Cook.** Nobody uses another name. Runs the cook tent, hears everything.
- **Colm**, head teamster. Drives the supply cart and knows the road west.
- **Pip**, chore boy. Fifteen, and the only one openly thrilled to see
  adventurers.

**Why they are suspicious.** The party walks in from the east — the one direction
nobody comes from — armed, with a steel construct. An orc and a half-orc raise no
eyebrows; the crew has both.

**What the party can get.**
- **Supper and the spare tent:** about 5 sp a head, paid up front, gone by first
  light.
- **Supplies from the tool wagon:** rations, rope, waterskins, blankets, lamp
  oil, a hatchet. Plain goods at a fair price.
- **No potions, no magic, no mounts for sale.** *(Healing potions arrive only
  in Voss's basket, if she visits — decision 18.)*
- **Directions,** freely, once Agda has decided they are not trouble.

**The two disasters** (*placeholders — replace freely*).
- A generation ago, a crew cut too far east. Neither men nor oxen came back.
- About fifteen years ago the company paid sellswords to kill him. None
  returned, and then he visited the old camp.

**What they can answer.**

| Asked about | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where are we? | Kettle Bend, the company's west cut, in Neverwinter Wood. |
| Nearest town? | Neverwinter, west along the track. *Two long days on foot at the map's assumed scale — unconfirmed.* |
| The Green? | "We stay our side of the creek and he stays his." |
| The ruin on the road? | "Nobody goes in. Track goes round." |
| The smoking mountain? | "Always smokes. Hasn't done more in my time." |
| News from the city? | Weeks old. The supply cart last came in three weeks ago. |
| Other strangers lately? | None. |
| The Academy, liches, drow business, giants in a cave, anything else | "Can't say I'm familiar." |

**Sample lines.**
- Agda: "You came from *that* way. On foot. And you're all still here."
- Agda: "Supper and the spare tent, paid up front, gone by first light."
- Agda, if they mention the dragon by name: "He has a *name*?"
- Cook: "Eat. Talk outside."
- Colm: "Keep the river on your right till the track leaves it. Don't follow the
  river past that."
- Pip: "Did you see him? Is he really as big as they say?"

### If Voss visits — there and back

What the rules and the repo already fix:

- **The Academy has a permanent teleportation circle** — the sanctioned faculty
  teleport ring in [[main_complex]]. Its existence is public knowledge to these
  characters, and Guntrah "has seen teleportation circles at the Academy"
  (Session 3). *Crumb bringing a party home to that ring in One Shot 5 is the
  players' memory from their retired characters, not these characters'.*
- ***Teleport* (7th level) is the only way out to a roadside, and it carries its
  caster.** Whoever sends Voss arrives with her. To a place known only from a
  description it goes wrong 43 times in 100 by the book, at 3d10 force damage a
  time — a real reason a frail administrator does this once.
- ***Teleportation circle* (5th level) is the way back.** It opens a door to the
  ring for a few seconds. Anybody may step through, and the caster may stay
  behind.
- **Silas cannot cast *teleport*** — his top slot is 6th — and from a scroll it
  would carry him off with her. **He can cast *teleportation circle* from a
  scroll with no roll**, because it is a wizard spell within his slots. But he
  must know the ring's sigil sequence to do it, and by the book he then keeps
  it.

**Decided (DM, 2026-10-05):**

- **Her escort brings her and takes her back** (decision 22). No Silas, no
  sequence.
- **The party does not get the ring's sigil sequence for a while** (decision
  23). *When she does eventually give it, the party is one 5th-level spell from
  home for good — Silas can take* teleportation circle *at level 12. That is a
  far cheaper road home than the two-hop* plane shift.
- **If a player asks her for the ring** — likely, once she is on the line — the
  reason in decision 23 is her answer, and she can give it plainly. Four
  graduates who vanished after a break-in she said "cannot have come from me"
  must not turn up in the Administration Building's own register.

**The escort (*proposal — awaiting the DM*).**

- **Recommended: [[prof_brasca_ironquill]], head of the Wizards Department.**
  - She can do both legs — *teleport* out, the ring home — and she is senior
    staff, which is who the ring is for. The log shows nothing odd about *her*.
  - She is Crumb's department head and privately finds his end of the tower
    "evasive". She is nobody's creature on that front.
  - She is Silas's head of department, so one of the four is hers. The
    professor who gave Silas his parting gift was Crumb.
  - She "asks follow-up questions the way other people cross-examine
    witnesses" — the right instrument for making the party say out loud what
    they found.
  - *Costs:* she is blunt and keeps her own private records, so Voss has had to
    tell her something true. Her entity also carries a slow-burn grievance over
    the Wizards Tower's seventh floor that leads back to the Voss family;
    putting the two of them in each other's debt warms that up.
  - *Assumes she can cast 7th-level spells. No level is recorded for her.*
- **Alternative: [[nyella_sarreth]], the Silverymoon operative.** A wizard,
  already inside the Ninth Thesis, a name the party holds, and she "has never
  missed a request from Voss in eleven years." But she is not staff. Her using
  the ring is exactly the traceable use Voss fears, so the return would need a
  second high-level casting or a scroll.

*Considered and not chosen:* Silas reading a scroll keyed to the ring, which
leaves a door to campus standing open in front of the party; and Voss simply
handing Silas the sequence.

### Keeping them out of the Breach (*proposal*)

The rule for it not reading as a railroad: **the door closes off-screen, for a
reason the party caused, and they hear about it as news before anyone suggests
going.** A wall that appears when they walk toward it is a railroad; a
consequence they are told about at dawn is the world moving.

- **Ted sealed it overnight.** The fox reported the noise coming up the throat.
  With the two fomorians dead there is nothing inside to dig its way back out,
  so he brought the entrance down from outside. He could never fit *in* the
  hole; collapsing it is a different job. He says so at dawn, in passing, as a
  thing already done. **This is the only measure that actually prevents it; the
  rest only remove reasons to want it.**
- **The contract is dead anyway.** "A lie for a lie" voids the deal, and Tito's
  renegotiation clause goes with it. There is no longer a claim on Ted to be
  earned down there.
- **There was nothing in it.** They searched the cavern on a 17 and found 80 gp.
  The campaign is milestone, and Tavian said so at the table.
- **It is the wrong way.** Six miles north, when everything they want is
  south-west and a dragon has just told them they are not leaving.
- **Optional tie:** the nearest way up from the drow country is that same hole.
  If the three assassins came up it before it was sealed, the thing that was
  "getting closer" comes to the party on the road, and the thread is paid off
  without anyone going back underground. *Loose on timing; use only if wanted.*

If they walk there regardless, they find a hillside clawed down over the
opening and days of digging. No fight is available.
