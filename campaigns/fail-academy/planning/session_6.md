- **Knows the plane, not the place.** The scry was prepped for Session 5 and not
  run. When it does land it finds a dismembered dragon and Vrenn standing among
  the people who killed it.
- **Vrenn's estimate was two to four days from arrival. The third day has
  begun.** Vrenn's whole plan for keeping him off was the dragon.
- **He never appears.** His reach, as prepped: **three elite drow assassins**,
  brought to the edge of the Wood by the fork in Silas's pack, tracking on foot
  from there. They want the fork — and with Vrenn present, Vrenn as well.
