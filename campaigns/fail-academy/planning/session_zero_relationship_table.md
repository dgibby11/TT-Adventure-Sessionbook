# Shared History Table — Academy Final Year (lore-anchored revision)

_Source: DM's Google Doc "academy-relationship-table.md". This version swaps generic
flavor for real, already-canon FAIL Academy people/places/traditions so the table reads
like it belongs to this specific school. PLAYER-FACING — nothing below references any
dm-only lore; everything cited (Midnight Run, Dueling Society, Goldvein's detentions,
FAIL Period, etc.) is already visibility:"player" in the live data._

_Class-specific blanks (e.g. "what subject does each of you carry the other in") are left
open on purpose — those are meant to be filled in live at the table once races/classes are
rolled, not pre-written here._

Roll a d10 for each pair of characters. Duplicates are rerolled, so every pair's bond is
unique. Where a moment has two distinct roles, roll a d2 (or flip a coin) to decide who's
who. After rolling, each pair answers the listed questions together — short answers are
fine. The goal is a hook you can play at the table, not a novel.

## 1. The All-Nighter

You crammed together through the worst nights of FAIL Period — "Failure Week," the
mandatory two-week practical-and-written gauntlet that closes out Term 2 — for the
Monster Identification practical, fueled by the Food Court's notoriously bad late-night
coffee and mounting panic. You both barely passed, and you've been study partners ever
since.

**The bond:** Comfortable, low-drama friendship. You trust each other's competence
because you've seen each other at your worst and dumbest.

**Answer together:**
- What subject does each of you carry the other in? *(fill in once you know your classes)*
- What's the snack or ritual that always shows up when you study together?

## 2. The Cover Story

One of you tried the Midnight Run — the standing, unofficial dare to cross the entire
campus perimeter after midnight without the faculty watch catching you. You very nearly
made it. Captain Hadra's night patrol stopped you a hundred yards short, and the other
lied straight to her face to cover for you. The lie worked — Hadra's sharp enough that she
probably clocked it and let it go anyway, for reasons of her own. You've never spoken
about what the Run was actually for.

**Roles (d2):** the Sneaker / the Alibi.

**The bond:** Loyalty proven under pressure — with a thread of mystery the DM can pull on
later.

**Answer together:**
- The Sneaker decides (privately, with the DM if they want): where were you really going?
- The Alibi: why did you lie without hesitating? What did it cost you?

*(Hadra remembers faces. Don't be surprised if this comes up again.)*

**Rolled:** Guntrah (Sneaker) & Tavian (Alibi). Still working out details; one certainty
already locked in — Guntrah was furious that Lug refused to cast Pass Without Trace on him
beforehand, and partly blames him for getting caught.

## 3. Training Yard Rivals

You were paired against each other at the Arcane Dueling Society's Tuesday open sessions
in the Main Hall East Annexe. Professor Varn runs it loosely enough that rivalries are
practically encouraged, and yours calcified fast — genuine dislike that hardened into
fierce mutual respect. Your names have sat near each other on the Ranking Board outside
the Annexe ever since, and you still keep score.

**The bond:** Competitive friendship. You push each other to be better and would never
admit how much you'd miss it.

**Answer together:**
- What's the current score on the Ranking Board, and who's ahead?
- What was the moment the dislike turned into respect?

**Rolled:** Silas & Tito — fits well, since they're both casters. Still working out
details; one certainty already locked in — Tito's gotten the better of Silas slightly
more often.

## 4. Champions of the Dungeon Games

Last year, you were paired as a team of two for the Dungeon Games — the Academy's
fourth-year-only festival, a gauntlet of challenges run through a sealed-off stretch of
the service tunnels and department training halls, re-routed every year so no one can
study the course in advance. Nobody expected much from your pairing. You won, or came so
gloriously close that it didn't matter. A year later, you're still riding the high.

**The bond:** Victory chemistry. You just *work* well together, and you both know it.
When things get dicey, you instinctively look for each other.

**Answer together:**
- Which challenge was your finest moment — and which one nearly ended you?
- What's the trophy or memento from the Games, and who keeps it? *(Every team's is
  different — the Year-End Duel has its battered enchanted gauntlet; yours can be
  whatever fits.)*
- What's the signature move or call-out you developed as a team?

## 5. Partners in Crime

You pulled off the best prank in Academy history together — and Professor Fenwick, forty
years tenured and famously unremovable, is still convinced it was a specific pair of
students three years above you. To this day, the actual faculty doesn't know it was you.
If one goes down, both go down.

**The bond:** Total mutual trust — the kind that only comes from shared, unpunished guilt.

**Answer together:**
- What was the prank? (Go big.)
- How close has Fenwick come to figuring it out — and what does he do, every time, when
  he passes you in the hall?

## 6. The Bad Semester

One of you nearly dropped out — family trouble, a spiral of failed classes, lost nerve,
or a stretch in the Healing Ward that shook you more than you told anyone. The other
talked you into staying.

**Roles (d2):** the One Who Almost Left / the One Who Stayed Up Talking.

**The bond:** Quiet and serious. You don't bring it up much, but it's the foundation
under everything.

**Answer together:**
- What was the crisis? (The player of the One Who Almost Left decides how much is public
  knowledge.)
- What did the other one say — or do — that worked?

**Rolled:** Tavian (the One Who Almost Left) & Silas (the One Who Stayed Up Talking).
Still working out details.

## 7. The Falling Out *(a rough one)*

You were inseparable in first year, then had a bitter fight over something that now seems
small. You've only recently started speaking again, and it's still awkward — not that
you've had much choice, given how often the Food Court's seating puts you three feet
apart at the long tables.

**The bond:** A friendship in repair. Old warmth underneath, sharp edges on top.

**Answer together:**
- What was the fight actually about — and what was it *really* about?
- Who reached out first, and how recently?

---

## 8. The Stolen Credit *(a rough one)*

One of you received public praise — an award, a commendation, a professor's unmistakable
nod of approval in front of the whole class — for an achievement that was mostly the
other's work. The wronged party never reported it, but it stings.

**Roles (d2):** the Credited / the Overlooked.

**The bond:** Unresolved tension under a functional partnership. Great fuel for roleplay;
keep it simmering, not boiling.

**Answer together:**
- What was the achievement?
- Why didn't the Overlooked say anything — and does the Credited even realize what
  happened?

*(If this happened in a practical class, Professor Varn's blunt public "full credit"
calls make an especially sharp version — she doesn't hand them out lightly, which is
exactly why it hurt.)*

## 9. Kitchen Duty Comrades

You both landed the same detention — Lenny Goldvein doesn't ask many questions before
signing off on kitchen duty — and spent a month scrubbing pots in the Food Court's
kitchen alcoves together. Nothing bonds people like shared misery and stolen pastries.

**The bond:** Easy, joking camaraderie. You've seen each other elbow-deep in gray
dishwater; there's no pretense left.

**Answer together:**
- What did each of you do to earn the detention? (Doesn't have to be the same offense.)
- What's the inside joke that survived the kitchen?

**Rolled:** Guntrah & Tito. Still working out details.

## 10. The Secret Keeper

One of you knows something about the other that no one else at the Academy does.

**Roles (d2):** the Keeper / the Known.

**The bond:** Trust with weight to it. The Known chose (or was forced by circumstance) to
let someone in.

**Answer together:**
- The Known's player decides the secret — with the DM, privately, if they prefer.
- How did the Keeper find out: were they told, or did they discover it?
- Has keeping it ever been hard?

*(If you want a place this happened: the Old Library's quieter corners near the
restricted archive, or a late night by the Founder's Statue, both fit the mood — but the
secret itself is yours to invent.)*
