An outside arcane trading concern with a standing interest in the rare materials found in the [[nether_mountains|Nether Mountains]]. Their only confirmed foothold is a modest brokerage office in Neverwinter, west of the Silver Marches — unremarkable enough that most people who deal with them never think to ask where instructions actually come from. Nobody outside the organization can say where it's actually run from, and it isn't obviously clear that everyone inside it could either. [[oswald_crumb|Professor Crumb]] is the Academy's official point of contact for their expedition-based component sourcing.

DM Only

Structure: Deliberately decentralized — a network of small, unremarkable front operations rather than one visible institution. Known fronts: the Neverwinter brokerage (their most substantial known presence); [[silverymoon|Silverymoon's]] "Gravelstone & Associates, Arcane Procurement" (Market District storefront); Everlund's Harwick Provisions (routes "Specimen Containment — Biological" orders); and Gravelstone Recovery, the shell supplier behind [[academy_provisions|The Prov's]] Special Acquisitions cabinet. No two fronts are run by people who seem to know much about the others. That compartmentalization is the point, not an accident.

The red flag: No one has ever been able to name who's actually in charge, or where. It's not that the Consortium hides its leadership behind a locked door — it's that the people who work its own storefronts and satellite offices genuinely don't seem to know, and don't act troubled by not knowing. For an organization old and established enough to run multi-city supply lines, that's not normal operational security — that's a tell, and it should read as one the first time a party member with any business sense stops to think about it.

Leadership: At its core, a cult of Orcus worshippers — there is no singular founder-figure to eventually meet; whoever originally established the Consortium is long since dead. Current inner-circle leadership is not yet established (needed before Phase 3 — see campaign_arc.json / phase2_the_one_who_got_away.json open_questions). The decentralization above is how a cult keeps a "legitimate trading concern" face on for centuries without any one front being able to expose the whole.

Crumb: Fifty years on the Academy's faculty, and the Consortium's supply contact for roughly forty of them — nearly the entire span of his unnaturally long tenure. He is a point of contact between the Academy and outside arcane powers, not a member; he views hired party members as "bodyguards," not colleagues — not malicious, just genuinely oblivious to the stakes he imposes on others.

The Consortium's Nether Mountains research site has been attacked twice in the past six months by orc warbands; Crumb reported both incidents to the Consortium, not to Academy administration.

What the previous party (One Shots 1-5) learned: During [[one_shot_5|One Shot 5]], that party broke [[oswald_crumb|Professor Crumb]] down mid-expedition until he admitted the truth: his "academic component-gathering" trips are a supply run for the Consortium, and they'd just been hired as unwitting bodyguards for one. They also learned the components aren't academic curiosities — they're bound for dangerous spellwork, though not which spells or for whom. See oswald_crumb.html for the full account, including the deal they cut with him for their silence.

For the current (Session Zero) party: None of the above is theirs to know yet. They've never heard of the Thornwick Arcane Consortium in any form — not even as campus rumor. Any investigation of them in this campaign should be earned entirely fresh, not assumed from the old party's history, and nothing on this page should leak into Player View before that happens.

Session 1 baseline — what's discoverable now: Nothing, publicly. No campus rumor, no paper trail a casual look would turn up. [[oswald_crumb|Crumb]] is the only thread, and Session 1 has no scripted moment that would crack him open the way the old party's mid-expedition confrontation did — this is a parting-gifts/recruitment session, not an interrogation. If a player independently presses him hard enough in improvised play, he can be walked into admitting "an outside arcane business arrangement" and, further, into naming [[garrick_nash|Garrick Nash]] as his contact — but nothing about dangerous spellwork, decentralization, or Thornwick's leadership, since Crumb doesn't know that much himself. Garrick, if the party ever tracks him down and approaches him in good faith rather than by force, would confirm the Consortium's name and point toward the Neverwinter office — a genuine next step, not a dead end. Don't let any of this move faster than the table earns it.

What Voss's Ninth Thesis contacts turn up (off-screen, post-Session 1): Real and active in Silverymoon and Everlund trade records, no ownership or founding date on file anywhere, nobody her contacts spoke to could name who actually runs it. That's it — her operatives have no reason to look toward the Academy, so none of this points at Crumb or Neverwinter. What ties Crumb in is Voss's own memory of the mark, not their research — see session_1.html's off-screen notes for the full beat. This keeps her two possible leads meaningfully different in shape: her operatives are broader (multiple cities, confirms the org is real and the "nobody knows who runs it" red flag) but shallower (no names, no Neverwinter, no campus connection); Crumb/Garrick is narrower but deeper (an actual name and a concrete next step, if he can be gotten to talk). Neither alone should feel like the whole picture.

The Prov's "Special Acquisitions" supplier — a shell operation called Gravelstone Recovery, see [[academy_provisions|the Provisions Office]] — is a Thornwick front. [[osric_morne|Osric]] doesn't know this. Neither party has reason yet to connect the two.
