[[oswald_crumb|Professor Oswald Crumb]]

An outside arcane organization, three weeks' travel east, with a standing interest in the rare materials found in the [[nether_mountains|Nether Mountains]]. During [[session_5|Session 5]] the party broke [[oswald_crumb|Professor Crumb]] down mid-expedition until he admitted the truth: his "academic component-gathering" trips are a supply run on the Consortium's behalf, and the party had just been hired as unwitting bodyguards for one. Worse, the components themselves aren't academic curiosities — the party now knows they're bound for dangerous spellwork, though not which spells or for whom.

DM Only

The Consortium establishes that outside powers are reaching into the academy's operations. Their full aims, leverage over Crumb, and relationship to the school's administration are still undefined — the party knows Crumb sells to them and that the goods are dangerous, not why the Consortium wants an Academy professor as a supplier specifically, or what else Crumb has traded away to keep the arrangement. What the "dangerous spellwork" actually is remains undecided — don't commit to specifics until it matters at the table.

The Prov's "Special Acquisitions" supplier — a shell operation called Gravelstone Recovery, see [[academy_provisions|the Provisions Office]] — is a Thornwick front. [[silas_morne|Silas]] doesn't know this. The party has no reason yet to connect the two.
