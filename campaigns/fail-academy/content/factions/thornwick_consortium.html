An outside arcane organization, three weeks' travel east, with a standing interest in the rare materials found in the [[nether_mountains|Nether Mountains]]. Publicly, a legitimate research consortium that periodically partners with academic institutions for expedition-based component sourcing — [[oswald_crumb|Professor Crumb]]'s official campus contact for this kind of work. There are older rumors, never confirmed publicly, that a past student expedition to the Nether Mountains turned up more than the Consortium's brochures let on.

DM Only

The Consortium establishes that outside powers are reaching into the academy's operations. Their full aims, leverage over Crumb, and relationship to the school's administration are still undefined. What the "dangerous spellwork" actually is remains undecided — don't commit to specifics until it matters at the table.

What the previous party (One Shots 1-5) learned: During [[one_shot_5|One Shot 5]], that party broke [[oswald_crumb|Professor Crumb]] down mid-expedition until he admitted the truth: his "academic component-gathering" trips are a supply run for the Consortium, and they'd just been hired as unwitting bodyguards for one. They also learned the components aren't academic curiosities — they're bound for dangerous spellwork, though not which spells or for whom. See oswald_crumb.html for the full account, including the deal they cut with him for their silence.
