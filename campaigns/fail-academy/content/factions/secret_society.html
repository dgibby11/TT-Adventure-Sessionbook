The Rumor

Every few years, a new crop of students rediscovers "the Secret Society" — a clandestine student organization, so the story goes, that meets in secret, guards forbidden knowledge, and quietly indoctrinates a chosen few. It has never once been confirmed by anyone with an actual answer, and it never quite dies, because two genuinely separate things keep feeding it.

The first is [[brennan_ashcroft|Brennan Ashcroft]]'s Alumni Society — real, mundane, and entirely legitimate: alumni networking and institutional support, nothing hidden about it except that its founding-era records went missing from the [[old_library|restricted archive]] (see [[brennan_ashcroft|Ashcroft's entry]]), which is exactly the kind of detail campus rumor can't resist. The second is [[ninth_thesis|The Ninth Thesis]] — which actually is secret, actually does operate in the shadows, and occasionally leaves a trace (a clandestine gathering, an odd absence, a rumor from someone who saw something they can't explain) that gets folded into the same "Secret Society" story instead of recognized as its own thing.

Students conflate the two because the rumor mill doesn't distinguish sources — a real oddity about the Ninth Thesis and a total non-mystery about the Alumni Society end up told as the same story, with the same certainty.

One Shot 2 — Reconsidered

The prior party's clandestine gathering at the [[founders_statue|Founder's Statue]] during [[one_shot_2]] was filed, at the time, under "the Secret Society" — the only label campus rumor offered them. It's now understood (see [[ninth_thesis|The Ninth Thesis]]) that what they actually witnessed was genuine Ninth Thesis activity. The ritualistic behavior, the magical manifestations, the hidden doctrine — all real, none of it "the Secret Society." There was never a third organization behind the statue; there was only ever the one they didn't have a name for yet.

DM Only

Playing this at the table: if the party goes looking for "the Secret Society" directly, they'll find exactly what's here — a real, boring Alumni Society with nothing to hide, and a rumor mill that can't tell its own threads apart. There's no hidden headquarters, no indoctrination, no third thing waiting to be discovered. If they pull hard enough on the right thread, they end up at the Ninth Thesis instead — which is the actual payoff, not a red herring.
