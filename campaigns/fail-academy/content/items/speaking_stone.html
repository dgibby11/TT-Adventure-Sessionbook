At Commencement, [[session_2|Session 2]]

Not a stone at all, to look at: a rounded ball of bronze, engraved all over in swirls, with a piece that slides out to speak into and back again when he is done. It is one of a pair. Guntrah's father made or acquired it, meant to send it a year ago, lost it somewhere in his own workshop, and found it again just in time before they left for the ceremony.

Why he gave it

"I wanted to make sure that you always have a way to reach us."

Guntrah knew what it was on sight. His parents intend to use it at least once a day — that was their promise, not his, and they meant it.

[[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] asked whether there was a second one going spare, thinking the party could use it to coordinate during the Provisions break-in. There wasn't.

How It Works

Twenty-five words each way, once. After that neither stone can be used again until the next dawn. It lights up while it is speaking and looks like nothing in particular afterwards.

It was used for the first time in [[session_5|Session 5]], in a clearing in [[neverwinter_wood|Neverwinter Wood]]. His father had tried it the day before and got nothing. "Your mother's fretting. Are you eating? Do you need anything, son?" Guntrah told him he was eating well and out at his new job with the boys. It was the first the rest of the party had seen of it.

DM Only — SETTLED AT THE TABLE (Session 5, 2026-10-06)

It is a pair of sending stones, run as written: the sending spell through an object, once a day. The players remembered a version that goes back and forth; that was a custom rule from the previous campaign, and you ruled it out on the spot.

The look was invented at the table, at your invitation, by Guntrah's player — an artificer's son carries something mechanical, not a rock. An earlier line in the same exchange has it "go back to a rock"; the bronze ball supersedes it.

The planar question below was never answered out loud. His father "tried the stone yesterday, got nothing" — and yesterday the party were in this forest, inside a Tiny Hut, which blocks sending. So the silence his parents got has a cause the party do not know, and whether the stone reaches across planes is still open.

What his parents now believe: that he is at a new job, eating well. They will try again tomorrow.

DM Only — as written before Session 5

Mechanics undecided. Most likely a pair of sending stones, but nothing has been pinned at the table — Guntrah simply recognised it and named it a "speaking stone." Settle it before Session 3, because it matters immediately: see below.

The question Session 2 forced. The party is on another plane. Two ordinary gnomes are going to try this stone within a day, and every day after that. Decide deliberately whether it reaches across planes:

  • It doesn't — the quiet, cruel option. Guntrah's parents get silence and draw their own conclusions, and Guntrah doesn't even know it's happening. Pays off later, in person.
  • It does — the party's only line home, and a way to get word to [[ellery_voss|Voss]] via two gnomes who have no idea what they're relaying. Powerful, and it defuses some of the isolation the ending is built on.
  • It half-does — it connects, badly, in fragments. Which is thematically perfect and also a lot of work to run consistently.

Standard sending stones are silent on planar range in a way that lands the ruling squarely on you. Whatever you pick, pick it before the first time a player reaches for the stone at the table.
