A crimson half-cape of theater-curtain velvet with gold cord trim and bullion tassels, clasped by a brass brooch shaped as a pair of applauding hands. "Tito" is embroidered in gold along one inner hem; the initials "V.G." are stitched, slightly unevenly, into the other.

  • Grace Under Pressure. While worn, advantage on death saving throws.
  • The Show Must Go On. Once per day, when reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, drop to 1 HP instead as the cape flares and distant applause sounds. Recharges at dawn.

The Giver

[[viola_gossamer|Viola Gossamer]] — a fourth-year bard, the other fey in the bardic college, and the only person besides Tito who'd know what this cape is really about. The velvet is Gossamer family stock, cut from a bolt meant for an archfey's opera house; the enchantment is her own secret capstone project.

In her second year, Viola froze mid-recital — until Tito stood and applauded as if the silence were an intentional dramatic pause, turning the hall's snickering into an ovation. She finished the piece. He barely remembers it; she never forgot. The cape is that moment returned with interest: he turned her failure into applause, so she wove him a cape that does the same. Her devotion is crush-adjacent and entirely unspoken — the "V.G." initials are either a maker's mark or a signature on a love letter, and she isn't telling.

DM Only

Viola graduates next year — a natural recurring NPC, whether or not Tito ever addresses the crush directly. The Gossamer family "would love to meet the young man our Viola speaks so highly of" — playable either as a warm dinner invitation or a fey-court obligation with real teeth, DM's choice, whenever it's useful to pull on. There's also a small planted detail worth remembering: Viola tells Tito not to look too closely at a flaw in the hem stitching on the initials side — harmless as written, but a loose thread if a hook is ever needed.
