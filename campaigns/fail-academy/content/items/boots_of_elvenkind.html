Soft leather boots, elven-made, worn thin at the toe from a previous owner who is not present to ask about it. While worn, the wearer's steps make no sound, regardless of terrain, and moving normally does not betray the wearer's location by sound alone. The wearer also has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks that rely on moving silently.

Caelum bought them the same afternoon the party stocked up before the Nether Mountains expedition — one of four purchases made in a hurry, alongside [[lugeiros_serise|Lug]]'s longbow, [[gunnar|Gunnar]]'s armor, and [[bloodraven|BloodRaven]]'s helm.

DM Only

No known curse or catch — unlike the [[shield_of_missile_attraction|Shield of Missile Attraction]] still sitting unclaimed on the mountain, this one is exactly what it looks like.
