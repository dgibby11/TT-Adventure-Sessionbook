What
[[guntrah|Guntrah]]'s Steel Defender — a small construct
Build
Roughly a ball, with a couple of stabby arms and legs
Size
Medium — bigger than he looks, and bigger than the token used for him
Time to build
Three and a half years

Guntrah built him. Not bought, not infused from someone else's schematic — built, over three and a half years of "cleaning duty" in the boiler rooms, out of fifth-year textbooks he shouldn't have had and components discarded by wealthier students.

The design is very nearly his father's. [[silas_and_breena|Silas]] showed it to him years ago when they were practicing together and never got it working himself; Guntrah recognised his own father's hand in what he'd made long before he finished it. At Commencement Silas took one look and said so out loud: "it's actually part of my design — he just finished it, exactly like we planned."

[[prof_tavel|Professor Tavel]] helped, in the boiler room, off the books, the way he has helped with everything. His verdict, to Guntrah's father: "look at Ollie here, he's brilliant."

The Name

Arrived at the table by committee and stuck immediately. "Bally," on the grounds that he is ball-shaped, was floated and rejected. "Ollie Doo" is what he gets called when someone is being asked to find a clue.

He walked at Guntrah's heel across the Commencement stage, which was not an accident — it was the point. He can ride on Guntrah's back in a docile state while Guntrah flies. He is a construct, and therefore, as established at the table, incorruptible.

Established in [[session_3|Session 3]]

  • He is now the backpack. Guntrah owns no pack of any kind — a fact nobody noticed until the party went looking for waterskins on a plane where dehydration costs a level of exhaustion an hour. Standing plan from that moment: an adventuring pack, built into Ollie, with a water spout, and Guntrah pulling gear out of him as needed.
  • He takes standing orders. Left at the top of the basement stairs with a conditional instruction — if something happens up here, come in, close the door, and run down — which the DM ruled a readied reaction. He has no telepathy; the orders have to be given out loud, in advance.
  • He fights. Brought down the stairs mid-battle on a shout, and landed rend attacks for 9 apiece on both [[basement_oni|oni]]. He acts on Guntrah's initiative.
  • He does not carry water. Yet. This is the entire reason for the spout.

Established in [[session_4|Session 4]] — his best session

  • He has a flamethrower, and it is how the party makes fire now. [[tedrovaxilliath|Ted]] threw a whole tree into the clearing as firewood; [[guntrah|Guntrah]] chopped it with his greataxe while Ollie hauled the pieces, and then "fire comes out of him and starts it." He is, in Guntrah's words, "a little lighter."
  • He rides on Guntrah's back and gets latched in. Pulled down by a latch to deploy, put back and secured after the fight. The backpack plan is now how he is actually carried.
  • Natural 20 Rend for 15 against a webbed [[fomorians|fomorian]], after closing forty feet.
  • Deflect Attack, used properly and for somebody else. A reaction imposing disadvantage on an attack against a creature within 5 ft of him — spent on a greatclub swing aimed at [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]], which then missed on a natural 1. The fomorian had walked straight over the top of Ollie without appearing to notice he was there.
  • Turn order confirmed: he takes his turn immediately after Guntrah, not simultaneously — checked at the table against [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]]'s spectral steed, which may act at the same time as its rider.
  • Still no water spout. Two sessions running, and the party ran out of water again.

Established in [[session_5|Session 5]]

  • He has a launcher now. [[guntrah|Guntrah]] powered him down by the fire, opened him up and fitted a spring catapult loaded with three tinkered stones: two that shed light, one that stinks. What each stone does is fixed when it is made, not when it is fired.
  • "Protect them." When Guntrah went up as bait he left Ollie on the ground with the other three, and Ollie stayed with them.
  • He fought [[tedrovaxilliath|a dragon]] at arm's length. Caught by the thorn vines, tore himself free, and charged. A rend for 5, the second breath shrugged off for half, both wing-beats ridden out, and one tail strike taken square. He finished the fight standing, on about a dozen hit points of 62.
  • Ruled at the table: he makes his own saving throws and gets none of Guntrah's bonuses, he has no bonus-action Dash, and he acts on Guntrah's initiative.

DM Only

Subclass question — CLOSED (DM, 2026-09-03). Ollie was always the clearest evidence that the imported Session 0 sheet was out of date, because a Steel Defender is a subclass mismatch rather than an equipment one. The answer is simply that the player changed subclass early in the campaign: Guntrah is Artificer 10, Battle Smith, and the 2026-09-02 export confirms it. Ollie, Arcane Jolt and Battle Ready are all his by the book now, and [[guntrah|his entity]] has been rebuilt from that sheet. The Armorer/Guardian labelling and the Thunder Gauntlets are gone.

And it retroactively clears every other "sheet drift" flagged after Session 2, which all turn out to be legitimate Battle Smith or orc features rather than errors:

  • the magical heavy crossbow at +10 with no loading property — Repeating Shot infusion, and Battle Ready putting INT 20 on the attack;
  • multiattack — Battle Smith's Extra Attack at 5th;
  • flight — the Winged Boots infusion;
  • dash as a bonus action — the orc Adrenaline Rush;
  • Branding Smite, a paladin spell — an always-prepared Battle Smith spell at 2nd level.

The one thing that was genuinely wrong — seven active infusions against a cap of four — was caught and corrected at the table in [[session_3|Session 3]]. Nothing is outstanding.

Size: a Steel Defender is Medium, AC 15, speed 40 ft. The token used at the table is much smaller and the DM read him as Tiny for a while; not worth chasing unless it matters tactically, but he takes up a square and he can be squeezed past as difficult terrain, both of which came up in the basement.

Narrative value: Ollie is the physical proof of the whole Tavel arrangement and of Guntrah's father's teaching in one object. If the Holdar thread ever comes to a head, Ollie is the exhibit.
