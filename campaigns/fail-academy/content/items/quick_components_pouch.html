A component pouch of oiled goblin-hide, every interior pocket labeled in Crumb's immaculate copperplate, components sorted alphabetically and by part of speech.

  • Functions as a component pouch and spellcasting focus.
  • A Properly Punctuated Aside. Once per day, when Zarad casts a spell of level 3 or lower with a casting time of an action, he may cast it as a bonus action instead. The clasp clicks shut on its own when the property is expended, reopening at dawn. (Table ruling: pairs with a cantrip, not a second leveled spell — standard one-leveled-spell-per-turn rule.)
  • Nested in the center pocket, in a compartment labeled simply "(pearl)," sits a Pearl of Power (official item, requires attunement, tracked separately) — Crumb insists it is a separate gift and should be footnoted accordingly.

Attached note, in flawless script: "A wizard who runs out of components mid-incantation has committed the arcane equivalent of a sentence fragment. Mind your accuracy. — O.C."

Why Crumb, Why Zarad

Crumb has watched Zarad for years the way he reads student essays — hunting for errors and finding, to his mounting irritation, very few. Zarad's spellbook is the only student grimoire Crumb has ever graded that he didn't cover in red ink. Crumb would never say he's proud; he calls Zarad "the least embarrassing evoker this institution has produced in a decade," which everyone who knows Crumb understands to be a eulogy-grade compliment. He built the pouch's labeling system himself over a winter break and sourced it through his component business's finest supplier, because nothing less would satisfy him.

DM Only

The pouch is Thornwick-sourced via Crumb's own component supply chain (see [[oswald_crumb|Crumb's]] and [[thornwick_consortium|Thornwick's]] existing dm-only notes). Beneath the center pocket's lining, partially obscured by the "(pearl)" label, is a maker's stamp — the same Thornwick Arcane Consortium mark as the tainted component pouch from the three campus incidents Voss's notes describe (see session1_something_is_happening.json / phase1). It doesn't implicate the pouch itself and doesn't implicate Crumb — it's a second, independent thread pointing at the same supply chain, good material for Phase 2's item-forensics track once the party has reason to compare the two.

  • Discovery: Investigation DC 15 on a thorough examination, or automatic if directly compared against the tainted pouch. Detect magic shows only the pouch's own benign enchantment.
  • Crumb is uncompromised. If confronted, he's bewildered, then quietly alarmed — and can name his supplier contact, which becomes a lead. This is exactly the kind of physical-evidence thread Guntrah (Investigation +9, Tool Expertise) is built to pull on — see party_overview.html.
