What
A credit note: one sheet of heavy paper, folded in three, in an oilskin wallet
Says
Pay the bearer, and a sum written out in a clerk's hand. No name anywhere on it
Marked
A maker's stamp in the corner: three letters worked into one — T, A, C

The stamp is a T with an A beneath it and a C hooked through, set in a diamond with a small spike at each corner. It is pressed into the paper, not drawn. Whoever issued the note expected the mark to be enough.

DM Only — UNPLAYED. Built for [[session_5|Session 5]] on 2026-10-06.

Where it turns up (DM, 2026-10-05): on the body of one of the three elite drow assassins who ambush the party on [[thundertree_road|the road]]. One note, on one body; the other two are clean.

What the mark is: the maker's stamp of the [[thornwick_consortium|Thornwick Arcane Consortium]]. [[morvek|Morvek]] makes the goods; the Consortium is the surface end that moves them, and his hired blades were paid through one of its fronts. The note is drawn on its brokerage in Neverwinter.

THE NAME IS NOT SAID. The party has never heard it. The mark is three letters and a question they carry west. The one exception is [[ellery_voss|Voss]] in person: she turned the name up from this mark in her own inquiries, and if she ever stands in front of them she says it.

  • The sum is the DM's to set. Proposal: 500 gp.
  • Where it can be cashed is not written on it. How [[tito|Tito]] finds out is a later conversation. [[anselm_ferreck|Ferreck]] is the man who can pull the brokerage's filings.
  • If nobody searches the bodies, or a drow escapes with it, let the same stamp surface another way: inside a scabbard, or on the wax of a vial.
  • The camp set it up. The [[logging_crew|loggers]] are paid by company note cashed in Neverwinter, so the party has been told what a thing like this is.
  • Whether the party has seen this mark before is not on record. Voss has: she holds both the name and the stamp from her own inquiries after [[session_1|Session 1]]. Ask before assuming either way about the party.

The image is a copy of the Consortium's emblem under a plain file name, so that nothing a player can see names it.
