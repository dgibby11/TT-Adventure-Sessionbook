Type
Wondrous item (fixed installation), extremely rare — attunes only to Gossamer blood, or whoever the family designates
Location
[[viola_gossamer|Viola Gossamer]]'s closet, Bards Department housing
Destination
A fairy ring on the Gossamer family's Feywild estate

A heavy drop of crimson velvet — the same theater-curtain stock the family is known for — hung on a brass rod bolted into the back wall of Viola's closet, wide enough to fill the frame behind her hanging clothes. Gold thread traces a border of interlocking runes along its edge, dull to the eye until drawn open by someone it recognizes.

How It Works

  • Drawn open by Viola — currently the only person outside the family the curtain answers to — it doesn't part onto the closet wall. It parts onto a ring of pale mushrooms in a clearing on Gossamer land, half a world and a plane away.
  • Usable once per moon cycle, and carries exactly one traveler per use. No entourage, no smuggling someone else through, no second trip to fetch something forgotten.
  • To anyone it isn't attuned to, it's simply a very expensive curtain.

How It Got There

Viola arrived at the Academy with it already installed. The Gossamers negotiated the arrangement with [[headmistress_dowe|Headmistress Dowe]] before she enrolled — an exception unheard of for student housing. [[maestro_lira|Maestro Lira]] signed off too, as department head, since it lives inside her building. What the family offered or promised in exchange has never been part of the official record.

DM Only

The once-a-month, one-passenger limit is a hard mechanical ceiling, not a house rule Viola imposes on herself — pushing it should have real consequences if it's ever useful as a pressure valve. It also doubles as a standing answer to "how does the party reach the Feywild," if that's ever needed, though it comes with strings: it's Viola's, it's rare enough to draw serious attention if word of it got out beyond the family, and it only ever goes to that one clearing. Whether Viola would offer it up herself is a character call worth making in the moment, not deciding now.
