A long-bladed sword of storm-gray steel, its fuller inlaid with a row of five rune slots. The first — the Rune of the Oath — glows with pale silver light, and since the morning a dragon died in [[neverwinter_wood|Neverwinter Wood]], so does a second. The other three sit dark and empty, waiting. When Orvyn carried this blade himself, every rune blazed; the moment he released his attunement to gift it, the light of his legend faded from the steel. Witness remembers every deed it has ever seen, but reflects only the glory of the hand that holds it now.

Core Properties

Attack and damage bonus determined by how many Legend Runes are currently lit (see below). Begins at +1 for a newly attuned wielder.

Insight of Ages (1/day). When making an Intelligence check or Intelligence-based skill check, the wielder may use Witness's Intelligence score (17) in place of their own — a few quiet words pressed directly into the mind, never more than needed.

The Blade Remembers. Witness retains perfect memory of everything it perceived while a wielder was attuned to it — events, faces, words spoken in its presence — though it shares these recollections only when it chooses to.

The Runes of Legend

The Rune of the Oath ignites on attunement and grants the base +1. The four Legend Runes ignite only in answer to deeds that meet all three of Witness's criteria: done, not said (genuine heroism or sacrifice, not a boast or a plan), witnessed (seen by someone other than the wielder), and retold (the story spreads — sung, gossiped, carved into a plaque). The DM decides when a deed qualifies; a new rune ignites in silver fire at the next dawn.

Fading Glory. If Tavian's attunement ends for any reason, every Legend Rune goes dark and the sword reverts to +1 for its next wielder. Witness does not forget the deeds — it simply refuses to lend one hero's glory to another.

Insight of Ages fired for the first time in [[session_3|Session 3]], in a lich's basement, over a glowing planar circle worked in a language Tavian cannot read. He drew the blade and asked it out loud: "If ever there was a time to help me, please help me." It lent him its mind, he rolled a 12 with it, and it was not enough. Established at the table as the register in which Witness reliably speaks — asked directly, for help, in a moment that matters.

The second rune lit in [[session_5|Session 5]]. Tavian charged [[tedrovaxilliath|a green dragon]] with it, was put on the ground by its breath, and was not on his feet when it died. When the others pulled him up he drove the blade into its skull — and one of the dark runes was lit.

Sentience

Witness is a sentient neutral good weapon (INT 17, WIS 15, CHA 14; hearing and darkvision to 120 ft). It communicates telepathically with its attuned wielder in Common or Celestial — but almost never speaks. It makes itself known through presence instead: a sudden weight in the hand as a warning, a warmth in the grip as approval, a faint hum near a worthy challenge, a chill when its bearer strays. To anyone but the wielder, it is ordinary steel.

The spirit inside is Aelrindel Dawnbringer, an elf paladin who fought at Orvyn's side for years — closer than a comrade, near enough to a brother. The two of them chased glory together as the fists of Tempus. When Aelrindel took a mortal wound in battle, faster than Orvyn could reach him, he struck his own bargain with Tempus rather than let his story end unfinished: his soul bound into his own blade, to live on in steel since his body could not go on. Famously sparing with words in life, teaching by demonstration and judging by observation; in death, he has only grown quieter. Orvyn buried his friend, carried the sword afterward for years, and earned every rune himself — alongside a private conviction, held for a long time before he made peace with it, that he should have been fast enough or strong enough to save him instead. Now Aelrindel hangs silent in a new hand, watching to see what Tavian's guileless, deed-first instinct will show him.

DM Only

Conflict. Witness has an Ego of 16 (sentient weapon conflict rules, DMG). If Tavian acts against the spirit of the Oath of Glory — fleeing a worthy challenge out of cowardice, claiming credit for another's deed, giving up while the deed is still possible — the blade grows heavy and cold, an unmistakable verdict without a word. If he persists, a conflict of wills occurs: he must succeed on a DC 14 Charisma saving throw or find the blade inert and leaden (treat as a nonmagical longsword) until he makes amends through action. It does not argue. It simply withdraws.

Roleplay hooks. Budget its true sentences like legendary loot — a handful per campaign arc; the first time it speaks unprompted should mark a turning point. The weight/warmth/hum/chill language is a clean DM signal channel for feeding Tavian instinct-flavored information without exposition (hums near a disguised fiend, goes cold at a false quest-giver's flattery) — it plays as "Tavian's gut," and only the DM and the blade know the difference. Orvyn knows exactly who's bound in the sword — he carried Aelrindel himself for years — and whether/when he tells Tavian is a rich, unforced choice; "Has it spoken yet?" is a scene that costs three words, any time after graduation. Worth noting: Orvyn's monthly Memorial Garden visits to update his "outstanding obligations" (see commander_orvyn.html) plausibly include Aelrindel, even though his spirit rests in the blade rather than the ground.

Runes 4–5. Since the bonus caps at +3, treat these as pure story milestones marking Tavian's ascension from hero to legend. If mechanical teeth are wanted later: rune 4 could let the blade cast Heroism on Tavian 1/day; rune 5 could have it speak his legend aloud once, in the old commander's true voice, and thereafter count as +3 for any future wielder it deems worthy. Entirely optional.

Setup note. D&D Beyond doesn't support evolving items natively — easiest to model as separate homebrew versions ("Witness (2 Runes, +1)," "(3 Runes, +2)," etc.), swapped in as runes ignite, with the sentience/lore text pasted into each. Model Insight of Ages as a Limited Use (1/day) property or a custom action on Tavian's sheet.
