Perrin Oakhollow keeps the Bards Department's costumes mended, its stage properties catalogued, and its backstage storerooms at [[bards_stage|the Performance Stage]] tidy — quiet, competent, entirely unremarkable work that puts them within sight of Viola's rehearsals, performances, and daily comings and goings without anyone ever wondering why. Elvish longevity does the rest: no one on staff remembers Perrin not being there, and no one's looked closely enough to ask.

DM Only

Perrin answers to the Gossamer family, not the Academy, whatever the employment paperwork says. Placing an elf was deliberate on the family's part — unremarkable staff, where another fairy on the Bards Department payroll would have prompted questions Viola herself might have asked. This almost certainly never comes up at the table; it exists so that if something threatens Viola directly, there's already an answer for who notices and what they're capable of. Stat and characterize Perrin on the spot if that day ever arrives — nothing about the quiet cover should be read as a ceiling on competence.
