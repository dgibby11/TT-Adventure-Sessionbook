A diminutive goblin with ink-stained fingers, perpetually disheveled robes, and wire-rimmed spectacles. Played at the table as well into his eighties — an almost unheard-of lifespan for a goblin, and a detail the [[session_5|Nether Mountains party]] noticed but never got an explanation for. Speaks with pedantic precision and corrects grammar constantly — even mid-crisis. Obsessed with proper cataloging and documentation. Has led at least one student expedition to the [[nether_mountains|Nether Mountains]] under the guise of academic component gathering.

Access to his tower is controlled — a letter of introduction from a senior faculty member is required for non-students to enter. Catchphrases: "Precisely!" and "Now, where was I?" In combat he's easy to underestimate — during [[session_5|the Nether Mountains ambush]] a single, well-placed Chromatic Orb turned the fight.

DM Only

The true purpose of the Nether Mountains expedition was a supply run for the [[thornwick_consortium|Thornwick Arcane Consortium]], a prestigious arcane university three weeks' travel east. Crumb is a point of contact between the Academy and outside arcane powers. He views hired party members as "bodyguards," not colleagues — not malicious, just genuinely oblivious to the stakes he imposes on others. As of [[session_5]], the party broke him down mid-expedition and he confirmed all of this to their faces — this is no longer a hidden thread as far as they're concerned.

Despite his shabby appearance, Crumb is very wealthy — gold concealed in various pouches and a lockbox he never lets leave his sight. He is a terrible liar and will eventually admit everything if pressed. His scattered demeanor masks a shrewd businessman who knows exactly what his components are worth.

The Consortium's Nether Mountains research site has been attacked twice in the past six months by orc warbands; Crumb reported both incidents to the Consortium, not to Academy administration.

The vanished professor: When [[bloodraven|BloodRaven]] recalled a professor who disappeared on a similar expedition roughly fifty years ago, Crumb claimed the man sacrificed himself so Crumb and his students could escape. The party didn't believe him. He was telling the truth, full stop — no hedging on this one. Crumb has never told anyone that story willingly before or since — worth returning to if you want to humanize him later.

The party's cut: Crumb offered — and the party accepted — a standing 15% cut of his Thornwick profits, split evenly across all four of them, in exchange for their silence. [[lugeiros_serise|Lug]] was the one who fought it hardest before folding under group pressure; the other three had no such hesitation.

What they know it's for: Between the field notes and Crumb's cracked-open confession, the party now understands the components aren't just rare — they're used in dangerous spellwork. Crumb hasn't said what, specifically, and the party hasn't pushed. It's the first real evidence that the Consortium's interest in the Academy might not be benign.
