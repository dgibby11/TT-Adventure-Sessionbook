A diminutive goblin with ink-stained fingers, perpetually disheveled robes, and wire-rimmed spectacles. Played at the table as well into his eighties — an almost unheard-of lifespan for a goblin, and something he's never offered an explanation for. Speaks with pedantic precision and corrects grammar constantly — even mid-crisis. Obsessed with proper cataloging and documentation. Has led at least one student expedition to the [[nether_mountains|Nether Mountains]] under the guise of academic component gathering.

Access to his tower is controlled — a letter of introduction from a senior faculty member is required for non-students to enter. Catchphrases: "Precisely!" and "Now, where was I?" In combat he's easy to underestimate — quietly a capable battlefield caster when actually pressed into one.

DM Only

The true purpose of the Nether Mountains expedition was a supply run for the [[thornwick_consortium|Thornwick Arcane Consortium]], a prestigious arcane university three weeks' travel east. Crumb is a point of contact between the Academy and outside arcane powers. He views hired party members as "bodyguards," not colleagues — not malicious, just genuinely oblivious to the stakes he imposes on others.

Despite his shabby appearance, Crumb is very wealthy — gold concealed in various pouches and a lockbox he never lets leave his sight. He is a terrible liar and will eventually admit everything if pressed. His scattered demeanor masks a shrewd businessman who knows exactly what his components are worth.

Combat: see [[oswald_crumb_statblock|Oswald Crumb — Combat Stats]] for his full battlefield stat block (Sorcerer 10, built for the Nether Mountains expedition) — that's how he ended up turning the One Shot 5 ambush with a well-placed Chromatic Orb.

The Consortium's Nether Mountains research site has been attacked twice in the past six months by orc warbands; Crumb reported both incidents to the Consortium, not to Academy administration.

What the previous party (One Shots 1–5) learned: During [[session_5|the Nether Mountains ambush]], a single well-placed Chromatic Orb from Crumb turned the fight — the first sign he wasn't the harmless academic he appeared. The party then broke him down mid-expedition and he confirmed everything above to their faces — no longer a hidden thread as far as they were concerned. When [[bloodraven|BloodRaven]] recalled a professor who disappeared on a similar expedition roughly fifty years ago, Crumb claimed the man sacrificed himself so Crumb and his students could escape; the party didn't believe him, but he was telling the truth, full stop. Crumb offered, and the party accepted, a standing 15% cut of his Thornwick profits in exchange for their silence — [[lugeiros_serise|Lug]] fought it hardest before folding under group pressure, the other three had no such hesitation. Between the field notes and Crumb's confession, that party came to understand the components aren't just rare — they're bound for dangerous spellwork, though Crumb never said what specifically, and they never pushed.

For the current (Session Zero) party: None of the above is theirs to know yet. As long-tenured students they likely know Crumb personally — his reputation, his tower, his pedantry — but not his Thornwick arrangement, his wealth, the vanished-professor story, or anything about "dangerous spellwork." That has to be (re-)earned through play, same as the rest of the Thornwick thread (see thornwick_consortium.html).
