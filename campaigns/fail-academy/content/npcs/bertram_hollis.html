Title
Councilor, [[silverymoon|Silverymoon]] Merchant Council
Species
Human — stout, well-fed, a little porky
Seen
Guest speaker at Commencement, [[session_2|Session 2]]
Reputation
Nobody has a great deal of respect for him, and most students knew that before he stood up

Dressed nicely. Clearly delighted to have been asked, and just as clearly unaware how little that delight was shared.

He gave the address in the specific cadence of a man who has given this exact speech, or a close cousin of it, at several other institutions this year already — warm, generic, entirely divorced from anything the Academy actually does, and completely forgettable. He ran over his time. The hall got hot and restless. He closed on "each and every one of you has the power to make a real difference in this world," trailed off, and got a small smattering of applause that was mostly courtesy and arguably gratitude for his leaving the stage.

[[headmistress_dowe|Headmistress Dowe]] introduced him and reclaimed the stage immediately afterward.

DM Only

Invented on the spot and deliberately disposable — created as a comic-relief set piece for the ceremony, not as a thread. He is not tied to [[thornwick_consortium|Thornwick]], the [[ninth_thesis|Ninth Thesis]], or anything else, and the original design note is explicit about keeping him that way unless there is a specific reason to complicate him.

Given an entity anyway (2026-08-24) because he was played on screen in front of the party and because a sitting Silverymoon Merchant Council councilor is a cheap, plausible door into that city's commerce if one is ever wanted. If you do use him later, the useful thing about him is exactly his uselessness: a man with real access and no discernment, who would be flattered into helping and would never work out why he was asked.

He was present when all four PCs had their ring patterns announced from the stage.
