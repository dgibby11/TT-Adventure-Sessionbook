Appearance

Headmistress Seranthia Dowe is a tiefling of moderate height with deep burgundy skin, small swept-back horns, and amber eyes that seem to read whatever room she walks into before she finishes walking into it. She dresses in black with a deep red academic sash bearing the Academy crest. She moves quietly. Students often notice she has arrived before they saw her enter.

Public Role

Dowe is the public face and administrative authority of FAIL Academy. She chairs the Academic Council, presides over [[academy_traditions|all major ceremonies]], signs every expulsion, and personally reads every disciplinary report. She gives a speech at the start of each academic year that is exactly seventeen minutes long. It has never been the same speech twice, and students who pay close attention notice it gets a little darker each year.

She is scrupulously fair. Students who go to her with genuine grievances find she listens carefully and acts. Students who try to manipulate her find the conversation ends much faster than they expected.

Personality

Dowe is formal but not cold. She is precise but not unkind. She finds humor in unexpected places and does not broadcast it. She has an unsettling habit of answering questions that weren't quite asked.

She knows [[lenny_goldvein|Lenny Goldvein]] is cutting corners somewhere and has not yet decided what to do about it. She has a respectful but wary relationship with [[ellery_voss|Dr. Ellery Voss]] that she describes as "collegial."

DM Only

Dowe knows the [[ninth_thesis|Ninth Thesis]] exists and operates on her campus. She has known since her second year in post — [[ellery_voss|Voss]] read her in personally, having judged her someone who could be trusted with it. She has never obstructed their work and has never officially acknowledged it either; the arrangement suits them both. It's also the real reason behind the unusual professional latitude Voss gets that other senior faculty have noticed but never understood.

She is not naive about what she's tolerating: an organization operating on her campus, outside her direct authority, answerable to no one but itself. She has decided, deliberately and more than once, that the Ninth Thesis's judgment is sound enough to be worth that risk. Whether that decision holds up under real pressure — rather than as an abstract principle — is a question for later in the campaign.

Using Dowe: She can be an ally, an antagonist, or (ideally) both at different moments. If PCs bring her credible evidence of something dangerous happening below the school, she will act — but on her own terms and timeline, not theirs. She is the most powerful individual in the building. She is also the most isolated.

Secret: Her locked office drawer (see [[headmistress_office|the Headmistress's Study]]) holds her correspondence with her predecessor — the actual record of how the Ninth Thesis arrangement was handed down to her, not an incident report. She has read it twice and doesn't intend to read it again; it's less what it says than what it plainly cost her predecessor to write it that stays with her.
