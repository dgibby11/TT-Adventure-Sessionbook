Viola and Tito are the only two non-elven fey in the bardic college — half the department can claim some fey ancestry, but no one mistakes an elf for a fairy or a satyr. She's Small, bright-eyed, dressed a shade more finely than most students in Gossamer-woven fabric that shifts color at the edges when she moves. Her wings are a mood ring: a slow, contented shimmer normally, a fast electric flutter when she's excited, and — around Tito specifically — a stuttering, off-rhythm blur that gives her away before she's said a word, along with a faint dusting of glitter shed from her skin that she has never once managed to hide. Rehearsed speeches fall apart the moment she's actually in front of him; she talks fast, over-explains, laughs half a beat too early at her own jokes, and retreats before a scene can overstay itself. Away from him she's composed, even sharp — it's specifically Tito who undoes her.

She wove [[standing_ovation|The Standing Ovation]] for him rather than anything with her own name on it — family craft for the base of it (see the item's own entry for how), her own bardic magic and every stitch of the embroidery for the part that actually matters.

DM Only

Her devotion is crush-adjacent and entirely unspoken, and she'd rather die than confirm it. She graduates a year after this party — a natural recurring NPC either way. Her family's wealth and standing in fey high society is a real, usable hook: an invitation to "meet the young man our Viola speaks so highly of" can play as warm or as a genuine fey-court obligation, DM's choice, whenever it's useful. There's also real tension with the Gossamer family underneath all of that — what they expect of her, what role in the family trade (or fey-court marriage market) she's being groomed for — that hasn't been dramatized yet. Leave it as an open thread rather than deciding it now; it's the kind of thing that can surface on its own once she's a recurring presence.

Two more family threads, both meant to stay background unless a DM needs them: [[green_room_curtain|the curtain in her closet]] that gets her home to the Feywild once a month, and [[perrin_oakhollow|a quiet member of the Bards Department wardrobe staff]] the family placed to watch over her before she ever enrolled — without her knowledge. Neither is meant to surface in the ordinary run of play, but both exist if a plot ever needs them.
