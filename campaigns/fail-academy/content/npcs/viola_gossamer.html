Viola and Tito are the only two fey in the bardic college. Small, quick, and prone to shedding a faint dusting of glitter whenever she's flustered — which around Tito is often. Rehearsed speeches fall apart the moment she's actually in front of him; she talks fast, over-explains, and retreats before a scene can overstay itself. She wove her graduation capstone — a genuinely accomplished piece of enchantment work, per [[maestro_lira|Maestro Lira]] — into a cloak for him rather than into anything with her own name on it.

DM Only

Her devotion is crush-adjacent and entirely unspoken, and she'd rather die than confirm it. She graduates a year after this party — a natural recurring NPC either way. Her family's wealth and standing in fey high society is a real, usable hook: an invitation to "meet the young man our Viola speaks so highly of" can play as warm or as a genuine fey-court obligation, DM's choice, whenever it's useful.
