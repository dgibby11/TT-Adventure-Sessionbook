Unknown. That is the entry.

The furthest name on the list by an order of magnitude — the Moonshaes are a sea voyage from the Sword Coast, not a road. Nothing happening in the North reaches Caer Westphal quickly, and nothing happening in Caer Westphal reaches the North at all.

That is exactly why Voss wrote him down. Every other name on the list is somewhere a determined enemy could plausibly look. Emeric is the one that isn't. If the four of them ever need to be somewhere that nothing can follow them to, the answer is a long, dull, expensive boat.

He was, by every account Voss has ever given, the best student she ever had — and she is not generous with that kind of statement. He has not written in two years.

DM Only

Do not decide what the silence means yet, and do not let it become a Phase 1 or Phase 2 thread. The Moonshaes are off the map for this arc. Three readings, all live, none chosen: (a) entirely mundane — post across the Sea of Swords is slow, unreliable, and expensive, and he was never much of a letter-writer even on campus; (b) he is deep enough in something that going quiet was a choice; (c) something happened. Leave it open until the party has an actual reason to care, and resist the pull to make it (c) just because it is the most interesting.

Voss's tell: she wrote him down anyway, knowing he's out of contact, on a list explicitly meant for emergencies. The silence is written on the slip itself, in the same flat hand as everything else — she notes it and does not remark on it aloud, which is a more revealing thing than concealing it would have been. If asked directly she elaborates plainly and without drama; she does not perform worry. She is not hiding it. She simply has not decided what to do about it.

If the party ever gets there, he is a genuinely capable operative and a good one, and the reunion with Voss's name attached should be warm. He is not a betrayal setup. If option (c) ever gets chosen, it should be something that happened to him, not something he did.

Naming note: the entity is deliberately thin — he exists to make the list feel like a real network with real edges to it, not as a scene NPC. Read this note before expanding him.
