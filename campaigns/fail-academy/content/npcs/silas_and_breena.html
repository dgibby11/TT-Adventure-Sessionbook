Guntrah was abandoned as a toddler in a trade town and taken in by a working-class gnomish couple who ran a repair shop. Growing up an orc in a house built for gnomes is most of why he moves the way he does — the careful, deliberate grace people don't expect from someone his size.

Silas ran the shop on one rule: chores and lessons before play. While other children ran the streets with wooden swords, Guntrah sat hunched at a tiny workbench stripping gears and reassembling clockwork. Silas taught him that magic wasn't mystical hand-waving — it was physics, discipline, and elbow grease. He is scattered, loses things in his own workshop for a year at a time, and is quietly, enormously proud.

Breena is the one who saw it coming. When Guntrah told his father he wasn't returning to the shop, the reply was "your mother told me this was a probability."

At Commencement

Both made the trip and sat in the crowd like children in the chairs, feet nowhere near the floor, barely able to speak. Guntrah found them afterward — "Pops? Mom?" — "I did it."

He told them he wasn't coming back to the shop yet: he and his friends have opportunities through the university, and he wants more of the world first. It landed well, with the sadness underneath showing. He asked whether they'd manage, whether the shop was all right, and got "we've got dozens of years left" — a phrasing that stopped him dead until his father clarified he meant the two of them together. Guntrah promised to be back before he's an old man.

Then his father produced [[speaking_stone|a speaking stone]] he'd meant to send a year ago and lost in his own workshop, found again just in time. They intend to use it at least once a day.

Guntrah took them both over to meet [[prof_tavel|Professor Tavel]], and Silas turned out to be starstruck — Tavel is genuinely famous outside the Academy, and Guntrah had no idea. Silas got a look at [[ollie|Ollie]] and recognised his own design in it immediately: "it's actually part of my design — he just finished it, exactly like we planned."

Silas, on being introduced to the man who taught his son

"Everybody needs a little bit of help from time to time. You should never turn away anybody that offers to help you."

Guntrah picked them both up, hugged them, and left.

DM Only

The name collision is deliberate and load-bearing. Guntrah's father is named Silas, and so is a party member. Guntrah calls the PC "Sil" specifically to avoid using his father's name — established at the table 2026-08-20. Note that at the ceremony Guntrah floated the joke to his father ("I have a friend with the same name as you, a half-orc, isn't that strange — that's a gnomish name") and his father proposed going to find them and make introductions. That introduction never happened. It is sitting there, free, whenever you want it.

Kept as one entity rather than two on purpose — they only ever appear together, and splitting them would need an id for Silas that doesn't collide with the PC's. Split later if either ever needs their own scene.

Surnames undecided. Neither has been given one at the table and neither needs one yet. Spelling note: the DM initially said "Brynna" without knowing the file already read Breena from Session 0; Breena stands unless a player says otherwise.

Live thread: daily contact via the stone was promised by them, not by Guntrah. Two ordinary gnomes who will notice within a day or two that their son has stopped answering — and the party is currently on another plane. Whether the stone reaches across planes is your call, and a good one to make deliberately rather than in the moment.
