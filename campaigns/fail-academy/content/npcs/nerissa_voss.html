Nerissa Voss was never one of the three officially-credited founders — that distinction belongs to her brother [[aldric_voss|Aldric]], [[isolde_orath|Isolde Orath]], and [[brennan_ashcroft|Brennan Ashcroft]] alone. She served the founding-era Academy as its first archivist instead: the person responsible for what got recorded, filed, and kept, in an institution that was still deciding what kind of place it was going to be.

The Ninth Thesis

Every graduate of the Academy's first class — nine students — was required to write a thesis on what they intended to become. The ninth, Wren Halloway, wrote something the institution wasn't ready for and it was quietly pulled from the record rather than filed alongside the other eight. Nerissa was the one person positioned to notice a true document disappear and do something about it. She kept it, and built a private, careful organization around it — alongside the Academy's official wards, not instead of them, and without altering or embellishing a word of what Halloway had written. See [[ninth_thesis|the Ninth Thesis]] for the organization she founded and its unbroken line of Voss leadership since.

Legacy

She is not memorialized among the three founder stones in the [[memorial_garden|Founder's Memorial Garden]] — that recognition was never hers to claim, and by all accounts she wasn't looking for it. What she left behind instead was the organization itself, passed down through her own line of descendants rather than any public record. Nothing in the Academy's official history credits her with anything beyond her archivist's title.

DM Only

Kept deliberately thin by design — Nerissa's role is textual/historical, not a character the party will meet or interact with directly. Her only real narrative function is as the Ninth Thesis's origin point and the reason Ellery Voss's authority within it is hereditary rather than earned through rank. Do not invent additional biographical detail (cause of death, exact dates, personality specifics) unless a specific scene calls for it — see the_ninth_thesis.json in planning/ for the full design history.

Tone guardrail, consistent with the founders-persona rewrite: write any expansion of her as prudent and quietly principled, not conspiratorial. She preserved a true document and built sensible contingency around it — she didn't fabricate, coerce, or manipulate anyone.
