A brisk, unremarkable middleman — the kind of face that's forgettable on purpose. Businesslike to a fault, mildly impatient with anything that isn't the transaction in front of him. He's been Crumb's contact for about a dozen years, taking over from a predecessor Crumb has mentioned exactly once and never again. He doesn't come to campus; contact happens by sealed courier letter, or an occasional prearranged meeting off-campus when a shipment needs sorting out in person.

Genuinely low on the Consortium's ladder and knows it — this is a job, not a calling. He doesn't know who he ultimately answers to, doesn't ask, and isn't bothered by not knowing (see thornwick_consortium.html's note on compartmentalization). If a party approaches him honestly rather than by force, he has little reason to stonewall: he'll confirm the Consortium's name and that he's "just the buyer," and can point toward the Neverwinter office as where paperwork and decisions ultimately get filed. He does not know why the components matter, what they're for, or anything about Thornwick's leadership — that's genuinely above his pay grade.
