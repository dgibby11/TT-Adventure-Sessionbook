Disposition
Warm immediately. Useful only once he believes them.

A round, cheerful, entirely unthreatening man in his late thirties who looks exactly like what he is paid to look like: a career clerk who has found his level and is content at it. He is delighted to meet Academy people. He will feed them, put them up, ask after professors by name, and tell a long story about a practical exam that went wrong in his third year.

He will not give them a single thing that matters until he is satisfied they are who they say they are.

What he can do, once he is: records, and the kind of access records buy. Who came through the gates and when. Which warehouses changed hands. What was filed, what was withdrawn, and by whom. A writ that makes an awkward question look official. He is also the closest thing the party has to a legal shield in a city that has no reason to like four armed strangers.

DM Only

This is the magistrate's-office operative previously sketched unnamed in ninth_thesis.html and flagged in session_plan.json's open_items as the likely Neverwinter contact. That item is now resolved — the sketch and Anselm are the same person.

The trust problem, and how it's actually solved. Voss handed the party the list at a run, in a corridor, on graduation morning — and never gave them a countersign, because there wasn't time and because she did not expect them to need the list before nightfall. Anselm knows this. His verification is not a password; it is a question about Voss that only somebody who genuinely knows her could answer. Something small, domestic, and impossible to research: what she does with her hands when she's about to say no, what's on the corner of her desk, what she calls the practical exam when she isn't being formal.

Pick the specific detail yourself from established Voss material so it matches what's actually been true at your table across five years of office hours and practicals — do not invent a new one, and do not accept a plausible guess. This is the beat that pays off the campaign's premise that these four have a real history with her. Let them get it. If they somehow can't, he stays friendly, stays useless, and writes to Voss for confirmation — a tenday round trip that costs them time rather than the relationship.

He is not a fighter and will not become one. Fourteen years behind a desk. If the party drags him into anything physical he is a liability and knows it, and he'll say so plainly rather than posture. His value is entirely paper, access, and a legitimate face.

Forward hook (do not push): Thornwick's only known foothold is a brokerage office in Neverwinter (see thornwick_consortium.html). Anselm is the person who could pull its filings. Do not surface this on his own initiative — the current party has never heard the name, and he has no reason to volunteer it. He answers the question he is asked.
