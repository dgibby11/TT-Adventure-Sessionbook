DM Only — the whole of it

A century of a green dragon living in one place turned a radius of forest sour — twisted growth, thorn where there was none, water going wrong. The treant eventually concluded that [[tedrovaxilliath|Tedrovaxilliath]] was a disease in the Wood, and came to remove him.

It fought him. It tore his wing. It did not win, and it did not die either — it withdrew, rooted, and has not moved since.

Treants can stand still for decades. Ted has taken ten years of stillness as proof of a kill and stopped thinking about it. He is wrong, he has no idea he is wrong, and it is standing somewhere in his forest with a settled opinion about him.

Why this is worth keeping

  • Both of them were right. The corruption is real and it is Ted's, and he cannot see it because he measures the Wood by the health of its game. The treant weighed a hundred yards of poisoned ground against eight hundred square miles Ted genuinely keeps in order, and picked the hundred yards. Neither of them is the villain.
  • It is a second opinion. If the party ever need somebody in this forest who is not Ted — an ally, a check on him, or simply a different account of the same history — this is it, and it is already planted.
  • It is findable by accident. Anyone following the water upstream toward [[teds_lair|the sinkhole]] is walking into the worst of the corruption, which is exactly where a treant that came to stop it would have made its stand. A tree the party walk past in Session 4 can open its eyes in Session 9.

Running it, if it ever wakes

Treant, CR 9. It does not care about the Academy, Morvek, the Abyss, or anything the party want. It cares about one forest and it has been standing in the ruined part of it for ten years, thinking. It will be slow — in speech, in judgement, in deciding whether the party are a third thing or simply more of the dragon's problem.

Do not make it a quest-giver. Its value is entirely as the other half of a story the party have only heard one side of, from somebody who was sincere and wrong.

Undecided and best left so: whether it can still fight, whether it wants to, and whether ten years of standing in poisoned ground has done something to it.
