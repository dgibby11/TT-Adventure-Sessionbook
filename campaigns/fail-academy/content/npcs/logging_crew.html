Who
The crew of [[logging_camp|the logging camp]] beside Thundertree — about thirty people
Made up of
A camp boss, a cook and a chore boy, two teamsters, a saw-filer who doubles as smith, and two dozen fallers, haulers and bargemen
Species
Mostly human, a few dwarves, two half-orcs
Disposition
Suspicious of anybody who walks out of [[neverwinter_wood|the Wood]]. Decent, once they have made up their minds

Working people in a working camp. They are up before dawn and asleep at dark, nobody talks at meals, and a stranger pays for supper before sitting down to it. They are not unfriendly. They have simply never seen anybody come out of the trees from that direction.

DM Only — UNPLAYED. Built for [[session_5|Session 5]] on 2026-10-06. The names and every line below are proposals.

Four names is enough

  • Agda Stroud, camp boss. Fifties, blunt, fair. She decides whether the party stays.
  • Cook. Nobody uses another name. Runs the cook tent and hears everything.
  • Colm, head teamster. Knows the road west.
  • Pip, chore boy. Fifteen, and the only one openly thrilled to see adventurers.

Nobody else needs a name.

Why they are suspicious

The party walks out of the wood proper — the one direction nobody comes from — armed, and with [[ollie|a steel construct]]. An orc and a half-orc raise no eyebrows; the crew has both. A drow would, if [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] were still with them.

What they know about the dragon (DM canon, 2026-10-05)

  • The camp used to stand in the wood proper. Many years ago [[tedrovaxilliath|he]] killed a dozen good men, and they withdrew to the edge.
  • They cannot afford to pay adventurers in Neverwinter enough to come and deal with him.
  • They joke that once the party is stronger, perhaps it will come back and do it.
  • They call him "the Green". They have no idea he has a name, and no idea anybody has spoken to him.

Small talk at the fire

  • The firs. "Bends before it breaks. Grows nowhere nearer the city, and the shipwrights pay for every stick."
  • The old camp. "We were in the wood proper once. He killed twelve good men. That was years back, and we've been out here since."
  • The price of a dragon. "We asked in Neverwinter what it would cost to be rid of him. We stopped asking."
  • The ruins. "There was a dragon in there once. A young one. Adventurers killed it twenty years back, and the dead things with it. Been quiet since."
  • The joke. "That lot did for the little one. Come back when you've grown a bit and try the big one. We'll pass a hat."
  • The barges. "Bound up and floated down to the city. Nothing goes down that river without an escort."
  • The carts. "Those? Those are for us. Tents and tools. The timber swims."
  • Thundertree. "The mountain did that. Decades back, before my time."

Sample lines

  • Agda: "You came out of there. On foot. And you're all still here."
  • Agda: "Supper and the spare tent, paid up front, gone by first light."
  • Agda, if they mention the dragon by name: "He has a name?"
  • Cook: "Eat. Talk outside."
  • Colm: "Old road runs west from the ruins. Stay on it and you'll see the city walls."
  • Pip: "Did you see him? Is he really as big as they say?"

The joke lands on this party

By the time they hear it they have either fought [[tedrovaxilliath|the Green]] or left a friend in his keeping. Somebody will answer. "He's dead" and "he's our friend" both land hard on people who lost twelve men to him.

After Session 5 it is the first one, and it is true. They killed him that morning, in a clearing, in five rounds, and [[guntrah|Guntrah]] took the head off. The crew have not been met yet. Agda's first line — "You came out of there. On foot. And you're all still here" — now has an answer she is not ready for.
