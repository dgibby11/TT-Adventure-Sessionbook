Overview

Warden Rowan Ashgrove runs the [[rangers|Rangers Department]] the way she runs a tracking line — without wasted motion. She rarely raises her voice above conversational, never repeats an instruction twice, and expects students to notice things themselves rather than be told. The department's reputation for quiet is hers by design, not accident: she built the culture around the belief that a student who can't sit still in a room will never sit still in a forest, and a forest doesn't forgive impatience.

She inherited the [[owlbear_cave|Owlbear Cave]] stewardship from her own predecessor, in the same quiet, unceremonious handoff she'll one day give to whoever succeeds her — Rangers faculty don't announce the responsibility, they simply carry it. She takes [[kass|Kass]]'s care personally rather than administratively, and has for longer than most students have been alive.

Personality

Patient to a degree that unnerves students who mistake it for indifference. She answers questions with fewer words than were asked, and lets silence do the rest of the work — most people fill a silence with more than they meant to say, and she's content to let them. Not cold, exactly: she notices everything about her students, she just doesn't narrate the noticing.

Relationship to the Party

A long-tenured fifth-year would know her by reputation more than by warmth — she isn't a department any of the four belong to, but everyone on campus has had at least one class or field exercise run through the Rangers longhouse, and everyone remembers the first time she let a silence sit until they broke it. [[torvald_thatch|Torvald]] and the Wardens have worked alongside each other, groundskeeper and department, for years — a professional respect built on both preferring not to explain themselves.

She can be the one who briefs Torvald, or be present when the party arrives, at the DM's discretion. Nothing about her should read as unusual or suspicious — she's exactly as reliable and unreadable as she always is.

Undeveloped beyond this as of this writing — no established secrets, plot ties, or personal history beyond what's above. A steady, competent presence to reach for whenever the Rangers Department needs a voice.
