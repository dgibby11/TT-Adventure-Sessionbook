Runs the practical FAIL examinations from the [[fail_chamber|FAIL Examination Chamber]]. Notably composed under pressure, and holds unusual latitude in how she runs her department — she doesn't answer questions about methodology. More competent than the average faculty member, which is exactly why she is hard to read.

A long-tenured fifth-year would know her well by now: years of practicals, office hours, and department dealings, not a first introduction. Her normal demeanor is unflappable and exacting — precise word choice, no wasted motion, a dry rather than warm sense of humor, and a quietly protective streak toward students she rates highly that she'd never admit to out loud. Students who know her can tell the difference between "Voss is being Voss" and something actually being wrong.

DM Only — WHAT SHE CAN AND CANNOT DO (DM, 2026-10-05). Read this before giving her anything to do.

She is competent, not powerful. "More competent than the average faculty member" means judgement, composure and paperwork. It has never meant spellcasting or skill at arms: she is fairly weak, and no more capable in a skirmish than anybody else who works at a desk.

Her job is administrative. The DM put it this way at the table in [[session_2|Session 2]]: "the administrator of the midterm facilities and offices" — a position she cannot afford to lose, and the reason she keeps to the rules everywhere except her "side gig", the [[ninth_thesis|Ninth Thesis]]. The [[fail_chamber|FAIL Chamber]] exercises are infrastructure she oversees, not magic she performs.

She rarely leaves campus and does not do field work. She relies on other, more capable people. That is what her operatives are for, and it is why — once her own attempt on [[academy_provisions|Provisions]] had failed — she asked four graduates to go in for her.

She cannot cast her own teleportation. Other people do it for her. So her reach is always somebody else: somebody else's spell, somebody else's favour, somebody else's bill.

  • Do not write her as a high-level caster, a rescuer or a combat presence. In a fight she is somebody to be protected.
  • If she travels by magic, say who did it for her and what it cost. The Academy's sanctioned teleport ring is in [[main_complex|the Main Complex]]; she cannot work it herself.
  • The ring's sigil sequence is not hers to hand round. That the ring exists is public; its sequence is closely held, and every use is logged. If the party's use of it were ever traced back to her she would almost certainly lose her job. She may give it to them eventually — not for a while (DM, 2026-10-05).
  • Not settled: whether lesser magic — a sending, say — is her own or arranged. Ask; do not assume either way.
  • No class, level or stat block is recorded for her anywhere in this repo, and none should be inferred.

DM Only — Session 2: what she did, and what she doesn't know

She missed Commencement — the first ceremony she has ever missed in the party's five years, and three of the four noticed. She used the ceremony's cover (everyone accounted for, Provisions reliably unmanned) to try the back room herself, and ran straight into [[osric_morne|Osric]], who had stayed back deliberately. The argument was real, entirely off-screen, and she pushed harder than her composure normally allows before catching herself.

When the party came to her office she did not perform composure for them. "You missed graduation." — "I did, yes." — "Are you okay?" — "No, I'm not. Come in quick." Ten visits to Osric in ten days, no paperwork, no audits, no books. She asked them — carefully, without dressing it up as an order — to look for her, gave them a set of lockpicks she had confiscated three weeks earlier, told them to dispose of it and that none of it came from her, and then went and held Osric at the front counter herself for six minutes.

She was standing at that counter when four of her graduates vanished off the plane. She does not know. Nobody was left behind to tell her. As far as the Academy is concerned, four students went to a party and never came back — and she is the one who sent them.

This is a live, unmanaged problem sitting on the Material Plane for the entire time the party is away, and it is hers. Decide what she does with it before the party has any way to contact her.
