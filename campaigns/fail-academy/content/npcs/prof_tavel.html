Overview

Gruff, exacting, and visibly unimpressed by most things — which happens to be exactly the cover a secret needs. In [[guntrah|Guntrah]]'s second year, Tavel caught him alone, flawlessly rewiring a destroyed training golem with nothing but a bent fork. Rather than report it, he struck a deal on the spot.

The Arrangement

Officially, Guntrah has been serving an unusually long stretch of "cleaning duty" in the boiler rooms ever since — punishment, on paper, for insubordination that Tavel documented himself. Unofficially, that's where the real instruction happens: fifth-year engineering textbooks Tavel shouldn't have loaned out, and a steady supply of discarded components from the department's wealthier students — [[julian_vance|Julian Vance]] chief among them, though Julian has no idea his own castoffs have been funding his rival's education. Tavel has never once acknowledged, even to Guntrah directly, that the "punishment" is anything else.

DM Only

Tavel has been running this arrangement since Guntrah's second year — going on four years now. If [[prof_bram_holdar|Holdar]] or the administration ever looked closely at the boiler room attendance logs against what Guntrah can actually do, both the "punishment" and Guntrah's real transcript would unravel fast. Tavel has never treated that as a live risk. It is one.
