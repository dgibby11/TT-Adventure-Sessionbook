Overview

Ghurza works out of a cramped, meticulously organized corner of the [[artificers_forge|forge]] that the rest of the department has quietly agreed is hers, though no one remembers deciding that. Where the department's culture runs on "did it work," Ghurza runs on "did you understand why it worked" — she'll let a student's project fail forever before she'll let them succeed by accident. Dunn defers to her on anything requiring patience, which is most things he doesn't personally have the patience for.

Undeveloped beyond that as of this writing — no established backstory, students, or plot ties yet. A name and a personality on the shelf for whenever the department needs a second voice.
