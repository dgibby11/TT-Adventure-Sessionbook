Cold. Will help anyway.

The nearest name on [[ellery_voss|Voss]]'s list by a wide margin — a little over two days north of campus, the same walk any fifth-year has made a dozen times. Slight, dark-haired, dressed for a records room rather than a road. Speaks in complete sentences and no more of them than the situation requires.

She works among Silverymoon's record-keepers, which makes her the most useful operative on the list for anything that was ever written down: shipping manifests, guild registrations, land transfers, who paid whom and when. Her cover is not a cover so much as an actual career she is actually good at.

Meeting her is not warm. She will read Voss's name, look at the four of them for a beat too long, and then do precisely what the obligation requires — a floor to sleep on, a meal, an honest answer to a question she can answer — and nothing beyond it. No offers, no follow-up, no interest in what they're doing or how it turns out. She does not ask them to stay.

DM Only

Play the frost, but don't let it read as betrayal. She is completely reliable — she has never missed a request from Voss in eleven years and will not start now. The risk at the table is that a party primed for conspiracy reads "unfriendly operative" as "compromised operative" and burns a session shadowing her. If they start down that road, give them the tell: everything she actually does is correct, prompt, and slightly more than she was obliged to do. The coldness is entirely in the delivery.

Why she's like this — deliberately unresolved. Pick at the table if it ever matters, or leave it: (a) she asked Voss for something once — an extraction, a transfer, backing in a dispute — and Voss said no, correctly, and she has never gotten over being right about the cost; (b) she does the work out of obligation to whoever recruited her rather than any belief in it, and resents being reminded it exists; (c) nothing happened at all and this is simply who she is, and the party is projecting. Option (c) is the funniest and the most likely to be true. Don't decide early.

Thawing her. She does not respond to charm, deference, or name-dropping Voss harder. She responds to competence — a question that shows they've already done their own reading, a piece of information she didn't have, or being wrong about something in front of her and correcting it cleanly. Reward that with one genuinely useful thing she wasn't obliged to give, and let her be almost pleasant about it exactly once.

Position note: Silverymoon is the operative the party can reach easily from campus — and completely the wrong direction once Session 2 strands them in Neverwinter Wood. That inversion is worth playing: the closest, most convenient name on the list becomes the least reachable the moment they actually need it.
