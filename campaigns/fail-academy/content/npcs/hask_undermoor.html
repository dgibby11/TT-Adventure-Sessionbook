A big, unhurried fourth-year who spent five years in the same department as [[guntrah|Guntrah]] without ever being told the truth about him — and who took it better than anyone expected.

When Guntrah crossed the Commencement stage from the Barbarians with a self-built construct walking at his heel, a good number of his classmates took it badly. Not raging, exactly, but visibly annoyed, and several of them plainly felt they had been had. Guntrah went over to the Barbarians' block afterward anyway, to say a real goodbye rather than slip away.

Hask stepped forward out of the group.

What he said

"Actually, I think it's pretty cool what you did. I don't know — most of us can't do half the things you can do, and the fact that you were able to trick us for so long, that's pretty cool."

Guntrah told him straight that passing on a different set of skills had been a genuine challenge, and that he appreciated the department pushing him, because it had made him grow in a direction he wouldn't have chosen. "Perhaps this is not goodbye. But if it is — thanks."

They parted on the traditional greeting: the clasped hand, the pull, and the headbutt. Hask is genuinely strong. Guntrah — running on enchanted gear rather than muscle, and rolling considerably better than either of them had any right to expect — threw him around a bit in the process. It read as entirely real.

"Goddamn it," said Hask, shaking his head, and went back to wrestling his friends on the ground.

DM Only

Invented 2026-08-24 to give a face to the unnamed fourth-year who spoke up at Commencement (Session 2). The name is free — rename without hesitation if the DM wants something else. The DM's own expectation is that he probably won't be needed again and exists mainly so the moment has somebody in it.

That said, he is a slightly better hook than he looks. He is the only person in the Barbarians Department who reacted to Guntrah's deception with admiration rather than resentment, and unlike [[prof_bram_holdar|Holdar]] he said it to Guntrah's face. He also has another year to run, in a department whose head has just had a very bad afternoon and left early. If anything is ever needed from inside the Barbarians — gossip, an account of Holdar's mood, a friendly hand — Hask is the obvious and only door.

He has no idea about the [[prof_tavel|Tavel]] arrangement, the boiler rooms, or the fact that Holdar intercepted Guntrah's artificer acceptance in the first place. He thinks Guntrah beat the department at its own game by sheer nerve, and he admires that. Nobody has corrected him.
