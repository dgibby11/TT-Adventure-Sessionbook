Disposition
Genuinely glad to see them. Genuinely, impossibly busy.

Easy to find and hard to reach. She is well enough known in Waterdeep that "the Sunite who trained at the Academy" gets the party pointed the right way by roughly the third person they ask, and she is precisely as striking as her reputation says — which she treats as a devotion and a discipline rather than an accident or a joke.

She will be pleased to see them, and she will mean it. She will also have somewhere to be in a quarter of an hour, and somewhere after that. Getting five unbroken minutes of her actual attention takes an appointment days out, a favour called in, or making enough of a public nuisance of themselves that she takes them somewhere private — which she will not thank them for.

What she is worth: healing, sanctuary, coin, and doors. She can get four road-worn strangers into a Waterdhavian room that no amount of paperwork would ever open, and she can put them somewhere nobody will look for a tenday. What she is not worth: discretion. She is a public figure in a city built on gossip and does not pretend otherwise.

DM Only

Play her as more formidable than the setup suggests. A table that hears "beautiful cleric of the love goddess" will arrive expecting a joke. Her faith is real, her conviction is sharp, and she is entirely unembarrassed by any of it — a player who tries to be clever at her expense should come off worse and be surprised about it. Sune's people are not soft.

The obstacle is her visibility, not her willingness. She wants to help and will help. The complication is that anything she does for the party happens where people can see it. Voss uses her as a safe harbour and a social lever and never, ever as a covert asset — she knows this about herself and is not wounded by it.

She does not know she is unusual within the network. Most operatives work quietly. She works in the open because that's what she was recruited for. If the party asks her about the organization she is markedly more relaxed about naming it than Nyella or Ledger would be — not indiscreet, just untroubled. That contrast is worth playing straight rather than turning into a security problem.

Distance note: Waterdeep is a long way south of the party's Session 2 landing point. She is a Phase 2 resource at the earliest, not a Session 3 answer.
