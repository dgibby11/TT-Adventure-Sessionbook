Overview

Professor Brasca Ironquill has run the Wizards Department for the last eleven years, inherited a tower full of faculty who had never been meaningfully supervised, and has spent every year since imposing the kind of structure that makes the place function without making it comfortable. She did not build the department's culture of precise language and low tolerance for imprecision — that predates her — but she has weaponized it administratively in a way her predecessors never bothered to.

She keeps her own parallel set of department records, more detailed than the official ones, because she does not fully trust the official ones. She has been right often enough about this that no one argues anymore.

Running the Tower

Her actual job is largely diplomatic: reconciling [[prof_aldous_fenwick|Professor Fenwick's]] tenured immovability and thirty-year-old curriculum with [[oswald_crumb|Professor Crumb's]] more collegial, more current, and — in her private assessment — more evasive section of the tower. She has never won an argument with Fenwick and has stopped trying to win them outright, settling instead for winning them slowly, over years, through attrition and paperwork. Crumb she finds harder to read, which bothers her considerably more than she lets on.

Students experience her mostly as the person who enforces lab safety protocols with genuine teeth and who personally reviews every research proposal above second-year level. She rejects roughly a third of them on procedural grounds before anyone gets to the merits.

Personality

Blunt, exacting, allergic to hedging. She asks follow-up questions the way other people cross-examine witnesses, and she remembers the answers. She has no patience for faculty politics conducted through implication rather than statement, which puts her at odds with most of the rest of senior faculty more often than she'd prefer to admit.

DM Only

Ironquill has run the tower for eleven years and has never once been granted access to the seventh floor — officially "administrative," officially not her department's business, despite sitting at the top of the building she is nominally in charge of. She has requested access through every legitimate channel available to her, been refused every time without a real explanation, and has quietly concluded that whoever is stonewalling her outranks the org chart she was given. She has not been told it connects to the [[aldric_voss|Voss family]], and has not made that connection herself — but she has kept a meticulous, private log of every denial, every excuse, and every faculty member who changed the subject when she asked. If a party ever needs a paper trail on the seventh floor's existence without needing to breach it themselves, her log is exactly that, and she would be strongly motivated to hand it over to anyone who could actually get her an answer.

This is a slow-burn hook, not an active thread — she has no idea anything is actually wrong, only that she has been denied information she believes she's entitled to, and that offends her professionally more than anything else could.
