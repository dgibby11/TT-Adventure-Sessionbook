Name
Morvek — spelled out at the table, letter by letter, by the man he owns
What he is
A lich. [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] said the word plainly and did not soften it
Learned
[[session_3|Session 3]], behind a rock on the ash plain, about four minutes after the party learned Vrenn's name
Seen
Never. Not once, by anybody

He owns the operation, the ritual site, the goods, and [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] — that last one literally, and Vrenn says so in exactly those words. He is the reason there is a workbench on a rock in the Abyss with a drow tied to a schedule at it, and he is the reason the packages go out.

Everything Vrenn said about him, in one place

"His name is Morvek. M-O-R-V-E-K. He's a lich. He's the one who's having me do all this. And he owns me."

"Don't go looking for him. He's too powerful. He's got me trapped."

"He has my — it's basically the Abyssal equivalent to a soul coin. He has another name for it."

"If I were to leave the plane he would know immediately, and he would follow me, and he would kill all of you."

What the Party Knows

  • He is not the top of anything. He rents. [[the_house|The house]] belongs to a minor demon who is away and who comes back to collect; Morvek is a tenant in it, and Vrenn was clear about the arrangement without being asked.
  • He travels by the basement circle at that house — he arrives through it and leaves through it, which is the only reason the party ever went down those stairs.
  • He gave Vrenn the [[steel_tuning_fork|steel tuning fork]], because he wanted goods going to the Material Plane. He named Neverwinter for one scroll, and gave Vrenn an image rather than a name for the other — the one that put four graduates on a rock in the Abyss.
  • He keeps a room upstairs, locked, arcanely warded, and the one thing in that crumbling building that is cared for. Vrenn's advice on it was one sentence: don't dare.
  • He was due back at dawn. The party left several hours before that, through his floor, having killed two of his guards.
  • He holds Vrenn's soul in an object on his person, and that object is a tracker. Vrenn believes this absolutely.

Nobody has described him. Nobody has seen a portrait, a body, or a mark carrying his name. He exists so far entirely as a thing other people are afraid of.

DM Only — the hard constraint, and it still holds

Morvek does not appear in person and is not a combat presence. Any lich is well past a level-10 party. He is an absence, an authority, and a deadline, and he has now been all three for a full session without ever being in a room. That worked; keep doing it.

Where he sits: a lesser lich running the fabrication and shipping operation on [[ritual_platform|Thanatos]], the 333rd layer, answering to Harthoon one tier up and to Orcus above that. Harthoon is Phase 3 material and must not be named for a long time; "somebody above him" is stronger and costs nothing.

He is not the landlord's superior. The minor demon lord who owns [[the_house|the house]] is lateral — roughly Morvek's equal, slightly under, forced to house him by Orcus's writ carried down through Harthoon, and quietly resentful about it. A deliberate choice (DM, 2026-08-31) to keep the chain of command at two rungs the party can perceive rather than four they cannot. The chain is Vrenn → Morvek → Harthoon → Orcus, and the party has the first two.

The Grave Token

The object Vrenn could not name is a Grave Token, and Morvek wears it on his belt. It is an Abyssal soul-binding invented for this campaign (DM, 2026-08-29) and is deliberately not an infernal soul coin — nothing in this campaign touches the Nine Hells and no description of it should imply devils. If it ever needs explaining in-world it is a lich's work: a phylactery with the polarity reversed. Do not say "Grave Token" at the table until somebody actually sees one. Vrenn's own phrasing — the Abyssal equivalent of a soul coin, he has another name for it — landed perfectly in [[session_3|Session 3]] and is the register to keep.

It is a beacon, not an anchor. Nothing physically stopped Vrenn crossing planes, and in Session 3 he crossed. The Token tells Morvek where its soul is, always. This is now live — see [[what_follows_vrenn|What Follows Vrenn]] and session_3.html's Session 4 block, where the three shapes (slow / fast / Vrenn's read is wrong) are laid out. It wants deciding before Session 4 opens.

His arrogance, which is the usable flaw

He taught a captive plane shift and handed him a 250 gp steel fork, because he was certain the Token made it irrelevant. That certainty is the crack in him. The party has now walked off with the fork and Vrenn has stripped the components out of his basement, and both of those happened because he was sure they could not matter.
