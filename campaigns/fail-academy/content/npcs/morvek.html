He owns the operation, the ritual site, the goods, and [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] — that last one literally, and Vrenn says so in exactly those words. He is the reason there is a workbench on a rock in the Abyss with a drow tied to a schedule at it, and he is the reason the packages go out.

Everything Vrenn said about him, in one place

"His name is Morvek. M-O-R-V-E-K. He's a lich. He's the one who's having me do all this. And he owns me."

"Don't go looking for him. He's too powerful. He's got me trapped."

"He has my — it's basically the Abyssal equivalent to a soul coin. He has another name for it."

"If I were to leave the plane he would know immediately, and he would follow me, and he would kill all of you."

What the Party Knows

  • He is not the top of anything. He rents. [[the_house|The house]] belongs to a minor demon who is away and who comes back to collect; Morvek is a tenant in it, and Vrenn was clear about the arrangement without being asked.
  • He travels by the basement circle at that house — he arrives through it and leaves through it, which is the only reason the party ever went down those stairs.
  • He gave Vrenn the [[steel_tuning_fork|steel tuning fork]], because he wanted goods going to the Material Plane. He named Neverwinter for one scroll, and gave Vrenn an image rather than a name for the other — the one that put four graduates on a rock in the Abyss.
  • He keeps a room upstairs, locked, arcanely warded, and the one thing in that crumbling building that is cared for. Vrenn's advice on it was one sentence: don't dare.
  • He was due back at dawn. The party left several hours before that, through his floor, having killed two of his guards.
  • He holds Vrenn's soul in an object on his person, and that object is a tracker. Vrenn believes this absolutely, and [[session_4|Session 4]] proved him right.

What They Learned in [[session_4|Session 4]]

All of it out of Vrenn, in a dome, on the first quiet night any of them had had.

  • He calls the object a grave token, and he invented the thing. He went to the Nine Hells, learned how soul coins work, and brought the idea back to the Abyss with him. A lich who travelled to another cosmology to steal a financial instrument.
  • He bought Vrenn's soul by holding his sister. Vrenn traded himself for her.
  • He wears the amulet everywhere he goes. About his phylactery, Vrenn knows nothing whatsoever.
  • He knew Vrenn had left the plane the same evening. Vrenn went to his knees holding his head for twenty seconds and came up with "Morvek knows. He knows I'm here." How he knows, Vrenn could not say: "that could be more the grave token speaking to him, and maybe its connection to me tells him where I am."
  • He cannot scry across planes, so he has to arrive on the Material Plane before he can find anybody. And he needs a tuning fork to do it — Vrenn does not think there is one left in the basement, and expects him to get one very soon.
  • Two to four days, per Vrenn: two if he is determined, three or four if he has other priorities.
  • Vrenn would not bet on him against a green dragon. Asked whether Morvek could take [[tedrovaxilliath|Ted]]: "if I was a betting man, I wouldn't pick either one. They're both probably pretty capable."

Nobody has described him. Nobody has seen a portrait, a body, or a mark carrying his name. He exists so far entirely as a thing other people are afraid of.

What They Learned in [[session_5|Session 5]]

  • He sent Vrenn a message on the first evening, and it said he was coming. Vrenn told them so himself, sitting beside a dead dragon. "I made a mistake, and he was going to come for me."
  • He does not usually do his own dirty business. He contracts it out. Vrenn thinks this might be the exception.
  • He can scry on Vrenn through the token, and whoever is standing beside Vrenn will be in the picture.
  • The package was posted on his orders. Vrenn: "If I hadn't done what Morvek asked me to do and mailed that package, none of this would have happened."
  • Vrenn has dreamed for years about killing him. "Not today, though."

DM Only — the hard constraint, and it still holds

Morvek does not appear in person and is not a combat presence. Any lich is well past a level-10 party. He is an absence, an authority, and a deadline, and he has now been all three for a full session without ever being in a room. That worked; keep doing it.

Where he sits: a lesser lich running the fabrication and shipping operation on [[ritual_platform|Thanatos]], the 333rd layer, answering to Harthoon one tier up and to Orcus above that. Harthoon is Phase 3 material and must not be named for a long time; "somebody above him" is stronger and costs nothing.

He is not the landlord's superior. The minor demon lord who owns [[the_house|the house]] is lateral — roughly Morvek's equal, slightly under, forced to house him by Orcus's writ carried down through Harthoon, and quietly resentful about it. A deliberate choice (DM, 2026-08-31) to keep the chain of command at two rungs the party can perceive rather than four they cannot. The chain is Vrenn → Morvek → Harthoon → Orcus, and the party has the first two.

The Grave Token — SESSION 4 REVERSED TWO LOCKED DECISIONS ABOUT IT. Flagged, not absorbed.

Morvek wears it on his belt. It is his own invention: a soul-binding, and in-world a phylactery with the polarity reversed.

1. "Grave token" was said out loud to the party (2026-09-24). The standing instruction was not to use the words at the table until somebody saw one. Vrenn used them unprompted, as the name his owner uses, when the party asked him directly what the thing on the belt was. Player knowledge now.

2. THE NINE HELLS ARE IN THE CAMPAIGN. The locked decision (DM, 2026-08-29) was that this is "deliberately not an infernal soul coin — nothing in this campaign touches the Nine Hells and no description of it should imply devils." Vrenn's explanation did the opposite, deliberately and at length: soul coins are real, they are currency in the Hells, Morvek went down there personally and learned all about them, and he "wanted to bring that knowledge with him to the Abyss" — so he built grave tokens. This is a much better lich and a much better object, and it answers why an Abyssal operation has soul-binding in it at all. It also opens a door that was nailed shut on purpose. Worth a deliberate decision about how far that door swings.

It is a beacon, not an anchor, and [[session_4|Session 4]] settled exactly how good a beacon: it gives him the plane, instantly, and nothing else. He still has to cross and then scry. See [[what_follows_vrenn|What Follows Vrenn]] — the three shapes are resolved and it is slow.

What the party have not been told about the first evening (moved out of the player-facing text 2026-10-06)

He knew Vrenn had left the plane within a minute of it happening, and he spoke. The Token told him within a minute of the crossing. All the party saw was Vrenn on his knees holding his head for twenty seconds — it was a sending, and they were never told. Morvek's first act on losing his property was not to hunt it. It was to talk to it. Partly spent in [[session_5|Session 5]]: Vrenn told them there was a message and that it said he was coming. That it was a sending, and that Vrenn could have answered and did not, is still untold.

The fork he is missing is the one the party took — "which the party unknowingly removed from his basement", as the list above used to put it. They have not joined that up, and it is being saved. And on Vrenn's "three or four if he has other priorities": he has other things to answer for. He has plenty to answer for.

His arrogance, which is the usable flaw — and it has now cost him days

He taught a captive plane shift and handed him a 250 gp steel fork, because he was certain the Token made it irrelevant. That certainty is the crack in him. The party has walked off with the fork and Vrenn stripped the components out of his basement, and both of those happened because he was sure they could not matter.

AND THE FORK IS NOW THE PLOT. Vrenn's read (2026-09-24) is that there is not another one left in that basement, so the lich who wanted goods going to the Material Plane cannot presently get there himself. He has to source a new steel fork before he can begin. He gave away the key to his own front door and the people he gave it to took it with them. That is the arrogance paying off exactly, and neither the party nor Morvek has said it out loud. See [[steel_tuning_fork|the fork]].
