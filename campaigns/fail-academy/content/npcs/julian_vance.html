Overview

Julian Vance is a wealthy, genuinely gifted Artificer prodigy who has never had to think about where his materials come from — which is more or less the entire problem. He wastes pristine magical components without noticing, discards anything with so much as a dent, and treats the Academy's martial departments with an open, unembarrassed contempt he'd never extend to a fellow Artificer. He is, by any objective measure, talented. He has also never once had to make something work with what was actually in front of him.

Guntrah

Julian doesn't know [[guntrah|Guntrah]] is an artificer at all — as far as he's concerned, Guntrah is Barbarians Department muscle who occasionally wanders too close to the forge. What Julian throws away, [[prof_tavel|Professor Tavel]] quietly collects for Guntrah's real education, which means Julian has been unknowingly outfitting his own rival for years. At this year's FAIL Period practical trial, Guntrah's scrap-built gear outperformed Julian's expensive kit in front of faculty and students both — Julian has an explanation for why that doesn't count, and will offer it whether or not anyone asks.

DM Only

Julian has no idea how deep the rivalry actually runs, or that his own discards are the reason Guntrah's gear works as well as it does. If that ever comes out — to Julian, or to Holdar — it plays either way: humiliation for Julian, or the moment Guntrah's cover finally cracks.
