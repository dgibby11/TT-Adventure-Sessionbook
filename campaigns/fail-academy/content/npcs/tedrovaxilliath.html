DM Only — the fact sheet and framing as written (moved out of the player-facing text 2026-10-06)

  • What: adult green dragon — six or seven hundred years old. Deep into adult, not ancient, and close enough to the line to be thinking about it.
  • Where: all of it. He does not recognise any other claim.
  • Mark: the wing is old, badly healed, and permanent.
  • Disposition: paranoid, and correct to be. Not hostile. Will not be lied to twice. Transactional to the letter.
  • Met: a deal was struck and both sides kept it. Session 5 has him go back on it; see [[session_5|Session 5]].

He is the apex predator of a forest he maintains, and he does not consider those two things to be in tension.

On his own lines, below: the whole of the relationship is in them, and they are worth keeping together because the party's feelings about him have already drifted a long way from them. The limits are ones he volunteered and the party have not absorbed.

On [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]]: he did not want him. [[tito|Tito]] lied to a 30 that the drow was their prisoner, and by morning the price was labour instead. He asked about him once and never again.

On the Academy: that he knew nothing is exactly right and is worth protecting. He is a local expert on one forest.

He spoke before he showed himself — a deep, almost snarling voice out of the trees, asking who had sent them — and then put a very large green head through the thicket.

The Name — as it actually went

[[guntrah|Guntrah]] asked him for it politely — "Mr. Dragon? What's your name, sir?" — and he gave the whole thing with ceremony. Asked whether he had a shorter name, he supplied two, unprompted:

[[session_4|Session 4]], as played

"Tedrovaxilliath."

"Do you have a shorter name?"

"Thax. You can call me Ted."

DM Only — what the name did to the design

Nobody ever shortened it at him, so the designed humiliation is dead and cannot be recovered. The authored beat was that a player would say "Ted" and he would go silent for a moment and then magnanimously allow it, and remember. Instead he handed it over himself, in answer to a courteous question, on the first night. He chose "Ted", which means there is nothing to resent and nothing owed.

Consequence, and it runs through everything below: he is materially warmer than he was designed to be. Not soft — see the limits, which he stated himself and stated flatly — but a creature who names himself to a polite stranger, throws a whole tree into a clearing because somebody asked nicely for firewood, allows a paladin to put hands on his ruined wing, grants a second night's rest for the asking and lends out an escort. Play the warmth. Do not play it as affection.

DM Only — what he cares about

What He Cares About

The Wood, and every living thing in it that is not a person. The herds, the watercourses, the health of the canopy, the balance of predator and prey. He knows every stream in his range by a name he assigned it.

This is husbandry, not kindness. He maintains the forest because a healthy forest feeds him for another eight hundred years. People sit outside that system — he will talk to them, bargain with them, and eat them, and none of those are in tension for him either.

What He Said, and What He Refused — [[session_4|Session 4]]

His own words, kept together.

The deal

"I will give you free passage to leave this wood wherever you choose, but you can keep your drow. I need you to take care of a nuisance of mine."

"To the north, about six miles, at the edge of my domain, there is a crack in the ground. There are two beasts — monsters, I don't know what they are — scavenging in my forest and retreating into their cave. I need them eliminated."

Why didn't you do it? — "Because I can't fit in the hole." Poison breath? — "I have. Once." How deep does it go? — "I wouldn't know. Anything that I send in there gets eaten."

The limits, which he volunteered

"I don't have patience for pleasantries." (to "Morning, Ted")

"I have ears and eyes all through the forest." (on whether he needs proof)

"I owe nothing to you except to allow you to leave my forest alive." (asked for directions to a town, after the job was done)

"There are towns all around this wood. They invade my space and I eat them."

If we're in trouble, will you help us? — "Probably not."

You will not be here that long. (to Guntrah offering to come back and repair his wing) — and then: "If you want to be in my good graces, you will do what I've asked you."

He never asked for [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]]. He said "that one I cannot trust" on the first night, [[tito|Tito]] told him the drow was their prisoner on a Deception of 30, and by morning the price was labour.

He was asked directly about the Academy and knew nothing — "I know nothing of this academy you speak of."

DM Only — the three things he did that were not in the design, and they are the character now

  1. The tree. Guntrah asked, politely and with an explicit escape hatch ("it's okay if it's too much"), whether he could gather firewood. There was no verbal answer. A whole tree came out of the woods and landed next to him. Theatrical, generous, enormously superior, and not a favour he would ever discuss. This is his register for saying yes.
  2. He sent them to bed. He opened the negotiation before the party could rest, looked at [[silas|Silas]] standing on a handful of hit points, and said "first of all, you need some rest — I'll be here when you wake up," and withdrew. He did the threat and the courtesy in one move and got a better deal in the morning for it.
  3. He let a paladin touch the wing. [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] asked consent, and Ted hesitated, did not back away, and tracked him with his face the whole time — if you do something, I will eat you. Then allowed it. It did nothing (a decade-old scar is not a wound and hit points do not touch it), but he permitted it, which is the most he has ever permitted anybody.

What that adds up to: he rewards courtesy and he charges for everything. Guntrah's politeness bought a name, firewood, a guide, honest answers about the cave and a second night's rest — and bought nothing he considered a debt. The moment the job was done and Guntrah tried to convert goodwill into directions, he was told precisely what he owed. Hold that line. The party will keep testing it, because they like him.

THE PARTY WANT HIM IN THE PARTY. More than one player said so sincerely at the end of the session. Do not soften him toward that — the gap between what they feel and what he has said out loud is the best thing Session 4 produced, and it is the thing that will hurt if they ever rely on him.

GUNTRAH OWES HIM A WING BRACE. He offered to build a mesh or brace, failed Persuasion on a 6, nearly gave offence by flying up to demonstrate his competence ("Are you taunting me?"), and got "I'll consider it." He is an artificer with Spell-Storing Item and a Steel Defender. If he ever comes back with the thing actually built, that is not a favour, it is a relationship — and it goes straight at the blind spot, because in six hundred years nobody has ever tried to repair Ted.

The Wing

[[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] spotted it on the second morning and nudged [[tito|Tito]]: a tear through the membrane of one wing, long since scarred over. It looks usable, and it does not look like something he enjoys using. The DM's aside at the table: it might have something to do with why he asked strangers to deal with his nuisance for him.

  • [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] asked his consent and laid hands on it. He allowed it, watching the whole time. It did nothing: it is a scar, not a wound.
  • [[guntrah|Guntrah]] offered to build him a brace if they ever come back through. "You will not be here that long." And then: "I'll consider it."

He has never said how it happened.

DM Only — THE SCAR, AND THE TREANT. Unspent: the party do not know there is a story

The Scar, and the Treant

He has not told this story yet. Vrenn spotted the scarred wing on a Perception of 24 and nudged Tito; the party all saw it; two of them tried to do something about it; and Ted volunteered nothing at all. The party know he is damaged and have no idea how. [[the_standing_treant|The treant]] is completely unspent.

When it does come out, he tells it readily, because it is not an embarrassment. He was not beaten and he did not overreach; as far as he is concerned he was attacked for existing.

His account, and he believes every word

"A tree came to kill me. You may take a moment with that."

"It had decided I was a disease. Because the ground near where I sleep has gone sour — and it has, I do not deny it, perhaps a hundred yards of it. It stood in eight hundred square miles of forest that I have kept in order for six centuries, counted a hundred yards of bad thorn, and concluded that I was the thing wrong with it."

"I keep the herds from starving in the fourth year. I keep the water moving. I have killed three things in this wood that would have hollowed it out entirely and none of them are in anybody's songs." (a pause) "It got a wing. I got everything else. It has not moved in ten years."

Both of them were right, and that is the point. His blind spot is that the corruption is real and it is his, and he cannot see it because he measures the Wood by the health of its game. A player who names that out loud has genuinely got somewhere with him — further than any Persuasion roll.

DM Only — playing him

Playing Him

  • Cultured, dry, and far too quiet for his size. He does not roar. He has not needed to in centuries.
  • Paranoid, and justified. Five armed strangers and a drow materialised out of a portal in the middle of his forest stinking of the Abyss. Any sensible creature treats that as a probe, and he says so.
  • He asks questions designed to catch a lie, lets the conversation move on, then circles back to the same ones later to see whether the answers match.
  • He listens to reason. He is careful, not mad, and he is not hunting for an excuse. A straight answer given the same way twice actually moves him.
  • He is vain about the forest, and it is the one subject on which he stops interrogating and starts talking.

DM Only — the scene, and the one thing it turns on

HOW THE NEGOTIATION ACTUALLY WENT (2026-09-24) — the designed centrepiece never happened

The party never tried the truth, so he never got the chance to disbelieve it. The authored beat was that "we arrived here by accident" is true and sounds exactly like a lie, and that the answer is consistency rather than [[tito|Tito]]'s Deception +12. Tito simply used the Deception. Twice, enormously, and it worked both times:

  • "We belong here" — 26. A genuine double meaning (Faerûn, not the forest), which is why the DM offered advantage and then withdrew it once Tito committed to meaning the forest. Ted's reply was the odd, unexplained "I think you're mistaken. You belong to the West" — which nobody understood and nobody followed up.
  • "He is our prisoner" — 30. The one that mattered, and it reversed the price: Ted had just said "that one I cannot trust", and by morning he wanted labour instead of the drow.
  • A natural 1 on a follow-up Persuasion ("we are but passing through, to the southwest, with no harm intended") which did not go over — and would have succeeded on his modifier alone. See the rulings in [[session_4|the session file]].

SO THE SEAM IS STILL THERE, AND IT IS NOW WORTH MORE. He has been told the party are locals passing through and that a Wizard 13 drow is their prisoner. Both are false, he is a creature who circles back to old answers to check them, and he has [[teds_fox|animals everywhere]]. He is owed a scene where he finds out. Do not waste it by having him shrug.

What did move him, in the order it happened: that they wanted to leave · [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] reading as somebody's property rather than anyone's spearhead · [[guntrah|Guntrah]] being consistently, disarmingly polite, which was not on the list and turned out to be the single most effective thing anybody did · [[silas|Silas]] visibly being one hit from dead, which bought them the night.

Still true and still unspent: he is vain about the forest and stops interrogating the moment somebody asks him about it — nobody asked. And somebody noticing the wing without making it an insult moves him; two people managed it, and both of them then failed to do anything with it.

He already knows more than he should, and this is how. Authored as the silent spies — a clearing of animals holding still and watching. That was not what got played (2026-09-24): he simply told them, flatly, "I have ears and eyes all through the forest," and then [[teds_fox|a fox]] did the whole job in person — guided the party six miles, sat outside the cave, followed them in and watched the entire fight from cover, and reported back telepathically. The fox is much better than the set-piece and should be the standing mechanism. Assume he knows anything done in front of an animal.

Stat block: adult green dragon, CR 15, flexing to CR 22 if a fight ever happens. It should not, and after Session 4 it is much less likely to — the party ended the session wanting him to travel with them. Never printed or described in game terms at the table: he was given as "not particularly old, maybe in that adult-to-ancient range, hard to say exactly" under a dark canopy, which is the right amount. Describe him so that attacking reads as signing a death warrant. If it goes wrong anyway, the scene is a flight, not a fight — the same rule as the caged thing in Session 3.

The wing, which he has still never explained — and he was asked (2026-09-24): the damage is permanent and it means reduced range. He does not patrol as wide as he used to, so his effective territory has shrunk — which is why two [[fomorians|fomorians]] eating his game is an existential problem rather than an irritation, and why he is reduced to asking strangers to go into a hole for him. A whole, younger dragon would have handled it. Do not state this. Let them infer it from the scar. Note that at the table he stated the operative half outright — "because I can't fit in the hole" — so the party have his reason without having the wing's reason, which is the better split anyway.

THE TREANT IS ALIVE. He believes it dead because it has not moved in ten years, and treants can stand still for decades. He is wrong, he has no idea he is wrong, and it is standing somewhere in his forest with an opinion about him. See [[the_standing_treant|the treant]].

THEY MAY COME FOR HIM — DID NOT HAPPEN, STILL PREPPED. The [[fomorians|fomorians]] are dead and the party never spoke a word to them, so no language key was ever needed. The branch is intact and now much more expensive to reach, because the party finished the session liking him and asking whether he could travel with them. If it ever does open it will be about the corruption or the [[the_standing_treant|treant]], not the giants. The original reasoning held — and a defensible one, since he threatened them inside thirty seconds, eats people, and is demonstrably poisoning his own forest. Do not run that fight straight at CR 15. The CR 15–22 licence runs downward too; a beaten-off dragon with a ruined wing is a far better outcome than a dead party. Full branch, and the much better information-based version of it, in [[session_4|the session file]].

What he does not know and does not care about: mortal politics, the Academy, the Ninth Thesis, who rules Neverwinter now, anything about the Abyss beyond "it smells and I want it out of my wood." Do not use him as an exposition dump for the main plot. He is a local expert on one forest, and that is exactly what the party needs.

DM Only — THE REWARD. ENTIRELY UNSPENT — HE GAVE THEM NOTHING (played 2026-09-24)

THE JOB WAS DONE AND NO ITEM CHANGED HANDS. The party killed both [[fomorians|fomorians]], came back, and took passage, one direction, a [[teds_fox|guide]] and a second night's rest — which is option 5 on the list below and nothing else. Nobody asked for anything. [[tito|Tito]] opened with "that offer is not enough", was warned that dragons get harder to move the more you ask, and wisely rewrote the ask as a renegotiation clause instead of a price. Then the fight went so cleanly that by the time they got back the mood was gratitude rather than invoicing.

THE CONSEQUENCE IS THE LARGEST OPEN ITEM IN THE CAMPAIGN: the party is level 11, carrying 80 gp, and has no healing potions at all. That has now been true since Commencement. Either the potions arrive from somewhere else, or it becomes a pressure the next session honours deliberately. Decide; do not let it drift.

Everything below is still his, still available, and can be offered later — he has a standing unpaid offer of a wing brace from [[guntrah|Guntrah]] to trade against, which is a far better occasion for the potions than the job was.

A dragon values size, permanence, beauty and leverage. Anything consumable, drab, or sized for small folk is beneath his notice — which happily covers most of what this party actually needs.

  1. A rotted adventurer's pack, still sealed at the neck — four potions. Two healing, one greater healing, one unlabelled. His line: "They spoil. They are of no use to me." NOT GIVEN IN SESSION 4, and the party still has none — a hoarder's contempt for consumables is exactly why he would hand them over without a second thought, so this stays the cheapest available fix for the problem the moment there is an occasion. The unpaid wing brace is a better occasion than the job was.
  2. A bag of holding. Contemptible to him because it is a sack. No lustre, no size, no presence. "A sack. You understand. A sack." Funny, useful, and pointed squarely at [[guntrah|Guntrah]], who owns no backpack of any kind and has been using [[ollie|Ollie]] as one since Session 3. Still true at level 11.
  3. A cloak of elvenkind. Drab grey-green — to him, the colour of nothing. Answers the party's worst mechanical weakness, which is that their stealth is dreadful.
  4. An elven blade out of the ruins, a +1 weapon, which he considers a toy: too small to admire, too plain to be beautiful.
  5. Safe passage, directions, and the lie of the land. THIS IS THE ONLY ONE HE ACTUALLY PAID (2026-09-24) — and he paid the thinnest possible version of it: free passage out of the wood wherever they chose, confirmation that there is a town to the south-west, and a [[teds_fox|fox]] to walk them there. He refused to volunteer anything else ("I owe nothing to you except to allow you to leave my forest alive"), and was never asked what year it is, which water is safe, or what lies in the other three directions. All of that is still on the table and still costs him nothing. Very green of him.

FLAGGED, not recommended — a scroll of sending. Consumable, so worthless to him, and it would put twenty-five words in front of [[ellery_voss|Voss]], answering "find a way to contact the Academy" in a single object. That is a plot lever, not loot: it short-circuits a problem the arc may want them to carry, and it steps on [[anselm_ferreck|Ferreck]]'s scene as the lifeline. Deliberate or not at all.

What he will not part with: the records of who has been sent after him · the maps of the Wood · the few beautiful things · and any hint about his lair, including — pointedly — the state of the water upstream, which he will change the subject away from.
