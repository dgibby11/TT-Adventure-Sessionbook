What
A company logging camp — about thirty people under canvas, with tents, tools and carts
Where
Just beside the ruins of Thundertree, at the western edge of [[neverwinter_wood|Neverwinter Wood]]. South-west of the arrival clearing; see [[neverwinter_wood_map|the regional map]]
Status
Unvisited. The party has seen its smoke from above the canopy and nothing else
Good for
Supper, a tent, plain supplies, directions and small talk. A pass-through, with no secrets in it

DM Only — A STUB FOR THE DASHBOARD. The camp is still being planned.

This entity exists so that the dashboard has somewhere to stand when the party walks in: its Environment and Curiosities panels are filled, and the party can be moved here. It is deliberately not a second copy of the plan.

  • The working detail — who is there, what they sell, what they can answer, the small talk and the contingencies — is in planning/session_5.md, under "The logging camp", and was still changing on the day this was written (2026-10-05). Bring it across when Session 5 is promoted.
  • The settled history — that the camp used to stand in the wood proper, why it withdrew, and why anybody works this close to [[tedrovaxilliath|the dragon]] — is recorded on [[neverwinter_wood|Neverwinter Wood]].
  • The name is not settled. "Kettle Bend" is a proposal for what the crew call it. The id here is logging_camp so that the name can change without breaking a link.
  • How far it is, is open — fifteen miles by the DM's figure, about twice that by the map.
  • The whole entity is dm-only until it has been played.

A workplace under canvas, not a village: a dozen tents in two rows, a cook tent, an ox line, and fir logs being bound into loads on the bank. A burn pile is kept going day and night to clear brush, and that is the smoke [[guntrah|Guntrah]] saw.
