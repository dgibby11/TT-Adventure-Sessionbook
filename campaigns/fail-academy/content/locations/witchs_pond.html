What
A small, very swampy pond in [[neverwinter_wood|Neverwinter Wood]], ringed with thorny brush — and something was built here once
Where
Down the side path [[tedrovaxilliath|Ted]] took when he left the party on the walk north. Roughly thirty minutes off the line between the arrival clearing and [[underdark_breach|the Breach]]
Found
[[session_4|Session 4]], on the walk back. The fox led them to it
Used as
Where [[tedrovaxilliath|Ted]] chose to wait for them, and where the deal was settled
Not
His lair. That is [[teds_lair|the sinkhole]], somewhere else entirely

The fox brought them here at about four in the afternoon on the second day, and the dragon was waiting. A pond too swampy to be pleasant, a great deal of thorny brush, and the general impression that it is not quite a natural place.

As described at the table

"You're not exactly sure. It looks like maybe there's something slightly unnatural about this place — like maybe it used to be a witch's hut or something. I don't know, because there's some straight lines that look like there might have been something man-made here at some point."

Out of old timber.

This is where [[tedrovaxilliath|Ted]] took the fox's report, accepted that the job was done, and told the party exactly what the relationship was: "I owe nothing to you except to allow you to leave my forest alive." He granted them a guide and one more night's rest, and sent them back to the clearing.

DM Only — an unclaimed seed, offered by the DM in the moment

Nothing is decided about this place and nothing needs to be. The DM invented it on the spot as the dragon's waiting-ground and then flagged the straight lines unprompted, which makes it a deliberate loose end rather than scenery. It is entirely unconnected to anything in the campaign as of the end of Session 4.

What it already has going for it, if it is ever wanted:

  • Ted chose it. A green dragon with eight hundred square miles to stand in picked this spot to conduct business in, twice. That is either meaningless or it is not, and he would not explain either way.
  • It is inside his radius and it is not his lair, which makes it the only place in the Wood the party have been invited to twice.
  • Worked timber that has outlasted a century of a green dragon's corruption is a small oddity on its own. Most things here do not last.
  • It is thirty minutes off the route to [[underdark_breach|the Breach]], so the party can find their way back to it without being led.

Deliberately NOT connected to [[teds_lair|the sinkhole]] or to the upstream corruption gradient. Do not quietly turn this into the lair or into a signpost toward it — the lair is found by following the water, and that route is still unspent.
