DM Only — AFTER SESSION 5 (played 2026-10-06): HE IS DEAD, AND THIS IS LYING OPEN

The party killed [[tedrovaxilliath|Ted]] in the arrival clearing and have not looked for this. They do not know it exists as a place. What they have is what the walk north gave them in Session 4: the growth is worst nearest to wherever he lives.

Everything written below is now unowned: the hoard, the reward table that was never paid, the maps, and the records of who has been sent to kill him. It is the natural home for the healing potions this party has gone five sessions without.

Three things to settle before anybody goes looking:

  • Does the trail last? The mist left the clearing ten minutes after he died. If the corruption fades across the Wood, the only route they were given fades with it.
  • The way in is under water, and he never said so. [[guntrah|Guntrah]] has a Ring of Swimming.
  • Who else would come for it. A logging company once asked in Neverwinter what it would cost to be rid of him, and [[teds_fox|a fox]] knows the way home.

DM Only — WHERE IT STANDS AFTER SESSION 4 (played 2026-09-24)

The party never came near it, never asked about it, and never asked about anything in the hoard. [[tito|Tito]] spent the session hunting for treasure and got as far as asking a [[teds_fox|fox]] where its master keeps it, which was the funniest thing in the session and produced nothing. Everything below is intact.

But the route in is now half-given. The vegetation arm of the gradient was described in full on the walk north — twisted growth, thorn, vines, nothing growing straight — including the giveaway: it weakens the further you get from his lair. That is the same sentence as it is worst at the lair, said out loud, to a party with an artificer who can fly. The water arm was not given: they crossed a stream and nobody looked at it.

So they can find this place by walking uphill into the worst growth, and it has not occurred to them. Do not point at it. If they ever do come, they arrive already knowing he has been lying to himself, which remains the best way to open a second conversation with him.

He did volunteer one thing about his own range: six miles, and that he controls the critters and the growth throughout it. He said nothing whatsoever about where he sleeps.

DM Only — where it is

A collapsed limestone sinkhole, deep in [[neverwinter_wood|the Wood]], well away from both the arrival clearing and [[underdark_breach|the Breach]]. From above it reads as nothing at all: an unremarkable tangle of thorn and vine a hundred yards across that nothing walks through.

The entrance is under water. A feeder channel of the same creek the party can hear from the clearing runs beneath the thorn-mass and opens into a drowned antechamber. Ted goes in submerged, and has to fold the bad wing to do it. He hates that, and it is part of why he flies less than he should.

DM Only — how it can be found without a map

Follow the water upstream and the corruption inward. This is [[the_standing_treant|the treant]]'s entire grievance made physical, and it is a far better route to a dragon's lair than a map:

  • The creek by the arrival clearing runs out of his territory and is already slightly wrong — a green cast, a taste, fewer things living in it than there should be.
  • Walk it up and it degrades: silt, then colour, then a smell, then nothing alive in it at all.
  • The growth changes alongside — thicker, thornier, more twisted, fewer species, until it is a single species of thorn and nothing else.
  • The deadest, worst ground is directly over the sinkhole.

Any druid, ranger or attentive traveller can read that gradient. It also means that if the party ever go looking, they arrive already knowing he has been lying to himself — which is a very strong way to open a second conversation with him.

DM Only — the hoard

Not a heap of gold. A green's hoard is half-drowned, silted and grown over.

  • The floor of the main chamber is a shallow standing pool, and most of the coinage is under it — silted in, corroded, much of it fused into masses that will never be counted. Centuries of caravan takings off the High Road and the Triboar Trail.
  • Trophies sit on dry ledges above the waterline, arranged with care. This is the part he thinks of as the hoard.
  • Three sources, and he can tell you which is which: overland trade; the elven ruins the Wood has swallowed; and debris scoured out of Neverwinter when Mount Hotenow erupted — a quiet tie to one of the four directions if it ever matters.
  • Roots have come through the ceiling and grown down into it. He has never cut them. The lair is slowly being eaten by the forest he maintains, and he does not appear to have noticed the irony.

What he actually values, and it is not the gold. He is a green: he hoards leverage.

  • Maps and records of the Wood, some centuries old, several of his own making. Every watercourse in his range is named — names he assigned.
  • Anything identifying who sent an intruder. He keeps the sigils, letters and commissions found on people who came to kill him, long after discarding their gear. He has a record of every party ever sent, and by whom.
  • A small number of beautiful things he simply likes and will not discuss.

None of that is on the reward table, and if the party ask for the records specifically, he becomes extremely interested in why. See [[tedrovaxilliath|his entity]] for what he will part with.
