Location
Central campus — the junction of the three main campus paths
Depicts
All three of the Academy's original founders together — [[aldric_voss|Aldric Voss]], [[isolde_orath|Isolde Orath]], and [[brennan_ashcroft|Brennan Ashcroft]]
Age
Older than the plinth it stands on by at least a decade; predates the official dedication

Physical Description

Three tall stone figures share a single raised plinth, carved in a style that predates the surrounding architecture by several generations. The stone is worn smooth — features slightly indistinct in a way that is more erosion than time should fully account for. The three stand close together, arms at their sides, all of them looking east. The dedication plaque at the base is from Year 7 and names them only as the Founders, without saying which figure is which. Which is which has been the subject of occasional academic interest. Nothing conclusive has been published.

Campus Significance

The group sits where it does because it marks the geometric center of the original campus layout — or near enough to it that the discrepancy is generally attributed to survey error. Students walk past it dozens of times a week without stopping. It is not prominent in marketing materials for the Academy. It is, however, where Commencement is held every year, which is the one day a year anybody looks at it directly.

DM Only

Ritual significance (not player-facing): The statue is prominent in the [[ninth_thesis|Ninth Thesis]]'s ritual geography — long mistaken by campus rumor for something to do with the [[secret_society|"Secret Society"]] legend, which is a different, unrelated confusion entirely. The current party knows none of this. Note that [[session_2|Session 2]]'s graduation ceremony is staged here deliberately, as a quiet authorial echo — nothing in that scene should surface this connection at the table.

The statue was not built for this plinth. It arrived in Year 7 from somewhere else on campus — or somewhere off campus. The founding records for Year 7 contain a budget entry for "statue installation and preparation" but no origin listed. The plinth was constructed specifically to receive it.

The three sets of initials carved into the back of the plinth (A.V., I.O., B.A.) match the three figures above them, in order left to right. They predate the plaque. Someone carved them before the statue arrived — or while it was being prepared for installation. The initials are not visible from the front; you have to go behind the plinth to find them.

The abjuration aura is not a protective ward. It is a recording anchor — a form of magical notation that captures and holds the nature of significant events that occur near it. The anchor has been active for as long as any magical survey has examined it. Whatever it has recorded, it is not accessible through any method currently in the Wizards Department library.

Ritual use: The [[ninth_thesis|Ninth Thesis]] rituals conducted here during [[one_shot_2]] disturbed something in the anchor. Players who are magically sensitive may begin detecting a residual impression — fragments of something witnessed, not a message but an accumulation.
