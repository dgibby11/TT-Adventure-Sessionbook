Older than the camp and older than the ruin: packed earth over cobbles nobody has reset in fifty years, wide enough for a cart. The Wood falls behind as a dark line, and ahead there is open country and a very great deal of sky.

DM Only — UNPLAYED. A stub for [[session_5|Session 5]], built 2026-10-06.

This entity exists so that the dashboard has somewhere to stand for the last beat of Session 5: the ambush by three elite drow assassins, and [[tac_credit_note|the credit note]] found afterwards. The beat itself is on [[session_5|the session page]].

  • The road is a proposal. The DM's canon is only that the party leaves the camp for Neverwinter and is ambushed on the way.
  • It is open sky from here on. Drow fight badly in sunlight, so the ambush comes at dusk, at dawn, or in shade.
  • How long it is: about a day and a half on foot from Thundertree to Neverwinter by [[neverwinter_wood_map|the regional map]]. Session 5 is not meant to reach the city.
  • The barges use the river, not the road.
