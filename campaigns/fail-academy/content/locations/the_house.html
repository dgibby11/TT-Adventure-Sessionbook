[[morvek|Morvek]]'s lodgings — and the second place on this layer where planar travel works
Where
About an hour's walk east of [[ritual_platform|the ritual platform]], across open ash
Entered
[[session_3|Session 3]], through the back door, on a lock [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] picked
Left
Through the floor. Two dead guards behind them and something screaming in a cage

Vrenn called it a house all evening. It is not a house. It is an outpost, or a very small fortress — squat, black stone, built low and defensive against a plain that plainly tries to take things down. Two floors, perhaps a dozen rooms, cracked all over, and not really livable any more. No watchtower. No lights. Nobody looking out. From the side they approached, a flat wall with no windows at all.

It does not belong to Morvek. It belongs to a minor demon who was away and was due back the following day to collect his goods; Morvek is a tenant in it. Vrenn has been inside a handful of times, always with Morvek, always told exactly what to do, and was never permitted in the basement.

What the Party Saw

  • Cracks in the masonry big enough for the smaller ones to squeeze through. Pointed out, never used.
  • A back door and a front door. The back one has an ordinary lock and nothing magical on it — [[guntrah|Guntrah]] checked with Arcana. The front was never approached.
  • The ground floor: a back hallway, something that barely qualifies as a kitchen, a couple of shut doors, and a rotting iron-banded door with a lock on it that goes down.
  • Skeleton servants. One was seen, carrying something down a hall on an errand it had been given. It did not look at anybody. Vrenn's briefing: they wander, they do enchanted chores, and they do not attack.
  • Upstairs: [[morvek|Morvek]]'s room. Locked, and warded with something arcane on top of the lock. Vrenn's advice was don't dare try to go in there, and nobody did.
  • The basement is one room under the whole building — fifty to sixty feet across, seventy to eighty long, a fifteen-foot ceiling on four pillars, torchlit, and just as crumbled as everything above it.

The Basement

  • The circle. Fifteen to twenty feet across in the middle of the floor, inlaid rather than carved, worked in a material that is not the stone — and faintly, steadily glowing. It is the same family of thing as the dead circle on [[ritual_platform|the platform]], and this one is alive.
  • Four small altars with bowls, set out near the pillars at the corners. These are the switch: fill all four and the circle will fire.
  • Two workbenches, with two figures at them in tattered clothes, hunched over exactly the way Vrenn was when the party first found him. They were not people. See [[basement_oni|the two at the benches]].
  • A grate in the back wall beneath a mounted skull, badly lit, with nothing but blackness behind it — and something in that blackness that snarls, and that got more agitated every time somebody died in that room. See [[behind_the_grate|What Is Behind the Grate]].

The party fought in it, won, charged the four bowls under fire, and left through the floor with about one round to spare. The building is still standing, and they closed nothing behind them.

DM Only — naming

Filed in the design documents as the Bonehold. That word must not be spoken at the table. Vrenn calls it "the house" and so did everybody else for the whole of [[session_3|Session 3]]; the flatness is the characterisation and it costs the players nothing. The name budget survived the session intact and this is one of the reasons why.

DM Only — the landlord

A minor demon lord, unnamed and never seen, in fealty to Orcus. [[morvek|Morvek]] is billeted on him by Orcus's writ carried down through Harthoon and cannot be refused. He is roughly Morvek's equal, slightly under, which is what makes it a humiliation rather than an arrangement — and he is lateral, not a rung in the chain of command (DM, 2026-08-31).

He dislikes his tenant, and that is a deliberate asset. He would be pleased if something inconvenient happened to Morvek — not pleased enough to act, but enough to look away. Flagged as a future diplomatic option, and considerably more interesting now that the party has burgled his house, killed two of his tenant's guards, and woken his pet.

Three things he explains for free: the building's split personality (a demon's hall with a lich's working basement fitted badly into it); why the basement guards were Morvek's problem to place in somebody else's house; and why the thing behind the grate is the landlord's insurance, kept next to Morvek's valuables, which Morvek quietly resents.

He can still be cut with no loss — a skull is Orcus's own symbol, so the crest over the grate needs no landlord to explain it.

DM Only — why the circle works here

Planar travel only works where the boundary is thin (DM, 2026-09-02), which is why [[ritual_platform|the ritual site]] was built where it was. The house manages it a different way: amplification glyphs worked into the floor around the circle, riding the building's own arcane infrastructure. Not a thin place — a machine that makes one.

[[silas|Silas]] worked this out himself on a natural 20 in Session 3, which is a much better delivery than Vrenn explaining it, and it is now player knowledge in the party's own words.

Ruled planar and NOT dispellable — treat it as a permanent anchor rather than a spell effect. They can use it and they cannot aim it, the same limitation as Vrenn's scroll and for the same reason: it is keyed to where this operation ships.

It is still there and still working. Vrenn stripped most of the loose components out of the room on his way out, deliberately, to slow anything following — but he does not know what else is in that basement and said so. Reopening this lane is a real option for the party later, and a real risk.

DM Only — what is still in there, unspent

The party went in the back door and straight down the stairs. Everything designed for the ground floor was bypassed and is intact:

  • A glyph of warding alarm on the front door and a shriek-ward at the head of the basement stair. Never found, never tripped, both still armed. Both 3rd level, so both dispel automatically once found.
  • Morvek's door. A very sophisticated mechanical lock plus a ward. Key the ward to the door OPENING by any means, not to lock-tampering — [[silas|Silas]] has knock, which declares any lock unlocked with no check at all. Recommended ward: damage plus a mark that lets Morvek find whoever opened it. Nothing behind it is required.
  • The primed goods and the consignment record. Half-finished corrupted items in the style of the ones planted around the Academy, and a manifest showing shipments to more than one Material Plane destination. This clue has now failed to land twice. Do not site it in a third room — Vrenn made the contents and is travelling with the party, so put it in his mouth.
  • Opening a sealed crate detonates it (DM, 2026-09-01), roughly 8d6–10d6 in 20 ft, Dexterity save for half. The real cost is the noise, not the dice. It punishes looting, never investigation.
  • The three skeletons, the intake hall, the gaps, the weighing table. Unspent.
  • The printed battle map was only partly used. The embalming slab, the blood pools, the west drainage grates, the bone benches, and the empty unmarked crate matching the one from the Provisions Annex were never described.
