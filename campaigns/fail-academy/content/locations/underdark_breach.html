The approach, as described at the table

Past a stream — more than a stream, nearly a river, and the place the party topped off empty waterskins — the ground rises into an incline, still well inside the forest.

It is not a crack. It is almost a full-blown cave — a large opening perhaps fifteen to twenty feet high, coming to a point at the top. Far too tight a squeeze for a dragon. Easy for something slightly smaller than one.

There is a decent number of bones on the ground outside, and you can see far enough in to know there are more inside, along with reddish splatter on the stone. And high up, where the opening narrows toward its point, the rock has been scraped — not a lot, but noticeably — by something that has been going in and out and grinding along it.

The Layout — as played

  • The descent. About forty feet down a narrowish passage, roughly ten feet wide with a fifteen-to-twenty-foot ceiling. Steep enough that climbing back out takes a moment.
  • The cavern. Very large, forty to fifty foot ceilings, with an underground river running through it — the ground slopes down, so the water is only just subterranean. Glowing mushrooms in pockets. Stalactites above and stalagmites everywhere below, some of them ten feet high and impossible simply to step over.
  • The throat at the back. The rear of the cavern goes deeper still, through a narrow gap about as tight as the way in. This is what Ted does not fit into, and it is where the noises came from.

This is the mouth of the Underdark proper, and [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] knew it on sight.

[[session_4|Session 4]] — Vrenn recognises it

"Stop — this actually seems really familiar. I think this is where I— oh my God, it's the same. It's the same cave."

Almost ten years ago, coming up from a hundred miles of Underdark, fleeing Menzoberranzan. And, flatly: "I can assure you that there were no creatures guarding the exit to that cave. I just ran right in."

What Happened Here

The party went in invisible — [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] upcast it at 7th level over all five — behind a spectral warhorse with light cast on it, sent in as scout and lamp. The horse kicked a stalagmite to raise an echo, the echo went everywhere, and the first fomorian came in swinging and destroyed it. [[guntrah|Guntrah]]'s readied web pinned it; the second arrived a round later. Both died in two rounds, and the only damage any player character took was 13 psychic to [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]]. Full account in [[session_4|the session file]].

  • Eighty gold pieces total, between the two of them. Nothing else on the bodies.
  • No treasure anywhere in the cavern. Searched on a 17 with the Help action.
  • [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] cut a left ear off each as proof, then dropped them, because Ted had already said he would know.
  • [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] came out of it cursed — a fomorian's Evil Eye, which he failed even with a paladin's aura and an artificer's Flash of Genius stacked onto the save.

DM Only — THE PREP WAS NOT USED. This entity is now the played version.

Placement: the northern edge of his range, deliberately far from his lair. See [[neverwinter_wood_map|the regional map]].

Struck: the fallen forest giant and its two-storey root-plate; the twenty-foot scramble; the sixty-foot low fouled antechamber with a ceiling running from fifteen feet down to eight; the body-wide fissure at the back. None of it was described at the table and the played cave is bigger, wetter and much more impressive — a genuine Underdark mouth rather than a den.

What that cost tactically, and it is worth knowing: the prepped low ceiling was meant to be the encounter — Huge creatures stooping, intruders arriving one at a time into a room they did not know. The played cavern has forty-to-fifty-foot ceilings, which is why [[guntrah|Guntrah]] spent the whole fight hovering at the roof taking free shots, and part of why the fight was so one-sided. If a fight in here is ever wanted to be dangerous, it has to happen in the descent or the throat, not the cavern.

What it bought: the underground river and the glowing mushrooms are good, and the sheer size of it sells "this goes all the way down" far better than a den would.

DM Only — SOMETHING IS COMING UP. Live, and the party know it.

Flying a circuit after the fight, Guntrah heard grinding and stomping from the throat at the back, and it seemed to be getting closer. Vrenn's read: fomorians are tribal, two is unusual, and these may have been separated from — or fugitives from — their own tribe, "like I was."

Nothing has been decided about what is down there and nothing needs to be. Three things make it more valuable than it looks:

  • The party chose to walk away from it, knowingly. [[guntrah|Guntrah]] wanted to hold the choke point and grind; [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] said "I'm pretty sure this is Milestone" and they left. That was a decision, not an oversight.
  • There is a contract with a trigger in it. [[tito|Tito]] explicitly negotiated: "I will accept that deal for two beasts. However, if there is more than two, this deal will need to be renegotiated." Ted said "very well." More fomorians is a live claim on the dragon, and the party have a better negotiating position than they realise.
  • Ted has already told them he cannot solve it. "Anything that I send in there gets eaten." He has breathed poison down it once. He does not know how deep it goes.

It also sits next to [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]], who came up out of that country. A drow who escaped Menzoberranzan standing at the top of the hole he escaped through, as somebody else's property again, and going down it anyway — that already happened once and it was free. It is available again and worth more the second time.

Do not connect this to the Wood's past. The [[tedrovaxilliath|dragon]]–[[the_standing_treant|treant]] fight was ten years ago; the fomorians arrived this spring. Anything found here points down, not backwards.
