What
A place where the deep country comes up into [[neverwinter_wood|the Wood]]
Where
The northern edge of [[tedrovaxilliath|Tedrovaxilliath]]'s range, deliberately far from his lair
Occupied by
Two [[fomorians|fomorians]], since the spring
Ted's word for it
"A hole in the north of my range where the deep places come up"
Read aloud — the approach

A forest giant came down here a long time ago — a tree big enough that its root-plate stands two storeys high, tipped up on its edge like a wall, with soil and stone still gripped in it. What it tore out of the ground when it fell was not just a hole. It opened something.

Beneath the root-plate the ground drops away into a throat of broken limestone, and the air coming up out of it is colder than the forest and completely still, and it does not smell of anything growing.

You can smell the camp before you see it. Old kills. A great many of them.

The Layout

  • The throat. A steep scramble down through collapsed limestone under the root-plate. Twenty feet or so, awkward but not technical. Tavian in plate will make noise.
  • The antechamber. Wide and low — perhaps sixty feet across with a ceiling that runs from fifteen feet down to eight at the edges. This is where the fomorians sleep, and it is thoroughly fouled: bones stripped and cracked for marrow, hides going bad, a great deal of what a season of stolen deer leaves behind.
  • The crack. At the back, a fissure barely a body wide that goes down, and down, and keeps going. This is the thing Ted does not fit into. It is how the fomorians escaped him and how they will try to escape the party.

DM Only — running it

The low ceiling is the whole tactical point. The fomorians are Huge and have to stoop in the antechamber, which is why they chose it — anything that comes down the throat arrives one at a time, in the open, into a room its occupants know and the intruders do not. Give the party one round of arriving badly unless they scout.

[[silas|Silas]] can scout it for free. He has clairvoyance (1 mile) and arcane eye. If the party think to use either, give them the room honestly and let the preparation matter — this group has historically charged in, and rewarding the one time they don't is worth more than the surprise.

The crack is the real decision. If the fight turns, the fomorians go down it exactly as they did on the dragon, and the party has to decide how far into the Underdark they are willing to follow two wounded giants. That choice is the climax, not the hit points. Ted's job is technically unfinished if they let them go — and he will know, because the wildlife will tell him.

A note worth having ready: [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] escaped Menzoberranzan through country like this in his youth, and was tracked down and dragged back down a hole not unlike this one. If he is still with the party, standing at the top of that crack should visibly cost him something. Whether that resonance is deliberate campaign geography or a coincidence is the DM's call — but a player will notice it, so decide before they do.

What is down there beyond the crack is entirely undefined and does not need defining this session. Anything that comes up it later is free.
