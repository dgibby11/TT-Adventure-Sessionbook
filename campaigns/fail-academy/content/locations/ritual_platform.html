An open-air ritual platform, ancient and weathered, sitting on a rock outcrop like an island cut out of the earth. The ground simply stops at its edge and a moat of open lava runs the whole way around, throwing orange light up the sides. Beyond that is smoke, and past the smoke, mountains barely visible through it. Nothing else is visible in any direction at all.

Overhead: dark red sky, heavy cloud. There is rumbling underfoot at intervals, and screaming — distant, large, and more than one thing.

The Layout

  • A large runic circle worked into the stone at the centre, with concentric rings and a sigil. Several landing places are set around it — the party arrived lying on them. Nobody has gone near it.
  • Two ritual statues to one side: carved idol-busts, faces worn and unmistakably demonic.
  • Covered colonnades radiating off the centre, with small round alcove chambers at their ends.
  • A workbench in one of the colonnades, where a drow was working when the party arrived. Whatever he was building is still on it.
  • A single permanent stone bridge across the moat — no mechanism, just stone — leading to a straight causeway running away south into the haze. This is where the hooved things came from.

Present State — as of the end of [[session_3|Session 3]]

Two of [[hooved_demons|the hooved things]] dead on the stone. Most of a stone wall, with a hole knocked through it, long since expired. A spent scroll. And nobody on it.

[[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] swept his workbench into a satchel and left with the party inside about a minute, taking the vellum, the inks, the components and the [[steel_tuning_fork|steel tuning fork]] with him. They crossed the lava moat behind the two idol-busts, where the gap narrows from fifteen feet to about twelve and the stone is scuffed from previous crossings — the only way off the outcrop that is not the bridge.

Before they jumped, Vrenn filled his waterskin at the covered cistern under the workbench and made everyone else fill whatever they had. Off the outcrop the heat is punishing, and there is no water anywhere on that plain.

DM Only — what [[morvek|Morvek]] found

He arrived at dawn the following morning, as scheduled, and found two dead guards, an emptied workbench, no scroll, no tuning fork, and no drow. Nobody in the party witnessed this and nobody has worked it out; it is a DM fact, and it is the starting position for whatever he does next. See [[what_follows_vrenn|What Follows Vrenn]].

DM Only — What This Place Actually Is

Thanatos, the 333rd layer of the Abyss, where Orcus resides (campaign_arc.json's premise). This is a fabrication and ritual outpost — one of the sites where the corrupting items seeded around the Academy this year are actually made. It belongs to Morvek, a lesser lich who runs this operation on the Abyssal side and to whom the central dais is, in practice, an altar. Morvek answers to Harthoon one tier up; that is background and Harthoon has no presence here.

Why the site is HERE (DM, 2026-09-02). The boundary between planes is thin at this spot, which is the whole reason anyone built on it — a shipping operation needs somewhere planar travel actually works. Casting a travelling spell anywhere else on this layer fails and consumes the scroll. The only other place the party will learn of is the House basement, which manages it a different way: amplification glyphs riding the building's arcane infrastructure. Getting into the Abyss is easy; getting out is not — the constraint is on leaving, which is why the package could fire from a supply cupboard at the Academy and land them here. See session_3.

The platform is habitable, and that is deliberate (DM, 2026-09-02). Off the outcrop the heat forces Constitution saves against exhaustion every hour; on it, nothing. The reason is a covered cistern behind the workbench, kept clean — the site was built to be worked in, because somebody has to work in it. Food arrives with the consignments on the same cadence the goods go out. This makes [[the_captive_drow|the drow's]] warning actionable: fill everything before you leave, because there is no water anywhere out there. See session_3, "The Heat".

Hard constraint: Morvek does not appear in person. Any lich is well past a level 10 party. He is felt as an absence and an authority — the altar, marks bearing his name, the drow's reaction to it — never a body in the room.

Player knowledge as of 2026-08-20: that this is somewhere in the Abyss (DM ruling 2026-08-24 — the lava moat, the idol-busts and the sky were enough, no roll needed). They do not have: the layer's name, Orcus, Morvek, or any idea what the site produces. Nobody examined the workbench, the statues, the dais or the tools.

The way out — REVISED 2026-08-29 (DM rulings). This supersedes the 2026-08-19 "forked rod set to a delivery lane" design, which is preserved intact in session_2.html's Carried Forward block. Plane shift's material component is, verbatim, "a forked, metal rod worth at least 250 gp, attuned to a particular plane of existence." That object is the tuning fork at [[the_captive_drow|the drow's]] workbench — the only one on this site, not consumed by casting, and carrying most of the operation's capital value. It is why Morvek comes in person.

The runic circle is NOT an exit (DM, 2026-08-29). It is the altar and the receiving end — how Morvek reaches in, how finished goods are collected. It does not fire on demand and it was never the drow's to work. His answer if a player points at it: "That's not for going. That's for him." The old "force the dais under an Arcana check" exit is retired.

What replaces it: the drow can transcribe a fresh plane shift scroll — roughly an hour's work at dawn, with prepared vellum, prepared inks and the fork. One scroll only, and Morvek is coming for it. Full mechanism, the destination and the cost to him: see [[the_captive_drow|his entity]] and planning/session3_draft.html.

The fork leaves with whoever holds it, because the component is not consumed. That makes taking it a genuine choice at the moment of exit — physical proof and a future asset, against the one thing standing between the drow and Morvek's temper. Either way it is not an escape hatch: plane shift is 7th level and the party's highest slot is 5th. They will carry the means to go home and be unable to use it for a long time. Do not soften that.

SUPERSEDED BY PLAY (Session 3): the party took the second option, walked east, and left through [[the_house|the house]]'s basement circle the same night. There was no dawn, no second scroll, and no return to this platform. The fork went with them — see [[steel_tuning_fork|the steel tuning fork]] — and so did Vrenn, which was not supposed to be possible. The design as written below is preserved as the record.

Exits, as they stood at the start of Session 3: the Session 2 scroll was spent — the drow gave it away unprompted and [[silas|Silas]] burned it on a natural 1. The runic circle is not an exit. That leaves exactly two, and the drow names both: hide out and come back at dawn for a new scroll, or the structure east, which has a way out in it and is not undefended. The southern causeway leads deeper into the site, not to safety. The alcoves are defensible dead-ends and the best place for the drow to actually talk.

Everything the party failed to examine is still physically here and reachable during a fighting retreat: the half-finished items on the workbench matching the campus incidents, an empty crate matching the one from the Annex, a maker's mark bearing Morvek's name on the altar or stamped into the tools (DC 13 Investigation), and tallies showing shipment frequency climbing (DC 15 Arcana or History). Do not re-seed these elsewhere yet.
