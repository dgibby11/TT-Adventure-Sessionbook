Currently
The party is still standing on it, with no way home

An open-air ritual platform, ancient and weathered, sitting on a rock outcrop like an island cut out of the earth. The ground simply stops at its edge and a moat of open lava runs the whole way around, throwing orange light up the sides. Beyond that is smoke, and past the smoke, mountains barely visible through it. Nothing else is visible in any direction at all.

Overhead: dark red sky, heavy cloud. There is rumbling underfoot at intervals, and screaming — distant, large, and more than one thing.

The Layout

  • A large runic circle worked into the stone at the centre, with concentric rings and a sigil. Several landing places are set around it — the party arrived lying on them. Nobody has gone near it.
  • Two ritual statues to one side: carved idol-busts, faces worn and unmistakably demonic.
  • Covered colonnades radiating off the centre, with small round alcove chambers at their ends.
  • A workbench in one of the colonnades, where a drow was working when the party arrived. Whatever he was building is still on it.
  • A single permanent stone bridge across the moat — no mechanism, just stone — leading to a straight causeway running away south into the haze. This is where the hooved things came from.

Present State

Two large corpses on the platform. Most of a stone wall still standing, with a hole knocked through it. [[the_captive_drow|The drow]] still crouched under his workbench. A spent scroll. And, by his own estimate, about a minute before roughly fifteen more of the things arrive.

DM Only — What This Place Actually Is

Thanatos, the 333rd layer of the Abyss, where Orcus resides (campaign_arc.json's premise). This is a fabrication and ritual outpost — one of the sites where the corrupting items seeded around the Academy this year are actually made. It belongs to Vrask, a lesser lich who runs this operation on the Abyssal side and to whom the central dais is, in practice, an altar. Vrask answers to Harthoon one tier up; that is background and Harthoon has no presence here.

Hard constraint: Vrask does not appear in person. Any lich is well past a level 10 party. He is felt as an absence and an authority — the altar, marks bearing his name, the drow's reaction to it — never a body in the room.

Player knowledge as of 2026-08-20: that this is somewhere in the Abyss (DM ruling 2026-08-24 — the lava moat, the idol-busts and the sky were enough, no roll needed). They do not have: the layer's name, Orcus, Vrask, or any idea what the site produces. Nobody examined the workbench, the statues, the dais or the tools.

The way out (LOCKED 2026-08-19, still entirely unspent). Plane shift needs a forked metal rod attuned to the destination plane. This site ships finished goods to the Material Plane and therefore must own one — it sits in or beside the central dais as part of the working anchor, and it is set to a delivery lane rather than to any chosen place. There is more than one lane. The Academy's consignment has already gone out (it was the package in the Annex), so the rod is currently set to the other one, which terminates in Neverwinter Wood. That is why they will land where they land, and it means somebody else is being supplied out there too — a real clue, available without a roll if the party thinks about it, and a quiet seed for Thundertree.

The rod is portable and they may take it. It is proof and a future asset, but not an escape hatch: plane shift is 7th level and the party's highest slot is 5th. They will carry the means to go home and be unable to use it for a long time. Do not soften that.

Exits, current status: the dais is untouched and now the only real one. The drow's scroll is spent — he gave it away unprompted and [[silas|Silas]] burned it on a natural 1. The southern causeway leads deeper into the site, not to safety. The alcoves are defensible dead-ends and the best place for the drow to actually talk.

Everything the party failed to examine is still physically here and reachable during a fighting retreat: the half-finished items on the workbench matching the campus incidents, an empty crate matching the one from the Annex, a maker's mark bearing Vrask's name on the altar or stamped into the tools (DC 13 Investigation), and tallies showing shipment frequency climbing (DC 15 Arcana or History). Do not re-seed these elsewhere yet.
