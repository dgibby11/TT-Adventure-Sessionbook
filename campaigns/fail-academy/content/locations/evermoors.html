West and north across the [[river_rauvin|River Rauvin]], beginning within sight of the Academy's northern bank
Terrain
Rolling heath, bog, standing water, and broken rock; almost no cover and almost no landmarks

Off-limits. Not "discouraged" — off-limits.

Overview

Where the [[high_forest|High Forest]] gives the Academy its southern horizon, the Evermoors give it the opposite: cross the [[river_rauvin|Rauvin]] and the land opens out into grey, wet, treeless moor that runs to the edge of sight. Students who have never left campus still know what the Evermoors look like, because on a clear day you can stand on the north bank and see a great deal of them at once, and there is nothing in that view to hold the eye.

The moors have a reputation older than the Academy and considerably worse. Trolls are the reason most often given, and it is a good reason — but the moor kills people who never see one. It is exposed, it is wet, the footing lies about its depth, and a fog can arrive in the time it takes to notice it arriving.

Relation to the Academy

The Evermoors are the one nearby feature the Academy is genuinely unambiguous about. Field exercises do not cross the river northward. There is no faculty-supervised exception, no upper-year privilege, and no version of the paperwork that ends in approval. The overland road to [[silverymoon|Silverymoon]] keeps to the river's near side for exactly this reason, and takes a little over two days doing it.

This produces the standing dare that every cohort reinvents: not to cross the moors, which nobody proposes, but to cross the river, stand on the far bank, and come back. Whether anyone has actually done this is the sort of thing every year is certain about and no year can prove.

DM Only

Created 2026-08-15 as part of the geography correction — the Evermoors were referenced in planning docs (the old, superseded "Evermoors border" placement of the Academy) but had no entity of their own, so they never appeared in the Index despite being one of the most visible landmarks from campus.

Deliberately written as scenery and boundary rather than content: there is no adventure hook seeded here, no hidden site, no reason for the party to go. The value is that it explains the shape of the map — the Academy is hemmed by deep forest to the south and impassable moor to the north, with a single viable road running the narrow ground between them toward Silverymoon. That constraint is worth having established before any travel arc.

If a use is ever wanted, the standing river-crossing dare is the natural door, and trolls are the obvious threat — but nothing currently depends on it.
