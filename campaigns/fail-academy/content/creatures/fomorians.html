What
Two fomorians — Huge, misshapen Underdark giants, deformed by an ancient curse
Where
Camped just inside [[underdark_breach|the Breach]], at the northern edge of [[tedrovaxilliath|Tedrovaxilliath]]'s range
Since
The spring. They came up and stayed
Why they matter
They are eating [[neverwinter_wood|the Wood]]'s game, and the dragon cannot reach them

They came up out of the deep places and did not go back down. Since then they have been taking deer, elk and anything else large enough to be worth the walk — and, crucially, not hunting so much as stripping. Ted makes a point of the distinction and expects nobody to understand it.

They are enormous, bent, and asymmetrical, with one eye grown larger and more intelligent than the other. They are not stupid. They are cruel in a patient, planning way, and they have already beaten a dragon once by the simple method of going somewhere he did not fit.

DM Only — the fight

Two fomorians, CR 8 each. 3,900 XP apiece, 7,800 doubled to ~11,700 adjusted for a pair — four level-10 PCs sit at Hard 7,600 and Deadly 11,200, so this is just past Deadly: the same number as the two oni in Session 3. That fight produced one PC at 0 with two death saves. Price this one the same way.

Do not let them take it unrested. If the party skip the long rest and go straight at the cave, drop one fomorian or give the second a reason to be elsewhere.

Shape of the block (pull the real one before the table): Huge giant, AC 14, around 150 HP each, darkvision 120 ft, Multiattack of two greatclub swings — or one club and an Evil Eye.

EVIL EYE is the thing to plan around. A magical curse on one creature it can see within 60 ft, Charisma save or take psychic damage and suffer a lingering curse. Against this party that is pointed:

  • [[silas|Silas]] has CHA 8 and 72 HP at AC 11. He is the obvious target and he will fail.
  • [[guntrah|Guntrah]] has CHA 12, though a Cloak of Protection helps.
  • [[tito|Tito]] is nearly immune — CHA 18 plus satyr Magic Resistance means advantage on every save against a spell. RULED (2026-09-24): Magic Resistance DOES apply, even though a curse is not strictly a spell. He has earned it and it is a nice moment for the player.
  • [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]]'s Aura of Protection is the counter: +3 to all saves for anybody within 10 feet of him. The tactical answer to Evil Eye is for the casters to stand next to the paladin, which is a lesson worth letting them learn in real time.

Terrain is the encounter. They fight in a cave they fit and the party does not — see [[underdark_breach|the Breach]]. Low ceilings in the antechamber mean the fomorians stoop and their reach matters less; the crack at the back means they can disengage downward exactly as they did on the dragon, and if they run, the party has to decide how far into the Underdark they are willing to follow. That decision is the real climax, not the hit points.

THEY CAN BE TALKED TO. INT 9 is not stupid — they can bargain, and the DM is prepared for the party to recruit them against [[tedrovaxilliath|Ted]] rather than kill them. They speak Undercommon and Giant, and no PC speaks either; the keys are [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] (drow, Undercommon), Tito's Universal Speech, or Silas's free daily comprehend languages. They will agree to nearly anything and betray the party at the first profitable moment — the hoard is right there. Full branch in [[session_4|the session file]].

TIMELINE — do not let the cave speak to the Wood's past. The [[tedrovaxilliath|dragon]]-[[the_standing_treant|treant]] fight was about ten years ago; these two arrived in the spring. They were not here and they know nothing about it. Anything found in this cave has to point down, not backwards.

The better hook, and it has no timeline problem: why did they come up NOW? They are recent arrivals from a long way below, and nothing has been decided about what pushed them or sent them. Evidence about the deep country — something they carried up, something they are avoiding, something following them — costs nothing, contradicts nothing, and pays off the crack at the back of [[underdark_breach|the Breach]] rather than the forest's history. It also sits next to [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]], who came up out of that country himself. Optional; entirely undecided.
