Location
[[jungle_site|Jungle Field Site]]
First encountered
[[one_shot_3]] (prior party)

Multiple dinosaur species inhabit the jungle field site, escalating in aggression. During the prior party's extraction they entered a blood frenzy — triggered by the [[sunken_fang_spear]] — and a Triceratops charged through the river in pursuit, breaching campus through the portal doorway before faculty killed it. The breach itself is common knowledge campus-wide; the cause (the spear) is not.

DM Only

Something in the jungle activates with the Sunken Fang Spear. Creatures can breach campus through unstable portals — a reusable threat vector.
