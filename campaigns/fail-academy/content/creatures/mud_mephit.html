Overview

A smaller, weaker cousin of the Dust Mephit, formed where earth and water mix rather than dry stone. Not currently scripted into One Shot 5's Elemental Combat beat (that's the [[earth_elemental|Earth Elemental]] and three [[dust_mephit|Dust Mephits]]) — kept on hand as an easy reserve encounter if the party needs a lighter fight, or a wetter alternate site is used instead.

STR 8 (-1), DEX 12 (+1), CON 12 (+1), INT 9 (-1), WIS 11 (+0), CHA 7 (-2). Skills: Stealth +3. Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10. Immune to poison and exhaustion/poisoned.

Combat Profile

  • Death Burst: Explodes on death — each creature within 5 ft. makes a DC 11 DEX save, restrained until the end of its next turn on a fail
  • Slam: Melee, +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. — 4 (1d6+1) bludgeoning
  • Mud Breath (recharge): DC 11 DEX save vs. one creature within 15 ft. — restrained until the end of the mephit's next turn on a fail

DM Only — Tactical Notes

Much softer than its Dust cousin — CR 1/4, no ranged blind, no innate spell. A pack of 2-3 is a fair easy-to-moderate skirmish for a Level 5 party on its own. Mud Breath restraining a PC is its only real trick; use it to peel someone away from a more dangerous fight if pairing it with something else.

Not tied to a specific scripted moment — reach for this one if you need a quick, low-stakes combat beat elsewhere in the expedition, or as a substitute if the ridge encounter needs to be softened on the fly.
