Overview

Small, cackling elemental spirits of choking dust and grit, usually found in the company of something bigger that stirred up the ground. Three of these swirl up from the disturbed earth on the [[nether_mountains|Nether Mountains]] ridge alongside the [[earth_elemental|Earth Elemental]], simultaneous with its eruption.

STR 5 (-3), DEX 14 (+2), CON 10 (+0), INT 9 (-1), WIS 11 (+0), CHA 10 (+0). Skills: Perception +2, Stealth +4. Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12. Immune to poison and exhaustion/poisoned; vulnerable to fire.

Combat Profile

  • Death Burst: Explodes on death — each creature within 5 ft. makes a DC 10 DEX save, taking 5 (2d4) bludgeoning on a fail, half on a success
  • Claw: Melee, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. — 4 (1d4+2) slashing
  • Blinding Breath (recharge): DC 10 DEX save, 15-ft. cone — target blinded until the end of the mephit's next turn on a fail
  • Sleep (1/day): Casts Sleep (DC 10, Charisma-based), no components required

DM Only — Tactical Notes

Three of these plus the [[earth_elemental|Earth Elemental]] is a hard encounter for a Level 5 party (per One Shot 5's Elemental Combat beat) — treat the mephits as the elemental's harassment layer: they fly, so they can stay out of melee range and use Blinding Breath on whoever's fighting the elemental to strip them of a clear shot. Falling rocks/dust already reduce visibility per the scene's own description — Blinding Breath compounds that.

Death Burst rewards focus-firing them down rather than ignoring them — a dying mephit next to a PC still deals a little damage, so don't let three of them die in the same 5-ft. square as a low-HP ally without warning the player.

As played: the actual [[one_shot_5|One Shot 5]] ambush ran four of these instead of the planned three — noted here in case the count matters for a rematch or a "there are more of these somewhere" hook.
