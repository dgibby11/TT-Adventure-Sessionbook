Overview

A primal spirit of soil and stone fused into a towering, roughly humanoid mass of rock and ore. Earth elementals are usually bound or summoned rather than encountered wild — this one erupted from a rocky ridge above 8,000 ft. on the [[nether_mountains|Nether Mountains]] expedition, triggered by [[oswald_crumb|Professor Crumb]]'s excavation vibrations while he collected samples.

STR 20 (+5), DEX 8 (-1), CON 20 (+5), INT 5 (-3), WIS 10 (+0), CHA 5 (-3). Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., passive Perception 10. Immune to poison; vulnerable to thunder; immune to exhaustion, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, unconscious.

Combat Profile

  • Earth Glide: Burrows through nonmagical, unworked earth and stone without disturbing the material
  • Siege Monster: Deals double damage to objects and structures
  • Multiattack: Two attacks, using Slam or Rock Launch in any combination
  • Slam: Melee, +8 to hit, reach 10 ft. — 14 (2d8+5) bludgeoning
  • Rock Launch: Ranged, +8 to hit, range 60 ft. — 8 (1d6+5) bludgeoning; Large or smaller target falls prone on a hit

DM Only — Tactical Notes

CR 5 vs. a Level 5 party is a genuinely dangerous fight on its own — paired with 3 [[dust_mephit|Dust Mephits]] and a "difficult terrain from falling rocks + reduced visibility from dust" battlefield (per One Shot 5's Elemental Combat beat), this reads as hard, not moderate. Don't pull punches, but remember [[oswald_crumb|Crumb]] casts Shield if directly struck and can absorb one hit if the party needs the breathing room.

Rock Launch knocking a PC prone into difficult terrain is the elemental's best tool for isolating a target — use it on whoever is closest to Crumb if he needs rescuing mid-fight.
