[[tedrovaxilliath|Ted]] took the party most of the way north himself, flattening brush as he went, and then stopped and said "this is as far as I'll go — follow this fox." It hopped out of the thicket, stood beside him for a moment looking mesmerised rather than enchanted — simply in awe of the thing next to it — and then set off at a pace four adventurers had to run to keep up with.

It brought them to the cave and sat down fifty feet short of it, and would not go closer. It then followed them in and watched the entire fight from the shadows, which nobody noticed until Vrenn pointed it out afterwards. It led them back out, two hours south to where Ted had left them and thirty minutes down his side path to [[witchs_pond|the pond]], hopped over to him, appeared to report telepathically — and Ted said "ah, very well, thank you."

The best scene it got — [[tito|Tito]]'s Universal Speech

Out of the dragon's earshot, Tito spent his once-per-long-rest Universal Speech on it and told it to slow down.

It stopped. It stood up on its hind legs, looked all around, and then stared at him like a creature seeing for the first time in its life. It has no intelligence, and it understood him perfectly. It cocked its head, and halved its pace.

Then he asked it where its master keeps his treasures. It paused, cocked its head, looked around — and stood there. He had asked a question it could not answer, and could not tell whether that meant it did not know.

DM Only — what it is for, and why it matters more than it looks

This is the Silent Spies, in one body, and it is much better than the set-piece it replaced. The prepped omen — a clearing of animals all holding still and watching — was never played. The fox did the same job by being a specific creature the party talked to, thanked, greeted and worried about, and it turned an atmospheric effect into a relationship. Keep using the fox and leave the staring-animals version on the shelf.

It is a channel, in both directions. Ted's line was "I have ears and eyes all through the forest," and the fox is the demonstration. It watched the fomorian fight from cover and reported. Assume Ted knows everything the party did in that cavern, and that anything they do in front of it gets back to him.

Its limits are exact and worth holding. It understands speech only while somebody is spending magic on it. It cannot talk back, ever. It has no knowledge of its own to give up — it can be sent, and it can observe, and that is all. Do not let it become a familiar or a translator. Its charm is entirely that it is a real fox being used as a tool by something enormous.

What Ted gets out of it is that he never has to be present. He can grant safe passage, provide an escort and keep surveillance running without going anywhere near the party — which is exactly how a paranoid territorial creature with a bad wing would prefer to work.

Unresolved, and free: whether the mesmerisation is a regional effect, active charm, or simply what small mammals do around apex predators. The DM described it as more of a mesmerisation than an enchantment and left it there. It is also the cleanest available way to show the party what living inside this dragon's radius actually does to things, if that ever needs showing.
