Key Features

  • Silver Tongue (Eloquence): Treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10 on Persuasion/Deception checks
  • Unsettling Words: Bonus action, spend a Bardic Inspiration die to subtract it from a creature's next saving throw
  • Unfailing Inspiration: A creature keeps its Bardic Inspiration die even on a failed roll
  • Universal Speech: 1/long rest, up to 4 creatures magically understand you for 1 hour
  • Countercharm: Allies within 30 ft get advantage on saves vs. Charmed/Frightened
  • Magical Secrets: Can prepare spells from Cleric, Druid, or Wizard lists in addition to Bard
  • Magic Resistance (Satyr): Advantage on all saving throws vs. spells
  • Mirthful Leaps (Satyr): +1d8 to long/high jump distance
  • Feats: Lucky (4 Luck Points/long rest — advantage on a roll, or impose disadvantage on an attack against him)

DM Notes

The party's social lead by a wide margin — Deception/Persuasion +12 with Silver Tongue effectively floors his d20 at 10 on those checks, and Magic Resistance means charm/fear-based tricks against him rarely land. Costumes ×3 + a Disguise-adjacent kit suggest he leans into impersonation and theatrics; a good fit for the more performative Phase 2 hooks (the assembly scene, playing along with Voss's "found the notes" wink).
