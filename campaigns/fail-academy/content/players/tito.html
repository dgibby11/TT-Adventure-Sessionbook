Character Notes

Alignment Neutral. Full backstory, personality traits, ideals, bonds, and flaws are still largely blank as of Session 0 beyond the relationships above — to be filled in through play.

Established at the table, 2026-08-20 ([[session_2|Session 2]]): Tito is 23, an only child of two satyr parents, and comes from Baldur's Gate — roughly two weeks away by decent wagon, though his family have faster ways of travelling and use them. His father runs a merchant house in import-export. The family is genuinely wealthy but neither flashy nor ostentatious; Tito's own framing is "Tony Soprano rich" rather than anything grander. He went to a private school, shipped off here at eighteen, and stayed on track the whole five years.

He has heard things about what his father actually does — possibly illicit, possibly illegal — but only ever as hearsay, and his father has deliberately sheltered him from it until he came of age. There is an unspoken and entirely clear expectation that Tito will come home and take over the business. It is being offered, not imposed, and he has not said no. Both parents came to Commencement, arriving a day early to catch his thesis performance; his mother asked whether he owns any shirts anymore. He begged off the family party for his own. As his mother turned away, his father winked and slipped him a sack of gold without her knowing. Tito has not counted it.

His final thesis was a performance, not a written examination — a piece of storytelling rather than music: two star-crossed lovers, both parts played by him alone, turning side to side with a different mask for each. It was one of the best in his year, if not the best, and the Bards' rows have thought of him that way ever since.

Presents as bare-chested under a red cloak with golden tassels and clasp, a dark green loincloth, and strapped hoof-guards rather than boots; ram-like horns, tight and curved, ivory shading to tan; piercing brown eyes with a yellow ring at the outer edge. Visibly ripped, with a strength modifier of +0. His fraternity, given straight after a joke setup, is Phi Beta Kappa.

Established at the table, [[session_3|Session 3]]:

  • He killed the second oni. Unsettling Words to sandbag the save, then a 4th-level blight for 30 necrotic. He also opened with a 3rd-level chromatic orb in cold, which came in noticeably light — real intel: cold is a poor choice against [[basement_oni|these things]].
  • He can convert spell slots back into Bardic Inspiration for free — no action required — and did it three times in one fight. It ate most of his lower slots, which is exactly the trade.
  • Mirthful Leaps cleared a twelve-foot lava moat without a check.
  • Three waterskins. The only member of the party who was comfortably supplied when the heat came due, and it turns out a standard waterskin is five hours' worth on that plane.
  • He was the one who pushed for [[the_house|the house]] over hiding out — "it's only an hour away, it sounds safer" — and the one who cheerfully proposed robbing a lich blind.

Established at the table, [[session_4|Session 4]]:

  • He talked the party out of a green dragon's territory, twice. "We belong here" to 26 — with a genuine double meaning (Faerûn, not the forest) that he then deliberately gave up in order to roll it straight. And "he is our prisoner", about [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]], to 30, which turned the price of passage from a person into a job.
  • And then he chose not to push, which was the smartest thing he did. Told that dragons negotiate well and get harder to move the more you ask for, he dropped "that offer is not enough" and wrote a clause instead: "I will accept that deal for two beasts. However, if there is more than two, this deal will need to be renegotiated." Ted said "very well." There is now a contract with a trigger in it.
  • He rolled a natural 1 on Persuasion and it mattered — the "we are but passing through" bow did not go over. Ruled this session: nat 1s and nat 20s apply to ability checks, both directions. His own Silver Tongue would have floored it at 10, which the table then argued about for several minutes with Tito arguing against his own feature for the fun of it. He lost.
  • He spent Universal Speech on a fox — the best scene in the session. Told it to slow down (it did, and stood up and stared at him like a creature seeing for the first time in its life), then asked it where its master keeps his treasure and got nothing, because it is a fox. See [[teds_fox|the fox]].
  • He is the treasure-hunter and it is hardening into a character trait. Actively perceiving for treasure the whole walk north, searched the fomorian cavern on a 17 with help, and found 80 gp and nothing else. "I'm trying to make a name for myself." The DM's read: a rich kid carrying a purse less full than he would like.
  • He is the only one who cannot see in the dark, which shaped the entire approach to the cave — and he solved it himself with a pre-lit, hooded bullseye lantern, unhooded as a free action mid-fight for a 60-ft bright cone plus 60 dim. Both [[guntrah|Guntrah]] and [[silas|Silas]] have offered him darkvision in future.
  • His damage has arrived. Chromatic orb (8d8, bounces) for 23 acid and 32 on the bounce; then Unsettling Words for −9 on a saving throw followed by blight for 34 necrotic to finish a fomorian. "It's all about debuffs."
  • He was the one willing to hand Vrenn over, on the straightforward grounds that a lich hunting them is worse than a dragon eating a drow. He lost that argument to the rest of the party in about four lines and did not press it.

Established at the table, [[session_5|Session 5]]:

  • He killed [[tedrovaxilliath|the dragon]]. Three phantasmal killers in three rounds, each with Unsettling Words in front of it — and the last one cast on 1 hit point, for 32. He talked it to death.
  • He owned the lie, and then told another. "It was me that said it." Thirty seconds later: such a great beast could not possibly have been deceived by four weak travellers. A 33, and the dragon allowed that he might have misheard.
  • And then he confessed, at the top of his voice, into an empty forest, with vicious mockery behind it: "We did deceive you." Nobody asked him to.
  • He was the first to say they should leave [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] with the dragon, and Vrenn agreed with him. "That's very heroic of you, and I applaud you, and I encourage this plan."
  • [[standing_ovation|The cape]] saved his life. The second breath put him down and The Show Must Go On stood him back up on 1.
  • His Ring of Free Action made him the only one who could move in that undergrowth, and the thorn vines never held him.
  • Two fireballs — 41 at 6th level, and 30 threaded past the dragon through thirty feet of forest.
  • He burned 1st-level slots for Bardic Inspiration twice to keep Unsettling Words coming. It is why the last spell landed: the dragon missed by one.
  • Three of his four Luck Points, and a natural 20 off one of them against the first breath.
  • He walked the clearing the night before looking for treasure. There was none.

DM Only — one to tell the player (DM, 2026-10-07)

He put his own Bardic Inspiration on his own rolls — the Deception against the dragon, and the save against the first breath. It is not allowed, and it was missed at the table. The results stand. Without the die the Deception was 24 against about 29.

Note for the table: as a satyr he has no darkvision — the only party member without it, which mattered in the dark of the Provisions back room and will matter again.

DM Notes

The party's social lead by a wide margin — Deception/Persuasion +12 with Silver Tongue effectively floors his d20 at 10 on those checks, and Magic Resistance means charm/fear-based tricks against him rarely land. Costumes ×3 + a Disguise-adjacent kit suggest he leans into impersonation and theatrics; a good fit for the more performative Phase 2 hooks (the assembly scene, playing along with Voss's "found the notes" wink).
