Character Notes

Alignment Lawful Neutral, leaning Neutral Good. Guntrah was abandoned as a toddler in a trade town and adopted by Silas and Breena, a working-class Gnomish couple who ran a cluttered mechanical repair shop. Growing up a massive Orc in a house built for Gnomes taught him to be hyper-aware of his size — a gentle, deliberate grace most people don't expect from someone his build. Silas ran the shop on one rule: chores and lessons before play. While other kids ran the streets with wooden swords, Guntrah sat hunched at a tiny workbench stripping gears and reassembling broken clockwork. Silas taught him that magic wasn't mystical hand-waving; it was physics, discipline, and elbow grease.

He arrived at the Academy with an artificer's acceptance already in hand. [[prof_bram_holdar|Professor Holdar]] intercepted it before it ever reached the Artificers Department — half-orc himself, and genuinely convinced, in his own warped and protective way, that the Academy's arcane departments would only mock or discard an orc. Better, in Holdar's mind, to make Guntrah undeniable the one way he trusts: strength nobody can argue with. Guntrah never fought the placement outright. He just kept training as an artificer anyway, on his own time.

[[prof_tavel|Professor Tavel]], a gruff dwarven Artificer instructor, caught him in his second year flawlessly rewiring a destroyed training golem with nothing but a bent fork — and struck a deal instead of reporting him. Officially, Guntrah's been serving an unusually long stretch of "cleaning duty" in the boiler rooms ever since, punishment for insubordination Tavel documented himself. Unofficially, that's where the real education has happened: smuggled fifth-year textbooks and the discarded components of wealthier Artificer students, [[julian_vance|Julian Vance]] chief among them. He's never had actual Barbarian levels, or actual Rage — Rage would cost him the ability to cast, and casting is the whole point. What his peers read as trance-rage before a fight is closer to meditation: he goes quiet, does the structural math, and lets his genuine Orc strength and his own infused gear supply the real violence. When a spell's needed mid-fight, it's disguised as adjusting his gear — a click of a gear, a twist of a valve, nothing anyone's caught yet. The hits are real. The rage never was.

This year's FAIL Period practical trial was the closest the act has come to slipping. Holdar expected a living trophy for the Barbarian showcase; what he got was Guntrah's scrap-built gear quietly outperforming Julian Vance's expensive kit in front of faculty and students both. Julian has an explanation for why that doesn't count. Holdar still doesn't know there's anything to explain.

He wants, eventually, to be a Forensic Artificer — the kind city detective units or high-end investigative guilds recruit outright. Where anyone else sees wreckage, Guntrah sees structural physics, chemical residue, and trajectory (see the Equipment section below).

Personality traits, ideals, bonds, and flaws beyond this are still largely blank as of Session 0 — to be filled in through play.

Established at the table, 2026-08-20 ([[session_2|Session 2]]): his construct is named Ollie — a Steel Defender, ball-shaped with a couple of stabby arms and legs, three and a half years in the building. It is very nearly the design his father showed him years ago when they were practicing, finished with [[prof_tavel|Professor Tavel]]'s help; his father recognised it on sight and said so with real pride.

Silas and Breena came to Commencement. Guntrah told them he isn't coming back to the shop yet — he wants more of the world first — and promised to return before he's an old man. They took it well, with the sadness underneath showing. His father gave him a speaking stone, paired to their own, that he'd meant to send a year ago and lost in his own workshop; they intend to use it at least once a day. His father's parting philosophy, which is the whole of the man: "Everybody needs a little bit of help from time to time. You should never turn away anybody that offers to help you."

He calls [[silas|Silas]] "Sil" — deliberately, so as not to use his father's name.

Established at the table, [[session_3|Session 3]]:

  • He reads, writes and speaks Abyssal. Common and Abyssal are his two languages and it had simply never come up. It came up in a lich's basement, where Arcana 24 on the inlaid circle got him most of the way to how it worked. It would have been extremely useful an hour earlier, when one of [[hooved_demons|the hooved things]] shouted something at seven of its friends.
  • His All-Purpose Tool becomes thieves' tools as an action, and can be lent — attunement stays with him and the tool holds its shape. That is how a party with no rogue got through two locked doors, both picked by [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] at +6.
  • He caught the ambush and said nothing. He was the only one who noticed one of the [[basement_oni|things at the workbenches]] tracking him while he was invisible, and he sat on it for a whole scene, readying an action and working out in character whether shooting first would be murder. Then he shot first, for 35 damage, and started the fight on the party's terms.
  • Flash of Genius earns its keep. Five per long rest, and he spent almost all of them on other people — a stealth check, Vrenn's deception, Silas's whisper. Ruled that it does not break invisibility.

The Holdar thread finally moved. Guntrah crossed the Commencement stage from the Barbarians with Ollie walking beside him and several of his classmates visibly feeling tricked — and then looked [[prof_bram_holdar|Holdar]] straight in the eye from the stage. Holdar looked away. He would not meet the look. What Guntrah read as shame was only half of it: Holdar turned away partly out of embarrassment — and partly out of disgust. He had left before Guntrah could find him afterward. Guntrah did not push it, and Holdar does not know that Guntrah knows. He also said a genuine, unbitter goodbye to the Barbarians themselves, and one of them — a year below — told him he thought what Guntrah had done was pretty cool.
