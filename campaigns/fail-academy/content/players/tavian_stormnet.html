Key Features

  • Divine Smite: Expend a spell slot on a melee hit for +2d8 radiant (+1d8/slot level above 1st, max 5d8; +1d8 more vs. undead/fiends)
  • Lay on Hands: 50 HP pool/long rest — heal, or spend 5 HP to cure disease/neutralize poison
  • Channel Divinity: Peerless Athlete (advantage on Athletics/Acrobatics, double carry/jump, 10 min) · Inspiring Smite (distribute 2d8+10 temp HP after a Divine Smite)
  • Aura of Protection: +3 to all saving throws for himself and allies within 10 ft
  • Aura of Courage: He and allies within 10 ft can't be frightened
  • Aura of Alacrity: +10 ft speed to himself; any ally who starts their turn within 5 ft also gains +10 ft that turn
  • Extra Attack, Divine Sense, Divine Health (immune to disease)
  • Mingle with the Wind (Air Genasi): Shocking Grasp at will; Feather Fall/Levitate 1/long rest each, no components

DM Notes

The party's frontline anchor and social heavyweight at once — AC 20 / 74 HP with Persuasion +12 and CHA 16 is an unusual but strong combo for a Paladin. Aura of Protection + Aura of Courage make him a hard target for fear/debuff-heavy encounters; DEX −2 and INT −3 are his real weaknesses (poor Dex saves, terrible Investigation). Fisher background + Common Sign Language is a nice hook for river/dock-adjacent scenes. Treat exact skill numbers here as reference only; D&D Beyond is authoritative if it matters at the table.
