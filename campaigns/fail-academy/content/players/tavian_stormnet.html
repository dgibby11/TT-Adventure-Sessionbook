Key Features

  • Divine Smite: Expend a spell slot on a melee hit for +2d8 radiant (+1d8/slot level above 1st, max 5d8; +1d8 more vs. undead/fiends)
  • Improved Divine Smite (level 11, [[session_4|Session 4]]): Every melee weapon hit deals +1d8 radiant, with no spell slot spent. Deliberate Divine Smites stack on top of it.
  • Lay on Hands: 50 HP pool/long rest — heal, or spend 5 HP to cure disease/neutralize poison
  • Channel Divinity: Peerless Athlete (advantage on Athletics/Acrobatics, double carry/jump, 10 min) · Inspiring Smite (distribute 2d8+10 temp HP after a Divine Smite)
  • Aura of Protection: +3 to all saving throws for himself and allies within 10 ft
  • Aura of Courage: He and allies within 10 ft can't be frightened
  • Aura of Alacrity: +10 ft speed to himself; any ally who starts their turn within 5 ft also gains +10 ft that turn
  • Extra Attack, Divine Sense, Divine Health (immune to disease). Divine Sense was used in [[session_4|Session 4]] on the voice in the trees and correctly returned nothing — a dragon is neither celestial, fiend nor undead.
  • Find Steed: a spectral warhorse, Roach — summoned in Session 4, telepathically linked, indefinite duration, and re-summonable as the same spirit after it drops to 0.
  • Fighting Style: Interception — established in [[session_3|Session 3]]. Reaction: when a creature he can see hits a target other than him within 5 ft, reduce the damage by 1d10 + 4. He reached for it in the basement and was correctly ruled out of range, which is the good kind of ruling.
  • Mingle with the Wind (Air Genasi): Shocking Grasp at will; Feather Fall/Levitate 1/long rest each, no components

Character Notes

Alignment Neutral Good. Full backstory, personality traits, ideals, bonds, and flaws are still largely blank as of Session 0 beyond the relationships above — to be filled in through play.

Established at the table, 2026-08-20 ([[session_2|Session 2]]): Tavian has no family. He washed up in a fishing village (still unnamed — the player names it) and was taken in there; he learned to fish, and they kept him. He came to the Academy on a scholarship. He expected nobody at Commencement, and about six people from the village made the trip — including the man who took him in — and were the loudest cheer in the building when he crossed the stage. Good shoes, no holes in the overalls, one set of good fish waders, and, unmistakably, an accent. The affection between them is real and entirely unceremonious.

Table shorthand for how he reads: an enormously likeable, charming bro whom everyone knows isn't the sharpest — the Gronkowski register. Played warm, loud, and generous, with no self-consciousness about the Intelligence 4.

[[commander_orvyn|Orvyn]]'s hook is still open. He sought Orvyn out after the ceremony and got a firm handshake and a real send-off — "I think you're bound for very good things… as long as you stay focused and hold to your oath" — closing with a deliberate restatement of the promise from [[session_1|Session 1]]: "And don't forget — I owe you another story." The story about the young tiefling boy who first set foot on the campus fifty years ago has still not been told.

Established at the table, [[session_3|Session 3]]:

  • He emptied the Ring of the Ram in one shot. All three charges into a single attack roll at +7, with a Bardic d10 behind it for 21 — 22 force damage and an oni thrown fifteen feet under a workbench. The DM's own aside: another few feet and it would have gone through the wall and brought the building down.
  • He killed the first oni with two hits from [[witness|Witness]] and two upcast Divine Smites, then used Inspiring Smite to push 20 temporary hit points into [[tito|Tito]].
  • He got [[silas|Silas]] up. Lay on Hands, one hit point, one round before the grate gave way. He had also opened the session by spending 21 points of it on [[guntrah|Guntrah]].
  • [[witness|Witness]] spoke to him. He drew the blade over the circle and asked it out loud for help, and used its Intelligence 17 in place of his own 4 — Insight of Ages, firing for the first time. Established as the register in which the sword reliably talks.
  • He shared his water with Guntrah, who had no container, and took himself down to three hours' worth to do it.
  • He asked [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] to come with them, out loud, mid-fight, before anybody knew that would end up happening. He also nodded at him when he chose to fight, and said it meant something.
  • Plate on stone is loud, and there was no fixing it. He floated find steed as a quieter option and dropped it. It nearly cost the party the walk.

Established at the table, [[session_4|Session 4]]:

  • Level 11 — Improved Divine Smite. An extra 1d8 radiant on every melee hit with no slot spent, and deliberate smites stack on top. Also a third 3rd-level slot.
  • He cast find steed and named it Roach. A translucent spectral warhorse in his own air-genasi colours — destroyed in the fomorian cave by a single greatclub swing, after being sent in as scout and lamp with light cast on it. It is a summoned spirit: it vanishes rather than dying, and the same steed returns when he calls it. Ruled: it shares his initiative and may act at the same time as him rather than strictly after. They communicate telepathically.
  • His Charisma save is absurd and it saved him. A fomorian's Evil Eye — natural 20, plus 10 — for half damage and no curse.
  • Aura of Protection is now load-bearing for the NPC. [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] worked out that standing next to him feels better and did it deliberately, picking up +3 on every save for it. It still was not enough: Vrenn failed Evil Eye at 11 with the aura and [[guntrah|Guntrah]]'s Flash of Genius both on the roll, and is now cursed.
  • Interception finally landed. Reduced 13 off a greatclub hit on Vrenn — the Session 3 ruling that put him out of range paid off here.
  • He walked up to a green dragon, asked its consent, and laid hands on its ruined wing. His immunity to being frightened is why he could. It did nothing — an old scar is not a wound — but [[tedrovaxilliath|Ted]] allowed it.
  • He is the one who remembered the mission. Mid-forest, unprompted: "We've got to touch base with Dr. Voss. She's got to be wondering where the hell we are. We went into some office and then just vanished." And he asked a dragon whether it had heard of the Academy. He still has [[ellery_voss|Voss]]'s contact list in his pocket.
  • He called the XP question correctly. Offered the chance to grind more fomorians: "I'm not a smart man, but I'm pretty sure this is Milestone."
  • Ring of the Ram: 1 charge. Rolled 1d6 for the daily recharge and got a 1.
  • He also cut a left ear off each dead giant for proof, on the ruling that a left ear is distinguishable from a right one — and then dropped them, because Ted had said he would know.

DM Notes

The party's frontline anchor and social heavyweight at once — AC 20 / 74 HP with Persuasion +12 and CHA 16 is an unusual but strong combo for a Paladin. Aura of Protection + Aura of Courage make him a hard target for fear/debuff-heavy encounters; DEX −2 and INT −3 are his real weaknesses (poor Dex saves, terrible Investigation). Fisher background + Common Sign Language is a nice hook for river/dock-adjacent scenes. Treat exact skill numbers here as reference only; D&D Beyond is authoritative if it matters at the table.
