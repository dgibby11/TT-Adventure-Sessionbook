DM Notes — the spells that will actually change scenes

  • Leomund's Tiny Hut is a ritual. He has it every night, for free, whatever he prepared. Ten minutes to cast, 8 hours, nothing gets in. Any "survive the night" scene has to be built around it existing rather than around whether he has it — see planning/session3_draft.html, Route A. Its levers are that the dome is opaque and conspicuous from outside, immobile, ends if he leaves it, and runs exactly 8 hours.
  • Alarm is also a ritual — 8 hours of free early warning. Assume it is up any time they camp.
  • Comprehend Languages is a ritual. Any written clue in a language nobody speaks is a ten-minute delay, not an obstacle. Worth knowing before authoring the next one.
  • Wall of Stone ended the Session 2 fight in one round. He also has Wall of Force (immune to damage, cannot be broken through at all) and Otiluke's Resilient Sphere. Plan encounters around these rather than walking into them.
  • Clairvoyance (1 mile) and Arcane Eye let the party scout a location thoroughly without entering it. Directly relevant to any infiltration design.
  • Darkvision solves [[tito|Tito—'s blindness for 8 hours if anyone thinks of it.
  • Protection from Energy answers the armanites' lightning lance.
  • Augury is a ritual in his book — worth remembering alongside the untaken Ring of Augury and the Savras thread.
  • He is fragile where it counts: AC 11, DEX 13, 72 HP. Anything that reaches him hurts him.

Key Features

  • Empowered Evocation: Add INT modifier (+5) to one damage roll of any Wizard evocation spell cast
  • Sculpt Spells: Choose creatures equal to 1 + spell level to auto-succeed and take no damage from his own evocation spells
  • Evocation Savant: Half cost/time to copy evocation spells into his spellbook
  • Arcane Recovery: 1/long rest, recover expended slots totaling up to level 5 (none 6th+) on a short rest
  • Cantrip Formulas: Swap one known cantrip for another from the wizard list after a long rest
  • Relentless Endurance (Half-Orc): 1/long rest, drop to 1 HP instead of 0
  • Savage Attacks (Half-Orc): On a crit, roll one extra weapon damage die

Character Notes

Alignment Neutral · Medium · Blue eyes · Silver hair. Full backstory, personality traits, ideals, bonds, and flaws are still largely blank as of Session 0 beyond the relationships above — to be filled in through play.

Established at the table, 2026-08-20 ([[session_2|Session 2]]): Silas is a half-orc — orc father, human mother, both of whom came to Commencement. His father is a man of few words and hugs rather than shakes hands; his mother cries easily. Home is Rosgaunt. He came to the Academy on a lottery place rather than by merit or money, was given a hard time for it, and nearly went home in his first year (see The Bad Semester, above). Silas himself is soft-hearted and cries easily, and knows it. He is 14 — half-orcs reach adulthood at that age — and describes himself, cheerfully, as having no notable personality traits and no flaws.

Physically: silver-and-black salt-and-pepper hair, blue eyes with a single fleck of brown that seems to move, multiple ear piercings, two small protruding lower tusks, a goatee, and a low-crowned purple hat that is now firmly part of his look. Fun-loving and a bit goofy.

[[guntrah|Guntrah]] calls him "Sil" — deliberately, because Guntrah's adoptive father is also named Silas and he doesn't want to use the name.

DM Notes

Glass-cannon evoker: INT 20, Spell DC 17, Attack +9, but STR 7 / CHA 8 make him useless outside a fight or a library. Empowered Evocation + Sculpt Spells means he can safely blast a mixed melee/caster scrum without a second thought — expect him to lean on area spells once he has more than a cantrip prepared.
