Character Notes

Alignment Neutral · Medium · Blue eyes · Silver hair. Full backstory, personality traits, ideals, bonds, and flaws are still largely blank as of Session 0 beyond the relationships above — to be filled in through play.

Established at the table, 2026-08-20 ([[session_2|Session 2]]): Silas is a half-orc — orc father, human mother, both of whom came to Commencement. His father is a man of few words and hugs rather than shakes hands; his mother cries easily. Home is Rosgaunt. He came to the Academy on a lottery place rather than by merit or money, was given a hard time for it, and nearly went home in his first year (see The Bad Semester, above). Silas himself is soft-hearted and cries easily, and knows it. He is 14 — half-orcs reach adulthood at that age — and describes himself, cheerfully, as having no notable personality traits and no flaws.

Physically: silver-and-black salt-and-pepper hair, blue eyes with a single fleck of brown that seems to move, multiple ear piercings, two small protruding lower tusks, a goatee, and a low-crowned purple hat that is now firmly part of his look. Fun-loving and a bit goofy.

[[guntrah|Guntrah]] calls him "Sil" — deliberately, because Guntrah's adoptive father is also named Silas and he doesn't want to use the name.
