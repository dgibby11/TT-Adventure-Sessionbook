From [[session_4|Session 4]] — Neverwinter Wood

  • How did [[tedrovaxilliath|Ted]] get the torn wing? The party have seen it, two of them tried to fix it, and he has never explained it. They do not know there is a story. (There is: [[the_standing_treant|the treant]].)
  • What is coming up the back of [[underdark_breach|the Breach]]? Grinding and stomping, getting closer, and the party chose to leave. [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] thinks fomorians are tribal and two is unusual — these may have been fugitives from their own tribe. [[tito|Tito]] wrote a renegotiation clause into the deal for more than two, and Ted agreed to it.
  • What is the settlement to the south-west? A fire seen twelve miles off from above the canopy; Ted confirmed a town in that direction and nothing more. The regional map suggests Logger's Camp.
  • What did Ted mean by "you belong to the West"? Said to [[tito|Tito]] in reply to a Deception of 26. It made no sense to anybody and nobody followed it up.
  • Can [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]]'s curse be lifted? A fomorian's Evil Eye deformity. He repeats the save on each long rest and it survived its caster's death.
  • Does destroying a grave token return the soul in it? Vrenn does not know. Nobody has seen one. His plan is to pit the dragon against the lich and collect it off the corpse, and he does not think it will work.
  • Where is [[morvek|Morvek]] going to get a tuning fork? Vrenn thinks there is not one left in the basement, because the party took it. That is the only reason they have days rather than hours.
  • How far do the Nine Hells now reach into this campaign? Established in play: Morvek went there personally to learn how soul coins work. Nothing else is decided.
  • What was built at [[witchs_pond|the witch's pond]]? Straight lines in old timber, in a swamp inside a green dragon's radius, and he chose to conduct business there twice.
  • Where are [[guntrah|Guntrah]], [[tito|Tito]] and [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] actually from? Asked at the table and unanswered — "somewhere down south", "wherever the gnome shop was", nothing on record. [[silas|Silas]] is Baldur's Gate via his background.
