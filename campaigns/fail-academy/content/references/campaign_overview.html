Setting Summary

FAIL Academy is a magical combat academy built on systems that were never fully understood, are no longer fully controlled, and may have been corrupt from the beginning. The school hides dangerous things, runs real off-campus operations, and treats students as expendable resources. One Shots 1–5 followed one party of students through a string of incidents that ended in that party being quietly compromised. That party has since graduated/retired; a new party of fifth-years — about to graduate themselves — is now picking up where the world (not that specific party) left off. See [[party_overview|The Party — Overview]] for the current roster.

What the Current Party Knows

The new party has no personal investigative history with any of the above — they know it the way any other student does, as campus history/rumor, not as something they lived through or extracted from anyone under pressure. Safe to surface to the CURRENT table:

  • A few years back, a group of students got sealed in during a detention and barely made it out — the story's been retold enough that details vary, but everyone agrees something down there was very wrong. (rumor level of One Shot 1)
  • There's old chatter about a "Secret Society" of students who supposedly know more about the school than they let on — most people treat it as an urban legend. (rumor level of One Shot 2)
  • A dinosaur breached campus once and had to be put down by faculty — this is common knowledge, no mystery attached to it publicly. (rumor level of One Shot 3)
  • The Academy has a sanctioned faculty teleportation network for official use — this is public/administrative knowledge, not a secret.

Everything below the line — Crumb's Thornwick arrangement, the prior party's complicity, the true nature of the Secret Society/Ninth Thesis, anything about the veil — is NOT public and must be (re-)earned by the current party through play. Do not surface it just because the reader already knows it from the old campaign.

DM Only — Established Canon (permanent world truths)

  • Sealed underground areas beneath campus contain dangerous creatures.
  • Students are routinely placed in situations they are not fully informed about.
  • The administration actively covers up dangerous incidents; staff are manipulated or complicit.
  • The [[secret_society]] conducts rituals near the [[founders_statue]]; the statue is significant to the school's true nature. (Being rewritten per campaign_arc.json's founders-persona pass — the founders/Secret Society were sincerely well-intentioned, not conspiratorial; don't play this beat as villainous until that rewrite lands.)
  • The school operates remote field sites with real danger disguised as simulations; teleportation portals are unstable and can breach.
  • Multiple artifacts exist with unclear origins; creatures can be influenced or triggered by them.
  • Containment systems are imperfect and failing.
  • Prior party only (One Shots 1–5): They became notable/known campus-wide after their incidents. Faculty have their own undisclosed side arrangements with outside powers — [[oswald_crumb|Crumb]] and the [[thornwick_consortium|Thornwick Consortium]] are the first confirmed case, and the entire prior party (not just [[lugeiros_serise|Lug]]) ended up quietly complicit, each taking a 15% cut. None of this is known to, or true of, the current party — see oswald_crumb.html and thornwick_consortium.html for how each is scoped to old vs. new.
  • The Thornwick Consortium's interest in the Academy is not benign — the components Crumb supplies them are used in dangerous spellwork, not routine study. What spells, and to what end, is still undecided.
