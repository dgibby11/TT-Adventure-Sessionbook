DM Only — Party Notes

  • As long-tenured fifth-years, this party knows Academy faculty and staff on a personal level (years of history, not stranger introductions) — see campaign_arc.json's party section. They have no personal investigative history with the Phase 1/2 incidents, though — engagement should be curiosity-driven ("something's off about someone I know"), not procedural.
  • Tito's Deception/Persuasion floor (Silver Tongue treats a roll of 9 or lower as a 10) plus Magic Resistance make him very hard to fool or coerce in return — expect him to talk the party out of (or into) most situations.
  • Guntrah is the best-equipped party member for the item-forensics half of Phase 2's dual-thread plan (see phase2_the_one_who_got_away.json, thread_b_item_forensics) — Investigation +9 and Tool Expertise are tailor-made for tracing the planted items back to Torvald.
  • No dedicated healer beyond Tavian's Lay on Hands pool (50 HP/long rest) — track its depletion, since it's the party's only non-potion recovery.
