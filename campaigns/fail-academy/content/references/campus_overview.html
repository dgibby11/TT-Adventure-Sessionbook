FAIL Academy is a magical combat academy designed to train students in various forms of combat, magic, and specialized disciplines. The academy is organized into distinct departments, each housing faculty and students dedicated to specific schools of magic and combat styles.

The physical campus is laid out as a sprawling complex cut out of forest, with multiple interconnected buildings arranged around a central courtyard. The [[river_rauvin|River Rauvin]] runs a short distance beyond the northern treeline. The campus includes dedicated facilities for each of the twelve major training disciplines, and support structures such as a food court and administrative buildings.

Geographic Context

Setting
Forgotten Realms, Northwest Faerûn — present campaign year is 1500 DR
Position
Inside the [[high_forest|High Forest]], at its northwestern-most edge; south of the [[river_rauvin|River Rauvin]]
Northern boundary
The Rauvin, roughly 200 yards past the treeline; the [[evermoors|Evermoors]] open out beyond it
River crossing
Ferry or seasonal ford; the Triboar Rauvin Bridge is the only reliable crossing
Surrounding terrain
Forest on every side, but only just to the north — south and east it runs on for days and deepens
Roads
The Triboar Road crosses the Rauvin at the Triboar Rauvin Bridge, then runs southwest and west toward Triboar
Nearest city
[[silverymoon|Silverymoon]], a little over two days' walk north

The campus was deliberately situated with the river as a natural northern boundary and the forest closing in everywhere else — a shallow edge on one side, and a great deal of depth on the other three.

The [[high_forest|High Forest]] itself is held to offer the Academy a measure of protection. It is old, and the ambient magic in it runs deep enough that the woods are widely believed to turn aside much of what might otherwise reach the campus — paths that do not stay where they were left, weather that breaks against the canopy, and a general reluctance in things that mean harm to press too far in. None of this is secret or scholarly; it is the sort of thing students repeat as plain fact, and the faculty have never troubled to correct them.

DM Only

Founding records suggest this positioning was chosen by Aldric Voss for reasons beyond aesthetics — the elevated terrain to the north, and the river as a ward boundary, appear in early architectural diagrams annotated with containment notation.
