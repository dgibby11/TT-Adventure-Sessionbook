FAIL Academy is a magical combat academy designed to train students in various forms of combat, magic, and specialized disciplines. The academy is organized into distinct departments, each housing faculty and students dedicated to specific schools of magic and combat styles. It features both above-ground structures and extensive underground infrastructure, though much of what lies beneath the surface remains unknown to the general student body.

The physical campus is laid out as a sprawling complex cut out of forest, with multiple interconnected buildings arranged around a central courtyard. The [[river_rauvin|River Rauvin]] runs a short distance beyond the northern treeline. The campus includes dedicated facilities for each of the twelve major training disciplines, support structures (such as a food court and administrative buildings), and hidden systems of underground chambers and storage areas.

Geographic Context

Setting
Forgotten Realms, Northwest Faerûn — present campaign year is 1500 DR
Position
Inside the [[high_forest|High Forest]], at its northwestern-most edge; south of the [[river_rauvin|River Rauvin]]
Northern boundary
The Rauvin, roughly 200 yards past the treeline; the [[evermoors|Evermoors]] open out beyond it
River crossing
Ferry or seasonal ford; the Faculty Road bridge is the only reliable crossing
Surrounding terrain
Forest on every side, but only just to the north — south and east it runs on for days and deepens
Nearest city
[[silverymoon|Silverymoon]], a little over two days' walk north

The campus was deliberately situated with the river as a natural northern boundary and the forest closing in everywhere else — a shallow edge on one side, and a great deal of depth on the other three.

DM Only

Founding records suggest this positioning was chosen by Aldric Voss for reasons beyond aesthetics — the elevated terrain to the north, and the river as a ward boundary, appear in early architectural diagrams annotated with containment notation.
