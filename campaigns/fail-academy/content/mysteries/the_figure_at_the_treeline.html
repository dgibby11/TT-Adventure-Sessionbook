Coming out of the treeline after the bulette hunt, the party saw [[torvald_thatch|Torvald Thatch]] about fifty feet off, mid-conversation with someone. Whoever it was walked away before anyone got close enough to identify them.

That is the entire event. Nobody was named, nothing was overheard, and Torvald did not raise it.

DM Only

This was genuinely unscripted and is not tied to anything. It is not Garrick Nash. It is not any existing NPC. It was left deliberately open per the DM's own call in the Session 1 reconciliation, with no obligation to resolve it.

Keep it separate from [[the_groundskeepers_package|the package]]. The two are not connected in the design, and the temptation to retroactively make them one thing should be resisted — partly because it is the obvious move, and partly because a campaign benefits from one loose thread that means nothing yet. If it is ever cashed in, it should be because a better use turned up, not to tidy the board.

Note the party may now connect it themselves, unprompted, since Torvald has just become interesting for unrelated reasons. Let them theorise; do not confirm.
