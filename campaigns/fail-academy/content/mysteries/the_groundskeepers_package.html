Sitting unprocessed in the Intake Annex while everything around it was labelled and shelved: [[torvald_package|a plain container]] with no shipping label, no sender, and no markings of any kind — except for three things written on the outside by hand.

  1. An address. To the groundskeeper.
  2. Beneath it, in Common: an instruction that Dr. Voss is not to see this. By name.
  3. Beneath that, several lines in Celestial, which nobody read.

[[guntrah|Guntrah]] opened it. The package vanished, and so did two of the party — into a rift onto another plane.

DM Only

The Celestial was a 7th-level glyph of warding storing plane shift. As originally designed it fired on a spoken trigger word, which made it inert to anyone who could not read Celestial — aimed squarely at an interceptor rather than the recipient. That was a real, reasonable-from-the-evidence clue about the sender. The DM changed it at the table to fire on opening, which is canon now, and which costs that inference: anyone can open a box. If you still want the party to reach a conclusion about who sends these, it needs a different route.

Torvald is the campus insider distributing corrupted items, and is not evil — he is an unwitting instrument, corrupted by a cursed ring gifted by the antagonist, and redeemable if it comes off. None of that is exposed. All the party has is an address and an instruction.

His exposure is running roughly three sessions early against torvald_the_insider.json and session_plan.json, which pencilled his interrogation for Session 5. Logged as an accepted cost twice. Expect the party to reach him warier and sooner than those docs assume.
