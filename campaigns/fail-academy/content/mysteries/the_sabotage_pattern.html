Known incidents
Three inside the last year, plus whatever was in the Provisions Annex

Three separate incidents over the past year, each involving a magical artifact that turned up somewhere it had no business being and caused disruption or outright chaos. Voss's read, and the reason she pulled the four of them in at all: these were not accidents. Somebody is placing these things deliberately, and there is a pattern in where and when.

What the Party Has

  • Voss's own notes, performed aloud in her office — three incidents, a pattern she considers unmistakable, and the fact that she has been chasing a missing requisition record for one of the items ever since.
  • The record is genuinely gone from [[academy_provisions|Provisions]] — not misfiled. A clean gap where an entry should be.
  • [[osric_morne|Osric Morne]] insists the items never came through his office at all and that there is therefore no paperwork to find. He may well be telling the truth.
  • Something was in the Annex after all: [[torvald_package|an unmarked package]]. See [[the_groundskeepers_package|its own entry]].

Open Questions

  • If the items never passed through Provisions, how are they getting onto campus?
  • Who benefits? Nothing has been stolen and nobody has been killed. The incidents look designed to disrupt.
  • Is there a fourth incident nobody has connected yet?

DM Only

The answer is the supply chain the party has now physically stood inside: items primed on [[ritual_platform|the platform]], finished elsewhere, and shipped to a delivery address on campus. The route bypasses Provisions entirely, which is exactly why Osric has no paperwork and exactly why he is not lying.

The party is one observation away from most of this and does not know it — they never examined the workbench, so they have not connected what is made on the platform to what has been going wrong at the Academy. That connection is the single highest-value thing available in Session 3.

Distinguish carefully, as Voss herself does, between deliberate sabotage (this thread) and ambient veil-bleed (see [[the_bulette_that_didnt_fit|the bulette]]). She refused to merge them when [[tito|Tito]] tried, and she was right to.
