Status
Open — and left open, deliberately, by about one round
Where
The back wall of the basement of [[the_house|the house]]
Raised
[[session_3|Session 3]], the moment the room went quiet enough to hear it

[[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] mentioned it on the walk in, before anybody had seen the building properly. He has never been in that basement — he is not allowed — but he has heard something down there. Loud. Some kind of creature. He was not especially worried about it getting out.

The party found it by accident. The back of the room is badly lit, and there is a skull mounted on the wall back there. Standing still for the first time all evening, they realised the wall behind the skull is not a wall. It is a grate, and behind the grate is nothing but black.

And out of it came a low guttural snarl.

Both of the [[basement_oni|things at the workbenches]] laughed at that, softly, and the laugh did not sound human. It was the moment everything about that room reversed.

What It Did

  • When the first oni died, the cage rattled and snarled — whatever is in there started hitting it, hard, and got very agitated.
  • When the second oni died, it stopped being agitated and started working on the grate. The DM rolled a d6 in the open for how many rounds it had left.
  • He rolled a 1.

Three of the four bowls were charged. [[silas|Silas]] was unconscious inside the circle. The party spent their one round getting him to his feet and firing the circle, and left.

Open Questions

  • Is it still in there? Nobody stayed to find out. It had a round.
  • Whose is it? It is caged beside the circle rather than at the front door, which means it is not guarding the building — it is guarding that.
  • Is it one of the things they have been hearing? Since the moment they arrived on this plane the party has heard screaming in the distance, large and more than one thing, and nobody has ever explained it.
  • What happens to a working planar circle in a room with two dead guards, an open cage, and a lich due back at dawn?

DM Only — the loaded gun, still loaded

Behind the grate is a beast too dangerous even for this realm (DM, 2026-08-31). It is an in-case-of-emergency measure, it belongs to the landlord rather than to [[morvek|Morvek]], and it exists to apply pressure — not to be fought.

The rule that makes it work: it cannot leave the basement. Releasing it does not add a monster to the room; it makes the only exit unreachable, because the circle is in there with it. Threat and escape share a floor. That is also why it is caged next to the teleporter rather than at the door — it is defending the asset, which quietly tells the party what the circle is worth.

DO NOT STAT IT. The moment it has a block, somebody wants to fight it. If it comes out, the scene is a flight, not a fight. If a block is ever genuinely needed in your back pocket: Nightwalker, CR 20, MToF — undead, so it belongs in a lich's basement, and canonically a thing that other undead are afraid of.

Sensory detail if it is ever revisited: the wear marks are on the inside of the bars, and the stone around the frame is polished smooth where it has pressed.

What Session 3 changed

It is now awake, agitated, and one round from out as of the moment the party left. That is the state anyone walks in on. It also means the escalation this thing was designed to provide is still entirely unspent — the party never opened it, the oni never threatened to (the ward is Morvek's and was never theirs to touch), and the "a losing party cracks the gate deliberately for the chaos" option was never reached.

It is also, unheralded, a very good reason not to go back the way they came. Nobody at the table has said that out loud yet.

Its cousins are still out on the plain: [[session_2|Session 2]]'s "loud, very distant screaming monsters", plural, large, and never explained. This one is one of those, caught and kept.
