Subject
[[ellery_voss|Dr. Ellery Voss]]

Voss runs the [[fail_chamber|FAIL examinations]] and the practical midterms, and she runs them her own way. She does not answer questions about methodology. She sends students to genuine remote sites by means nobody explains. She holds a degree of independence in her professional judgement that no other senior faculty member has, and [[headmistress_dowe|Headmistress Dowe]] — who signs every expulsion and reads every disciplinary report personally — does not appear to mind.

Five years of practicals, office hours and department dealings, and none of the four have ever had a straight answer about why.

She has told them this much herself: she is loosely descended from one of the Academy's founders, there has been a Voss here in some capacity for centuries, and some of them have led [[ninth_thesis|the Ninth Thesis]].

Open Questions

  • Which founder? She did not say, and they did not press.
  • Does Dowe know about the Ninth Thesis, or is Voss operating around her?
  • Is the latitude something she inherited, something she earned, or something she is owed?

DM Only

She descends from Nerissa Voss — Aldric's younger sister and the Academy's first archivist, never one of the three credited founders — who quietly founded the Ninth Thesis around Wren Halloway's suppressed thesis. Neither Nerissa's name nor Halloway's has been spoken to this party, and both stay gated.

Dowe knows. She knowingly tolerates the Ninth Thesis operating outside her authority because she trusts Voss's judgement — not because she is being deceived, and not because she is covering anything up. That is a much better answer than a conspiracy and should be allowed to land as one.

This mystery exists mainly so the app has somewhere to hang the question the party has been circling since Session 1 without ever formulating. There is no urgency to it and no scene that resolves it; it pays off gradually as the Ninth Thesis material surfaces.
