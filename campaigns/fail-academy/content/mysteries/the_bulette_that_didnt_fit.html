[[session_1|Session 1]], underground, in tunnels it dug itself

A bulette in the woods near campus, tracked and killed by the party. It hit far harder than a creature of its kind should have, and it fought underground on its own terms — burrowing, ambushing from below, several holes, no willingness to meet anyone in the open.

[[tito|Tito]] tried to fold it into [[the_sabotage_pattern|the sabotage pattern]]. [[ellery_voss|Voss]] pushed back. She logged it as a separate, unresolved category rather than confirming a connection, and did not explain why.

Open Questions

  • Why was it like that? Nothing was planted, nothing was placed — this is the first escalation the party has seen that nobody arranged.
  • Why is Voss so certain it belongs in a different box?
  • Is it going to happen again, and to what?

The party harvested its scales, which [[guntrah|Guntrah]] ruled usable in geomancy. Nobody has tried to sell them to [[oswald_crumb|Crumb]] yet.

DM Only

The bulette was corrupted, not possessed — the word matters. There is no intelligence riding it. It is ambient bleed from the destabilising veil: abnormal aggression and toughness from sheer proximity, nothing more.

Voss is right, and she cannot say why without giving away far more than she intends to. Her distinction between deliberate sabotage and ambient bleed is the load-bearing one in Phase 1, and the party sorting those two categories out for themselves is worth more than being told.

Tito's own in-character theory — a subplot to overthrow the Academy — is his guess, not truth. Neither confirm nor deny; let him keep chasing it.
