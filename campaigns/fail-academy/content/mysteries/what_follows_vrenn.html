[[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] told them plainly, on the ash plain and again at the foot of the basement stairs, why he could not leave with them.

What he said, before any of it happened

"He has my — it's basically the Abyssal equivalent to a soul coin. He has another name for it."

"I cannot go with you. I hope you understand that. I would love to. I would love to break free from this predicament, but I cannot go with you. If I were to leave the plane he would know immediately, and he would follow me, and he would kill all of you."

Then two [[basement_oni|oni]] dropped their faces, and he took a side. He cast into the fight, he stripped a lich's basement of everything that could be carried, and he charged two of the four bowls himself. By the time the circle was ready there was nothing left for him on that plane except a workbench he could no longer credibly return to, and about one round on the clock.

He came with them. He did not choose it. [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] had already asked him to.

Open Questions

  • Does [[morvek|Morvek]] know where he is right now? Vrenn believes it absolutely and says so without hedging — which is not the same as it being true. He is repeating something his owner told him.
  • How long is "follow me"? Vrenn has just spent a whole evening explaining that planar travel almost never works, that it needs a thin place, and that the Spellplague made it that way. Whatever comes has the same problem getting here that the party had getting out.
  • Does anything change now the components are gone? He emptied that basement into a satchel on purpose, to slow down whatever came next. He does not know what else was down there.
  • What happens to the person who is standing next to him? That was his stated reason, and it was an argument about protecting them — from a man who had handed four strangers his only scroll ninety seconds after meeting them.
  • Can the thing be taken off Morvek? Nobody has asked. Nobody has seen it. They do not know its name.

DM Only — this needs deciding before Session 4 opens

The object is a Grave Token and it is a beacon, not an anchor (DM, 2026-08-26/29). Nothing physically stopped him crossing. It tells Morvek where its soul is, always. Full detail on [[morvek|Morvek's entity]] and in planning/vrenn_the_captive.json.

The locked design said "he will leave the site, he will not leave the plane." The table overruled it, on the fiction rather than by accident, and the transcript wins. That is not a problem to fix — it is the campaign cashing its best promise a phase early, and it did so because the players earned it. But it does mean a decision that was comfortably in the future is now due.

Three shapes, and they are meaningfully different campaigns:

  1. Slow — recommended. Morvek knows, and cannot easily act on it. Crossing planes is constrained by exactly the rule Vrenn just explained to the party, and the party has personally taken the fork and stripped one end of the lane. He has to arrange it. Weeks, hanging over the whole of Phase 2. Preserves the hard "Morvek never appears in person" constraint, makes the party's own theft the thing buying them time, and gives Vrenn room to become a character rather than a countdown.
  2. Fast. Something arrives within a session or two — not Morvek, who is far past a level-10 party, but something sent. Keeps the pressure honest and cashes the promise immediately.
  3. Vrenn's read is wrong. Entirely legitimate: he is repeating what Morvek told him, and Morvek is the one who benefits from him believing it. It is the only reading that lets Vrenn ever be safe — and it retires the best consequence the campaign has produced. Use only if the table needs a companion rather than a fuse.

Whichever way it goes, the party has acquired a person. A Wizard 13 with plane shift, Arcana +15, and a satchel of very expensive components is not a follower a level-10 party can carry without a stated policy on what he does in a fight. Session 3 gave the honest answer already: he acts once, decisively, out of despair rather than tactics, and then does something practical and non-combat. Hold him at that dial.

The thread underneath the thread

He sold the Token himself, and not for himself. His sister was going to die at the hands of something in the Undermountain; somebody was willing to trade for that, and what he had to trade was his soul. She is alive (DM, 2026-08-29), and whether Vrenn knows that is deliberately undecided. He does not tell this story and should not be made to — if it surfaces it surfaces later, in fragments, somewhere safe. He is now travelling with the party, which means "somewhere safe" is finally a place that can exist.

Retroactively it also means bought and sold multiple times stops being metaphor. The Token changed hands and he went with it, every time. He was never a person being traded. He was a receipt.
