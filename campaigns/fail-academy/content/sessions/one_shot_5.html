Title
"The Nether Mountains Expedition"
Status
Complete — Hired Muscle
Party
[[lugeiros_serise|Lugeiros "Lug" Serise]] · [[gunnar|Gunnar]] · [[caelum_rivenstone|Caelum Rivenstone]] · [[bloodraven|BloodRaven]]

Premise: [[ellery_voss|Dr. Voss]] kept the party after class again, praised their handling of the FAIL midterm, and recommended them to [[oswald_crumb|Professor Crumb]] for a three-day expedition to the [[nether_mountains|Nether Mountains]] to gather rare spell components.

Reality: Crumb's "academic" trip was a supply run for an outside buyer — the party was hired muscle for a smuggling errand, and he only admitted it once they'd already broken him down over it mid-climb.

Key Events

  • Voss dismissed class and personally recommended the party to Crumb; they went to his tower to volunteer. Lug tried to secretly leave Crumb a pouch of uncommon spell components as a goodwill gift — Crumb refused it to his face, so Lug left it on his desk anyway. Crumb never figured out what it was or why it was there.
  • Crumb is played at the table as a goblin well into his eighties — an almost unheard-of lifespan for his species. He normally only takes fifth-years on these trips but agreed given how strongly Voss vouched for them. Destination given: the Nether Mountains, for rare spell components. Departure set for dawn.
  • The afternoon off was spent at [[academy_provisions|The Prov]] — see Party Preparations below.
  • Day 1: uneventful march to the foothills, Lug scouting ahead. At camp, the party clocked Crumb's gold-latched lockbox; his explanation (it's the gold he pays his cohort with) satisfied the question without settling their suspicion.
  • Day 2 climb: the party noticed Crumb steering them past common components toward something specific, which triggered the first argument. They compromised — harvest some common components on the way, keep climbing while the "good stuff" window was open.
  • An hour or two later, a second argument broke out over why they were really gathering all this. The party pushed until Crumb cracked: the true buyer is an outside consortium whose name meant nothing to any of them at the time, and between his field notes and his own admissions, it became clear the components aren't just rare — they're meant for dangerous spellwork. That's the first real hint that the buyer's interest in the Academy isn't benign. Crumb offered a standing 15% cut of his profits, split evenly across the whole party, in exchange for their silence. Lug struggled with it hardest but folded under group pressure; the other three took the deal without much fuss.
  • Just before that confrontation, BloodRaven recalled a story: roughly fifty years ago, another professor vanished on a similar expedition, officially ruled an accident with no foul play. Pressed on it, Crumb claimed the professor sacrificed himself so Crumb and his students could escape safely. The party didn't buy it — but it's true. Not a lie, not a half-truth. That's exactly what happened.
  • On flat, high ground the party gathered the dense, rare elemental dust. Thunderous rumbling underfoot signaled it was time to leave; Crumb dawdled anyway. Lug cast Pass without Trace for the descent but let it lapse after the first hour.
  • Once the spell ended: ambush. See The Elemental Ambush below.
  • The party pushed hard for the rest of the descent to camp, but Crumb chose not to walk it — he teleported the whole group straight back to campus, landing at the dedicated teleport ring in the [[main_complex|Administration Building]], "for everyone's safety." The planned Day 3 return march, and the debrief with Voss that would have come with it, never happened.
  • Back at his office, Crumb paid the party out and that was that. Lug was visibly furious that students had been put in danger by faculty again, and came close to throwing a punch before the others got him out the door.

Party Preparations — The Prov

With the afternoon free before departure, everyone spent their credits at [[academy_provisions|The Provisions Office]] — rung up by [[osric_morne|Osric]] with his usual scowl and zero discount:

  • [[lugeiros_serise|Lug]] — a +1 longbow and two additional Potions of Healing.
  • [[gunnar|Gunnar]] — +1 studded leather armor.
  • [[caelum_rivenstone|Caelum]] — Boots of Elvenkind.
  • [[bloodraven|BloodRaven]] — a +1 helm, an ornate bronze war-helm with a stylized crest — the kind of thing a king wears into a fight nobody expects him to survive.

Player sheets have been updated to reflect the new gear; see each character's entry for full stat changes. See [[boots_of_elvenkind|Boots of Elvenkind]] for the item entry.

The Elemental Ambush

The fight came in heavier than briefed: a Large [[earth_elemental|Earth Elemental]] erupted from the ridge alongside four [[dust_mephit|Dust Mephits]] — one more than planned. Gunnar took a serious beating before Crumb, of all people, turned the fight with a phenomenal Chromatic Orb. A second tremor rolled through just as the fight ended — the same one referenced in [[nether_mountains|the mountain's lore]] as the resident blue dragon taking notice. Crumb didn't mention it, and nobody asked.

Loot & Outcomes

  • The party kept the common components harvested during the first argument's compromise; Crumb kept every ounce of the elemental granite for the Consortium sale.
  • Crumb paid the party their fee before they left his office — no haggling this time; nobody had the stomach for it after the mountain.
  • The Perception DC 16 body-in-the-crevice hook never triggered — nobody rolled it or thought to look. The [[shield_of_missile_attraction|Shield of Missile Attraction]] and the 460 gp pouch are still up there, undiscovered.
  • The whole party is now quietly financially entangled in Crumb's outside side business — a standing 15% cut each, in exchange for looking the other way. Lug took the most convincing.

DM Only — Canon Established

  • The party knows, for a fact, that Crumb sells Academy expedition components to the [[thornwick_consortium|Thornwick Arcane Consortium]] — this is no longer a hidden thread on Crumb's or the Consortium's entries.
  • The party also knows the components are meant for dangerous spellwork, not routine academic use — the first real evidence that the Consortium's interest in the Academy isn't benign. What spells, specifically, is still undefined — good material for a future reveal.
  • All four PCs get a 15% cut of Crumb's Thornwick profits, not just Lug. Lug is the one carrying the moral weight of it; the others took the money without much friction.
  • Crumb's extreme age for a goblin is now established color — worth a future hook if you want one (unnatural longevity, a pact, Consortium alchemy — undecided, don't overcommit yet).
  • The "vanished professor" story is DM-true, plainly and without qualification: the professor really did sacrifice himself covering an earlier expedition's retreat. The party doesn't believe Crumb, which is more interesting than if they did — let that distrust simmer, but don't let it curdle into "Crumb secretly lies about everything," because on this one thing he didn't.
  • The Administration Building has a sanctioned, faculty-use teleport ring, distinct from the covert [[teleportation_network|FAIL Chamber network]] from one_shot_3. Crumb used it to bring the party home early. See [[main_complex|Main Complex]] for the note.
  • Voss never got her debrief — Crumb's teleport skipped the planned Day 3 return leg entirely. She'll hear about the expedition eventually, from whichever version reaches her first.
  • The blue dragon in the northern caves is still just watching. Not spent, not escalated — available whenever you want it.

Hooks for Next Time

  • The party's 15% cut of Crumb's outside-buyer profits — a standing moral compromise that can be leaned on later (blackmail, guilt, a contact from that buyer reaching out directly). Lug is the one most likely to crack first.
  • What are the "dangerous spells" the components are for, and who's casting them? First real thread suggesting the Consortium isn't just a greedy trading partner.
  • Lug's patience with faculty endangering students is visibly running out; a repeat incident may push him past words.
  • Voss doesn't yet know what really happened on the mountain.
  • The dead adventurer, the 460 gp, and the cursed [[shield_of_missile_attraction|Shield of Missile Attraction]] are all still sitting on that ridge.
  • The blue dragon has not been resolved in any direction.

Reputation Shifts

Quiet, internal to the party rather than campus-wide this time — no public incident, no faculty scramble. What changed is trust: the party's read on Crumb (and on faculty recommendations in general) has soured, and Lug in particular is now on a short fuse about it.
