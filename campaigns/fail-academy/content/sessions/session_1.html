Phase 1 — Something is Happening, condensed into a single on-campus session

Premise: The party's final year at [[campus_root|the Academy]], a few weeks out from graduation. Entirely on-campus. They've known these people for years — this isn't a stranger's introduction to [[ellery_voss|Dr. Voss]] or [[torvald_thatch|Torvald Thatch]], it's long-standing familiarity, which is exactly what lets them clock it when something's off.

Parting Gifts

Spread across the last few weeks before graduation — quick, warm vignettes to run before the plot intrudes. Order doesn't matter; drop them between classes, meals, or downtime scenes.

  • [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] — [[commander_orvyn|Commander Orvyn]] gives him [[witness|Witness]], a legendary sentient longsword: his own late instructor's blade, its runes gone dark since he released his attunement. A formal, slightly stiff ceremony in front of the department, exactly Orvyn's style — he says nothing of who's bound in the steel.
  • [[tito|Tito]] — a small voice catches him after a rehearsal, not a stranger: [[viola_gossamer|Viola Gossamer]], a fourth-year fairy bard, gives him [[standing_ovation|The Standing Ovation]], a cloak woven from her own graduation capstone enchantment — repaying the moment, two years back, when he turned her frozen recital into an ovation. Her devotion is unspoken and she isn't about to spell it out.
  • [[zarad|Zarad]] — summoned to [[oswald_crumb|Professor Crumb]]'s office, where the pedantic goblin professor hands over [[quick_components_pouch|The Quick Components Pouch]], meticulously labeled by his own hand, with a Pearl of Power tucked inside as a "separate, footnoted" gift. As close to open affection as Crumb gets.
  • [[guntrah|Guntrah]] — not a gift from anyone else: he made his own parting gift, the All-Purpose Tool, +1 he's now attuned to. A last piece of proof-of-work before he leaves the workshops behind, not a keepsake handed to him.

The Summons

The Summons Note

A short, unexplained note reaches all four of them independently: "My office. Thursday, after last bell. All four of you. — E.V." No context.

Given how well they know her, this is already strange — she doesn't operate this way, and she has no ordinary reason to want four students from four different departments in one room.

The Empty Office

They arrive on time. She isn't there. The door is unlocked, ajar. Her handwritten notes are sitting in plain view on the desk:

Voss's Notes — Handout / Read Aloud

Three this year. Three, this school year alone — not spread across some longer span I could talk myself out of.

The catalog item — someone fed something into the midterm requisitions that had no business being there. Attracted the pack, and worse, angered them past anything natural. I've asked Provisions for the acquisition record twice. Twice I've been told it "doesn't exist." A requisition doesn't stop existing.

The component pouch in the Arcane Tower stores. Wizards and Sorcerers share that supply room and spent a week blaming each other for what happened, which told me plainly that neither of them did it. Turned the pouch over myself after. Small mark stamped on the underside — [[thornwick_consortium|Thornwick Arcane Consortium]]. I've seen that mark before and never once thought about where it came from.

An instrument, left anonymous in the practice closet. Ordinary to look at. Enchanted to feed on its own sound — play it past a few minutes and whoever's holding it starts wanting to hurt something. Found because a rehearsal nearly ended in a fistfight over nothing.

The Academy's never been silent — I've read the incident logs back generations, and something goes wrong here every so often, has for centuries. That's not what stops me. What stops me is the shape of it. The old incidents are scattered — years apart, no common thread, easy enough to write off one at a time as bad luck. These three are not that. Three, inside a single year, and every one of them traces back to something that shouldn't have been where it was — placed there, not natural. That's not the campus's old checkered luck repeating. That's a pattern, close together and all the same shape, and patterns have authors.

"A secret, once it is understood for what it is, is a kind of power." I keep coming back to that line. Read it again last night. She wasn't writing about ghosts or thieves. She meant something living here, wearing the ordinary shape of the place, using its silence as cover.

If she was right — and I think she was — then something is doing exactly that. Somewhere on these grounds. I don't know what. I don't know where. I have three incidents, one old thesis, and a very bad feeling that we've been standing on top of something for three hundred and fifty years and calling it a floor.

Need more before I bring this to anyone. Cross-reference incident dates against the exam schedule and faculty travel logs. Start with Provisions — someone signed for that catalog entry.

— E.V.

[Whether she left these findable by accident or on purpose should read as genuinely ambiguous while the party is reading — play it straight here. The wink-wink version of this same move is Phase 2's job, not this session's.]

Voss's Return — The Ninth Thesis

She arrives to find them mid-read and doesn't pretend otherwise. The reason for the summons becomes obvious once they've seen the notes: she's recruiting them, as [[ninth_thesis|Ninth Thesis]] field operatives, to work off-campus once they graduate. She already has operatives. She believes this party can succeed where her existing people haven't.

The Guntrah Spotlight

She singles out [[guntrah|Guntrah]] directly — his Investigator background and his instinct for untangling exactly this kind of mystery make him, in her eyes, the party's biggest asset for this work. Say it to him, not about him.

She names the Ninth Thesis outright — a real recruitment pitch loses credibility if she's cagey about what she's even asking them to join — but stays vague on deep specifics: founding history, other members, full scope. Consistent with a woman who has never explained her methodology to anyone. [Whether the party answers on the spot or this stays a standing offer they sit with is left open — no need to force a yes/no at the table.]

DM Only

Nothing here needs to touch Corvin Ashworth, [[torvald_thatch|Torvald]], or [[thornwick_consortium|Thornwick]] specifics to land — "there's a dangerous pattern, my people haven't cracked it, I need outside help" is the whole pitch.

A Second, More Immediate Ask

Before they leave, she raises something else — same visit, same sitting. Not a trade, not leverage: a direct, genuine appeal. Faculty and rangers are already stretched thin managing the fallout from the three incidents above — parents asking questions, the administration wanting quiet reassurance, nobody with hands free for anything new. And now something is loose near the campus edge.

A bulette — displaced from wherever it denned, agitated rather than settling — has been working its way toward the [[rangers|Rangers Department]] and the treeline of the [[high_forest|High Forest]]. She doesn't have people to spare for it properly. She has four students she trusts, right now, not just as a future promise. She asks them to deal with it before it gets closer.

Torvald's Escort

[[torvald_thatch|Torvald Thatch]] meets them, same day, and walks them out toward the Rangers Department himself rather than sending word ahead — this matters enough to him to do in person. He's exactly as he always is: direct, economical, doesn't over-explain himself.

Torvald's Tracking Read

He confirms what the rangers have already flagged — fresh tracks circling the longhouse, large, three-toed, not from anything the rangers keep — and leads them along the treeline toward where the sightings cluster. He doesn't editorialize about what it means. He doesn't need to.

The Debrief

The Debrief

Back at her office, Voss's tone shifts entirely from the earlier pitch — no wink, no more asks. She tells them plainly to put this down and focus on finals. Graduation is close; she'll be in touch as it nears.

DM Only

In the meantime, off-screen, she reaches out to trusted [[ninth_thesis|Ninth Thesis]] operatives working the region — Silverymoon, Everlund, wherever she has a contact who owes her a favor — asking them to run the [[thornwick_consortium|Thornwick]] name and stamp against whatever trade and merchant records they can access. What comes back over the following weeks is real but thin: the name is genuine and active, attached to legitimate-looking component and specimen orders moving through at least Silverymoon and Everlund, but with no ownership record, no founding date, no name anyone can put to who actually runs it — which sharpens her unease rather than resolving it. None of it points at the Academy, at Crumb, or at anyone on campus; her operatives have no reason to look there.

What does point at Crumb is her own memory, not their research — the nagging "I've seen that mark before" from her original notes eventually resolves itself: she placed it on crates near his tower before one of his expeditions, years ago, and thought nothing of it at the time. She adds "talk to Crumb about his sourcing — if only to rule him out" to her working list. Not an accusation. Professional diligence from someone who reads incident logs for a hobby.

This is her own legwork feeding directly into Phase 2's Ninth Thesis investigation thread (the alumni/Thornwick-stamp cross-reference that eventually surfaces the campaign's antagonist) — see phase2_the_one_who_got_away.json. She withholds all of this from the party for now — not coyness, a genuine call to protect their focus through finals — but see the optional beat below if you want them to glimpse it anyway.

She doesn't stop them from seeing it and doesn't draw attention to it either — she's mid-thought, not performing. If nobody mentions it, it's just color for now; if someone does, resist letting it become a full scene here — a raised eyebrow and "huh, wonder what that's about" is plenty for Session 1. It's the seed for whenever the party gets curious enough to act on it themselves (see thornwick_consortium.html's Session 1 baseline note and [[oswald_crumb|Crumb's]] Contact paragraph).

Reputation Shifts

Quiet and personal, not campus-wide. The party's read on Voss deepens — she's not just a professor anymore, she's someone with a real ask and a real trust in them. Torvald's reliability is reinforced, nothing more, nothing less — exactly as it should read at this point in the story.
