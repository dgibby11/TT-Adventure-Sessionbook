Premise: the job was done and the bargain was kept. All that was left was a night's sleep and a walk south-west, toward a fire seen from above the canopy.

They killed [[tedrovaxilliath|Tedrovaxilliath]]. He told them at dawn that they were not leaving, turned down [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]]'s offer to stay in their place, and breathed poison across the clearing as they ran. Vrenn stopped, turned round and froze him in place with a spell. The four of them came back for him, and five rounds later the dragon was dead. [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] went down and was brought back. [[tito|Tito]] and [[silas|Silas]] finished the fight on 1 hit point each.

1. The Walk Back, and a Voice From the Academy

Evening, behind [[teds_fox|the fox]], with [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] still bent and slow from the fomorian's curse and doing his best to keep up. Somewhere on that walk a message arrived in [[silas|Silas]]'s head — words, but not in anybody's voice.

[[ellery_voss|Voss]], by sending — to Silas

"Voss. Yesterday's sending failed, which should not be possible. Where are you? What is your condition? And what did you find? Twenty-five words. Choose them."

[[guntrah|Guntrah]]'s guess at why the first one failed: "because we weren't on the same plane." The reply was put together by three of them, with [[tito|Tito]] supplying the direction:

The reply, as near as the recording gives it

"Came from the Abyss. Believed in Neverwinter Wood. Heading south-west, to a town."

That was all of it. It is one message there and one back, with no conversation after, unless she chooses to cast again.

DM Only — what that reply did not say, and why hers failed

Nothing about [[torvald_thatch|Thatch]], the package, [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]], a lich or a dragon, and no answer to "what is your condition" or "what did you find". Voss now knows a plane, a forest and a direction. If the reply had named Thatch or the package it would have been the most important thing she had heard in a month; it did not.

Why yesterday's failed is still unknown to them. It was the dome — Tiny Hut blocks sending. Guntrah's theory is wrong, nobody corrected it, and the fact is still yours.

It went to Silas on a d4. [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] holds her contact list and is the one who has been asking after her; he did not get the message.

2. What To Do With Vrenn

[[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] asked how he was. Not well, to look at, and in decent spirits all the same: "I'm okay. I'm just trying to figure out what to do next." Asked whether he regretted coming through the portal, he said he had not had a choice — whoever casts that spell has to go with it, and with [[silas|Silas]] down he had not been willing to risk anybody else trying.

Vrenn, on how long they have

"Could be tomorrow, could be three more days. I don't know. He's not usually one to do dirty business himself — he usually contracts things out. But this, he might just come himself."

"Regardless, I don't want you involved in that. He is not someone to trifle with. You should not be near me if and when he shows up."

"He can scry on me. He has the ability to know where I am at all times, with that grave token. If he scries on me he will probably see you, and I don't know what he's going to make of that. If he sees the dragon with me, I don't think he'll come for me."

It was [[tito|Tito]] who said it out loud: "We can leave Vrenn here with the dragon." Vrenn nodded. He had been thinking the same thing — "stuck between a rock and a hard place. Might not be so bad working for the dragon instead of working for a lich."

Tavian would not have it: "Vrenn, come on, you're part of the crew. Stay with us." — "I'm not. I'm not, frankly. If I follow you any further beyond today, I'm putting you at risk, and I'm not going to do that." Tito applauded the plan. [[guntrah|Guntrah]] thought five of them could protect him from a lich. Tavian: "I never leave anyone behind." Guntrah closed it: it is Vrenn's idea and Vrenn's choice, so sleep on it.

3. The Stone

In the clearing, before the dome, [[guntrah|Guntrah]]'s [[speaking_stone|speaking stone]] woke up.

[[silas_and_breena|Guntrah's father]], on the stone

"Gun, it's your father. Tried the stone yesterday, got nothing. Your mother's fretting. Are you eating? Do you need anything, son? Anything at all?"

Guntrah: "Dad? No, I'm good. I'm actually eating well. Just out here at my new job, with the boys."

The stone carries twenty-five words each way and then goes quiet until the next dawn. Nobody else in the party had seen it before, and [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] pointed out to [[silas|Silas]] that Gun was talking to a rock. It is not a rock. Settled at the table: a rounded bronze ball, engraved all over in swirls, with a piece that slides out to speak into and back again when he is done — his father's work, and it looks like it.

4. The Second Night

  • [[tito|Tito]] walked the clearing looking for treasure and found thorn, and growth that does not get out of the way. The green mist was still on the ground, a little thinner than it had been near the dragon.
  • [[guntrah|Guntrah]] chopped the last of the thrown log and lit the fire inside the dome again. Then he powered [[ollie|Ollie]] down, opened him up, and fitted a spring launcher loaded with three tinkered stones: two that shed light and one that stinks.
  • [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] took [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] aside to talk him into staying with the crew. Vrenn's answer started somewhere else.
Vrenn and Tavian, by the fire

"You've left out probably the most important part, which is that I'm responsible for all of this. If I hadn't done what Morvek asked me to do and mailed that package, none of this would have happened."

Tavian: "That's no fault of your own. I understand following orders."

"I believe in free will. If at any moment I choose to stop working for Morvek, I can do that — which I did, frankly, when I cast the spell and left the plane."

"I can't say I've ever had friends in my life. I guess you have probably come as close as anybody."

Tavian rolled a 9. Vrenn did not want to be rude about it: "I'll think about it."

The dome went up at dusk. In the morning [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]]'s Ring of the Ram had all three charges back.

5. The Curse Lifts

[[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] knew what was wrong with [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]], out of a half-remembered Academy lesson on common curses and maladies: the Evil Eye, and what it leaves behind. He sat up with him, laid a hand on him and talked him through the night. By morning it was gone. Vrenn woke up as himself — "this is awesome, I'm back to myself" — without knowing that was why.

6. Dawn — A Lie for a Lie

The dome dropped, and [[tedrovaxilliath|Ted]] was there the same way as the morning before: his head pushed out through the canopy to the north-east, looking down at them.

Tedrovaxilliath

"You told me that you belonged here. You told me that one was your prisoner."

"I was within hearing the first night, before your little dome went up. He is your friend. You said so. Several of you said so."

"I told you that you might leave my forest. I said it exactly the way you said that he was yours."

"A lie for a lie. That is the whole of what I owe you. You are not leaving."

[[guntrah|Guntrah]] tried first: sometimes prisoners become friends. "You don't persist in this lie, do you?" He tried again — they had been prisoners in Vrenn's realm, and then he in theirs, and after a while you come to admire people. Ted went straight past it: "So you lied when you said that he was the one I smelled the Abyss on, when it was all of you?" — "I'm not from the Abyss." — "I didn't ask if you were from the Abyss."

Guntrah and [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] both pointed out that neither of them had said the word prisoner. [[tito|Tito]]: "It was me that said it." Offered the chance to be honest instead, he chose the other thing.

Tito, to a green dragon, for a 33

"You must be hearing things. I am shocked that a green dragon of your nature could think that us four weak party members could deceive such a great beast. What we told you is the truth."

Ted: "Perhaps I did. Regardless, you are not leaving this forest."

It worked on his vanity, not on his belief. He let the accusation go and kept the verdict, and began to draw his head back into the trees.

7. Vrenn's Offer

Vrenn, to the dragon

"Wait. Wait, wait, wait."

"I don't know how much you've heard, but my soul kind of belongs to a lich, and he's sort of looking for me. These guys don't want me to leave — or at least most of them don't — but I think it's in my best interest if I'm not with them anymore."

"I know you were going to make a deal, earlier, for me to stay with you. If that offer's still on the table — I can just stay here, and you can let them go?"

Ted: "That offer is no longer on the table."

He went on withdrawing. Then there was no sound from the trees at all.

DM Only — that was the way out, and it missed on a 3

Vrenn's Persuasion is +0 and he rolled a 3. You said so to the table afterwards: this was always going to be the way out, the odds were poor with him arguing on his own, and it could have happened. Nobody backed him. [[guntrah|Guntrah]] had Flash of Genius, [[tito|Tito]] had Bardic Inspiration to give, and four people could have spoken up for the offer. [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]]'s only move was a tear.

This is the moment the plan stopped describing the session. The 2026-10-05 decision was that Vrenn stays in the Wood either way, and that Ted is "probably persuadable to Vrenn". He was not persuaded, and Vrenn is on the road with them.

8. Tito Tells Him

The party stood in an empty clearing, working out which way the fire had been. [[tito|Tito]] decided, on his own and without discussing it, to shout into the trees — with a cantrip behind it.

Tito, at a dragon he could not see

"We did deceive you, you little bitch. You've got to be the dumbest dragon we ever saw."

Nothing came back. Complete silence. "I knew he wasn't going to let us leave."

9. Running

All three who tried to read the situation read it the same way: fighting a dragon is dangerous, fighting one in its own lair is worse, and this was a moment to flee. [[guntrah|Guntrah]] made the plan. Go west, not south-west. As they reach the trees, [[silas|Silas]] puts a fireball into the north-west corner and [[tito|Tito]] puts one to the south; [[ollie|Ollie]] fires the two light stones past the flames; and while the dragon is looking at all that, they turn south.

They got to five feet short of the tree line. The breath came across the whole clearing from behind them. All five kept their feet under it — two of them on natural 20s — and all five took 40 points of poison for half. A failed save would have been 80, which is more than Silas or Tito had.

  • [[guntrah|Guntrah]] healed himself and went straight up on his Winged Boots, shouting "Over here, you green bastard!" to draw it off, having told [[ollie|Ollie]] to protect the others. The table's verdict as he left the ground: "That's suicide, man."
  • [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]] cast haste on himself and found it does nothing about undergrowth. [[tito|Tito]]'s Ring of Free Action does, and he was the only one moving freely.
  • The dragon came through the clearing after them on foot, as fast through his own forest as they were slow in it. He can fly, on a ruined wing, and does not unless he has to.
  • [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] did not follow. He stopped at the edge of the trees, turned round, and stood there waiting for the dragon.

10. Hold Monster

He had been holding a spell for the moment the dragon came into view. It landed. Mid-stride in the middle of the clearing, the largest thing any of them has ever seen simply stopped — and Vrenn stood at the tree line, straining, holding it there.

The four of them heard the crashing stop, and looked back. It took them less than a minute to decide. [[tito|Tito]]: "Oh, we're staying and fighting." [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]]: "This sounds like a glorious moment." [[silas|Silas]], on about half his hit points: "We're going to do a little bit of damage, and then he's probably going to break free and just kill us." [[guntrah|Guntrah]], from the air: "If they go, I go."

DM Only — the die, and the warning

The dragon's save was a 4, for 11 against DC 18. Silas's player was shown the die and read it as an 18; you read 4 and said it must have turned when you reached over. It stands as a 4, and the whole fight hangs on it, so the second hold monster was rolled in the open.

You told them plainly before initiative: "I don't want to kill everybody, I'm just putting that on the table." And before that: this is not a fight you are probably going to win head-on. They chose it anyway, all four, and that is the best thing about the session.

DM Only — the fight from your side

  • 397 points of damage in five rounds. He was not bloodied at 160, bloodied at 195 and still up at 365, which puts him between 366 and 390 hit points. You told them he is your own block, "a little bit of each" of the adult and the ancient: one would have been dead twenty minutes earlier and the other would have wiped them.
  • Both hold monsters landed, and you said it afterwards: they would never have won without them. One paralysed round each. Nobody got an automatic crit out of either, because nobody was within five feet while he was held.
  • He was owed about fifteen legendary actions and used five — three tails and two wing attacks — plus the vines once. You said as much.

He would not spend his 7th-level slot — "that's his ticket out" — and he still has it.

  • [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] missed three attack rolls on three natural 1s. Every spell he cast that needed him to hit, missed; both that needed the dragon to fail, landed.

13. Vrenn, Sitting Down

[[guntrah|Guntrah]] went over to thank him and found him on the ground with his head on his knees and his fingers in his hair, his hood back for perhaps the first time. The dragon had been his way of getting clear of them.

Vrenn and Guntrah

"I didn't know you were capable of defeating this dragon. So maybe you are my best hope. But I don't know that I'm yours."

"You don't understand. I've spent my whole life running away from people to protect them, and I don't know what else to do."

Guntrah: "Well, it looks like we're not going to run away from you."

"Morvek could be here today, or tomorrow. If he comes today, what are you going to do? What am I going to do? I have nothing left. Well — I have one thing left."

Guntrah: "We also have friends, which is something you said you didn't have. We're part of an order, and there's people that can assist us."

"He sent me a message the other day. I didn't tell you what he told me. Well — I guess I kind of did. He told me he was coming. I made a mistake, and he was going to come for me."

"But he has my soul." — "And we'll get that back too. Killing a lich kind of sounds fun." — "I've dreamed about it for years. Not today, though."

He is short of components — what he took from the basement was not chosen for the spells he has been casting — and would like to find somewhere to get more, quickly. Guntrah's answer was the plan as far as there is one: get back to the school, where he will be protected. Vrenn: "All right. If you say so." [[tito|Tito]], who had argued for leaving him behind, agreed about the school and pointed out how far away it is.

1. The Evening — Three Voices

They walk back to the clearing, and before the dome goes up three things reach them from three directions: the Academy, a gnome's workshop, and the Abyss. Let them compare notes. Then they rest.

Read aloud — back to the clearing

The fox takes its own way back, which is not yours: under things, through things, along a deer trail that was never meant for anybody carrying a greataxe. The light is going. Under this canopy it does not set so much as drain away.

Then the clearing, exactly as you left it. Flattened grass where a dome stood. The black ring of last night's fire. Most of a tree that somebody threw at you.

The fox sits down at the edge of the grass and watches. It will still be there in the morning.

Voss — a sending. 25 words. Pick one

It arrives in one head. Pick whose.

A. "Voss. Yesterday's sending failed, which should not be possible. Where are you, what is your condition, and what did you find? Twenty-five words. Choose them."

B. "It is Voss. I tried to reach you yesterday and the spell found nothing. Where are you? Are all four of you alive? Answer quickly."

They may answer at once, in twenty-five words. Let the table write the reply together.

Guntrah's father, on the speaking stone

The stone in [[guntrah|Guntrah]]'s pocket is warm, and then it is talking.

"Guntrah? It's your father. Tried the stone yesterday and got nothing. Your mother's fretting. Are you eating? Do you need anything, son? Anything at all?"

The scry — the lens closes in

Narrate it to the table, the way you run a scry.

A long way from here, something is looking for [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]]. It starts very high. A coastline. A city. A river running inland. Then a forest, growing until it is all there is, with more rivers through it than a forest should have.

Closer. A long green shape moving under the canopy, one wing carried wrong. Closer. A clearing. A burned-out fire. Five people and a fox.

And one of the five, grey-skinned and bent, looks straight up at nothing at all.

Vrenn: "He's found me. Not the plane — here. He saw the trees and the rivers and he saw that dragon, and there is only one of those. That is enough for him."

Vrenn — the long way home

"There is a way back to your Academy that is not walking. I cross to another plane, and I cross back — and coming back, I choose where we land. Two mornings."

"But every plane wants its own fork, and we have exactly one. Find me a second, for anywhere at all, and I can take you home."

Vrenn — the two vellums

He has had them in the satchel since the Abyss: two scrolls, written out and unfinished.

"Hold monster, and the one that makes you good at something for an hour. They want one casting each to finish, and you have both spells — I looked. I meant to give you these two nights ago, and then there was a dragon."

DM Only — running the three voices

Order (proposal): Voss as they settle · the stone while they are arguing over the reply · the scry last, so the evening ends on it.

  • [[ellery_voss|Voss]] asks for whereabouts and a status report, and why yesterday's sending failed. The answer is the dome — Tiny Hut blocks sending — and her question hands the party that fact. The reply is the scene: twenty-five words, and what they choose to spend them on.

  • If they ask her to fetch them, or for the teleport ring: no. She would rather they went on to Neverwinter, and says so tomorrow (beat 3). The ring's sigil sequence is closely held and the ring is logged; if their use of it were traced to her she would almost certainly lose her job. She may give it out eventually. Not now.
  • She is an administrator, not a power. See [[ellery_voss|her entity]] before giving her anything to do.
  • [[silas_and_breena|Guntrah's father]] is only checking in: does he need anything. Run the [[speaking_stone|stone]] as twenty-five words each way, once a day. He has no idea what he is talking into.
  • The scry is [[morvek|Morvek]] finding the place. He saw one specific dragon, and that fixes it. He still does not appear — what follows is his people, on the road.

Now legible, and still unspent: the table has just watched a sending arrive and be answered. On his first night here Vrenn got one from Morvek and did not reply, out of fear. Somebody may work that out backwards. If nobody does, beat 2 spends it.

THE SAVE. At the end of the rest Vrenn repeats the Evil Eye save: DC 14 Charisma, and his own bonus is +0. Alone that is one chance in three. With [[tavian_stormnet|Tavian]]'s aura (+3, if he wakes within ten feet) and [[guntrah|Guntrah]]'s Flash of Genius (+5, his choice to spend) it is three in four, and Vrenn has Lucky. Roll it in the open and let them stack it. It will most likely pass. If it fails: Tavian can prepare remove curse, and Vrenn has seeming in his book.

If they slip away in the night instead of resting: Ted has eyes everywhere and the fox is with them. Beat 2 happens wherever they are.

2. Dawn — A Lie for a Lie

The party rests. When the dome drops, [[tedrovaxilliath|Ted]] is standing where he stood yesterday, and he tells them they are not leaving. He was lied to, and he holds that it licenses a lie in return. It ends one of two ways: [[the_captive_drow|Vrenn]] stays with him of his own accord, or it comes to a fight.

Read aloud — the dome drops

Sound passes out of the dome, not in. He can speak first, and they cannot answer: "You can hear me. I know that you can. Come out when you are ready."

The dome goes at dawn, all at once, the way it does. He is where he was yesterday: ten feet away, all of him, his head halfway to the canopy. The fox is sitting between his forefeet.

He does not ask whether you are ready.

Ted — a lie for a lie

"You told me that you belonged here. You told me that one was your prisoner."

"I was within hearing the first night, before your little dome went up. He is your friend. You said so. Several of you said so."

"I told you that you might leave my forest. I said it exactly the way you said that he was yours."

"A lie for a lie. That is the whole of what I owe you. You are not leaving."

Ted — the crack is closed

Said in passing, as a thing already done.

"The crack in the north is closed. My fox tells me something was coming up it. I brought the hill down on it in the night. There is no more work for you there, and no more bargain to be had from it."

Vrenn — the offer

To the dragon: "You don't want four of them. You want something a lich will pay for, and that is me. He holds my soul, and he is coming to this forest to collect it — you heard me say so. Keep me, and when he comes, he comes to bargain with you. Let them walk."

To the party: "The first night, when I went down holding my head — that was him, speaking to me. I could have answered. The spell lets you. I didn't dare." (a breath) "This is my answer."

"The token points at me. Every mile I walk beside you, I am bringing him to you. Here there is a dragon in the way. I'll be fine. I mean it. Go."

Read aloud — out of his country

Somewhere past the sixth mile, the forest stops leaning. The trees stand straighter. The green goes out of the mist, and then there is no mist.

The fox stops. It sits down in the middle of the trail and looks at you, and it does not take another step.

DM Only — his grounds, and what moves him

His grounds are on the Session 4 recording. On the first evening, before the dome went up, the party talked in the open clearing about Morvek, the lich, the fork, and "he's our friend" — and Ted was ruled within earshot. The fox then watched the "prisoner" cast for them and fight beside them. He may have known before he ever made the deal, and let them do his work first.

  • He does not say what he wants them for, and does not need to. If pressed (proposal): they are the most useful thing to walk into his forest in a century, and he does not let useful things go.
  • [[tito|Tito]] lies again: he will not be lied to twice.
  • They tell him the truth before he accuses them: his entity says a straight answer, given the same way twice, moves him. Proposal: it changes his manner and not his price.
  • They offer something other than Vrenn — the wing brace, news of the lich, the fork. He is probably persuadable to Vrenn. What else he would take is yours to decide. The party will not offer their own magic items.
  • They tell him a lich is coming. He heard it already. He wants Vrenn because a lich does.
  • The Breach. That nobody goes back in is the DM's rule; having Ted collapse it overnight is a proposal for getting there without it reading as a railroad. It also kills Tito's renegotiation clause, because the deal it belonged to is void. If they walk there regardless they find a hillside clawed down over the opening and days of digging.

DM Only — Vrenn stays, either way

He does not travel on with the party. He pushes hard to remain, to keep Morvek away from them, and he may simply stand his ground. The party refused to give him up in four lines in Session 4; this time it is his choice, and it is the plan he stated himself: put a dragon between him and the lich.

  • What he hands over: the two vellums if he has not already, and whatever you like from the satchel.
  • The fork stays with [[silas|Silas]]. Vrenn does not know it can be followed. Do not have him warn them.
  • [[teds_fox|The fox]] stops at the edge of Ted's range. It has no reason to go further. The players like it and may coax it; if it ever crosses, it will be afraid of his country and will not go back.

3. The Logging Camp

South-west, at the edge of the Wood: a company cutting camp under canvas, pitched beside the ruins of Thundertree. About thirty people. Suspicious, decent, and somewhat helpful. There are no secrets here — supper, a tent, plain supplies, directions, and talk at the fire. The place is on [[logging_camp|its own page]] and the people are on [[logging_crew|theirs]].

Read aloud — walking in

You smell it before you see it: woodsmoke, cut timber, and somebody's supper. Then the trees simply stop. Stumps. Open sky. A burn pile the size of a cottage, going hard.

Two rows of canvas tents. An ox line. A big open-sided tent with plank tables under it. Down on the bank, fir logs are being roped onto a barge. And beyond the last tents, roofless walls — an old town, with young trees growing up through its floors.

About thirty people have stopped what they were doing.

Agda Stroud, camp boss

"You came out of there. On foot. And you're all still here."

Once she has decided they are not trouble: "Supper and the spare tent, paid up front, gone by first light."

If anyone calls the dragon Ted: "He has a name?"

Small talk at the fire

Nobody talks at meals. It all comes out afterwards.

  • The firs. "Bends before it breaks. Grows nowhere nearer the city, and the shipwrights pay for every stick."
  • The old camp. "We were in the wood proper once. He killed twelve good men. That was years back, and we've been out here since."
  • The price of a dragon. "We asked in Neverwinter what it would cost to be rid of him. We stopped asking."
  • The ruins. "There was a dragon in there once. A young one. Adventurers killed it twenty years back, and the dead things with it. Been quiet since."
  • The joke. "That lot did for the little one. Come back when you've grown a bit and try the big one. We'll pass a hat."
  • The barges. "Bound up and floated down to the city. Nothing goes down that river without an escort."
  • The carts. "Those? Those are for us. Tents and tools. The timber swims."
  • Thundertree. "The mountain did that. Decades back, before my time."
  • Anything else. "Can't say I'm familiar."
Voss — the second sending, the next evening. 25 words

As planned: "Do not come home yet. Go to Neverwinter and find Anselm Ferreck at the magistrate's office. He is mine. I will reach you there."

If they asked for the ring: "No ring. It is logged, and four missing graduates cannot appear in it. Neverwinter. Anselm Ferreck, the magistrate's office. I will reach you there."

DM Only — the camp, if it goes sideways
  • They ask about a barge. A barge is a fast, escorted ride to Neverwinter, and this session is not meant to reach it. Proposal: no load is ready. The next barge goes when the load is bound and the escort has come up from the city, several days off. Walking is quicker.
  • They take escort work on the next barge. A natural offer from people who cannot afford adventurers. If they wait for it, beat 4 happens at a night mooring instead, and the session still ends short of the city.
  • They go into the ruins. They are quiet. The young green dragon and the zombies of twenty years ago were killed by earlier adventurers, and nothing is waiting. An empty walk before supper, unless you want something found there.
  • The joke about killing the Green lands on a party that has just fought him, or has just left a friend in his keeping. Somebody will answer it. "He's dead" and "he's our friend" both land hard on people who lost twelve men to him.
  • They ask for a ride. The carts are the camp's own and are going nowhere.
  • They skip the camp and follow the river west. Beat 4 still finds them.
  • Vrenn is still with them. Agda's terms tighten: he stays where she can see him.
  • Potions. There are none to buy here.

4. The Road

West from the ruins, toward Neverwinter. Three elite drow assassins have been on the party's trail since the edge of the Wood, and they set the ambush where they choose. They are [[morvek|Morvek]]'s, and they want [[steel_tuning_fork|the fork]]. On one of the bodies is [[tac_credit_note|a credit note]]. The session ends on the road.

Read aloud — the old road

The road is older than the camp and older than the ruin: packed earth over cobbles nobody has reset in fifty years, wide enough for a cart. Behind you the Wood is a dark line. Ahead there is a very great deal of sky.

It is the first time in days that you can see where you are going.

The ambush — three elite drow assassins

They pick the hour: dusk before the dome goes up, dawn as it drops, or deep shade. Never open sun.

If they talk first: "The steel fork. The one that came out of his basement. Put it on the road and walk away from it."

On the body — the credit note

An oilskin wallet inside the leader's coat. In it, one sheet of heavy paper, folded in three. A sum written out in a clerk's hand. Pay the bearer. No name on it anywhere.

And pressed into the corner, a maker's stamp: three letters worked into one — a T, an A beneath it, a C hooked through — set in a diamond with a small spike at each corner.

Show them [[tac_credit_note|the mark]]. Do not name it.

Where it stops

The road goes on west. Somewhere ahead of you, not yet in sight, is the first city any of you has seen since you put on your rings.

DM Only — the drow
  • Whose they are: agents of [[morvek|Morvek]] from somewhere nearby — this country sits over a great many drow. He gave them their orders the moment he learned where Vrenn had gone. The stat blocks are the DM's own.
  • How they found the party: the fork brought them to the edge of the Wood. From there they are expert trackers following footprints, and getting rid of the fork on the road does not shake them. They assume the party has it until Morvek tells them otherwise.
  • If the fork was left with Vrenn, they may come anyway. Morvek has a grievance beyond the fork: the escape, and the damage done to his operation.
  • If the party hand it over: it may not end it. Decide whether these three were paid for the fork or for the party. Either way Morvek is no longer blocked, and the party has given up its own way home.
  • The dome. Nothing gets into it, so there is no night ambush while it stands. Drow fight badly in sunlight. [[tito|Tito]] cannot see in the dark.
  • A prisoner speaks Elvish and Undercommon; Tito speaks Elvish. A hired blade very likely knows nothing beyond the pay.
  • If the party loses: drow poison drops rather than kills. They take the fork and go, and the party wakes up alive and robbed.
  • If Vrenn is with them because beat 2 went to a fight and he did not stay: the drow want him as well.
DM Only — the note, and the mark on it

The mark is the maker's stamp of the [[thornwick_consortium|Thornwick Arcane Consortium]], the surface end of the operation Morvek makes goods for. His hired blades were paid through one of its fronts, and the note is drawn on its brokerage in Neverwinter. The party has never heard the name, and the name is not said. A mark with no explanation is a question they carry west. [[guntrah|Guntrah]] is the natural one to study it; [[tito|Tito]] is the natural one to want it cashed.

  • One note, on one body. The other two are clean.
  • The sum is yours to set. Proposal: 500 gp.
  • Where it can be cashed is not written on it. How Tito finds out is a later conversation.
  • If nobody searches the bodies, or one escapes with the note, have a second way for the mark to surface: stamped inside a scabbard, or on the wax of a vial.
  • The camp set this up. The loggers are paid by company note cashed in Neverwinter, so the party has already been told what a note like this is.
