Session 1: Smoke Check

SESSION · Planning

The fixture session. It exists to be launched by the Session Runner, to populate Prompts / Plan / Pinboard, and to make the Complete-Session reveal dialog reachable.

Opening

Read: "You are standing in [[tf_root]]. Nothing is happening, and nothing is going to." This is the first prompt card; if the Prompts panel is empty, extraction of .session-prompt has broken.

Cross-links

The party meets [[tf_npc]] of [[tf_faction]], who is carrying [[tf_item]]. All three links must resolve — any of them rendering struck-through means the cross-link resolver regressed.

Encounter

An [[tf_creature]] is discovered in [[tf_hall]]. It does not attack. It has no statistics worth using.

Closing

End the session and open the Complete-Session dialog. Its checkboxes come from this session's reveals[].

Key Beats

  • Launch the Runner and confirm Prompts, Plan and Pinboard all populate.
  • Confirm the Pinboard lists this session's related[] entities.
  • Open Complete Session and confirm the reveal checkboxes are grouped by type.

DM Only

A .dm-only block inside session content, so the Runner's handling of secret blocks is covered too. Mentions [[tf_mystery]].

Tone

Flat, synthetic, and entirely uninteresting. That is the point.
